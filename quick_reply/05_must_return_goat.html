Replacement Process

  • We will process your replacement and ship it as quickly as possible.
  • You will receive an email with your tracking number within 5-7 business days.
  • Once shipped, your replacement typically arrives within 5-10 business days, though delivery times may vary based on your location.

Your Current Unit

A return shipping label for your current unit will be issued and sent to you in a separate email after we dispatch the replacement unit to you. In the meantime, we will begin processing your replacement and arrange for shipment at the earliest opportunity. Once we arrange the shipment, the tracking details will be sent to you from a separate email address as soon as they become available.

After you receive the replacement unit, you can return the original unit using the return label provided. If you did not receive the label, please let us know, and we'll be happy to resend it.



Packing Guide

Please ensure to remove all accessories/consumables/add-Ons(Blades/Trimmer Brush/Trimmer Line/Garage/Wall Mount/Cellular Module) before returning. Kindly send back the machine with the charging station, along with the RTK if you have one. We will not be held liable for any accessories/consumables/Add-Ons that are shipped back to our center.

Warranty Details

  • The replacement unit will carry over the remaining warranty period of your original machine or be covered for 90 days, whichever is longer.
