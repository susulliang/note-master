OZMO Roller Leaving Water Marks During Mopping

Thank you for reaching out regarding the water marks from your DEEBOT with OZMO Roller Mop. Please find below step-by-step guidance to help address this issue:

1. Clean the Dirty Water Box

  • Hold down the blue latch at the back of the DEEBOT to release the Dirty Water Box.

Turn the Dirty Water Box over, press down and then lift up the outlet to allow dirty water to flow out.

If any water remains, open the cover and empty the box directly.

Inspect the small filter inside the Dirty Water Box, as well as the interior, for any blockages. Rinse both the filter and the inside of the box to remove residue or debris.

Reinstall the filter and the cover, then securely place the Dirty Water Box back into the DEEBOT.

2. Clean the Water Tray Filter

  • Remove the filter from the water tray.
  • Rinse thoroughly under running water and ensure it is free of debris.
  • Reinstall the filter securely before restarting the device.

3. Check the Roller Mop

  • Debris: Inspect the roller mop for any entangled debris. If found, remove it using the cleaning tool or a flathead screwdriver.
  • Heavily Stained Mop: If the mop is heavily stained with oil, we recommend washing it manually before reuse.

4. Common Causes of Water Marks

  • Floor Material: High-gloss surfaces such as tiles or marble absorb and evaporate water differently.
  • Repeated Wiping: When the robot passes over the same area multiple times, water can accumulate.
  • Insufficient Cloth Drying: If the cleaning cloth is not fully spun dry, excess water may leave marks.

5. Recommended Adjustments

  • Set the water level to Low and select a longer interval for Mop Auto Wash Frequency.
  • Allow the mop cleaning process to finish without interruption to ensure the mop is fully spun dry.
  • Keep the floor free of obstacles during cleaning to reduce collisions.

If the issue persists after following these steps, please do not hesitate to contact us.
