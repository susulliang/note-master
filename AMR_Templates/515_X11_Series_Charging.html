X11 Series: Charging Issue

We're really sorry to hear that you're having trouble with your Deebot X11 not charging. Let's work together to resolve this issue. Please follow the troubleshooting steps below:

  1. Open the top cover and confirm that the power switch on your Deebot is turned on. The switch should be set to "I" (ON) and not "O" (OFF).
  2. Ensure the charging base is securely plugged in and has power - Make sure the power switch is pressed down to the "I" position. After plugging it in correctly, listen for the sound of the lifting rod resetting. Then, place your Deebot correctly on the base. You should see the Deebot's 3 indicator lights on the Y symbol flashing white slowly in and out, indicating it's in charging status. You can also check the charging status in the app (after connecting the robot to a 2.4GHz home Wi-Fi network) to confirm if it's charging.
  3. Remove your Deebot from the base, then press and hold the Start/Pause button in the middle of the Deebot's Y symbol for about 3 seconds to recall it to the charging base. Wait for the Deebot to announce "Wait for charging," and ensure the Deebot's 3 indicator lights on the Y symbol flashing white slowly in and out.
  4. It's not recommended to manually place the Deebot back on the charging base, as this could misalign the Deebot's charging pins with the base station's charging contacts. If charging doesn't begin, starting a cleaning cycle could cause the Deebot to attempt repositioning, which might fail due to alignment issues.
  5. Check if the charging electrodes on both the Deebot and the charging base are recessed or dirty. If so, clean the electrodes and gently adjust them to ensure proper contact.

Please follow these steps and let us know if the issue persists. If you need any further assistance, we're more than happy to continue helping you.
