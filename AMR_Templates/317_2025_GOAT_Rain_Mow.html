2025 GOAT A Series - Can GOAT Mow in the Rain?

Template ID: 00Xcz000006VCe9EAG

The GOAT is IPX6-rated and can withstand rain. However, wet grass can easily clog the blades, and rain on the camera may interfere with the GOAT's visual navigation and obstacle avoidance.

Therefore, it is recommended to enable the Rain Sensor feature in Mowing Settings so that GOAT will begin mowing only after the lawn has dried. Additionally, you can set a delayed start time, allowing GOAT to begin working a specified period after the rain has stopped and the sensor has dried.
