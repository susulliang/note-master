  1. Please check and ensure that the mop cloth is installed correctly.
    • The mop cloth should be in close contact with the mopping plate and have a smooth surface when installed correctly. If the mop cloth is installed improperly or has wrinkles on its surface, it may result in uneven mopping. If you find any installation issues, please remove and reinstall the mop cloth.
  2. Please make sure the mop cloth is clean.
    • During use, the mop cloth will accumulate a large amount of dust and dirt. If it is not cleaned regularly, it may affect the mopping effect. It is recommended to clean the mop cloth regularly, based on your actual usage, to keep it clean.
  3. Please use a cloth to wipe the water outlets at the bottom of the machine to prevent impurities from clogging.

PS: We highly recommend using lukewarm water only, as other cleaning solutions or chemicals may block the water outlet or emitters.

  • After checking that, the above conditions are fine. Kindly wash the mopping pads thoroughly and wring them out before using the mopping mode. This will prevent those streaks due to it being a drag behind the mopping system.
  • Please set the clean times to once
  • Please set the water flow rate to Low
  • Please refer to Smart/Intelligent Cleaning and set the hot air drying time to 4 hours.
