LilMilo can be charged in two convenient ways: Fast Charging and Standard Charging. Choose the method that best fits your daily routine.

Method 1: Fast Charging

  • Take out the Charging Cable from the package.
  • Open the velcro on the left side of LilMilo's belly and plug the cable into the charging port.
  • Use your own 15V/3A power adapter (must support PD3.0, QC2.0, AFC, or FCP). Connect the cable and plug it in. The indicator will turn green.
  • A full charge takes approximately 110 minutes.

Method 2: Standard Charging (via Charging Station)

  • Use the included Charging Cable and Power Adapter.
  • Connect the cable to the Charging Station.
  • Plug the adapter into a power outlet. The indicator on the Charging Station will turn green.
  • Place LilMilo on the station. A full charge takes approximately 5 hours, making this method ideal for overnight charging or long idle periods.
