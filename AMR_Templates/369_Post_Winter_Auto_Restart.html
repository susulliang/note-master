Thank you for reaching out to us about your concerns.

Typically, repeated starting/frequent alarms may be related to the battery protection feature being triggered. This can occur when the battery/temperature is low after winter storage.

To better understand your situation, could you please confirm the following:

  1. Was the machine fully charged before storage, or was it stored with battery power remaining?
  2. Was the device completely powered off at the time of storage?
  3. Was the storage or charging environment exposed to low temperatures?

In the meantime, please try the following steps to help restore your device:

  1. Allow the Device to Warm Up: Move the machine to a room-temperature environment and allow it to gradually warm up.
  2. Charge for 2 hours: Place the GOAT back on its charging dock and allow it to charge continuously for at least 2 hours. Please avoid turning the device on or off during this charging period.
  3. Test the Device: After charging is complete, attempt to power on the machine and observe whether the issue has been resolved.

Should the issue continue after completing the above steps, please do not hesitate contact us again. We appreciate your cooperation and patience.
