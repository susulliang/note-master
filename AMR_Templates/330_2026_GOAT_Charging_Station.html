Placement Conditions:
WiFi Signal: Ensure strong, stable Wi-Fi at the installation site.
Terrain & Space: Choose a flat, open area. Avoid slopes or obstacles.
For RTK models: Be aware of mirrored or perforated surfaces.
Lawn Preparation: Trim grass around the station to a height of < 6 cm (2.36").
Effective Range: The station should cover a ~2 m (6.56 ft) radius.
Side Clearance: Ensure 40 cm (15.75 in) of unobstructed space on both sides.
