Thank you for contacting Ecovacs Robotics.

If you're experiencing issues with satellite connectivity on your GOAT O1000RTK / GOAT A2500RTK, please follow the steps below to resolve the issue:

Step 1: Ensure the Latest Firmware is Installed
Make sure both your GOAT mower and RTK base station have the latest firmware.

How to Check:

  • Navigate to Settings > Robot/RTK > Upgrade to check if a firmware update is available.
  • Follow the prompts to install any updates.

Step 2: Power Cycle the RTK Base Station

  • Turn off the RTK base station for a few minutes, then power it back on.
  • This simple reset can help resolve temporary satellite connectivity issues.

Step 3: Ensure Proper Placement of RTK Base Station and Charging Station

  • Open Area: Ensure both the RTK base station and the GOAT's charging station are placed in a clear, open area.
  • Minimum Distance: The RTK base station should be placed at least 2 meters away from walls, large trees, or any other structures.
  • Clear Line of Sight: The RTK base station requires an unobstructed line of sight to the sky.

If the Issue Persists:
Please provide the following details to help us further assist you:

  1. GOAT's current firmware version (found in Settings > Robot > Upgrade)
  2. GOAT's serial number (located at the rear of the GOAT next to the QR code or at the bottom of the mower)
  3. A screenshot of any problem or alarm notification sent by the GOAT through the Ecovacs Home App
  4. A digital copy or screenshot of your proof of purchase.

Should you require any further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
