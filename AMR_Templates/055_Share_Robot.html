Thank you for contacting Ecovacs Robotics. 

To share your Deebot with a second user's phone, please follow these steps:

  1. The second user must create an Ecovacs account using their email address.
  2. The second user then downloads the Ecovacs Home app and logs in using the same email address and password.
  3. On the first user's phone, open the Ecovacs Home app and locate the Deebot in the device list. Tap the ">" button next to the Deebot's name. 

  1. Press "Share" to enter the Share Details page. 

  1. At the bottom of the page, enter the second user's email and press "Share" again. 

  1. The second user will receive an invitation in their Ecovacs Home app. They will need to press "Accept."
  2. Once the sharing process is complete, both users will be able to access and control the Deebot.

If you have any questions or need further assistance, please feel free to reach out.
