When the WINBOT runs in a bow-shaped path, it will automatically lift the scrubber. During edge-running, it will lower one scrubber in the direction of movement to effectively clean corners and edges. The lifting scrubber operates in the following cleaning modes: Deep, Thorough, Edge, Zone, and Heavy Duty Clean.

Important Notes:

  • Fast/Spot/Remote Control Modes: In these modes, the lifting scrubber does not operate while WINBOT is running.
  • Frameless Glass Cleaning: When cleaning frameless glass, the lifting scrubber will not engage.
