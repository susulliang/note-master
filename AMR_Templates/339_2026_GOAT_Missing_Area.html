2026_GOAT_Missing_Area

Troubleshooting steps for missing areas:

1. Check for Temporary Obstacles: Ensure no obstacles < 1m in GOAT's path.
2. AI Recognition Settings: Disable AI Recognition if causing false avoidance.
3. Obstacle Avoidance Height: Check Settings > Mowing Settings > Area Mowing Preferences.
4. Obstacle Clearance: GOAT has 3cm clearance (A series: 4cm).
5. Grass Height: GOAT cannot cut grass taller than 12cm.
6. Slope Steepness: GOAT cannot climb slopes steeper than 24 degrees (A series: 27 degrees).
7. Narrow Paths: Paths narrower than 0.8m may limit full mowing.
