Thank you for reaching out to us regarding the "gurgling" sound (similar to a knocking noise) emitted by the OZMO Roller Mop module. We understand your concern, and we are here to help clarify the cause and provide solutions to address this issue.

Potential Cause of the Gurgling Sound:
This unusual sound is typically caused by a small amount of residual water in the Deebot's Dirty Water Box, which may have flowed back into the air pump when the Deebot was flipped over prior to use. This can occasionally happen when the device is turned upside down.


 

What Happens Next:

  1. When you start the vacuuming and mopping function, the air pump operates continuously.
  2. The residual water from the Dirty Water Box will naturally expel through the vents at the bottom of the machine.
  3. A small amount of residual water may be left on the floor during this process, which is completely normal.
  4. The gurgling noise will gradually decrease and disappear as the remaining water is expelled, usually within 5-10 minutes.
  5. After this, the device will return to normal operation.

Suggested Troubleshooting Steps:
To avoid this issue in the future, we recommend the following:

  • Before flipping the Deebot, please ensure that the Dirty Water Box is removed from the rear end of the Deebot. This will help prevent any water from flowing back into the pump and causing noise.

Rest assured, this is usually a temporary occurrence, and the machine should work perfectly once the residual water is expelled. Should you experience any further concerns or if the issue persists beyond 5-10 minutes, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

We hope this information helps, and we are always here to assist you should you need further support.
