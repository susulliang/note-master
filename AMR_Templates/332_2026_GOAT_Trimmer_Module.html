Trimmer Brush Installation for GOAT O1000 LiDAR PRO:

1. Place the Brush: Take one of the two brushes and align it with the buckle position on the Trimmer. Install the brush until you hear a "click" sound.
2. Install the Second Brush: Repeat step 1.

Warnings:
- If the Trimmer Brush is not installed, grass pulp may accumulate during trimming.
- Do not use the trimming function at night.
