Thank you for your feedback.

Regarding the issue of the mop plates falling off when the Deebot runs over carpeted areas, we recommend the following to ensure continued smooth operation:

  • Secure the edges of your carpet: If the carpet edges tend to curl, we suggest securing them properly to prevent the mop plates from being dislodged as the Deebot passes over.
  • Use the Virtual Boundary feature: You can set up virtual boundaries in the ECOVACS app to restrict Deebot from entering/mopping specific areas where such issues are more likely to occur.

Additionally, please reinstall the mop plates and ensure you hear a "click" sound to confirm they are securely attached. Then, place the Deebot on a flat, open surface and allow it to run so you can observe its behavior.
