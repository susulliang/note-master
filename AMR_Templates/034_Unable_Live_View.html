Unable to Use Live View/Video Manager

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz0000058aLCEAY

1. Update the Firmware:
Ensure your Deebot's firmware is updated to the latest version. To check, go to Settings > About Deebot (or Settings > Robot Advanced Settings > About Your Robot > Update).

2. Check Wi-Fi Connection:
Confirm that your Wi-Fi connection is stable. The Video Manager/Live View works best in areas with a strong Wi-Fi signal. If it doesn't load via Wi-Fi, try switching to cellular data to see if that works.

3. Clear App Cache and Reinstall:
Clear the app's cache by going to Mine > Settings > Clear Cache. Then, reinstall the app to ensure you have the latest version installed.

4. User Authorization:
The Video Manager/Live View requires user authorization. Please confirm that you've agreed to the corresponding privacy agreement before using it.

5. If the issue persists, please provide the following:

  • A screenshot of the error message at the end of the loading phase.
  • A picture of the serial number of your Deebot.
