T90_Main_Brush_Guide

If you notice clicking sounds, Main Brush alerts, or poor dust pickup:

  1. Turn OFF the Device
  2. Remove the Main Brush Assembly: Press clips on cover toward center, remove cover and brush.
  3. Clean the Main Brush: Remove tangled debris.
  4. Reinstall: Align bracket with motor, push in smoothly. Rotate to ensure free movement.
  5. Reattach Cover: Press until "click" sound.
