App Connecting Issue/How to Connect

  1. Please ensure your APP is updated to the latest version. Then you may refer to the "Scan to Connect" page. Then, use the Ecovacs Home App to scan the QR code on the DEEBOT. (If you do not have the QR code sticker, you may refer to the bottom to select a robot.)
  2. DEEBOT only supports 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. If you are using a dual-band router, kindly select the 2.4GHz network when setting up, or temporarily disable the 5GHz band in your router settings.
  3. Double-check your home Wi-Fi password. We recommend using a simple combination of letters and numbers. Special characters (such as Chinese symbols or underscores) may not be recognized by DEEBOT.
  4. Follow the instructions shown in the APP during the setup. (Tip: Short-press the RESET button until you hear the voice prompt "Ready for network setup/Bluetooth hotspot has been released," then continue to the next step.)

PS: Please do not hold down the RESET button for too long (More than 3 seconds). It will reset the robot to factory settings instead of initiating the network connection.

  1. Once your phone connects to the Wi-Fi signal "Ecovacs-XXXX" (Sometimes, it will show up as Ecovacs_XXXX wants to join your network, please press "Join") broadcast by DEEBOT, you should hear the prompt "DEEBOT is connected with the mobile phone, please wait for the result in the APP." (Note: Some Android phones may skip this step.) Make sure to return to the DEEBOT APP right away and wait there—otherwise, you may need to restart from Step 2.
  2. If your home has multiple Wi-Fi networks, we recommend turning off the unused ones temporarily. This ensures your phone stays connected to the correct Wi-Fi during the setup and avoids failed connections.
