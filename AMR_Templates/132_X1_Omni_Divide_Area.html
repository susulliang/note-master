For the area dividing function, please kindly refer to the information below:

D-ToF-equipped models are generally designed with this feature. The machine will automatically scan and map your home, dividing it into different areas. Once this is complete, you can intelligently select specific areas or key zones for cleaning.

Please note the following when dividing areas manually:

  • Ensure there are no obstacles in between the area you intend to divide.
  • The area must be enclosed (i.e., a closed space).
  • When drawing division lines in the app, please make sure the points you select "hit" the walls accurately.
