Before starting the connection process, please make sure of the following:

  • Bluetooth is enabled on your phone
  • Location/GPS access has been granted to the ECOVACS app
  • The app is updated to the latest version
  • Your Wi-Fi is on a 2.4GHz network (DEEBOT does not support 5GHz)

Connection Steps:

  1. In the app, select "Scan to Connect."
  2. Double-check that your Wi-Fi network is set to 2.4GHz.
  3. Please ensure your Wi-Fi password is entered correctly. We recommend using a simple combination of letters and numbers—avoid special characters like underscores (_) as DEEBOT may have trouble recognizing them.
  4. Make sure your DEEBOT's power switch is turned to the "I" position.

  5. Press the reset button briefly for one second. You should hear the voice prompt: "Bluetooth hotspot has been released."


  6. On your phone, connect to the Wi-Fi signal "ecovacs-xxxx" broadcast by your DEEBOT.
  7. After connecting, you should hear: "DEEBOT is connected with the mobile phone. Please wait for the distribution network result in the app."

(Some Android phones may skip this step.)
⚠️ Important: Please switch back to the ECOVACS app right after hearing the prompt. If you exit or minimize the app, the connection may fail, and you'll need to start over from Step 2.
📶 Reminder: If you're using a dual-band router, please ensure your phone connects only to the 2.4GHz network. You may need to temporarily disable the 5GHz band in your router settings if it interferes with the connection.
