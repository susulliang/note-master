There is a reset button located under the cover of the Deebot. For your reference, the reset button is marked in red in the attached image.

Follow these steps to reset the Deebot:

  1. Turn on the Auto button and ensure the blue light is illuminated. 
  2. Using a sharp tool, such as a needle, insert it into the reset hole. Press and hold the reset button until you hear three beeps.
