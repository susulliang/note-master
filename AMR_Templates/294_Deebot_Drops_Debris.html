Thank you for reaching out. In order to address the issue you are experiencing, please follow the steps below to help us diagnose the problem more effectively:

  1. Power off your Deebot and uninstall the Dustbin, Main Brush, Main Brush Cover, and Suction Inlet from the device.
  2. Power on the Deebot again and ensure it's properly connected to the Ecovacs Home App, showing itself as "online."
  3. On the Ecovacs Home App, tap on "Enter" and navigate to "Settings." Under "Station Advanced Settings", verify the following:
    • Under Auto Empty, confirm that "High Frequency" has been selected.
    • Check that the Dust Collection Power is set to "Standard."

Additionally, if possible, please record a video of the issue occurring. This will help us better identify the root cause and assist you more efficiently.

Once these steps are completed, kindly let us know how things progress or if you encounter any further issues.
