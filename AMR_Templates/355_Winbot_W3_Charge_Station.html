Winbot W3 - How to Charge the Station

  1. Open the rear flip cover at the bottom of the station and remove the charging cable. Take out the black power plug and align the battery pack connector with the black socket. Securely connect it and place the connected battery pack into the corresponding slot on the charging dock.
  2. Plug the power cord into a household outlet. During the charging process, you will see a charging animation displayed on the small screen.
