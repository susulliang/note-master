Winbot_W3_Charge_Station

  1. Open the rear flip cover at the bottom of the station and remove the charging cable.
  2. Take out the black power plug and align the battery pack connector with the black socket. Securely connect it.
  3. Place the connected battery pack into the slot on the charging dock.
  4. Plug the power cord into a household outlet. Charging animation will display on the screen.
