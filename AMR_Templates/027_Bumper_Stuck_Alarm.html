Checking the Anti-Collision Bumper:

  1. Please place the DEEBOT in an upright position.
  2. Gently tap the left, middle, and right sections of the Anti-Collision Bumper with your hand, then release it to see if it rebounds smoothly.
  • If it rebounds normally, kindly wipe the bumper with a clean, dry cloth and restart the machine.
  • If it does not rebound, please factory reset the DEEBOT.
Gentle Reminder:
Please note that resetting your DEEBOT will erase all data, including saved maps. After the reset, the robot will go offline, and you will need to go through the network setup again to restore online functionality.

How to Reset Your DEEBOT:
To perform a hard reset, press and hold the Reset button (the small grey button near the power switch) for about 5 seconds. You will hear the voice prompt "Reset to default settings" followed by the startup music, which indicates the default settings have been successfully restored.

⚠️ Reminder: Before use, please make sure all protective strips have been removed from the bumper
