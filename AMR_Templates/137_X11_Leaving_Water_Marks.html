To improve the mopping performance and reduce water marks, we recommend following these steps:

1. Use Mop after Vac Cleaning Mode:
Start by removing dust and debris with the vacuuming mode before activating the mopping function. This ensures the mop cloth is working on a cleaner surface and can better absorb dirt and moisture, reducing water marks.

2. Adjust Mopping Water Volume:
Increase the water volume setting for mopping. A higher water volume ensures the mop cloth has enough moisture to effectively lift dirt and stains, which should help minimize water marks on the floor.

3. Adjust Mop Cloth Re-Wash Time:
Set the Mop Auto-Wash Frequency to 10 minutes and Wash Method to Deep Wash. This will ensure the mop cloth is thoroughly cleaned between uses, further preventing any leftover residue or water marks.

4. Increase Mopping Frequency:
If marks persist, set the mopping frequency to 2 times. This will extend cleaning time and improve coverage. Please note that if your home is large, the Deebot may need to recharge to complete the full cleaning cycle.

X11 OZMO ROLLER Mopping System Features:

Nylon Bristles for Stubborn Stains: The X11 roller features a nylon bristle ring that tackles tough stains. However, nylon doesn't hold as much water as the common mop cloth, so you may notice faint water lines that evaporate shortly after.

Deep Cleaning for Better Results: If marks remain after evaporation, set Station advanced settings > Wash Method to Deep Wash function when the Deebot returns to its base. This will clean the mop thoroughly and reduce water marks.

Understanding Water Marks Based on Flooring Type:
Flooring Material Affects Water Marks: Glossy tiles or wood floors tend to form larger water droplets due to their hydrophobic nature, while matte or textured floors absorb water more efficiently, reducing visible marks.
