Mowing frosted lawns is not recommended, so we advise against using the GOAT in winter.

Additionally, to protect the battery, the GOAT is equipped with high/low temperature protection. This feature is triggered when the battery temperature falls below 0°C or rises above 55°C, and the robot will not operate under these conditions.
