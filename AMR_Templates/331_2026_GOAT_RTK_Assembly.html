RTK Reference Station Setup Notes:
1. Location: Choose an open area, at least 2 meters (6.56 feet) away from buildings and trees.
2. Indicator Light Check: After powering on, wait for one minute.
Steady Blue: Good signal.
Flashing: Weak signal, relocate.
3. Ground Condition: Avoid soggy or mushy ground.
4. Vertical Orientation: Ensure the RTK is vertically oriented.
