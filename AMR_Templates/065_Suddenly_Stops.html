Before performing maintenance, please ensure the DEEBOT is switched OFF.

1. Check the Side Brushes:
Confirm that the Side Brushes are installed correctly.

2. Inspect the Universal Wheel:
Toggle the Universal Wheel and check for any tangles or debris. Clean it if necessary.

3. Test the Driving Wheels:
Press down on both sides of the Driving Wheel and slide both wheels to ensure they can slide/rebound freely. Clean the wheels if needed.

4. Clean the Bumper:
Tap and wipe the bumper to ensure it's free from debris.

5. Switch ON and Test:
Turn the DEEBOT back ON and confirm if it is functioning properly.
