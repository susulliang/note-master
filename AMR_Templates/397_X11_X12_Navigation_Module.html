Power off the Deebot, and verify that the highlighted protective film is fully removed before use.

Please gently wipe the navigation module with a clean, dry cloth.

Power the Deebot back on to check whether the issue persists.

If the issue continues, please perform a factory reset on the Deebot.

Gentle Reminder: Kindly be informed that performing a reset on the Deebot will result in the deletion of all data, including the saved maps. Following the reset, Deebot will go offline and will require the network setup process to be redone to restore its online functionality.

How to reset your Deebot:
To initiate a hard reset of the Deebot, press the Reset Button (a tiny grey button near the power switch) for approximately 5 seconds. You will hear a voice prompt stating "Reset to default settings," followed by the commencement of the boot music. This indicates that the default settings have been successfully restored.
