Thank you for reaching out to us regarding the connection issue with your Deebot. In order to resolve the problem efficiently, please ensure that you have followed these steps:

1. Power Off and On:

  • Turn off the Deebot by holding the power button for about 3 seconds.
  • Then, turn it on again by pressing the power button for 3 seconds, until you hear the startup music.

2. Connect via App:

  • Scan the QR code near the dustbin with the Ecovacs Home App.
  • Ensure that your phone is connected to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.
  • Enter your home Wi-Fi name and password carefully.

3. Activate Network Setup:

  • Remove the top lid/cover of the Deebot.
  • Press and hold the Spot Clean and Return to Base (Home) buttons together for no more than 1 second. 
  • Listen for the "Entering Network Setup Mode" sound.
  • If you hear it, check the app for a notification to join the Deebot's Wi-Fi (e.g., Ecovacs_xxxx). Press "Join" and proceed. If not, press the Wi-Fi button again until you hear the sound.
  • If this doesn't work, locate the Reset button under the plastic cover above the QR code and press it briefly to release the signal.

4. Confirm Connection:

  • Once the connection is established, the Deebot will say, "Connected, please return to the app."
  • Stay in the app to finalize the setup.
  • If successful, you'll be prompted to name your Deebot.

If the issue persists:
In order to better investigate the issue, it would be highly appreciated if you could send us any relevant videos or screenshots that indicate the problem.

For quality assurance purposes, please kindly provide us with proof of purchase, including the order number, the date of purchase, the purchase channel, and the model of the device.
