What Is a Certified Refurbished Product?

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000005QqePEAS

A factory-renewed product has been tested and certified by the original manufacturer to be in perfect working order, with only minor visual imperfections (if any). The manufacturer thoroughly refurbishes the product and conducts a series of tests to ensure it meets functionality and quality standards. While the product may not come in its original retail packaging, it will be repackaged with original accessories and comes with a warranty backed by the manufacturer.
