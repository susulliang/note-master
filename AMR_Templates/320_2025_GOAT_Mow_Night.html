2025 GOAT A Series-RTK: Can the GOAT mow at night?

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000006VBWnEAO

If you disable Animal Protection (Do Not Disturb) mode in the Settings, GOAT will operate throughout the day. However, the GOAT vision module might not function properly at night due to lighting conditions. As a result, areas with weak RTK signals and lawn boundaries cannot be mowed during nighttime.

By default, GOAT will automatically trigger mowing for these areas at 8:00 a.m. the following day.
