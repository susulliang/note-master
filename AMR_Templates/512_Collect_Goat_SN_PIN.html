If your GOAT shows a Lock symbol in the top-left corner of the display when you first receive it, the device is locked before initial setup and binding to your Ecovacs account.

To unlock the GOAT, please provide the following information so we can issue a temporary PIN code:

  1. A picture of the GOAT's serial number: Located at the rear of the mower next to the QR code or on the bottom of the device.
  2. Proof of purchase: A complete invoice or receipt including order date, order number, purchase channel, original seller, and machine condition.

Once unlocked, you can perform a factory reset on the GOAT and complete the setup and binding process at your own pace.

GOAT Factory Reset Instructions:

  • Power on the GOAT: Press and hold the OK button for about 3 seconds.
  • Initiate Reset: Press and hold the Start and Home buttons simultaneously. You will be prompted to enter the temporary PIN. Once entered successfully, the GOAT will reboot.
  • Note: This reset will erase all stored data (including the existing/original map) on the device, except for the accessory lifespan (e.g., blades and camera brushes).
