1. Set up your DEEBOT
Download the ECOVACS App and complete the Wi-Fi setup for your DEEBOT.

2. Prepare Alexa

  • Download the Alexa App and sign in.
  • Power on your Echo device and connect it to a Wi-Fi network.
  • Test Alexa by talking to it to confirm it's working.

3. Link DEEBOT with Alexa

  • Open the Alexa App and search for the "ECOVACS" or "DEEBOT" skill.
  • Select Enable and sign in with your ECOVACS account to link your robot.

4. Video Reference

  • For step-by-step setup guidance, please refer to this video:

Alexa & DEEBOT Setup Tutorial

Reminders

  • If your ECOVACS account already has a supported DEEBOT connected to Wi-Fi, Alexa will display it in the device list.
  • If you see the message "You have no devices in your Ecovacs account or your devices are currently not supported," it means your model is not compatible with Alexa.
  • Once setup is complete, Alexa will confirm. You can then control your DEEBOT with voice commands, such as:
    • "Alexa, ask DEEBOT to start cleaning."
