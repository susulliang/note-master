OFF THE FLOOR / Machine Suspending Alarm

If your DEEBOT displays an "OFF THE FLOOR" or "Machine Suspending" alarm, it is typically caused by either manual movement of the machine, failure to overcome an obstacle, or an object causing the machine to lift off the ground. This issue is often resolved by simply repositioning the robot on a flat surface. However, if the issue persists after ruling out these factors, a restart or reset of the machine may be necessary.

To help resolve this issue and maintain optimal performance, please follow these steps:

1. Clean the Anti-Drop Sensors:
Wipe the Anti-Drop Sensors (located in front of the Universal Wheel/little front wheel at the bottom of the machine and near the Driving Wheels) with a clean cloth to ensure they are free from dust or debris.

2. Check the Driving Wheels:
Switch the DEEBOT OFF. Press the Driving Wheels at the bottom of the machine to check if it rebounds. If it does not, inspect for any tangles or blockages and clean as needed.

3. Test in Auto Clean Mode:
Restart the DEEBOT, and place it in an open space. Try running the DEEBOT in Auto Clean Mode to ensure the robot can function without interruption.

If you have excluded all the potential causes above and the issue continues, consider performing a factory reset on the machine, following the guidelines below:

How to reset your Deebot:
To initiate a hard reset of the Deebot, press the Reset Button (a tiny grey button near the power switch) for approximately 5 seconds. You will hear a voice prompt stating "Reset to default settings," followed by the commencement of the boot music. This indicates that the default settings have been successfully restored.

Gentle Reminder: Kindly be informed that performing a reset on the Deebot will result in the deletion of all data, including the saved maps. Following the reset, Deebot will go offline and will require the network setup process to be redone in order to restore its online functionality.
