  1. Kindly clean and press down the sealing plugs. Open the clean/dirty water tank and press the seal strip on the sides. Please also check if the lid of the clean/dirty water tank is closed and press downward to ensure it is installed properly.
  2. Please short-press and hold the "Start/Pause" button on the base station and end the current task after hearing the voice prompt.
  3. Then press for about 5s the [Add/Pump water in the cleaning tank] button on the station, and after hearing the voice prompt, press again for about 5s the button again to confirm whether the dirty water can be recycled normally.

If clean/dirty water can be recycled normally, please wipe the water level sensor on both sides with a dry cloth, shut down the robot and restart it, and then use and observe it.

If the clean/dirty water cannot be recycled, please check the clean/dirty water recycling port to see whether it is blocked by foreign objects. If it is, please clean it up with the cleaning brush and process the self-cleaning of the station.

Please kindly check both tanks and make sure that the magnets behind both tanks are intact.

You may also refer to the attached videos for how to clean the sealing plugs and the dirty water tank. And please try to remove the clean sink water filter (a plastic piece) and install it, then test again.
