Deebot Won't Hold a Charge

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000005JZDhEAO

1. Power Reset
Turn the robot's main power switch to the OFF position, then switch it back ON to reset it.

2. Clean the Contacts
Wipe the robot's contacts and the charging dock pins (charging electrodes) with a dry cloth to maintain optimal charging efficiency.

3. Place on Charging Dock
Place the DEEBOT on the charging dock, ensuring that the charging contacts on the DEEBOT align with the charging dock pins for a secure connection.

4. Try Another Power Outlet
If the robot is still not charging, plug the charging dock into a different power outlet and repeat step 3.

Note:
If the robot's battery is fully discharged or has not been used for a long period, its capacity may have decreased, which can affect charging performance.
