If you're experiencing issues with the firmware upgrade on your robot, this general troubleshooting guide will help you resolve the problem and ensure a smooth upgrade process.

Steps for a Successful Firmware Upgrade:

1. Ensure Your App is Up-to-Date:
Make sure the Ecovacs Home App is up to date. An outdated app can sometimes interfere with the upgrade process.

2. Ensure the DEEBOT/GOAT is On and On the Charging Dock:
Make sure your DEEBOT/GOAT is turned on and properly placed on the charging dock. The app will display a green lightning icon next to the power display when everything is set up correctly.

3. Charge the DEEBOT/GOAT or Ensure the Battery is Sufficient:
Charge the DEEBOT/GOAT for at least 2 hours or ensure the battery is above 50%.

4. Check Internet Connectivity:
Ensure both your mobile device and DEEBOT/GOAT are connected to the internet.

5. Proceed to Click the Firmware Upgrade Option in the App:
Go to the app and select the Firmware Upgrade option.

6. Wait for the Upgrade to Complete:
Once the firmware upgrade begins, allow the process to finish without interruption.

If the Update Fails:

  • Re-add the DEEBOT/GOAT to the App: Delete the robot from the app and re-add it.
  • Use Mobile Hotspot: Try connecting the DEEBOT/GOAT to your phone's mobile hotspot.

If the Issue Persists:

  • Ecovacs Home App version - Find this after you open the App, hit Mine > About > Version
  • DEEBOT/GOAT Serial Number - For DEEBOT, find it in Settings > Robot Advanced Settings > About Your Robot > Serial Number, or underneath the DEEBOT's dustbin. For GOAT, the serial number is at the rear end of the GOAT, next to the QR Code, or at the bottom of the GOAT.
  • Screenshot of the robot's firmware version - For DEEBOT, go to Settings > Robot Advanced Settings > About Your Robot > Update. For GOAT, go to Settings > Robot > Upgrade.
