2025 GOAT A Series - GOAT Worktime Limit

There is no limit to the working hours set in the GOAT's program. In theory, you could set the GOAT to mow 24 hours a day. When the battery level drops below 15%, GOAT will automatically return to the docking station to charge. Once the battery reaches 85%, GOAT will resume mowing from the last breakpoint location.

However, to ensure accurate positioning, optimal mowing performance, and animal safety, mowing in the rain or after dark is not recommended.
