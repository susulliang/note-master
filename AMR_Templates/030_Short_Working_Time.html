1. Charging

  • Ensure that DEEBOT is fully charged before starting a cleaning cycle.
  • You may check the battery status in the APP.
  • If the robot is not fully charged, please allow it to charge completely before use.

2. Dust Bin & Filters

  • Confirm whether the dust bin or filters have been washed.
  • Please make sure they are completely dry before the next use.

3. Auto Resume (for models with advanced mapping)

  • It is recommended to turn Auto Resume ON.
  • With Auto Resume enabled, the robot will:
  • Resume cleaning once the battery reaches about 50%.
  • Return automatically to the last unfinished area and continue cleaning until the next low-battery cycle.
  • ⚠️ Please note: Auto Resume will not activate if the cleaning task is manually interrupted.

Additional Note:
Work time may decrease if the vacuum power is set to Max or if the robot is used in Mopping Mode.
