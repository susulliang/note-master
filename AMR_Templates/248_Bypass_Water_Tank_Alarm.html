When the Deebot starts a cleaning task, it now performs a self-check of both the Deebot and the Docking Station. If insufficient clean water is detected and the issue is not addressed promptly, the system will trigger an error to prevent the cleaning task from continuing.

To avoid interruptions caused by low clean water or a full dirty water tank, we recommend enabling "Forced Operation Without Water" under Station Advanced Settings in the Settings. This setting allows the robot to continue cleaning even when these conditions are detected.
