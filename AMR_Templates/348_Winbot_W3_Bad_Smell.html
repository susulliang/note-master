We've outlined a few simple steps to help prevent or resolve the issue and keep your Winbot W3 OMNI functioning at its best:

  1. Use the Self-Clean Function: After the final cleaning cycle, activate the OMNI station's self-cleaning function. This will help thoroughly clean the internal components and the cleaning compartment.
  2. Dry Residual Moisture: Once the self-cleaning cycle is complete, open the cleaning compartment and allow it to air-dry. You may also use a clean cloth to wipe away any remaining moisture to prevent mold or mildew.
  3. Empty and Clean the Dirty Water Tank: Ensure that the wastewater tank is emptied and thoroughly cleaned. Prolonged accumulation of dirty water can lead to unpleasant odors. We recommend cleaning the tank regularly to maintain freshness.
  4. Ensure Wiping Pads Are Dry: After each cleaning session, make sure the wiping pads are completely dry before storing them. Storing damp pads can lead to musty odors over time.

We hope these steps help resolve the issue. If the odor persists or if you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to reach out. We're here to assist you!
