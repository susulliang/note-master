Thank you for reaching out to ECOVACS ROBOTICS.

To get started, please ensure you have logged in to your ECOVACS account on the ECOVACS HOME App and that your DEEBOT is connected and online. Then, simply enable the ECOVACS Skill in the Alexa App by tapping the "Enable" button. Once everything is set, you should be able to control your DEEBOT through voice commands.

If your DEEBOT is already linked to Alexa but is not responding to voice commands, please try the following steps:

  • Unlink and relink the ECOVACS Skill in the Alexa App to refresh the connection.
  • Ensure that both your DEEBOT and Alexa device are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  • When giving a command, attempt to use a format such as: "Alexa, ask DEEBOT to start cleaning." or "Alexa, ask DEEBOT to mop the master bedroom."

If you have renamed your DEEBOT in the ECOVACS App, please reference the new name when speaking the command.
