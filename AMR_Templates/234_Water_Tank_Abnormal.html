Remove the water tank from the station and inspect it carefully to ensure there is no damage or breakage.

Check and clean the sealing plugs, making sure they are in good condition and properly installed.

Reinstall the water tank onto the station. Then, power cycle the station by unplugging it for 60 seconds and plugging it back in. After that, check whether the alarm occurs again.
