Steps to Remove Hair Tanglement & Dust Residue from the Universal Wheel

  1. Power off the Deebot and place it upside down on a clean surface.
  2. Gently pull away any hair wrapped around the wheel axle using fingers, tweezers, or scissors.
  3. Wipe the wheel and the surrounding area with a dry cloth or cotton swab.
  4. For stubborn stains, use a lightly dampened cloth (avoid excessive moisture).
  5. After cleaning, restart the Deebot and run a short cycle to check smooth movement.
⚠️ Reminder:
  • Do not rinse the wheel module with water; moisture may damage internal components.
  • Regular cleaning of the universal wheel ensures smooth navigation and prevents unnecessary "wheel stuck" alerts.
