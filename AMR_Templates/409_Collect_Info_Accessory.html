Thank you for reaching out to us regarding your machine.

To assist you further, we would like to help you submit a request for the needed item(s). In order to move forward, we kindly ask for the following materials:

  1. Proof of Purchase: A screenshot or image showing the order number, purchase date, original seller, machine condition, and the model of the device. This will help us verify your warranty coverage and ensure we process your request accurately.
  2. Picture of the Robot's Serial Number: The serial number is essential for verifying the model and production details of your robot. It helps us confirm that the correct device is being serviced and ensures that any parts or service provided are compatible with your machine.
    • Deebot: Serial number located underneath the dustbin, starting with "E0".
    • Goat: Serial number located at the bottom of the mower or at the rear end of the unit next to the QR code.
    • Winbot: Serial number can be found via the Ecovacs Home App by going to Settings > Serial Number, attached to the safety rope/pod, on the corner of the storage suitcase, or on the back of the OMNI station (if applicable).
  3. Shipping Information: Full name (first and last), shipping address (including zip code, apartment/unit number if applicable), and contact number.

Once we receive the requested information, we'll be able to proceed and guide you through the next steps.

Should you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. We're here to help!
