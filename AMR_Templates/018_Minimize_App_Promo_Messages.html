Thank you for bringing this to our attention.

To minimize the promotional notifications and pop-ups in the ECOVACS Home App, please follow the steps below to adjust your settings:

1. Open the ECOVACS Home App, go to "Mine" → "Settings" → "Message Settings", then disable "Promotion Messages".

2. Under "Settings", navigate to "Recommendation Settings" and turn OFF the following options:

  • Subscribe to ECOVACS for operation instructions, membership benefits, and product recommendations
  • App Activity Messages
  • Allow to Receive Recommendations
  • Promotions/Offers/Events
  • User Surveys (optional)
  • Customized Ad Management

3. Finally, under "Settings", go to "Privacy" → "Data Authorization Settings", locate "Other Data Authorization", and turn off "Promotion of Product and Service".

We understand that this process may be a bit inconvenient, and we sincerely apologize for any disruption it may have caused. Please know that your feedback will be shared with our product team as we continue working to enhance the user experience.

In the meantime, we appreciate your patience and continued support. Should you need any further assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out — we're always happy to help.
