Ecovacs-provided Sunshade/Garage: Using the sunshade or garage provided by Ecovacs will not affect the robot's functionality. It is designed to be compatible with the GOAT.

DIY or Third-Party Sunshades: Using a DIY or third-party sunshade may cause unpredictable effects and could interfere with the robot's performance. We recommend using only the provided sunshade for optimal results.

Keep Reflective Films Uncovered: Ensure that the silver reflective films on the station remain uncovered. These films are essential for proper operation and should not be obstructed by any covers.
