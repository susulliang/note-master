Regarding your inquiry about the app connection issue, we would like to confirm whether you have processed the troubleshooting steps below:

  • Turn off the Deebot by pressing the power button for approximately 3 seconds.
  • Scan the QR code located next to the dustbin using the Ecovacs Home App.
  • Ensure your phone is connected to a 2.4GHz Wi-Fi network.
  • Enter the Wi-Fi name and password accurately.
  • Turn on the Deebot by pressing the power button for about 3 seconds. After the startup music plays, you will hear the Deebot's initial thank-you/greeting message.
  • Check that you've switched on the robot in the app, then proceed to the next step.
  • Remove the lid/cover from the top of the Deebot and locate the Wi-Fi button. Press it briefly for no more than 1 second. Listen for the Deebot to emit the sound "ready for network setup." Check if you receive a notification in the app asking you to join Ecovacs_xxxx. If you do, press "join" and proceed to the next step. If you don't hear the "ready for network setup" sound or see the Wi-Fi signal from the Deebot, press the Wi-Fi button again until you do.
  • Wait for the connection to initiate and listen for the Deebot to say "connected, please go back to the app."
  • Stay in the app to allow the setup to be configured.
  • If the connection is successful, you will be prompted to name your Deebot.

Please let us know if you need further assistance.
