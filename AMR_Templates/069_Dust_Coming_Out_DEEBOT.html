Dust Coming Out from DEEBOT

If you're noticing dust coming out of your DEEBOT, please follow these steps to ensure everything is clean and functioning properly:

1. Clean the Side Brushes
Remove the Side Brushes and clean both the brushes and their slots thoroughly. Pay attention to the color of the brushes and the slot condition when reinstalling to ensure they fit correctly.

2. Clean the Main Brush
Remove the Main Brush Cover and take out the Main Brush. Clean the brush and the accessories at both ends. When reinstalling the Main Brush Cover, make sure that all four sides of the cover are securely locked in place.

3. Clean the Dust Suction Port, Dust Bin, and Filter
Inspect and clean the dust suction port, Dust Bin, and Filter components. If the Dust Bin is washed with water, ensure it is completely dry before reinstalling it and using the robot again.

4. Check the Baffle in the Dust Bin
Verify whether the baffle at the suction port of the Dust Bin is missing or stuck. A malfunctioning or misaligned baffle can affect suction and cause dust to escape.

Additional Tip:
For optimal performance, it is recommended to regularly clean these components, especially the side brushes and main brush, to prevent dust buildup.

If the issue persists, please reach out to customer support for further assistance. We are here to help!
