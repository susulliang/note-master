We would like to clarify a few points regarding the path settings and area creation:

Area Merging: When a newly added area connects with or overlaps into an existing one, the areas will merge directly, forming a larger area.

Path Limit: A maximum of 15 paths can be created per area.

Path Creation Rules:
- Two paths may intersect, but they cannot span over existing lawn areas
- When adding a new area that overlaps an existing path, the path will not be retained

Unrecognized Area: If the creation of the new area does not originate from a known area, the new path will not be generated.
