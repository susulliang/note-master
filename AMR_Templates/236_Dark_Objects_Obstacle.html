When an obstacle along the edge has a relatively low front height, the robot may attempt to climb over it. This behavior is normal and does not indicate a malfunction.

Additionally, the robot's obstacle avoidance system may be less sensitive to black or dark-colored objects. Dark surfaces absorb more light and reflect less, which can result in weaker reflected signals being received by the sensors. As a result, the robot may have difficulty accurately detecting such obstacles.

For safer and more efficient operation, we recommend setting up Virtual Boundaries on the Map Management page, especially in areas where the robot needs to avoid dark-colored furniture or objects.
