  1. Reinstall the OZMO Pro or air freshener a few times and make sure the AUTO light turns blue.
  2. Try switching from the air freshener to the OZMO Pro and back again to see if it reconnects properly.
  3. Gently tap the mopping plate to ensure nothing is stuck or obstructing movement.
  4. Please only use disposable mopping pads with the OZMO Pro.
  5. Restart the robot: turn the power switch OFF ("O") and then back ON ("I").
  6. You may also adjust the mopping mode in the app: Click on Enter > Swipe up from the bottom > Tap Mopping Preference > Choose Quick or Deep Scrubbing.
  7. Reinstall the air freshener or water tank and gently wipe the area with a dry mop to clear any moisture or debris.
  8. If the issue still persists, we recommend performing a reset of your DEEBOT.
