Restore Map Manually

Description: 手动恢复地图（老型号适用）

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz00000578agEAA

  1. Remove the machine from the Charging Dock and turn it off. Wait a few seconds, then turn it back on. Reconnect the machine to the Charging Dock.
  2. It is recommended to keep some distance from the machine to avoid interference. The machine will announce "Map is recovering." After a short wait, it will confirm with "Map recovery succeeded." If the map recovery fails, we suggest trying the process a few more times.
  3. If the issue persists, we recommend rebuilding the map.
  • Gentle Reminder: The map cannot be restored if it has been reset, either through the map or the machine itself.
