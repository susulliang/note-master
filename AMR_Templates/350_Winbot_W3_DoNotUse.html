Thank you for choosing WINBOT! To ensure the best cleaning experience and avoid any potential issues, we would like to provide you with some important information on scenarios where using the device is not recommended:

Not Recommended for the Following Scenarios:

  • Glass with High Humidity, Grease, or Extreme Temperatures: Avoid using WINBOT on glass with excessive humidity, grease, or in extremely cold or hot environments. These conditions can affect its performance and may cause issues with suction or cleaning effectiveness.
  • Glass That Is Too Small or Has an Unusual Shape: WINBOT works best on standard-sized windows. It may struggle or be unable to perform effectively on glass that is too small or has irregular shapes.
  • Uneven Glass Surfaces: The device relies on a smooth surface for optimal cleaning. It may not function properly on windows that have an uneven texture.
  • Exterior Windows That Are Too Large: WINBOT is designed for residential window sizes. Using it on excessively large exterior windows may cause the device to lose suction or fall.
  • Narrow Spaces Near Glass: The WINBOT requires sufficient space around the edges of the glass. Narrow spaces or height constraints can prevent the device from moving freely, affecting its cleaning performance.

We Recommend:

  • Ensuring that the window is clean, smooth, and within the optimal size range for the device to operate efficiently.
  • Keeping the cleaning environment in moderate temperatures and low humidity for the best results.
