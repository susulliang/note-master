Winbot_W3_Wastewater

1. Inspect the Dirty Water Tank
Remove the Dirty Water Tank. Open the lid and inspect the seal ring and tank contact surface for debris.

2. Clean the Seal Plugs
Remove the Seal Plugs, clean them, reinstall ensuring no obstructions. Press down firmly.

3. Check the Washing Tub
Press "Open Tub" button. If drain tray is empty, wipe water level sensors. If it contains sewage, wipe dry and check sewage drain port for blockages.
