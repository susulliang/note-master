Trimmer Brush Installation for GOAT A2000/A3000 LiDAR PRO:

1. Turn off GOAT. Wear gloves.
2. Place the GOAT upright.
3. Align the Trimmer connector and insert it into GOAT's interface.
4. Secure with three screws using a PH2 Phillips screwdriver (>10cm length).
5. Install the brush until you hear a "click" sound.

Warnings:
- GOAT cannot operate without Trimmer Module properly installed.
- Trimmer Brush must be installed to prevent grass pulp clogs.
- Do not use trimming function at night.
