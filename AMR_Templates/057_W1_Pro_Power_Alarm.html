Thank you for reaching out to us regarding your Winbot. To ensure your Winbot charges properly, please follow the steps outlined below:

1. Safety Tether and Carabiner Connection
Ensure that the Safety Tether and Carabiner are properly connected to the Winbot before use. (These components are connected by default and should not be disconnected prior to use.)

2. Connecting or Reconnecting the Power Cord
Follow the steps below to connect or reconnect the Power Cord, Power Adapter, and Plug to the Winbot.

Ensure the components are connected in the specified order to prevent charging abnormalities or potential damage to the battery.

It is important to ensure the battery is fully charged before using the device. The charging time is approximately 2 hours.

Note: Please do not disconnect the power while the Winbot is running, as this may result in failure or damage.

3. Power Switch Position
Verify that the main power switch of the Winbot is in the "I" (ON) position.

4. Testing the Power Outlet
Test the power outlet to ensure it is functioning correctly.

If possible, try connecting the device to a different outlet to confirm whether the issue persists. If the current outlet is not working, please connect the Winbot to another outlet and test again.

If you continue to experience any issues or have further questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
