DEEBOT Keeps Going Back onto or Struggles with Carpet

If your DEEBOT is getting stuck or struggling to navigate onto or off carpets, please check the following:

1. Check Carpet Type

  • Long-Haired Carpet: DEEBOT may struggle to move on long-haired carpets (more than 2 cm in length). These carpets can be challenging for the robot to climb or clean effectively.
  • Short-Haired Carpet: If your carpet is short-haired (less than 2 cm), the robot should generally be able to move across it without issue.

2. Color of the Carpet
Dark-Colored Carpet: If your carpet is too dark, it may interfere with the anti-drop sensors on the DEEBOT, causing it to mistakenly detect a drop-off or ledge. This can result in the robot avoiding or getting stuck on the carpet. To prevent this, try adjusting the robot's path or use Virtual Walls to block dark carpets from being detected.

3. Cleaning Cloth Plate Installation (for older models without the mop plate lifting feature)
If the Cleaning Cloth Plate is installed, the DEEBOT may have difficulty navigating carpets. This attachment is designed for hard floors, and using it on carpets can affect the robot's ability to move properly.

Additional Suggestions:

  • Virtual Walls or No-Go Zones: If you want to prevent the DEEBOT from cleaning certain areas, including carpets, set up Virtual Walls or No-Go Zones using the ECOVACS Home app.
  • Carpet Detection: For models that support Carpet Detection, make sure the setting is enabled in the app so the robot adjusts suction power when moving across carpets.

If the issue continues, please reach out to customer support for further assistance. We're happy to help resolve this matter!
