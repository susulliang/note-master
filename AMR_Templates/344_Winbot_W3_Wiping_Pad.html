Wiping Pad Lifespan:

  • Replace after 30 cumulative uses
  • If using two cloths alternately, replace based on actual usage

What to Do if Fibers Feel Loose:
This is normal after station washing. The rubber strips on cleaning discs may shift fibers - does not affect performance.

When to Replace:
  • Frequent Low-Pressure Alerts
  • Significant Wear or Deformation
  • Decline in Cleaning Effectiveness
