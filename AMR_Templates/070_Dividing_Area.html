Dividing the Area

Folder: AMR Email Templates

1. Ensure No Obstacles
Kindly ensure that there are no obstacles within the selected area that could interfere with the division process.

2. Divide into a Close Area
The area you divide should be compact and close together. Avoid creating overly large or distant segments.

3. Check Point-to-Point Alignment in the APP
When dividing, make sure that the point-to-point lines in the app connect to the walls properly, ensuring they "hit" the wall to accurately define the area.
