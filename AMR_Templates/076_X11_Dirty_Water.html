We would like to clarify the following regarding your Deebot:

  • When the Deebot is charging at its base station, the indicator light on the power (start/pause) button will slowly flash white.
  • Once it's fully charged or during operation, the power button will be lit solid white.
  • If fully charged but not in operation, the indicator light will dim after turning solid white, and the Deebot will enter "sleep mode."

To "wake up" the Deebot from "sleep mode," simply send it out for a cleaning task, and the indicator light will light up again. If the Deebot has not been powered on, please press and hold the power button for 3 seconds to turn on the machine before charging it.

If you need further assistance, please let us know.
