This Deebot has a rectangular design at the front and a round shape at the rear. When it turns or changes direction, if the gap between the chair legs is approximately the same length as, or smaller than, the robot's diagonal, it may become stuck. This is a result of the product's design features.

To help prevent this issue, we recommend setting up a virtual boundary or adjusting the position of your chairs.
