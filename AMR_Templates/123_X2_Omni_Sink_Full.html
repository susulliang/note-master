Please kindly confirm the image of the X2 OMNI detachable tray sample below. We'd like to clarify that this part is designed to be symmetrical, and it is normal for only one side to have the part with the white plug.

Please also ensure that this piece is properly installed on the detachable tray.

Additionally, the "Sink Is Full" alarm may be caused by water on the metal components triggering the water level detector incorrectly. We recommend drying both the water level detector sensors and the sink area thoroughly before reinstalling the detachable tray.
