X11 Waste Water Not Drained

  1. Please first power off the station and the Deebot by pushing the power switch to the "O" side.
  2. Confirm that the cleaning sink (mop washing tray) is properly installed and securely in place.
  3. Ensure that the cube-shaped float is fully installed and that its magnet is still present.
  4. Ensure the Floating Sump is installed properly under the sockets.
  5. Check the cleaning sink for water:
    If there is no water:
    • Verify if the float is obstructed by any foreign objects. If so, clean it thoroughly.
    • Move the float up and down. If it falls back into place naturally, it is functioning properly.
    If there is water residue in the cleaning sink, follow these steps:
    • Open the dirty water tank and inspect the sealing ring for any visible foreign objects.
    • Uninstall the sealing ring and reinstalling it to ensure it is attached properly.
    • Remove the sealing plugs, rinse them, and reinstall them.
  6. Please ensure the overflow float ball can move up and down freely without being stuck.
  7. For test running:
    • Open the Ecovacs Home App, go to Settings, and select Device Relocation. Then click on Auxiliary Draining and check if the water can be drained.
    • If the water still cannot be drained, remove the cleaning sink, pour out any remaining water, clean the sink, and reinstall it.
