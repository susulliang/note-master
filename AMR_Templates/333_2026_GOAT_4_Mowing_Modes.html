2026_GOAT_4_Mowing_Modes

Auto Mode: GOAT will mow the entire area automatically. After completing logical mowing, it will mow along the boundaries.

Area Mode: Tap and select specific mowing areas. GOAT will mow the selected areas and boundaries.

Trim Mode: Tap and select edges to trim. GOAT will mow as close to the edge as possible. Maintain at least 2m distance during operation.

Manual Mode: Tap to enter manual mode with Bluetooth control. Remain within 6m of GOAT.
