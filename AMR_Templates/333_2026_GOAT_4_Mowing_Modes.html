Auto Mode: GOAT will mow the entire area automatically.

After completing logical mowing within the selected area, it will mow along the boundaries, including both Area and No-Entry Zone boundaries. This function can also be activated directly from GOAT's control panel.

Area Mode: Tap and select specific mowing areas.

GOAT will start mowing the selected areas. Once it finishes the logical mowing, it will also mow along the boundaries, including the Area and No-Entry Zone boundaries.

Trim Mode: Tap and select the edges to trim, including parts of the Area and No-Entry Zones boundaries.

Note:

  • To ensure optimal trimming, GOAT will mow as close to the edge as possible. Avoid selecting areas with objects that could be damaged during operation.
  • When the Trimmer is operating, the system will detect if a person is nearby and will automatically stop trimming. Maintain a distance of at least 2 meters (6.56') from GOAT during operation.
  • GOAT operates at a walking speed of 0.2 m/s during mowing to ensure optimal performance.

Manual Mode: Tap to enter manual mode, where you can mow or trim under Bluetooth control.

Note: Ensure you remain within 6 meters (19.69') of GOAT to maintain an effective Bluetooth connection for control.
