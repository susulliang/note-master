Warm Reminder

If your WINBOT becomes unresponsive, you can use the Reset button to restart the program and restore normal operation. Please follow the steps below:

  1. Ensure that the WINBOT is in standby mode (not operating on the window). Turn the device over and locate the small hole on the back labeled "Reset/Rewind".
  2. Use a pin or other pointed object to press and hold the reset button. Once the WINBOT's fan restarts, it will be ready for use again. Release the reset button when the indicator light on the bottom of the WINBOT turns off—this means the reset is complete.
  3. Turn the WINBOT off, then on again to restart it and confirm that the reset was successful.

For WINBOT models equipped with a station, a brief Bluetooth disconnection between the WINBOT and the station may occur after a factory reset. If this happens, simply turn the station off and then on again to restore normal operation.
