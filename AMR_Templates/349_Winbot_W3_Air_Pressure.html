Step 1: Remove and Re-attach the WINBOT
Use the App to remote control WINBOT to a safe position. Remove and reattach.

Step 2: Check the WINBOT and Window
Replace Wiping Pad if used 30 times or if frequent low-pressure alarms.
Ensure surface is gap-free.
Check for metal debris on the Wiping Pad magnet.
Inspect the red sealing cotton for secure attachment.
