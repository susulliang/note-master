Mop washing tray malfunction / Cleaning sink malfunction

  1. Confirm that the cleaning sink (mop washing tray) is properly installed and securely in place.
  2. Check the float for obstructions. Move it up and down - it should fall back naturally.
  3. Clean the sealing plugs and ensure no obstructions between the tanks and the station.
  4. Check the overflow float ball for free movement.
