Scenario 1: Manual Spraying Works, But Auto Spraying Does Not
To avoid spraying water onto window frames, software restricts automatic spraying in certain conditions.
Scenarios Where Spraying is Disabled:

  • Edge-Cleaning
  • Small Glass (< 55cm)
  • Returning to Starting Point
  • Omnidirectional Remote Control
  • Auto Spray function turned off in app

Scenario 2: Neither Manual Nor Auto Spraying Works
  1. Check Water Supply
  2. Ensure Auto Spray enabled in app
  3. Inspect nozzles for blockages
