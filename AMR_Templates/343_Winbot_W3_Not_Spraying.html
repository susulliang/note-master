If you're experiencing issues with WINBOT's water spraying function, please review the following scenarios and troubleshooting steps:

Scenario 1: Manual Spraying Works, But Auto Spraying Does Not

To avoid spraying water onto window frames, the software restricts automatic spraying in certain conditions (e.g., small glass or special scenarios). However, there is no need to worry about cleaning effectiveness, as the wiping pad is already damp, and the water volume is sufficient for cleaning.

Scenarios Where Spraying is Disabled:

  • Edge-Cleaning: No spraying occurs during the edge-cleaning process.
  • Small Glass: No spraying on glass with a width < 21.7 inches / 55 cm.
  • Returning to the Starting Point: No spraying when WINBOT is moving back to its original position.
  • Omnidirectional Remote Control: No spraying during remote control use.
  • App Settings: If the Auto Spray function is turned off in the app, WINBOT will not spray water.

Scenario 2: Neither Manual Nor Auto Spraying Works

  • Check Water Supply: Confirm that there is either water or the ECOVACS WINBOT Cleaning Solution in the water tank.
  • App Settings: Ensure that the Auto Spray function is not disabled in the app.
  • Nozzle Blockage: Inspect both the front and rear spray nozzles for any blockages.
