In an ideal open environment, the typical signal coverage range of an RTK base station is 300-400 meters (approximately 1,000-1,300 feet).

However, in more complex environments, such as courtyards with buildings and vegetation, the effective coverage range is usually reduced to around 80 meters (approximately 260 feet) due to signal obstructions.

Additionally, scenarios that span across multiple streets are typically not feasible.
