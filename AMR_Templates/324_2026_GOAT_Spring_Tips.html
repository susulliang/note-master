2026_GOAT_Spring_Tips

1. Reassemble and Install the Charging Station: Reassemble the charging station and place it at last year's original location.
2. Inspect Cables for Damage: Before reconnecting the cables, thoroughly inspect them for any damage. Replace any damaged cables to ensure safe operation.
3. Check Blades for Rust or Damage: Inspect the blades for rust, wear, or damage. If any issues are found, replace the blades immediately.
4. Clean the LiDAR and Camera: Gently wipe the top ring LiDAR and front AI fisheye camera to remove any dirt or debris.
5. Power On and Verify Operation: Power on the device and check for normal operation both via the app and directly on the machine. If any issues persist, recreate the map.
Note: Wear gloves during operation to avoid cuts from the blades.
