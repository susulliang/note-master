Not Recommended For:
  • Glass with High Humidity, Grease, or Extreme Temperatures
  • Glass That Is Too Small or Unusual Shape
  • Uneven Glass Surfaces
  • Exterior Windows That Are Too Large
  • Narrow Spaces Near Glass
We Recommend:
  • Clean, smooth window within optimal size range
  • Moderate temperatures and low humidity
