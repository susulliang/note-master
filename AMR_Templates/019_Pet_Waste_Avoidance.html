While your Deebot features Pet Waste Avoidance technology, its effectiveness can vary based on several factors. Pet waste can differ in size, shape, and color, which may impact recognition accuracy. Additionally, low lighting, complex floor patterns, or nearby clutter can further interfere with detection. Dirty or obstructed sensors and cameras may also reduce performance.

To help achieve the best cleaning results, we recommend the following:

  • Manually remove pet waste before starting a cleaning cycle.
  • Ensure the Deebot's sensors and camera are clean, and the appropriate Obstacle Avoidance Strategy is selected.
If your model features AIVI™ 3D (especially AIVI™ 3D 3.0), try setting the obstacle avoidance sensitivity to High instead of Standard. This allows the Deebot to respond more sharply to objects in your environment.
  • Run the robot in a well-lit environment. 
If your model supports Night Vision Supplementary Light, enable it in the app via: Settings > Robot Advanced Settings > Night Vision Supplementary Light
This allows the robot to automatically activate lighting in low-light areas, enhancing obstacle avoidance using AI vision.
  • Use Auto Clean mode to allow optimal mapping and full-scope object detection.

Additionally, to help create a safer environment for your pets, we suggest enabling Protective Navigation Mode (available on models such as X9 PRO OMNI and X11 OMNICYCLONE). This setting increases the robot's avoidance distance and automatically adjusts the side brush speed, providing extra protection for pets. Please note that enabling this mode may reduce cleaning coverage.
Performance may vary depending on environmental complexity, object shapes, and other factors.

If you have any further questions or need assistance with settings, please don't hesitate to reach out — we're here to help.
