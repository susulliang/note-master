Why 2.4GHz Only for Deebot

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000005JZX3EAO

Currently, our DEEBOT models only support 2.4GHz Wi-Fi. This is because 2.4GHz provides a more stable connection over a wider coverage area and is better at penetrating solid objects (such as walls and furniture). This ensures that your DEEBOT stays connected and reduces the chance of it going offline while cleaning around the house.

For optimal performance, please make sure your router is set to 2.4GHz when connecting your DEEBOT.
