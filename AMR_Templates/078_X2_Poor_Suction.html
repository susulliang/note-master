X2 - Poor Suction Power

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000005NgmrEAC

  1. Increase the suction power via the ECOVACS Home App, or try increasing the number of cleaning cycles.

  2. Check for blockages – the suction intake might be clogged, or the Main Brush/Side Brushes could be tangled with hair or debris. Regular maintenance helps ensure optimal performance. Here's what you can do:
    • Power off the machine.
    • Remove the dust bin, empty it completely, and reinstall it securely.
    • Turn the DEEBOT upside down, remove the Main Brush Cover, and take out the Main Brush.
    • Use the Multi-Function Cleaning Tool to cut and remove any hair or debris tangled around the brush, then reinstall it.
    • Remove the Side Brushes, wipe them and their mounting slots with a dry cloth, and reinstall them.
  3. Please refer to the image of the Main Brush Cover for guidance.

If the machine is missing areas during cleaning, please check the following:

  • Same area missed repeatedly:
    • Confirm whether any thresholds or steps in the area exceed 2cm in height. The DEEBOT may not be able to cross over them.
  • Different areas missed:
    • Try not to follow the robot while it's cleaning, as it could interfere with its navigation.
    • If the space has many obstacles or clutter, it may help to tidy up beforehand. You can also reduce sensitivity by turning off the AIVI™ 3D function in the ECOVACS Home App:
      • Go to Settings > Intelligence Settings and toggle off AIVI 3D.
