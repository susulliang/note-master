T80S - Deebot frequently returns to the station during operation

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000006bjabEAA

This may be caused by the following reasons:

  1. Dirty Water Box issues: Abnormalities with the Deebot's Dirty Water Box can trigger frequent returns to the docking station. Check the app for any Dirty Water Box error alerts. If an alert is present, follow these steps to clean the Dirty Water Box:
    • Press the blue release button at the rear of the Deebot to remove the Dirty Water Box.
    • Lift the Dirty Water Box lid from the blue latch position and pour out the dirty water inside.
    • Remove the Filter Net and clean both the Filter Net and the Dirty Water Box thoroughly (use cleaning tools if needed).
    • Reinstall the Filter Net and ensure the Dirty Water Box lid is securely closed.
    • Reinsert the Dirty Water Box into the rear of the Deebot. A "click" sound indicates proper installation.
  2. AI Stain Detection feature enabled in Robot Advanced Settings: If AI Stain Detection is enabled in the app, complex floor textures or lighting conditions may cause misidentification of stains. You can disable this feature in the app and monitor the machine's performance afterward.
