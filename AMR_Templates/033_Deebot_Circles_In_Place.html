1. Confirm whether the robot is in mopping mode. If yes, remove the cleaning mop plate first. (Ignore if the Deebot has two rotary mop plates or an OZMO Roller Mop.)

2. Confirm the robot is operating under AUTO Cleaning Mode.

3. Power OFF the Deebot. Remove the side brush(es) and clean both the brush(es) and their bearing(s).

  • Single side brush: simply clean the brush and bearing, then reinstall.
  • Dual side brushes: note the colors of the bearings (e.g., red and green) and ensure each brush is reinstalled in the correct slot matching its bearing color.

4. Remove the Main Brush Cover and take out the Main Brush. Disassemble both ends of the main brush and clean thoroughly using the multi-cleaning tool. Reinstall the main brush cover securely. (Ignore if no main brush is installed.)

5. Tap the left, middle, and right sections of the Anti-Collision Bumper to ensure it rebounds correctly.

6. Wipe the Anti-Drop Sensors on the bottom of the Deebot with a clean cloth.

7. Press down on both sides of the Driving Wheels while sliding them to ensure smooth rotation/rebound, and clean any debris.

8. Rotate the Universal Wheel and check for any tangled hair or debris.

9. Place the robot in an open environment and restart a cleaning cycle. Avoid strong light exposure or dark surfaces, as these may affect the Deebot's performance.
