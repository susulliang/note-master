Trimmer Brush Installation for GOAT A2000/A3000 LiDAR PRO:

  1. Turn off GOAT. Please wear gloves and turn off the GOAT before installing the Trimmer Module for safety.
  2. Place the GOAT upright. Ensure the GOAT is standing in an upright position for easy access.
  3. Align the Trimmer's connector and insert the Trimmer Module into GOAT's interface.
  4. To secure the Trimmer Module, install the three screws into the screw holes on the module. The Trimmer Module requires three screws: two screws are included in the spare parts kit, and one is pre-installed within the module. Use a PH2 Phillips screwdriver with a length of >10 cm. If aligning the screws is difficult, observe the screwdriver angle from the underside of the Trimmer Module to quickly align the screws for installation.
  5. Place the brush onto the buckle position on the Trimmer. Install the brush until you hear a "click" sound, confirming it is securely in place.

Warnings:

  • Trimmer Module Requirement: The GOAT system cannot be operated if the Trimmer Module is not properly installed.
  • Trimmer Brush Requirement: If the Trimmer Brush (adjacent to the Trimmer Module) is missing, grass pulp will adhere to the unit during operation, potentially causing difficult-to-remove clogs. Ensure the brush is installed and replace it regularly.
  • Operation Restriction: Do not use the trimming function at night, as it may lead to poor performance and safety concerns.
