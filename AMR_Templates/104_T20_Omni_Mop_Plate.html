T20 Omni: Mop Plate Drop Issue

Folder: AMR Email Templates

Template ID: 00Xcz000005NbC5EAK

  1. Verify Mopping Plate Installation
    • Ensure the mopping plate is properly installed by listening for an audible "click" sound when attaching it.
  2. Inspect Mopping Plate Condition
    • Check if the mopping plate is free from damage or excessive wear.
  3. Troubleshoot by Swapping Plates
    • If the issue continues, try swapping the mopping plate with the opposite side's plate to see if the problem persists.
