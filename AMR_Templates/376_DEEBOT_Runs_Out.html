DEEBOT Runs Out Without a Schedule

Please confirm whether the robot's time zone is correctly synced with your phone. You can find the robot's time zone under Settings → About Robot → Time Zone.

If the time zone is correct, please try deleting the current schedule and re-adding it.

Ensure the Charging Dock is properly connected. Make sure the Dock is securely plugged into a wall socket, as a loose connection may cause abnormal behavior.

If the issue persists, please perform a hard reset on the robot.
