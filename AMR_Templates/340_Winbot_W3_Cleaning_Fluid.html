To ensure optimal cleaning performance:

Use the ECOVACS Winbot Cleaning Solution Only. Do not dilute with water. Tap water may be used as an alternative, but purified water, car windshield washer fluid, or similar liquids are not recommended.

Cleaning Solution Reminder: When starting your Winbot, you will receive a voice prompt and app notification to add cleaning solution. These reminders are issued only once. If you prefer to use only the remaining wetness on the cleaning pad, you can disable the Auto Spray function in the app.
