The X8 PRO OMNI can generally climb slopes up to 0.78 inches (2 cm), but performance can vary depending on factors such as floor material, humidity, and dirt buildup on the wheels (which should be cleaned regularly).

To improve climbing, make sure the roller mop assembly is properly installed. You can also add a threshold ramp in the Ecovacs Home App, extending it up to 7.87 inches (20 cm), to help the robot navigate slopes more effectively. Additionally, using a physical threshold ramp matching your home's threshold height can enhance the robot's ability to climb over raised areas.
