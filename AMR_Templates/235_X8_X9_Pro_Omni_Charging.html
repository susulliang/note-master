X8/X9 Pro Omni: Charging issue

  1. Ensure the Deebot is correctly positioned in the charging dock: When placed properly in the OMNI Station, the three indicator lights on the Deebot should pulse in and out with a flashing white light, while the OMNI Station should display a continuous white light.
  2. If the OMNI Station's indicator light is not lit, ensure the power cord is securely connected to both the wall outlet and the station. Apply a slight additional force if needed to ensure a firm connection.
  3. Verify that the Rocker Switch on the back of the OMNI Station is turned on: Ensure the rocker switch on the back of the OMNI Station is in the "I" position, indicating that the station is powered on.
  4. Make sure to remove the lid cover from the top of the Deebot, and ensure the red switch is fully moved to the right (in the 'I' position) to activate the Deebot's main power. Then, repeat Step 1 to check if the Deebot charges properly.
