To clean the Scrubbers:

1. Turn Off the WINBOT Fan.
2. Place WINBOT on the Wiping Pad, ensuring both Scrubbers contact the pad.
3. Press both bumpers near the Scrubbers at the same time. When you hear "Start cleaning Scrubber," release your hands. The Scrubbers will begin rotating.
4. Once you hear "Scrubber cleaning completed," the process is finished.
