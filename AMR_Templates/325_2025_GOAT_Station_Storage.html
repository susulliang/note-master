2025_GOAT_Station_Storage

To prevent the signal cable and certain electronic components of the docking station from getting frozen, we recommend that you move the entire docking station indoors for better storage in winter when the GOAT is not in use.

*Moving the docking station will lead to positioning failure. If you move the docking station indoors for storage in winter, you must put it back in the original location before using the robot again. Otherwise, the positioning data will be lost and you will need to recreate the map.
