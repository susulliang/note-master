Steps to Reset the N79 Series:

  1. Place the Deebot on the charging dock. Turn off the main power switch, wait for 5 seconds, then turn it on again. Press the Auto button. Ensure the blue light is on.
  2. Press and hold the Auto button until you hear one initial beep, followed by three beeps.
