Possible Scenarios Causing Scheduling Issues with Your GOAT:

  1. GOAT is Not Powered On: Ensure that the GOAT is powered on and properly charged.
  2. GOAT is in Animal Protection Mode: If the GOAT is in Animal Protection mode, it may not respond to schedules. Check if this mode is activated and disable it if necessary.
  3. GOAT is in Rain Protection Mode: If the GOAT detects rain, it may enter rain protection mode with the Rain Sensor feature enabled in the app and will not follow scheduled tasks. Confirm whether this mode is active.
  4. GOAT Has Insufficient Battery Power: If the GOAT's battery is low, it may not be able to execute scheduled tasks. Check the battery level and charge if needed.
  5. GOAT is Performing Another Manually Triggered Task: If the GOAT is already performing a temporary task that was triggered manually, it may not execute scheduled tasks. Ensure no manual tasks are ongoing.
  6. Scheduling Settings Not Configured or Saved Correctly: Verify that the scheduling settings have been configured and saved correctly in the app.
  7. GOAT is in STOP State: If the GOAT is in the STOP state, it will not perform scheduled tasks. Ensure the device is in a ready state for operation.
  8. Time Zone Mismatch: Check in the app that the time zone set for the GOAT matches its current location. A mismatch can cause issues with scheduling.
