Possible Scenarios Causing Scheduling Issues with Your GOAT:

1. GOAT is Not Powered On: Ensure that the GOAT is powered on and properly charged.

2. GOAT is in Animal Protection Mode: If the GOAT is in Animal Protection mode, it may not respond to schedules. Check if this mode is activated and disable it if necessary.

3. GOAT is in Rain Protection Mode: If the GOAT detects rain, it may enter rain protection mode with the Rain Sensor feature enabled in the app and will not follow scheduled tasks.

4. GOAT Has Insufficient Battery Power: If the GOAT's battery is low, it may not be able to execute scheduled tasks.

5. GOAT is Performing Another Manually Triggered Task

6. Scheduling Settings Not Configured or Saved Correctly

7. GOAT is in STOP State

8. Time Zone Mismatch
