To ensure the Self-Cleaning Components are installed correctly for your Deebot, please follow the steps below:

  1. Place the components as shown in the picture.
  2. Refer to the picture that demonstrates the condition once you have uninstalled the plates' holder and the plates from the tray.
  3. Turn the plates' holder over and remove the plates. You will notice the L and R marks, which will guide you in installing the plates correctly.
  4. Ensure both arrows on the plates are aligned with the ones on the holder rack. The arrows should be facing the same direction.
  5. Once the plates are installed correctly, you should hear a quick, crisp clicking sound to confirm that they are securely in place.
