# How much coverage does the 50ml water tank provide?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How much coverage does the 50ml water tank provide?

## 答案 / Answer

Under lab conditions (1m x 1m glass), a full tank cleans approximately 30㎡. For a 100-120㎡ home (est. 80㎡+ total glass surface), this is for reference only and results may vary.
