# Why is there a lot of grass left on the edges?

## Question

Why is there a lot of grass left on the edges?

## Answer

Since the GOAT's blades are located in the center of the mower, the distance between the edge of the blades and the edge of the robot is about 10 cm. This causes a certain amount of grass to remain at the area close to the wall. Use a strimmer to trim this area.
