# Why am I unable to close a restricted area?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why am I unable to close a restricted area?

## 答案 / Answer

G00059

Mapping

The minimum diameter of a restricted area is 50 cm. Please check whether the remote control restricted area is too small.
