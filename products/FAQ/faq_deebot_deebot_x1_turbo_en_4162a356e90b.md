# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 TURBO / EN - Why don't the tables and chairs in my home appear on the 3D map?

## Question

Why don't the tables and chairs in my home appear on the 3D map?

## Answer

1. During mapping, make sure that DEEBOT has traveled to in the area where the dining table and chairs are and that the area is clean and free from obstacles.
2. To be automatically recognized, tables and chairs must be at least 1×1 meter in size.
3. You can manually add tables and chairs that match the ones in your home by going to "Home Furnishing" on the 3D map page.
