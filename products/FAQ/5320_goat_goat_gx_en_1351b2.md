# Is the GOAT more likely to get stuck when mowing along the edges?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Is the GOAT more likely to get stuck when mowing along the edges?

## 答案 / Answer

36

Mowing

When there are a lot of new obstacles along the boundary, it may cause some interference to the GOAT's visual recognition. It is recommended to clear away these obstacles first before mowing along the boundary.
