# 地图创建指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图创建指导

## 答案 / Answer

一：创建地图前准备
 1. 请打开房门并移除地面杂物并保证光线充足，以便地图能够覆盖整个家居环境；
 2. 掀开主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“I”开机，放置在基站正前方;
 3. 长按主机“auto”键，让机器自主返回充电，对接成功后开始充电。
 二：快速建图
 提示：确认您的机器人已连接网络，并处于在线状态。
 1. 使用APP
  打开ECOVACS HOME＞进入设备＞快速建图。
 2. 建图完成后机器人会有语音播报“建图已完成，请在app中查看”此时地图已创建成功。
 注：
 1.主机首次建图，需要从基站出发，若在中途干预如手动强制结束工作，手动强制回充或直接搬回基站都会导致建图失败。
 2.建议待地宝返回基站后再开展地图编辑操作，完成地图编辑后，需手动对地图进行备份。                                        3.建图后基站不要移动位置

I. Preparing for Map Creation
1. Open doors, clear floor obstructions, and ensure adequate lighting to enable full coverage of the home environment;
2. Lift the cover of the ROBOT, slide the Power Switch to "I" to turn it on, and place it directly in front of the station;
3. Press and hold the "auto" button on the ROBOT to initiate autonomous return to the charging dock. Charging will commence upon successful docking.
II: Quick Mapping
Note: Ensure your robot is connected to the network and online.
1. Using the App
    Open ECOVACS HOME ＞ Enter＞ Quick Mapping.
2. Upon completion, the robot will announce: "Mapping complete. View in the app." The map has been successfully created.
Note:
① The first mapping session must start from the station. If interrupted midway—such as by manually forcing the task to end, manually forcing a return to charge, or directly moving the device back to the station—mapping will fail.
② It is recommended to wait until the DEEBOT has returned to the station before performing map editing operations. After completing map editing, you must manually back up the map.
③Do not move the station after mapping is complete.
