## 答案 / Answer

How to change winter saving time and daylight saving time?

1. You can select your time zone in [App Settings - Bot Information - Time Zone Settings].
2. You can also set the time zone of your phone system to match the time zone of your residential area, and then select Sync phone time zone in the App Settings - Bot Information - Time Zone Settings
3. After the time zone is changed, the appointment and the time in the log will be changed synchronously.
