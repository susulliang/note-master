# 为什么APP不停提示我需要清洁我的AI camera / Why does the APP keep reminding me that I need to clean AI Camera

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么APP不停提示我需要清洁我的AI camera / Why does the APP keep reminding me that I need to clean AI Camera

## 答案 / Answer

Maintain

1.为了保证避障效果，GOAT需要你定期清理Front AI camera，如确认您的GOAT Front AI camera很干净，请忽略

To ensure obstacle avoidance effectiveness, GOAT requires you to regularly clean the Front AI camera. If you confirm that your GOAT Front AI camera is clean, please ignore it
