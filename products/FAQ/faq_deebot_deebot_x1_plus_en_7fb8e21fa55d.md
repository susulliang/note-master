# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 PLUS / EN - After starting, DEEBOT reports that the anti-drop sensor is covered with dust and is unable to clean.

## Question

After starting, DEEBOT reports that the anti-drop sensor is covered with dust and is unable to clean.

## Answer

Move DEEBOT out of the docking station. Hold the AUTO button on the DEEBOT or use the App to recall it to the docking station for a charge, then start cleaning again.
