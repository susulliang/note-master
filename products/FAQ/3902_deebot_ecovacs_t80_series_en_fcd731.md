# V-Shaped Comb Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

V-Shaped Comb Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

## 答案 / Answer

1.Power off the robot by switching the toggle to "O", remove the dirty water box, and flip the robot upside down.
2.Press the two tabs on the roller brush cover to release it, then remove the roller brush.
3.Detach the V-shaped comb (Tip: Rinse with water and ensure it’s fully dry before reuse).
4.Reinstall the V-shaped comb.
5.Reinstall the roller brush and rotate it manually to confirm smooth movement.
6.Align the roller brush cover with the slots on the robot and press firmly until a "click" confirms proper installation.
7.Reattach the dirty water box.
