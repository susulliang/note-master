# GOAT 为什么充不进电？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 为什么充不进电？

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

（1）确保基站插座通电，GOAT正确放置于基站并处于对接状态；
（2）确认基站蓝灯呼吸闪烁，显示屏电池图标闪烁；
（3）检查充电接触片是否清洁，若有铜锈可能导致无法充电；
（4）电池温度≤0℃或≥55℃时将触发高低温保护，无法充电；温度恢复后故障自动解除并继续工作（传感器读取存在一定误差）。
如以上方法仍无法排除故障，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门。

(1) Ensure the station outlet is powered, and GOAT is correctly positioned on the station in docking mode. 
 (2) Verify the station's blue light is breathing and the battery icon on the display is flashing. 
 (3) Inspect the charging contacts for cleanliness; copper corrosion may prevent charging.
 (4) Charging will be disabled when battery temperature is ≤0°C or ≥55°C due to thermal protection. The system will automatically resume operation once temperature returns to normal (note: sensor readings may have slight inaccuracies). 
 If the issue persists after trying the above steps, please contact Ecovacs After-Sales Service.
