# 机器人频繁回基站，如何处理？

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人频繁回基站，如何处理？

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 当您在使用过程中若您有遇到机器出现频繁回基站洗抹布、机器工作几分钟后回洗抹布等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
 1.取下主机正面上盖，将电源开关拨至“O”关机，将主机翻转，其底部朝上放置。
 2.按压主机底部排污口，查看是否卡滞或被异物堵塞。若有异物，请清理。正常状态下按压会出水。
 3.同时按压基站左右两侧充电簧片1-2s后松开观察基站排污顶杆，正常状态下顶杆会正常起落。
 4.请检查APP中是否开了AI污渍识别，请关闭AI污渍识别功能尝试。
 5.请检查APP中是否将“瞬时超充”功能设置为“自适应”模式，若有，请选择为“标准”模式

Friendly Reminder:
 If you encounter situations like the robot frequently returning to the station to wash its roller mop, or returning to wash the roller mop after only a few minutes of operation, try the following steps:
 1. Remove the cover of the ROBOT. Slide the Power Switch to "O" to turn it off. Flip the ROBOT over and place it with the bottom facing up.
 2. Press the drain port on the bottom of the ROBOT to check for blockages or foreign objects. If present, remove them. Water should flow out when pressed under normal conditions.
 3. Simultaneously press the charging contacts on both sides of the station for 1-2 seconds, then release and observe the station's drain rod. Under normal conditions, the rod should rise and fall smoothly.
 4. Check if AI stain recognition is enabled in the app. Disable this feature and attempt the operation.
 5. Verify if the "Instant Supercharge" function is set to "Adaptive" mode in the app. If so, switch it to "Standard" mode.
