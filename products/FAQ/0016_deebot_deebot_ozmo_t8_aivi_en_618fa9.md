# Could DEEBOT damage furniture by running into it?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Could DEEBOT damage furniture by running into it?

## 答案 / Answer

D20012

Enable

Usage advice

No, DEEBOT comes equipped with a two-part collision avoidance and protection feature, including infrared distance proximity sensors and protective bumpers. 
If DEEBOT has an unexpected collision, check whether the area it collided with is covered in reflective or dark-colored material.
