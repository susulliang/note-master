# How to use OK YIKO Voice Assistant correctly?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to use OK YIKO Voice Assistant correctly?

## 答案 / Answer

1.Open the APP＞Enter the device＞Tap "YIKO icon" ＞Tap "Settings"＞Open the voice assistant.
2.You can also view more voice commands in YIKO voice skills.
Note: DEEBOT light is "orange" when it is turned off.
