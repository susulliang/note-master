# GOAT / GX / ZH - Can the GOAT be used during cold winters?

## Question

Can the GOAT be used during cold winters?

## Answer

It is not recommended to mow frosted lawns, so we advise against mowing in winter. In addition, to ensure battery life, GOAT comes with high/low temperature protection, which will be triggered when the detected battery temperature is lower than 0° or higher than 55°. GOAT will not be able to work under such conditions.
