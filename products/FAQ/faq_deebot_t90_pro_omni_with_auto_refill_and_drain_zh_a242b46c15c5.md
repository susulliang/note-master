# Warning: Difficult operation, check Driving Wheels

## Question

Warning: Difficult operation, check Driving Wheels

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
 If you encounter operational difficulties or slippage during use, try the following steps:
 1. Remove the front cover of the ROBOT. Slide the Power Switch to "O" to power off. Flip the ROBOT over and place it with the bottom facing up.
 2. Press and rotate the Driving Wheels to clear any tangled debris. When rotating the Driving Wheels, maintain a single direction of rotation—do not alternate between clockwise and counterclockwise.
 3. Wipe the surface with a damp, wrung-out cloth or use a brush to remove surface stains.
