# Some water remains when the clean water tank and dirty water tank are removed.

## Question

Some water remains when the clean water tank and dirty water tank are removed.

## Answer

This is because the entire principle of cleaning the mopping pads involves drawing water from the cleaning water tank through a water pump and a suction pipe. After cleaning, the water is pumped through a water pump and a suction pipe to the dirty water tank. There may be some water residue at the connection, which will not affect operation and performance. Please wipe before use.
