1

## 答案 / Answer

1. Open the outer packaging and take out the ROBOT, base, station, and power cord. Remove the protective strips from the ROBOT and the protective bag from the station.

2. Attach the base to the station by pressing both sides until you hear a “click,” indicating it is securely in place.

3. Use the included power cord to connect to the Power Socket on the back of the station. Place the station against a wall in a suitable location and plug it in. (For the US version, also turn on the rocker switch at the back of the station.)
   (Place the station on a flat, hard surface. Keep at least 5 cm of clearance on both sides and 80 cm in front of the station.)

**Important Note:**
For best results, place the station on a tiled floor. If it is placed on a wooden floor, regularly check the bottom for any water residue and wipe it dry promptly to prevent moisture damage.
