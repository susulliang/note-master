# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - What is the difference between the auto-connection module and the cellular module? What scenarios are they applicable to?

## Question

What is the difference between the auto-connection module and the cellular module? What scenarios are they applicable to?

## Answer

Auto-connection module:
- Provides auto-connection service for plug and play.
- Auto-connection service can support all remote operations except OTA upgrade and viewing of real-time security video.
- Provides a SIM card slot which can be used after purchasing a SIM card and can support all remote operations.
Cellular module
- The SIM card needs to be purchased by the user to be inserted prior to use.
- All remote operations are supported.
