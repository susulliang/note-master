# 40

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

40

## 答案 / Answer

Check the APP settings:
① Open the App - Check if "TruEdge 3.0" in the Deebot advanced settings is set to "Extreme" mode.
② Open the App - In the "Pet Zone" under Deebot advanced settings, turn on the "Pet Avoidance" function.
Note: To prevent the robot from scratching furniture and other items during cleaning, the robot is designed to maintain a certain distance from baseboards, obstacles, etc. This is a normal phenomenon.
