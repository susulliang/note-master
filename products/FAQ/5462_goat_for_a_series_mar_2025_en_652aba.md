# Lidar传感器上的赃污会影响机器工作吗？ / Can dirt on the lidar sensor affect GOAT working?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Lidar传感器上的赃污会影响机器工作吗？ / Can dirt on the lidar sensor affect GOAT working?

## 答案 / Answer

LiDAR

会影响
（1）请定期使用柔软的清洁布清洁LiDAR
（2）特别脏污的情况下，GOAT会检测到脏污，机器暂停工作，APP提醒您进行清洁

It will affect
(1) Please regularly use a soft cleaning cloth to clean LiDAR
(2) In particularly dirty situations, GOAT will detect dirt and the GOAT will stop working. Then  APP will remind you to clean it.
