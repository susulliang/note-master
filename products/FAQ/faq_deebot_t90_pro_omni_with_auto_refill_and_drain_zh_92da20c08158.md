# DEEBOT / T90 PRO OMNI With Auto Refill And Drain / ZH - How to clean the mop washing tray?

## Question

How to clean the mop washing tray?

## Answer

1. Remove the entire mop washing tray, then detach the top filter layer.
2. Rinse the base and filter layer under running water to remove dirt. Use cleaning tools to assist in scrubbing away grime. After cleaning, wipe away water residue with a cloth.
3. After installing the cloth cleaning tray, reinstall it onto the station.

Note:
1. Ensure the overflow float ball is fully installed.
2. The cleaning tool shown in the video are not standard equipment and are for reference only.
