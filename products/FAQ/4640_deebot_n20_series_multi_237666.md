How to start a mopping task?(standard version)

1.Remove reservoir(pre-installed).                                                                                            2.Open the water inlet to add water.                                                                                      3.Install mopping pad assembly into reservoir, then put it back into the Robot, a 'click' sound indicates proper installation.                                                                                                   4.Short press power button to start mopping.
