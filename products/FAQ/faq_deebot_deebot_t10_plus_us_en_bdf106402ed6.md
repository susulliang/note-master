# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T10 PLUS（US） / EN - Can I disable DEEBOT T10's obstacle detection function and how do I do it?

## Question

Can I disable DEEBOT T10's obstacle detection function and how do I do it?

## Answer

(1) After completing network setup for your DEEBOT, open the App and go to Settings > Smart Cleaning Settings > AIVI module to disable.
(2) When the AIVI Smart Detection function is disabled, DEEBOT will not avoid low objects or detect steps lower than 2–6 cm when cleaning. When it encounters obstacles during cleaning, DEEBOT will slow down and bump into the obstacle gently at low speed, and use the DTOF sensor to avoid obstacles of about the same height as the robot.
