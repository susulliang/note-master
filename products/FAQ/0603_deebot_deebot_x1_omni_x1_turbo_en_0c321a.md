# Why is the white indicator on the docking station still lit up when it is not in operation?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the white indicator on the docking station still lit up when it is not in operation?

## 答案 / Answer

D20247

Station

The white light is a standby light. The white indicator on the body of the docking station will go off automatically 10 minutes after auto-emptying. It means the station is on standby.
