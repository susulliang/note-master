# Why does the station power indicator remain off after unplugging and reconnecting the power supply?

## Question

Why does the station power indicator remain off after unplugging and reconnecting the power supply?

## Answer

If you notice the station Indicator fails to illuminate after unplugging and reconnecting the power supply, we recommend first completely disconnecting the power, then waiting approximately 30 seconds before plugging it back in. This is because residual voltage may remain inside the station and has not yet fully dissipated. Immediately reconnecting the power at this point may prevent the station from booting up properly. Waiting briefly allows sufficient time for the voltage to dissipate, ensuring the device can start up smoothly.
