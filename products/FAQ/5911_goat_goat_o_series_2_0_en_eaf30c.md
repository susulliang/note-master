# GOAT 配网后，无法连接App？ / Why can't the GOAT connect to the App even after completing network setup?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 配网后，无法连接App？ / Why can't the GOAT connect to the App even after completing network setup?

## 答案 / Answer

6- APP其他

（1）请确认登录APP时选择的地区与主机当前所在的地区是一致的，跨区配网可能导致配网后APP无法使用
（2）检查GOAT显示屏WiFi icon是否亮起，确认Wi-Fi信号强度是否足够，若GOAT显示屏无WiFi icon显示，则GOAT已与路由器断连。若GOAT显示屏上有WiFi icon显示，则路由器可能故障
（3）恢复WiFi后，可尝试重新配网

(1) Ensure that the region you select when logging in to the APP is the same as the region where the host currently resides. The APP may be unavailable after the network is configured across regions.
(2) Check whether the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up to ensure the signal avilable. If not, it means the GOAT is disconnected from the router. If the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up, the router may be malfunctioning. 
(3) After restoring the WiFi connection, you can try to re-configure the network settings.
