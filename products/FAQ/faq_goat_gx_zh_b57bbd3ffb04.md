# Can the firmware upgrade process be canceled midway?

## Question

Can the firmware upgrade process be canceled midway?

## Answer

In order to ensure the stability of the OTA upgrade, after entering the upgrade process, the upgrade process cannot be canceled unless the robot's power has been turned off. It is recommended that the  Automatic Upgrade is enabled, so that when a new version is detected, it will automatically upgrade your GOAT.
