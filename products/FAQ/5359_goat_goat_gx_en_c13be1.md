# Why is "Indoor Use Only" displayed on the adapter?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is "Indoor Use Only" displayed on the adapter?

## 答案 / Answer

75

Additional Connection Module

Our adapters have high standards of design and manufacturing processes and are equipped with multiple protective measures, so there is no risk under normal use. However, in the event of extreme weather or natural disasters, we still recommend placing the adapter indoors or in a work space and using an extension cord to connect to the station to avoid any potential issues.
