What to do if the reservoir doesn't seep？(standard verison)

1.Open the water inlet to check if there is any water inside.                                                                                2.Check weather the mopping pad plate is installed properly.                                                                             3.Remove mopping pad plate to check if the small magnet is missing.                                                               4.Wipe the water outlet.                                                                                                                    5.Wipe the water filling nozzle.
