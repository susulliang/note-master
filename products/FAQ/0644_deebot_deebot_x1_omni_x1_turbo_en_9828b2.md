# Why does the robot turn every time I call YIKO?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why does the robot turn every time I call YIKO?

## 答案 / Answer

D20283

YIKO Voice Assistant

If your robot is not in the docking station, when you wake up YIKO, DEEBOT will automatically turn toward you to interact with you based on the direction of the sound source. Depending on the proximity of the sound source or whether there is anything affecting recognition of the sound source in between, the robot may not steer correctly.
