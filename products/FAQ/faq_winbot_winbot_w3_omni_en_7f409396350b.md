# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to Use Zone Cleaning?

## Question

How to Use Zone Cleaning?

## Answer

Reminder: If your home has an extra-wide floor-to-ceiling window with openable small windows on both sides, and the glass width exceeds 4 meters, you can use Zone Cleaning. Place the Winbot on the outside of the glass through the left and right small windows respectively, dividing one large glass panel into left and right sections for separate cleaning. This mode supports extra-large floor-to-ceiling windows up to 8 meters wide.
1.Place the station next to the left openable window. Attach the Winbot to the glass.
2.Use the app to remotely drive the Winbot to the middle of the glass. Tap Zone Cleaning, then select Cleaning leftward.
3.After cleaning is complete, repeat the above steps to clean the right side of the glass.
