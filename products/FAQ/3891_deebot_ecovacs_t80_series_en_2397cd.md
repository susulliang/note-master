# Guidelines for the correct installation of the robot

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Guidelines for the correct installation of the robot

## 答案 / Answer

1. Please open the outer box to take out Robot, Base, Station, Power Cord and remove the Robot impact protector, the protective bag on the  Station, etc.
2. Please turn the Robot over.
3. Please take out the side brush in the box, align it with the slot and press it down, when you hear a "tapping" sound, it is in place.
4. Please install the base, press both sides, and hear a "ta-da" sound to indicate that it is in place.
5. Please use the power cord to connect with the power connector on the back of the station(If it is a U.S. model, toggle the power switch on the back of the station to the 'ON' position），find a suitable location to place it against the wall, and turn on the power.
 (It is recommended to place the station on a flat hard surface, and do not place objects within 5cm from the left, right and 80cm from the front).
Warm tips:
Priority placed on tile floors, if you place it on the floor, please check the bottom of the station regularly to see if there is any water residue, if there is, please clean it up in time to prevent moisture.
