# 当前室外无雨，为何GOAT触发了雨淋保护？ / There is currently no rain outside. Why did the GOAT trigger the rain protection mode?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

当前室外无雨，为何GOAT触发了雨淋保护？ / There is currently no rain outside. Why did the GOAT trigger the rain protection mode?

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

雨淋传感器较为灵敏，如果长时间触摸传感器极片或传感器极片上有水会触发雨淋保护
可以使用布或者细砂纸轻轻擦拭雨淋传感器

The rain sensor is more sensitive. The rain protection mode will be triggered if the sensor is subject to long-term exposure to touch or water.
Please use a cloth or fine sandpaper to gently wipe the rain sensor.
