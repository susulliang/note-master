# GOAT / GX / ZH - About the use of wifi or wifi amplifiers?

## Question

About the use of wifi or wifi amplifiers?

## Answer

GOAT is not limited by the brand of router or wifi amplifier, as long as the device can output a 2.4Ghz WiFi signal or a hybrid 2.4/5Gh wifi signal.
