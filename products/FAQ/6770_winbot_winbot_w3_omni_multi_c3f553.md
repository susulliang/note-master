>

## 问题 / Question

25

## 答案 / Answer

1.Press and hold the START button to start the fan, place the WINBOT's bottom close to the window, then the WINBOT will attach itself.
2. You can also choose different cleaning modes, 
①Deep mode:
WINBOT walks through a dense bow-shaped path once, and then walks along the edge once (along four side).
②Thorough Mode:
WINBOT walks through a sparse bow-shaped path once, and then walks along the edge once (along four side).
③Fast Mode:
WINBOT walks through a sparse bow-shaped path once.
④Edge Mode:
WINBOT walks along the edge once (along four side).
⑤Spot Mode:
In remote control to click spot, WINBOT will wipe up and down four times, centering on the location of the WINBOT.
When the WINBOT starts to work, you can also click the remote control button to freely control WINBOT's direction with a 360° range of motion and manually spray water .
