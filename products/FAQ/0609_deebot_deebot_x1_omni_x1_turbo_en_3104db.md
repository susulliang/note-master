# I can't open the dust collection cabin of the docking station.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

I can't open the dust collection cabin of the docking station.

## 答案 / Answer

D20255

Station

Follow the docking station sticker prompts to open the dust collection cabin using the pop-up button hidden in the lower part of the dust cabin.
