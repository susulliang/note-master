# Why does the GOAT fail to reposition and trigger mowing after I move it?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why does the GOAT fail to reposition and trigger mowing after I move it?

## 答案 / Answer

G00094

Mowing

Triggering a task after moving the GOAT requires repositioning first. For safety reasons, the GOAT cannot be repositioned within 1.5 m from the map boundaries and restricted areas. You can move the GOAT to an open area far from the boundaries and try again
