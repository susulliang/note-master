# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - Alert: Dirty water tank is full or not installed. Please check.

## Question

Alert: Dirty water tank is full or not installed. Please
check.

## Answer

1.Remove the Dirty Water Tank, open the lid, and pour out the wastewater. (If sediment remains at the bottom of the tank, use the cleaning brush to remove it.)
2.Check the float inside the Dirty Water Tank. Ensure the float moves freely and returns to position when gently pushed, confirming normal operation.
3.Close the lid of the Dirty Water Tank securely, making sure the clip is fully locked.
Reinstall the Dirty Water Tank into the station.
Tip: The cleaning brush is not a standard accessory.
