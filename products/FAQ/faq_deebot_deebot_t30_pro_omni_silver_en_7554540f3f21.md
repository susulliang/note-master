# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T30 PRO OMNI Silver / EN - How do I charge my robot?

## Question

How do I charge my robot?

## Answer

Power on the robot (Long press the auto button).
1. Automatic robot docking and charging:
Place the robot in front of the station, short press the robot’s exit/return button to make the robot return to the station where it will dock and start charging.
2. Manual docking and charging:
When the battery level is low after power-on and the robot is unable to automatically return to the station to charge, you can manually dock the robot to charge it.
If the robot cannot power on, manually push the rear end of the robot into the station . Once docked, a voice prompt will be broadcast and the robot will start charging.

Note: It is recommended that the robot be fully charged before being used for the first time. In charging：white light flashing, fully charged: always on and then off.
