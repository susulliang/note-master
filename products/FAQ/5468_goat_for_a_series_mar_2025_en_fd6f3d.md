# 自动建图为什么暂停了？ / Why  automatic mapping paused?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

自动建图为什么暂停了？ / Why  automatic mapping paused?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

（1）请确认是否机器卡困导致自动建图无法完成
（2）自动建图过程中是否搬动机器？
 （3）确认是否有非草的区域，机器绕完一圈后认为建图完成。建议遥控机器到草坪边缘，重新自动建图，并过程中跟随，根据实际场景切换为遥控模式继续建图。

(1) Please check the fault information on the APP. GOAT may have encountered some faults and needs assistance
(2) Is GOAT moved during the automatic mapping process?
(3) Confirm whether there are any non-grass areas, and GOAT will consider the mapping complete after one full lap.
Please assist in remotely controlling GOAT to a clear boundary and restart automatic mapping
