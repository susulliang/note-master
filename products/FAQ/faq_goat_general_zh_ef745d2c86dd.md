# 搬动GOAT后触发割草，无法割草?

## Question

搬动GOAT后触发割草，无法割草?

## Answer

When the GOAT is moved, the positioning information of GOAT may be error. In this case, please move or remote control the GOAT to the docking station to complete the positioning update, then the GOAT can start the mowing task.
