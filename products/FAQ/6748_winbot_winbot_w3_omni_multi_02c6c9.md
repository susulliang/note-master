## 问题 / Question

3

## 答案 / Answer

1.Press and hold the "Start" button on the station for more than 2 seconds. winbot will immediately stop the cleaning task, and return to its initial position, and wait to be removed.
2.Press and hold the "Start" button on winbot itself for more than 2 seconds. After the fan stops, follow the sound prompt to remove winbot.
Tip: When removing winbot, please hold the handle to prevent it from falling.
