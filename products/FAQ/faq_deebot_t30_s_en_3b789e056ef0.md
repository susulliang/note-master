# How to connect a robot to the internet?

## Question

How to connect a robot to the internet?

## Answer

Preparations before networking:
1. Scan the QR code on the device and follow the prompts to download the 'ECOVACS HOME' app.(Please grant necessary permissions such as location information and access to the local network during installation)
2. Confirm that your router has enabled the 2.4GHz band wireless signal.
3. Confirm that the mobile phoneis connected to the home Wi-Fi network and can access the internet through any browser.
4. Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled on your phone.
Reminder:
1. If your router broadcasts both 2.4GHz and 5GHz signals, temporarily disable the 5GHz signal or ensure your phone is connected to the 2.4GHz network.
2. Ensure that your router's Wi-Fi signal is not hidden during and after the network setup process.
3. Currently, the Deebot network does not support VPN or proxy servers, and does not support network authentication and authentication through web pages
4.If MAC address filtering is enabled on your router, disable it before configuring the network. You can check the MAC address in the 'Settings - Deebot Information' section of the app after successfully configuring the network.
Additionally, if the device cannot be found via Bluetooth search, you can use AP mode for network configuration. To enter AP configuration mode, simultaneously press the spot cleaning and reset buttons.
