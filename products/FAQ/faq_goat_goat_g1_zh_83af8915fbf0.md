# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Will the automatic mapping be affected if the Bluetooth is disconnected or the APP is exited during the automatic mapping process?

## Question

Will the automatic mapping be affected if the Bluetooth is disconnected or the APP is exited during the automatic mapping process?

## Answer

An interruption of 1 hour will not affect map creation. After the automatic exploration along the edge is completed, must enter the APP and follow the APP prompts to let GOAT automatically dock with the station to complete the map. If manually remotely control GOAT to dock with the station, or push GOAT into the station, the map cannot be created normally.
