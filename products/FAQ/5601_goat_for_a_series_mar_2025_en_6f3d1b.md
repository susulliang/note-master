# 为什么RTK基站附近没有割草？（约1㎡） / Why isn't the grass cut near the RTK reference station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么RTK基站附近没有割草？（约1㎡） / Why isn't the grass cut near the RTK reference station?

## 答案 / Answer

为了保护RTK基站安全性，GOAT会主动无接触避障RTK基站杆，会导致大约1㎡漏割，请手动清理。

In order to protect the safety of RTK reference station, GOAT will actively avoid the RTK reference station without contact, there will be about 1㎡ of leackage， please manually mow the grass.
