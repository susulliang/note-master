# Why are there water marks on the glass after cleaning?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why are there water marks on the glass after cleaning?

## 答案 / Answer

1. The mopping pad might be too wet. Please make sure that you wring the mopping pad dry after it gets wet. 
2. If you clean the window with a low frequency (quarterly or semi-annually), more dust will accumulate on the glass and the mopping pad will get dirty easily. You will need to wash the mopping pad in time and clean the window a few more times to achieve a better cleaning effect.
