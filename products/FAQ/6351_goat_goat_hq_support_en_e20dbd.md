# After the map learning process is complete, I receive a prompt about insufficient beacon signal coverage. What should I do?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

After the map learning process is complete, I receive a prompt about insufficient beacon signal coverage. What should I do?

## 答案 / Answer

G00066

Enable

Mapping

To create a map, both of the following conditions must be met: 
(1) Areas without beacon signal coverage shall not exceed 10% of the map area. 
(2) 80% of the map area shall be covered by two or more Navigation Beacons at the same time. 
You can check the beacon signal coverage map and beacon installation rules in the App and make adjustments accordingly. You can also use the Navigation Beacon installation assistant (insert a QR code or webpage link), which will generate the appropriate installation positions after you draw the map of your garden. If the issue persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care for assistance.
