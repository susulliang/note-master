Wiping Pad: Usage & Care Tips

Wiping Pad Usage Tips
1.Both sides of the wiping pad are made of microfiber and can be used for cleaning.
2.Before use, dampen the wiping pad and wring it out well to avoid excess moisture.
3.Attach the wiping pad to the bottom of the robot and press it flat.
4.If one side gets dirty, avoid flipping it over to use the other side immediately.Dirt may stick to the Velcro, affecting attachment and making it hard to clean.
When to Replace or Wash the Wiping Pad
1.If the wiping pad gets dirty during use, replace or wash it promptly to maintain cleaning performance.
2.If you notice streaks on the glass, clean or replace the wiping pad.
3.After cleaning, remove the wiping pad, wash it, and let it dry before storage to prevent odors.
