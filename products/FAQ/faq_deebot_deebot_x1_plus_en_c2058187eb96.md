# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 PLUS / EN - Why can't I edit the area name in the App? I can do it in the T9 series.

## Question

Why can't I edit the area name in the App? I can do it in the T9 series.

## Answer

In order to optimize use of the OK YIKO voice assistant function, editing the area name does not work at this time in the App. You can modify the area type via the map edit function in the App.
