# 为什么GOAT的割草效率未达预期？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么GOAT的割草效率未达预期？

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

割草效率未达预期，可能原因如下：
1.草坪状况：草坪平整度、有无障碍物，以及草的长度和密度，均会影响效率。理想作业区域应为平整、无障碍物且草况良好的草坪。
2.电池状况：电池寿命、剩余电量及使用年限也会影响效率。若电池老化，可能需更换。
3.设备问题：割草机自身的软硬件设置或故障，也可能导致效率下降。

The following factors may contribute to lower-than-expected mowing efficiency:
 1. Lawn conditions: Efficiency is affected by lawn flatness, presence of obstacles, and grass length/density. Ideal operating areas feature flat, obstacle-free lawns with healthy grass.
 2. Battery status: Efficiency is impacted by battery lifespan, remaining charge, and age. Replace batteries if they show signs of aging.
 3. Equipment issues: Software/hardware settings or malfunctions within the mower itself may also cause efficiency drops.
