# WINBOT / W2/W2 OMNI / EN - How to store the cleaning solution effectively?

## Question

How to store the cleaning solution effectively?

## Answer

The shelf life of the cleaning solution is 2 years, and detailed instructions can be found on the bottle.

Storage tips: Keep away from heat sources, place in a dry and cool place, and it is suggested to store in a sealed environment at 0℃-30℃. After each use, please tighten the bottle cap in a timely manner.
