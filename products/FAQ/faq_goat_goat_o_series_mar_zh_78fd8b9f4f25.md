# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 为何GOAT 经常触发撞板异常故障

## Question

为何GOAT 经常触发撞板异常故障

## Answer

(1) Check whether the GOAT is in the docking station. If it is in the docking station, it may be because the GOAT didn't come into proper contact with the docking station when it returned. You can pull the GOAT out a bit, and the bumper will return to normal
(2) The GOAT may have bumped into and become trapped by obstacles while working, resulting in the bumper getting hit continuously. Please help the GOAT get out of the trap.
If the issue persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
