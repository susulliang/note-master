# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 为什么GOAT在狭窄通道里割草表现不好，还会出现漏割现象？

## Question

为什么GOAT在狭窄通道里割草表现不好，还会出现漏割现象？

## Answer

GOAT's narrow passage clearance is 0.7m.
 1. When passage width is less than 0.7m, it identifies the area as passable and proceeds directly, unable to perform zigzag mowing, resulting in missed areas.
 2. In narrow passages under 1m wide with high walls obstructing the path, weak positioning signals may cause GOAT to become stuck. It cannot perform zigzag mowing in such areas. Try manually guiding it along the edge.
