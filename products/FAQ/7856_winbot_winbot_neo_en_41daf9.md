# Can I use WINBOT on rainy days?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I use WINBOT on rainy days?

## 答案 / Answer

Do not use WINBOT on the outside of the window on rainy days to avoid the risk of damage or falls.
