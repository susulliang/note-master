# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Why does the WINBOT prompt "Please connect power" while plugged in?

## Question

Why does the WINBOT prompt "Please connect power" while plugged in?

## Answer

This is caused by low-temperature battery protection. Ensure use within the 0°C-40°C range. Below 0°C, do not use water or damp pads to avoid icing risks.
