# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 安装基站的注意事项

## Question

安装基站的注意事项

## Answer

1. Placement Area: 1.1 Position the station on a flat, open area within the lawn.
 1.2 To ensure effective recharging, avoid placing the station in narrow paths. Reserve 40cm of flat space on each side of the charging station. Keep the area within 2m in front of the charging station free of obstructions. Surrounding grass height should not exceed 6cm.
 1.3 Avoid placing the station where it is obstructed by trees or rooftops.
 1.4 The distance between the station and a grassy area with good RTK signal reception must be less than 7 meters. 1.5 For the O600 version, avoid positioning the rear directly facing glass curtain walls, perforated brick walls, or black-and-white checkered walls.
 2. Position Adjustment: The station position can be freely adjusted before map creation. Changing the station position after map generation will cause the positioning signal to shift, requiring the map to be recreated.
 3. Mounting Method: Secure the station to the ground using the included ground screws. Failure to use screws may cause station movement, recharge failures, and long-term deformation of the base plate, impairing recharge functionality.
 Friendly Reminder: Assess station placement suitability via the RTK signal pole's Light Effect during charging. A steady blue light (indicating strong RTK signal) confirms optimal positioning.
