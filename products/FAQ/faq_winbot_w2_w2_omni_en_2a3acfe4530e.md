# WINBOT / W2/W2 OMNI / EN - Tips for using the wiping pad

## Question

Tips for using the wiping pad

## Answer

Both the front and back of the wiping pad have ultra-fine fibers and can be used for cleaning.

Too much moisture in the wiping pad will affect the window cleaning effect, may leaving water marks or water stains or even causing WINBOT W2 to slip. It's suggested to manually wet the WINBOT W2 wiping pad, wring it dry as much as possible, attach it to the bottom of the robot and smooth it out before beginning cleaning.
