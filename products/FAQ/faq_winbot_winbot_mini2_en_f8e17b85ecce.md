# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Can I turn off the "No Water" reminder?

## Question

Can I turn off the "No Water" reminder?

## Answer

1. The prompt plays only once via voice and App and will not repeat.
2. To stop the reminders, disable the "Auto-spraying" function in the App.
