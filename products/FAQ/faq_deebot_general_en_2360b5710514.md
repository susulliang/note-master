# How do I upgrade the firmware version?

## Question

How do I upgrade the firmware version?

## Answer

1.Click "Enter Device", click "Settings" in the upper right corner of the page > Click "DEEBOT Advanced Settings" > Click "About Robot" > Click "DEEBOT Upgrade" and follow the prompts to upgrade.
