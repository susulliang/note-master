# 基站清洁槽内污水无法回收；语音报警“清洁槽水满”如何处理？ / What can I do if the cleaning sink is full of dirty water, the voice prompts that the cleaning sink is full.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

基站清洁槽内污水无法回收；语音报警“清洁槽水满”如何处理？ / What can I do if the cleaning sink is full of dirty water, the voice prompts that the cleaning sink is full.

## 答案 / Answer

1.打开污水箱上盖，检查密封圈与水箱接触面是否有明显异物，若有请擦拭清理；若无,请将上盖卡扣卡紧
2.取下污水箱，取下密封塞清理；重新安装密封塞，检查污水箱与基站之间无异物，放回污水箱并向下按压；确保污水箱安装到位
3.观察基站是否能够正常抽水
4.若无法抽水,取出清洁槽可拆底座，倒掉清洁槽内积水，清理清洁槽过滤件吸水口异物，将清洁槽可拆底座重新装回基站内，
5.重新插拔基站电源开关，再次使用自清洁观察抽水情况

1.Open the dirty water tank, check whether there are obvious foreign objects on the seal ring. If so, please wipe and clean; if not, please close the cover tightly.
2.Take out the dirty water tank, remove sealing plugs to clean, then reinstall them, check that there are no foreign objects between the tank and the station, then put back the water tank and press it down; make sure the tank is in place.
3.See if the station is able to pump water properly.
4.If not, remove the mop washing tray, pour the water out, clean the filter and remove foreign matter in the suction port, then reinstall the detachable base.
5.Re-plug the station power switch and start self-cleaning to observe.
