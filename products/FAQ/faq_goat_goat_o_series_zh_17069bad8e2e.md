# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - GOAT为何与基站对接时有异响？

## Question

GOAT为何与基站对接时有异响？

## Answer

Please check whether the charging contacts on the station and GOAT are clean. If there are debris or copper rust on them, it may cause abnormal noise when docking. It is recommended to wipe it regularly with a clean rag. Please note that the station and GOAT need to be powered off first.
