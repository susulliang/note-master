# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 生成的外轮廓与我实际遥控外轮廓不一致，有一部分轮廓消失了？

## Question

生成的外轮廓与我实际遥控外轮廓不一致，有一部分轮廓消失了？

## Answer

Make sure that the missing part is not a narrow area with a very small width. Areas that are too narrow sometimes cannot be identified due to positioning errors. Try to establish no-go zones in areas where the boundary has disappeared.
