## 问题 / Question

22

## 答案 / Answer

If you encounter situations such as the roller mop not being installed or the roller mop being tangled, you can try the following steps:
I.Remove the roller mop
1.Slightly lift the side of the robot where the roller mop is installed, release the blue buckle on the water tray, and remove the water tray.
2.Remove the robot's top cover, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, and flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
3.Press the roller release button on the side of the roller mop, gently lift it up, and pull out the roller mop.
4.Gently pull out the roller end cap with a bit of force to separate it from the roller mop.
5.Use a small knife or cleaning tool to remove any hair tangled around the connection shaft between the roller mop and the roller end cap.
II.Installing the Roller Mop
1.Reattach the roller end cap to the roller mop.
2.First, fit the roller mop securely onto the roller motor, then gently push it forward until it is fully in place.
3.After the mop is securely attached, align the roller release button with the groove on the side of the compartment and press it down. You should hear a "click" sound, which means the mop is installed correctly.
