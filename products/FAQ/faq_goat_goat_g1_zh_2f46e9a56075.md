# What kind of battery does the navigation beacon require? How often do I need to replace the battery?

## Question

What kind of battery does the navigation beacon require? How often do I need to replace the battery?

## Answer

Each navigation beacon requires three regular D batteries. The provided default batteries can be used for about one year.
