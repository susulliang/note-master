# GOAT出现某区域漏割的情况？ / Why is the GOAT missing some areas when mowing?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT出现某区域漏割的情况？ / Why is the GOAT missing some areas when mowing?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

（1）确认是否有临时障碍物阻挡了GOAT的行走路线，对于直径小于1m的临时障碍物区域本次除草中GOAT不会进行补割
（2）请检查是否在APP设置中打开了AI识别，此模式下可能带来一些偶尔的误避障
（3）请检测是否APP中的避障模式选择的避障高度过低导致了误避障带来一些漏割
（4）地图上是否存在小于1m的通道（包括外轮廓与禁区轮廓之间的狭窄区域、禁区轮廓之间的狭窄区域），GOAT目前对于小于1m的通道区域只来回割一道 
（5）GOAT的越障高度为4cm（基于实验室数据），超过该越障高度的台阶或路面GOAT无法通过
（6）GOAT无法切割太高的草，且过高的草可能被识别为障碍物，请确保草坪中的草高小于设置的避障高度
为保障割草效果，请维护您的草高＜10cm
（7）GOAT无法爬越高于27°（50%）的斜坡，建议将高于27°（50%）的坡和坑的区域圈为禁区
（8）悬空高度低于45cm的区域GOAT将不会进入
对于障碍物带来的漏割，当障碍物移除后下次割草时将会自动补割此区域；您也可以在APP中使用手动模式，手动遥控GOAT对漏割的区域进行补割

(1) Please check whether there are temporary obstacles blocking the route of the GOAT. Areas smaller than 1 m in diameter blocked by temporary obstacles will not be mowed by the GOAT. 
(2) Please check whether AI recognition is turned on in the app. These may occasionally result in false obstacle avoidances. 
(3) Please check whether the obstacle avoidance height you set in the App is too low. Lower obstacle avoidance heights may result in missed areas. 
(4) Please check whether there are passages narrower than 1 m on the map (including narrow areas surrounded by map boundaries and restricted areas or between restricted areas). Currently, the GOAT will mow passages narrower than 1 m only once.  
(5)The GOAT can clear obstacles with a height of up to 4 cm (based on laboratory data). Steps or roads with a greater height are inaccessible to the GOAT. 
(6) GOAT is unable to cut grass that is too high and may be recognized as an obstacle. Please ensure that the grass height in the lawn is less than the set obstacle avoidance height
To ensure the effectiveness of mowing, please maintain your grass height＜10cm
(7) The GOAT is unable to climb slopes with an inclination of greater than 27°(50%). It is recommended to set up physical enclosures (height greater than 15cm) for these slopes or set these slopes as no-go zone.
(8) GOAT will not enter areas with a suspended height below 45cm

If an area is missed due to temporary obstacles, it will be mowed next time after the obstacle has been removed. You can also use the App to manually control the GOAT and mow the missed areas
