# Will people walking behind the GOAT be recognized after safe mode is enabled?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Will people walking behind the GOAT be recognized after safe mode is enabled?

## 答案 / Answer

G00119

Intelligence & Privacy

After unattended mode is enabled, the GOAT will mainly recognize human figures in front of the mower and stop the blades.
