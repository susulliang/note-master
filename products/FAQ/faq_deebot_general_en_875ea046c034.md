# WINBOT Daily Maintenance and Cleaning Guide

## Question

WINBOT Daily Maintenance and Cleaning Guide

## Answer

1.Remove the safety lock and unplug the power cord.
2.Flip the device over, detach the wiping pad, rinse it with clean water, wring it dry, and air-dry completely to prevent odors/bacteria.
3.Check driving wheel tracks via the App. If dirty, wipe gently with a clean dust cloth.
4.Wipe the fan with a dry cloth to maintain suction power.
5.Clean spherical sensors，side rollers and TruEdge scrubber with a dry cloth to ensure sensitivity.
6.Wipe driving wheel tracks with a dry cloth for normal operation.
7.Empty the water tank if storing long-term to avoid residue/bacteria buildup.
