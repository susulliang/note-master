15 | W2S PRO/W2S PRO OMNI | Troubleshooting Hardware Alarms<br>("Obstruction Detected" or "Abnormal Status") | The WINBOT has a very low probability of encountering an occasional hardware anomaly. If this occurs, the robot will stop moving and play a voice alert. Please follow these steps:<br>1. Check if the WINBOT is placed on a glass surface with an excessive tilt angle. The robot is only suitable for tilted glass within a range of -45° to 45° relative to the vertical plane; it cannot operate properly beyond this range.<br>2. If the alarm persists or you have further concerns, please contact After-Sales Service.
16 | W2S PRO/W2S PRO OMNI | Causes and Solutions for Entrapment (Stuck Robot) | If the robot becomes stuck (especially on hard-to-reach exterior windows), please use the following troubleshooting steps:<br>1. For vertical narrow windows, adhere the robot vertically; for horizontal narrow windows, adhere the robot horizontally.<br>4. When cleaning glass with handles or hinges, start the cleaning task at least 10cm away from the frames or obstacles.<br>5. If the tracks have not been cleaned for a long time, remove the WINBOT and clean the driving wheel tracks following the guide in the App.<br>6. If the robot gets stuck again after these steps, we suggest using the Remote Control feature in the App to guide it manually.

19 | W2S PRO/W2S PRO OMNI | Why is the machine making noise during operation? | To ensure movement capability and adsorption safety, WINBOT uses MPU tires with a very high friction coefficient. The resulting friction sound is a sign of a reliable grip on the glass; please use it with confidence. Additionally, if the window is very dirty, the driving tracks may create a squealing sound (similar to car tires on a smooth garage floor) during turns, which is a normal physical phenomenon.

Let the WINBOT run on the glass for a few minutes to allow the system to recognize the water level.

25 | W2S PRO/W2S PRO OMNI | Glass Scratches: Policy and Troubleshooting | From the initial R&D stage, glass protection has been a top priority for WINBOT. We adopt soft rubber drive wheels, flexible plastic ball joints and soft microfiber cleaning pads. All components touching the glass have lower hardness than glass. Metal parts with higher hardness never come into contact with the glass surface. These strictly selected materials prevent scratches and protect glass gently during cleaning. The product has passed professional scratch resistance tests, and relevant inspection reports are available upon request.<br><br>We understand your concern. Possible causes for suspected scratches are listed below:<br>1. Original glass marks: Scratches may form during glass transportation, installation and daily cleaning. You may check other uncleaned glass panes for similar marks.<br>2. Sand and particle impact: Wind-blown fine grains may hit the glass and leave tiny scratches. Such marks are hidden by dust and become visible after cleaning.<br>3. Adhesive residue: Tiny adhesive substances may remain on newly installed glass. Traces left by robot operation can be mistaken for scratches.<br> <br>If you still have doubts, please follow the steps below for further troubleshooting:<br>① Test on another window: Choose an untouched glass pane free of scratches, particles and defects.<br>② Replace cleaning pad: Use a brand new or thoroughly cleaned pad, and remove any grit or sharp debris on it.<br>③ Full cleaning: Wipe drive wheels and ball joints completely to eliminate residual contaminants.<br>④ Record operation video: Run the cleaned Winbot on the inspected window.<br> <br>Please record the whole process. Send us the video if new scratches appear, and our technical team will conduct professional analysis. We strive to deliver optimal cleaning performance. Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
