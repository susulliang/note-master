# 基站能否放置在雨棚底下、车库中等非草坪区域？ / Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

基站能否放置在雨棚底下、车库中等非草坪区域？ / Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

可以不用在草坪上，但不建议放在雨棚下或者车库中。
1. 建议尽量接近RTK信号良好草坪区域，基站距离RTK信号良好区域＜7m.
2. 为了更好的回充效果，请勿将基站放置在狭窄通道中，充电基站左右各预留40cm的平坦区域，并且前方2m内不要有障碍物遮挡；
3. 建议将基站置于草坪中视野开阔且平坦的区域，并用附件的地钉将其固定在地上避免基站移动
4. 不建议将基站放置在被树木、屋顶遮挡的地方

(可以通过机器在基站内充电时，RTK信号杆的灯效来判断充电站的位置是否合适，若蓝灯常亮（RTK信号较好），充电站的位置比较合适。)

You can install the docking station on the non-grassy area. But it's not recommended to place it under an awning or in a garage.
There are some tips when you select the location of docking station:
1. It is recommended to approach the area with good RTK signal as close as possible, the distance is less than 7m.
2. For better positioning, do not place the docking station in a narrow passageway. Ensure that there are no objects (e.g. trees or houses) within 40 cm on either side and 2 m in front of the docking station.
3. It is recommended that the docking station is placed on a lawn with a flat area and wide visibility, and secured to the ground with the provided nails to prevent the station from moving.
4. It is not recommended to place the base station in a place that is shielded by trees or roofs.
(The position of the charging station can be judged by the light indicator of the RTK Reference Station when the GOAT is charging in the base station. If the blue light is steady on which means the RTK signal is good, the position of the charging station is appropriate. )
