# DEEBOT / FAQ T50 / EN - How to clean dirty water tank and clean water tank?

## Question

How to clean dirty water tank and clean water tank?

## Answer

1. Take out the dirty water tank,pour out the dirty water. (If dirt remains at the bottom of the tank, you can use a cleaning brush to assist in cleaning it thoroughly.), close the tank.
2.Take out the water tank, If there is dirt water inside, clean up, close the tank.
Note: After the dirty water tank is cleaned, the top cover of the water tank must be tightly closed.
