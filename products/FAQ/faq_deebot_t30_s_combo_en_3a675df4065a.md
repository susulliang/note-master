# DEEBOT / T30 S Combo / EN - How to operate the station brush head storage compartment when it is used or cannot be opened?

## Question

How to operate the station brush head storage compartment when it is used or cannot be opened?

## Answer

Use of base station brush head storage compartment
1. Press the station brush head to store the warehouse cover in the center position and open the warehouse cover slightly.
2. After opening the storage compartment, you can put in the mite removal brush, gap brush and flat head brush in turn.
3. Close the station brush head storage warehouse cover.
If you find that the station brush head storage compartment cannot be opened during use, what should you do?
1. Please press the storage warehouse around it separately, and you can try to open it.
2. Please tap the right side of the station storage compartment lightly, or slightly lift the side of the station storage compartment to hang and shake it to try to put the brush head back in place, and then try to press around to open the storage compartment;
