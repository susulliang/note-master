## 答案 / Answer

Robot can't be charged, what can I do?

1.Check whether the power cord is correctly plugged into the power outlet. You can also try a different outlet.                                                                                                                      2.Check whether the charging contacts of the Station are dented and whether they can rebound normally, wipe them with a clean dry cloth;                                                                                                      3.Wipe the charging contacts of the Robot too;                                                                                                    4.Long press power button to turn it on;                                                                                             5.Place the Robot in front of the Station, short press the return button to make it return automatically; If it can't be turned on, manually push it in, shake it gently left and right, and wait for the startup music to sound;                                                                                                                                  6.Please check charging status in the app if there is a lightning icon.
