# The robot has abnormal noise when cleaning.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

The robot has abnormal noise when cleaning.

## 答案 / Answer

The robot has abnormal noise when cleaning.

1. Confirm whether the robot’s main brush, side brush and driving wheel are stuck with foreign objects, whether the main brush cover is installed properly, and whether the suction is adjusted to a strong gear.
2. Whether the dust box is too full to cause abnormal noise, and whether the robot has swept the water.
