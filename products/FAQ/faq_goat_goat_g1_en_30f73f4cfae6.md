# Why is the outline position displayed on the App inconsistent with the actual one?

## Question

Why is the outline position displayed on the App inconsistent with the actual one?

## Answer

The position of the outline displayed on the App is the position where the centers of the two wheels of the GOAT pass by when remote controlled. There has been a certain error in positioning. If the position displayed on the App is significantly different from the actual position being remotely controlled, please confirm that the navigation beacon is installed correctly. Proceed to delete the picture and remap it under good weather conditions.
