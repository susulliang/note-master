# Are there any requirements on the tilt angle of the glass?

## Question

Are there any requirements on the tilt angle of the glass?

## Answer

WINBOT W2 is able to clean glass panes at an angle. The angle between the glass and the horizontal plane should be between 60° and 120°. If the angle exceeds this range, WINBOT W2 may circle on the spot, get stuck, or give an alarm.
