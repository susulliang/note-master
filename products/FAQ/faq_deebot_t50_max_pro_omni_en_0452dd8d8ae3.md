# DEEBOT / T50 MAX PRO OMNI / EN - The robot did not automatically perform cleaning work at the scheduled time?

## Question

The robot did not automatically perform cleaning work at the scheduled time?

## Answer

1.Make sure the DEEBOT is powered on;
2.And it is fully charged;
3.Confirm that the task is not within the Do Not Disturb time period
4.Check that the scheduled tasks in the app are set up or saved correctly.
