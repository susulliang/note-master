# Robots sometimes can't avoid obstacles.

## Question

Robots sometimes can't avoid obstacles.

## Answer

1.Please check the laser module for dust, if there is any dust, please wipe it gently with a dry paper towel.
Note: For black and linear objects the robot may slow down with light touch.
