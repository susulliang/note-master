# 基站相关功能使用？ / Base station related functions

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

基站相关功能使用？ / Base station related functions

## 答案 / Answer

1.打开App点击进入”进入设备”
2.点击“基站功能”图标进入基站控制面板，可依次根据机器的工作进程，选择集尘、烘干、清洗拖布
注：可实时在App上查看到基站工作状态

1.Open the App and click Enter.
2.Click the Station icon, and select dust collection, drying, and mop cleaning according to your demand. 
Note: The working status of the station can be viewed in real time on the App
