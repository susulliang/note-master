# 为什么GOAT的电池续航时间变短了？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么GOAT的电池续航时间变短了？

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

1.电池续航时间可能因草坪状况而异，包括斜坡、凸起或多边界连接。
2.复杂地图中频繁转向会影响续航。
3.续航时间基于草高1厘米测试，草越高耗电量越大。
4.不同草种也会影响续航，草种越厚硬耗电量越大。
5.电池老化会降低最大容量，导致性能下降。

1. Battery life may vary based on lawn conditions, including slopes, bumps, or multiple boundary connections.
 2. Frequent turns in complex maps can impact runtime.
 3. Runtime is based on testing with 1 cm grass height; taller grass consumes more power.
 4. Different grass types also affect runtime; thicker, tougher grass consumes more power.
 5. Battery aging reduces maximum capacity, leading to decreased performance.
