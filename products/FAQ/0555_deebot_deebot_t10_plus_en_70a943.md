# Why can't DEEBOT T10 find the docking station or recharge?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why can't DEEBOT T10 find the docking station or recharge?

## 答案 / Answer

D20249

Station

(1) As detailed in the instruction manual, please ensure that the docking station is placed on a hard surface, is plugged in, and that there is sufficient space around the docking station. 
(2) If the docking station is placed on a deep pile carpet or rug, DEEBOT will have more difficulty returning to charge. 
(3) The docking station powered down or was moved.
(4) The route for recharge is blocked. For example, the door of the room where the docking station is located is closed. 
If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
