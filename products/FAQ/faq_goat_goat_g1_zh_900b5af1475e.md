# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Can I create a restricted area anywhere?

## Question

Can I create a restricted area anywhere?

## Answer

The area 2 m around the docking station is the default access area for the GOAT to return to charge. It is recommended that no restricted area be set near the docking station. Setting a large restricted area in front of the docking station may prevent the GOAT from returning to charge. In addition, if the distance between the restricted area outlines or the restricted area outline and the outer outline is less than 2 m, a narrow passage will be created, which may lead to missed mowing in that area.
