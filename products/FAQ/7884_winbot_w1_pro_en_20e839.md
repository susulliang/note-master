# How to clean the glass of the shower room? Should I wait for it to dry completely before cleaning?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to clean the glass of the shower room? Should I wait for it to dry completely before cleaning?

## 答案 / Answer

W00120

Usage advice

Please try to avoid cleaning the glass immediately after a shower or when there are a lot of water droplets on the glass. It will increase the slippage and cause the falls.
