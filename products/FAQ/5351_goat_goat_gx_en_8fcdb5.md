# Why is the cover not tightly closed after installing the additional connection module?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the cover not tightly closed after installing the additional connection module?

## 答案 / Answer

67

Additional Connection Module

If theadditional connection module has been arranged, please make sure that no cable is pressed under the module, and after the battery cable is arranged, it is normal for the top cover to float slightly when closing it, because of the sealing strip structure. After tightening the screws, it will fit into the robot body.
