# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 我觉得边缘留草有点多?

## Question

我觉得边缘留草有点多?

## Answer

(1) GOAT AI function will work when cutting along edge to fine identify the edge of the lawn and adopt distance to edge according to different edge types to leave as little grass as possible.
(2) There is a margin of error in the positioning of GOAT. GOAT will will keep secure distance for cutting grass along the edge of cliffs and swimming pools.
(3) Since there is a certain distance between the blade disc of GOAT and the machine cover, there will be some grass left in areas such as along the wall, which you can use a trimmer every two weeks.
