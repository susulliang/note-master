# Can I charge WINBOT without powering it on (with the power switch on 0)?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I charge WINBOT without powering it on (with the power switch on 0)?

## 答案 / Answer

W00111

Charging

Yes, all the indicators will be off in this case. 
If you wonder the current charging status, you can power on the WINBOT. A blinking blue indicator means that WINBOT is charging. A solid blue indicator means that WINBOT is fully charged.
