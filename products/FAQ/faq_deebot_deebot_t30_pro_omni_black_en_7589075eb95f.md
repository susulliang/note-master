# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T30 PRO OMNI Black / EN - How to correctly use the robot Wi-Fi management function?

## Question

How to correctly use the robot Wi-Fi management function?

## Answer

1. Confirm that the machine has successfully connected to the APP and is online.
2. Open the APP-click enter-click settings in the upper right corner-click About DEEBOT-click Wi-Fi management-click "+" to add multiple Wi-Fi signals.
