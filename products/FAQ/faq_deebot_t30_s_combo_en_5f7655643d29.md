# How to install filtering components?

## Question

How to install filtering components?

## Answer

Installation of filter components
1. Open the cover of the clean water tank
2. Remove the filter assembly from the clean water tank
3. Remove the hose and clean the filter assembly; re-connect the hose and insert it to the end
4. Place the filter assembly flat in the guide position, press hard, and hear a "click" to indicate that the installation is in place.
