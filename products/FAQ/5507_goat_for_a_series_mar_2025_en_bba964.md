# GOAT可以在夜间割草吗？-LiDAR / Can the GOAT mow at night?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT可以在夜间割草吗？-LiDAR / Can the GOAT mow at night?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

如果您在设置中关闭的动物保护模式GOAT将会全天候工作，夜间割草GOAT视觉模块受光线影响无法正常工作，因此草坪边界等危险区域将无法夜间割草，可在白天手动触发沿边割草补割。

If you turn off the Do not disturb mode in the Settings, GOAT will work all day, but the GOAT vision module will not work properly due to the influence of light. Therefore, dangerous areas such as lawn boundary will not be able to mow at night，you can  triggered a edge mow task during the day.
