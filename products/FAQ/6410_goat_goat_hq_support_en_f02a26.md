# Can the firmware upgrade process be canceled midway?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the firmware upgrade process be canceled midway?

## 答案 / Answer

G00125

Disable

Others

In order to ensure the stability of the OTA upgrade, after entering the upgrade process, the upgrade process cannot be canceled unless the robot's power has been turned off. If the upgrade is not successful within 30 minutes, it will be automatically terminated. It is recommended that the Silent Upgrade function is enabled, so that when a new version is detected, it will automatically upgrade your GOAT.
