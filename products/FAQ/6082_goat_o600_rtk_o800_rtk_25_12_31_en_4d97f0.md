# GOAT有工作时长限制吗？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT有工作时长限制吗？

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

GOAT程序未设置工作时长限制，理论上可24小时持续除草。当电量低于15%时，GOAT会自动返回充电；电量充至80%后，将自动回到断点继续作业。为确保更佳的定位精度、割草效果及动物安全，建议避免在雨天或天黑后使用。

The GOAT program has no set working hour limit and can theoretically mow continuously for 24 hours. When the battery level falls below 15%, GOAT will automatically return to charge. After charging to 80%, it will automatically resume operation from the point where it left off. To ensure optimal positioning accuracy, mowing results, and animal safety, it is recommended to avoid using it in rainy weather or after dark.
