# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 我的花园里有狭窄通道（长满草），能否使用自动建图功能

## Question

我的花园里有狭窄通道（长满草），能否使用自动建图功能

## Answer

It depends on the width and length of the narrow passage. If the width of the narrow passage is greater than 0.7m and the length is less than 20m, the automatic mapping function can be used, otherwise it cannot be used.
