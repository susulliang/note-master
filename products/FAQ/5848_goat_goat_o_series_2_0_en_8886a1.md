# 我是否可以搬动机器到新的草坪区域，再新增新的地图区域？ / Can I move the machine to a new lawn area and extend the map area?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我是否可以搬动机器到新的草坪区域，再新增新的地图区域？ / Can I move the machine to a new lawn area and extend the map area?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

可以。
（1）新增草坪区域为了保证与原有地图区域关系，尽量从基站或已创建地图内出发进行区域新增。
（2）若新增的区域与原区域没有通道连接，GOAT自动割草时无法进到该新增区域工作，需要设置通道或者手动将GOAT移动到该新增区域内触发割草。

Yes.
(1)To ensure that the newly added lawn area is related to the original map area, it is recommended to start adding the new area by remoting the GOAT from the base station or within the map range.. 
(2) If the newly added area has no path that connected to the original area, the GOAT will not be able to enter the new area to work. You will need to set up a pathway or manually move the GOAT into the new area to trigger the mowing.
