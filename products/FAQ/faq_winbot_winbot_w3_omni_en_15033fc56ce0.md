# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - Alert: It looks like the clean water tank is almost empty. Please refill it soon.

## Question

Alert: It looks like the clean water tank is almost
empty. Please refill it soon.

## Answer

1.Take out the Clean Water Tank, open its lid, and refill it with clean water.
2.Check the float inside the tank. If the float moves freely and returns to its original position when gently pushed, it indicates normal function.
3.Close the lid of the Clean Water Tank and ensure the clips are securely fastened.
4.Reinstall the Clean Water Tank into the station.
