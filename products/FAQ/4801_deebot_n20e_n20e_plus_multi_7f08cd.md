## 答案 / Answer

Can't the robot find the charging dock or is it difficult to return to the charging dock?

Ensure that the door of the room where the charging dock is located is not closed.
Confirm that the charging dock is located 0.5m to the left and right, there are no obstacles within 1.5m in front, and there are no reflective objects (such as mirrors, metal, glass, etc.) around the charging dock.
(3) It is recommended that the robot start cleaning from the charging dock. (If the robot is outside, please move the robot to the front of the charging dock, press the recharge button briefly, and the robot will return to the charging dock.)
Ensure that the charging dock has not been powered off or moved by humans midway.
(5) Ensure that the charging dock is not placed on the carpet, as there is greater resistance to travel on the carpet, which affects the success rate of the robot's recharge.
Make sure the charging dock is placed correctly with the Ecovacs logo facing up.
