# DEEBOT / ECOVACS X9 PRO OMNI&YEEDI S16 PLUS 中英文版 / EN - Handling Missed Cleaning Areas

## Question

Handling Missed Cleaning Areas

## Answer

1.Check for rugs/mats blocking doorways. and check in the app-Map Management-Edit Map-Carpet Settings-Bypass.
2.The robot can climb up to 2cm. If thresholds/furniture exceed 2cm in height/thickness, the area will be skipped.
3.The robot has intelligent obstacle avoidance, but to prevent damaging furniture or thresholds, it may miss some areas.
Friendly Reminder: If there are many items in the environment, to avoid missing areas during cleaning, we recommend tidying up slightly before observing.
