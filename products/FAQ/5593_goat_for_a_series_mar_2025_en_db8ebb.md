# GOAT的电池包在待机的状态下能支持多久？ / How long will GOAT's battery last when it is standby?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT的电池包在待机的状态下能支持多久？ / How long will GOAT's battery last when it is standby?

## 答案 / Answer

- GOAT G1的电池包在无任务待机的情况下能支持20小时左右。

The GOAT GX's battery pack lasts about 20 hours when it is standby.
