# I moved the GOAT far away, why didn't it trigger the ”GOAT alarms when it goes beyond the station's signal coverage“?

## Question

I moved the GOAT far away, why didn't it trigger the ”GOAT alarms when it goes beyond the station's signal coverage“?

## Answer

(1) Please confirm whether GOAT alarms when it goes beyond the station's signal coverage" is turned on in "Settings" - "More Settings" - "Security Settings"
 (2) The GPS positioning accuracy deviation of GOAT is 50 meters. When GOAT is far away from the courtyard (150m~250m), the alarm will be automatically triggered.
