# 如何更换滚刷

> **产品线**：ULTRAMARINE　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何更换滚刷

## 答案 / Answer

请您先将设备关机，再按以下步骤操作：
1.找到机器滚刷的卡扣，一一解开，取下滚刷；
2.安装新滚刷，并依次扣上每个卡扣；

Please turn off the device first, then follow these steps:
1.Locate the clips on the roller brush, release them one by one, and remove the old roller brush.
2.Install the new roller brush and fasten all the clips securely.
