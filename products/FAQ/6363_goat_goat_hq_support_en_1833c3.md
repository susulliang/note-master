# Why is there a lot of grass left on the edges?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is there a lot of grass left on the edges?

## 答案 / Answer

G00078

Disable

Mowing

There has been a certain error in the positioning of the GOAT. For the sake of positioning safety, the GOAT has a 20 cm indentation protection for the map boundary. You can mark the edges that you think have more remaining grass in Map Editing ＞ Map Optimization ＞ Automatic Edge Optimization in the app. The GOAT will use AI to finely identify and automatically correct the marked edges to ensure better mowing of this area. In addition, since the GOAT's blades are located in the center of the mower, the distance between the edge of the blades and the edge of the robot is about 10 cm. This causes a certain amount of grass to remain at the area close to the wall. Use a strimmer to trim this area.
