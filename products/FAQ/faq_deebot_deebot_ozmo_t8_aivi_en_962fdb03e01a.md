# DEEBOT / DEEBOT OZMO T8 AIVI / EN - Why is DEEBOT cleaning for a shorter period of time?

## Question

Why is DEEBOT cleaning for a shorter period of time?

## Answer

(1) Cleaning time will become shorter when the battery is near the end of its service life. If the cleaning does not meet your needs, please replace the battery.
(2) If the problem persists after following the steps above, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
