## 答案 / Answer

Definition of missed cleaning: If areas remain uncleaned after the robot's voice prompt indicating completion, it is considered a missed cleaning.                                                                                                                             1. Open the App → Tap "Enter Device" → Settings → Robot Advanced Settings → AIVI 3D 3.0 → Standard
2. If a carpet or mat is placed at the room entrance: Confirm in App Map Management ＞ Map Edit ＞ Carpet Properties that the carpet is set to "Sweep Only."
3. The robot's obstacle clearance height is 2cm. If the height or thickness exceeds 2cm, the robot will not attempt to pass and will skip cleaning that area.
4. The robot possesses intelligent obstacle avoidance capabilities. To prevent damage to furniture or door thresholds near room entrances, it may occasionally skip cleaning certain areas.
Note: In cluttered environments, tidy up slightly before cleaning to prevent missed spots.
