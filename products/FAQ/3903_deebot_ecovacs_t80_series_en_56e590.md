# Main Brush Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Main Brush Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
If you notice abnormal clicking sounds, brush entanglement alerts, or reduced suction power after extended use, follow these steps:
1.Power off the robot by switching the toggle to "O", remove the dirty water box, and flip the robot upside down.
2.Press both tabs inward on the main brush cover to release it, then remove the main brush assembly.
3.Clean debris/hair Wrappings from the main brush.
4.Reinstall the main brush and rotate it manually to ensure smooth movement.
5.Align the main brush cover with the slots on the robot and press firmly until a "click" confirms proper installation.
6.Reattach the dirty water box.
