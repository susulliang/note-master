# GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

1.GOAT夜间视觉定位能力较弱，无法识别小动物，因此不支持夜间自动割草。
2.若天黑时任务未完成，GOAT会在次日早上8:00主动触发补割。
3.机器前端补光灯仅用于返回基站，不支持夜间割草。
4.如需夜间手动割草，可开启遥控割草功能，完成后遥控机器返回充电站。

1. GOAT's nighttime visual positioning capability is limited and cannot identify small animals, hence it does not support automatic nighttime mowing.
 2. If the task remains incomplete after dark, GOAT will automatically trigger a mow session at 8:00 AM the following day.
 3. The front-mounted Supplementary Light is solely for returning to the station and does not support nighttime mowing.
 4. To manually mow at night, activate the remote mowing function and guide the machine back to the charging station upon completion.
