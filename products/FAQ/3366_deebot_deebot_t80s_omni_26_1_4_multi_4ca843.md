## 答案 / Answer

Friendly reminder:
If you encounter situations such as the robot spinning in place or abnormal movement patterns during use, try the following steps:
1. Check if the laser module (located at the center of the front bumper) is dusty. If so, gently wipe it with a dry paper towel;
2. Check for excessive indoor obstacles. High-precision obstacle avoidance may cause the path to appear disordered; slight rearrangement is recommended.
3. Check if the Driving Wheels are dirty. If so, wipe them clean with a dry cloth.
