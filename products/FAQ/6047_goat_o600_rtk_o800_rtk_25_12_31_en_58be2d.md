# RTK基站应放置在何处？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

RTK基站应放置在何处？

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

RTK基站若受建筑物遮挡可能无法接收卫星信号，建议按以下原则判断安装位置是否合适：
1.RTK杆应远离墙体、大型树木等，建议距离＞2m；
2.基站上方需无遮挡，确保卫星信号正常接收；
3.信号头需竖直向上，不可倾斜；避免放置于松软地面，以防角度偏移；
4.通电1分钟后，若基站蓝色指示灯常亮即可使用。                                                                                                                                            5.若放置在高度低于基站的草丛中，请定期清理周边植物，防止其长高或缠绕基站杆。

If the RTK station is obstructed by buildings, satellite signals may be lost. Follow these principles to assess installation suitability:
 1. Position the RTK pole at least 2 meters away from walls, large trees, etc.
 2. Ensure no obstructions above the station for uninterrupted satellite signal reception.
 3. The signal head must be vertically oriented and not tilted; avoid placement on soft ground to prevent angle deviation;
 4. After powering on for 1 minute, the station is ready for use if the blue Indicator light remains steady. 5. If placed in grass shorter than the station, regularly trim surrounding vegetation to prevent overgrowth or entanglement around the pole.
