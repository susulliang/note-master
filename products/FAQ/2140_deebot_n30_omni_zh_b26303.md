What can I do if the mopping pad plate can not expand outward normally?

1.Check if Truedge Adaptive Edge Mopping is turned on. Enter APP-Click Robot Controls-Click Settings-Robot advanced settings-Truedge Adaptive Edge Mopping.                                                       2.Check if it is in mopping mode.                                                                                                                3.Try spoting cleaning to see if it is normal.
