## 问题 / Question

清洗GOAT的注意事项

## 答案 / Answer

8- 保养维护

1. 避免使用清洁喷剂或洗涤剂，以免损坏机身
2. 切勿使用高压水枪，高压水流将进入密封件内、并损坏电子机械部件
Tips:
* 在清洁GOAT前，请先关闭割草机电源；
* 清洁过程中，请始终佩戴防护手套以免刀片划伤。

1. Avoid using cleaning sprays or detergents to prevent damage to the body.
 2. Never use a high-pressure water gun, as high-pressure water may enter seals and damage electronic/mechanical components.
 Tips:
 * Before cleaning GOAT, turn off the mower's power first.
 * Always wear protective gloves during cleaning to avoid cuts from the blades.
