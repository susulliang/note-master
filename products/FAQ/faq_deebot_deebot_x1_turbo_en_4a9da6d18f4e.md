# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 TURBO / EN - Are voice command records saved on the server? Can I delete them?

## Question

Are voice command records saved on the server? Can I delete them?

## Answer

Voice command records are saved on the server and can be viewed and cleared in the App  > Settings > YIKO Voice Assistant >
