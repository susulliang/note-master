# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 / EN - Why does DEEBOT X1 slide when mopping?

## Question

Why does DEEBOT X1 slide when mopping?

## Answer

Different floor materials and the air humidity in different seasons will result in different amounts of surface water. Select the low water level in the App for mopping can reduce the chance of sliding.
