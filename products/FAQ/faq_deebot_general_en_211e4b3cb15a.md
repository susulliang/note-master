# Alert:WINBOT slips

## Question

Alert:WINBOT slips

## Answer

1.Please flip the robot over and use a clean cloth to wipe off dirt or oil from the drive tracks.
2.If the glass is too wet, wipe it with a clean cloth before attaching the WINBOT.
Tip: To prevent slipping, avoid using WINBOT on humid days (e.g., rainy weather), as water stains may form on the glass.
