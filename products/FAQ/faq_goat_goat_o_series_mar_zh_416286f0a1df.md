# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 地图上GOAT 的位置和实际和位置不符？

## Question

地图上GOAT 的位置和实际和位置不符？

## Answer

(1) Please confirm whether GOAT has been moved or dragged. If the task is not started after that, GOAT will not be relocated, the position of GOAT before it is moved will still be displayed on the APP map
(2) Please confirm whether GOAT is outside the charging station when the task is triggered. GOAT needs a certain amount of time to locate, during which time the GOAT location may not be displayed on the APP
(3) If the GOAT is in a location without RTK signal (e.g., in a shaded area), it will use visual sensor for positioning to ensure grass cutting effectiveness. The position of the GOAT may have slight deviations in such cases.

If the above situation has been eliminated, please check
(1) Whether the camera of GOAT is dirty
(2) Whether the RTK signal is in normal condition.
(3) Whether the current weather is fine.
If GOAT consistently appears incorrectly on the map, you can delete the map and rebuild it.
