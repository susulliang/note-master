# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Can I control the GOAT to be close to the garden or lawn boundary all the way?

## Question

Can I control the GOAT to be close to the garden or lawn boundary all the way?

## Answer

Due to a certain error in the positioning of the GOAT, we recommend that you keep a safe distance of more than 10 cm from a boundary with a drop height of more than 3 cm while remotely controlling the GOAT, to prevent the GOAT from falling or getting stuck due to a positioning error.
