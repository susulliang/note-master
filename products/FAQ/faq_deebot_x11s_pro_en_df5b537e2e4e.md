# How to add Disinfectant Cleaning Solution/Heavy-Duty Cleaning Solution?

## Question

How to add Disinfectant Cleaning Solution/Heavy-Duty Cleaning Solution?

## Answer

1. Remove the Cleaning Solution Box:
Remove the front cover of the station. Insert your finger into the recess at the bottom of the cleaning solution box, pull out the two boxes, and place them steadily on a hard, level surface.
2. Add Cleaning Solution and Reinstall:
Open the lids of the cleaning solution boxes. Add the Disinfectant Cleaning Solution to the blue box and the Heavy-Duty Cleaning Solution to the black box. Close the lids tightly and reinstall the box back into the station.
3. Reset the Station Cover:
Put the front cover back on the station and press down to ensure a proper seal.
4. Open the APP, go to Settings > Station Advanced Settings, and turn on the "Auto Cleaning Solution Dispense / Auto Heavy-duty Cleaning Solution Dispense" switches respectively.
Note: The machine will automatically add the cleaning solution in the correct proportion, so you no longer need to add cleaning solution to the clean water tank.
