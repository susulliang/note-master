# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？

## Question

GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？

## Answer

GOAT is not equipped with night vision and due to weak vision localization ability at night, it is unable to recognize small animals. Therefore, it cannot mow at night.
If you need to manually mow at night, you can enable the remote control mowing function and then, once the task is completed, use the remote control to guide the machine back to the charging station.
