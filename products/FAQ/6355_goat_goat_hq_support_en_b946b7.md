# Is there a limit on how long the GOAT G1 can work?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Is there a limit on how long the GOAT G1 can work?

## 答案 / Answer

G00070

Disable

Mowing

There is no limit to the working hours set in the program of the GOAT G1. In theory, you could set the GOAT to mow 24 hours a day. When power is less than 20%, the GOAT will automatically return to the docking station to charge. When power reaches 80%, the GOAT will automatically head to the breakpoint location to continue mowing. However, in order to ensure better positioning and mowing results, as well as animal safety,  it's not recommended to mow in the rain or after dark.
