# 集尘效果不好，如何处理？

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

集尘效果不好，如何处理？

## 答案 / Answer

1.请先把集尘档位设为标准档+高频后在尝试。
 2.请检查集尘舱门是否安装到位：若集尘舱门没有扣合到位会导致集尘仓漏气，导致真空度上不去，会造成无法机器正常集尘。
 3.请检查基站后方出风口是否离墙壁太近，太近会影响出风进而影响集尘效率，建议1.5cm以上离墙间隙。
 4.请检查主机集尘通道或基站集尘口是否有大垃圾卡滞阻塞通道（如牙签、小玩具等），如有需手动清理。
 5.请确定您的机器工作模式是否为边刷边拖，机器在边扫边拖的模式下，若地面水渍较多会造成尘盒内壁潮湿，毛絮垃圾挂壁会日积月累形成堆积，最终尘盒堵塞影响集尘效果，建议您定期检查并清理尘盒，或清洁方式改为先扫后拖效果更好。

1. First set the dust collection mode to Standard + High Frequency before attempting.
 2. Check if the Dust Collection Cabin Cover is properly installed: If the cover is not fully latched, air leakage from the Dust Collection Cabin will prevent vacuum pressure from building, causing the machine to fail to collect dust normally.
 3. Verify the station's rear exhaust vent is not too close to walls. Proximity reduces airflow and compromises dust collection efficiency. Maintain a minimum 1.5cm clearance from walls.
 4. Inspect the ROBOT's dust collection path or station's dust inlet for obstructions caused by large debris (e.g., toothpicks, small toys). Manually clear any blockages if found.
 5. Verify if your machine is operating in Simultaneous Sweeping and Mopping mode. In this mode, excessive moisture from water stains on the floor can cause the Dust Bin to become damp. Over time, lint and debris may accumulate inside the dust bin, eventually clogging the Dust Bin and impairing dust collection. We recommend regularly inspecting and cleaning the Dust Bin, or switching to Sweeping first, then Mopping for better results.
