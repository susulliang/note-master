# 机器人无法越过20mm的门槛. Robots cannot cross the 20mm threshold.

## Question

机器人无法越过20mm的门槛. Robots cannot cross the 20mm threshold.

## Answer

（1）20mm的越障高度是在科沃斯实验室标准条件下测得，由于不同用户家庭环境差异，门槛的形状，宽度，上门槛的角度等因素，都会对机器人的越障效果产生一定的影响，如无法解决请在科沃斯官方商城购买门槛条；
（2）驱动轮在运行过程中有可能沾上宠物和人体的毛发，会影响越障性能，建议越障失败时使用湿抹布/湿巾清理驱动轮；在日常使用中建议定时清理，以获得更佳的越障表现；                                                                                                                                                       （1）The obstacle-crossing height of 20mm was measured under the standard conditions of the Ecovacs laboratory. Due to differences in the home environment of different users, factors such as the shape, width, and angle of the threshold will have a certain impact on the obstacle-crossing effect of the robot. If it cannot be solved, please purchase the threshold strip on the Ecovacs official store.
(2) The driving wheel may come into contact with pet and human hair during operation, which will affect the obstacle crossing performance. It is recommended to use a wet cloth/wet wipe to clean the driving wheel when the obstacle crossing fails; it is recommended to clean it regularly in daily use to achieve better obstacle crossing performance.
