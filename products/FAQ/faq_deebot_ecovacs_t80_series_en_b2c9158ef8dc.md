# DEEBOT / ECOVACS T80 Series / EN - How do I create a multi-layer map?

## Question

How do I create a multi-layer map?

## Answer

1.Make sure Robot  has successfully connected to ECOVACS HOME App and is online.
2. Open the App and click "Enter ", click "Map" and click "+" to add a new map.
Note: Please move the Robot to the new environment first.
