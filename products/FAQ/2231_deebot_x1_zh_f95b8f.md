# DEEBOT Slips and cannot return to self-cleaning station or base station.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

DEEBOT Slips and cannot return to self-cleaning station or base station.

## 答案 / Answer

DEEBOT Slips and cannot return to self-cleaning station or base station.

1. Check whether the base station tray and the surrounding ground have many water stains. If so, please wipe the recharging tray and the surrounding ground with a dry cloth.
2. Add cleaning solution to the water tank, spray the cleaning solution for 5-8 times, and then fill it with water.
3. Adjust the water level in the app to low water flow level.
4. When using the mopping pad for the first time, it needs to be dry to stick on the mopping pad plate to avoid getting wet.
