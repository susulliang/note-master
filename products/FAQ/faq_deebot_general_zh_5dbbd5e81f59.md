# 快速建图后，再进行一次全屋清洁，分区怎么变掉了？ Why did the partition changes when finishes a whole house cleaning after quick mapping?

## Question

快速建图后，再进行一次全屋清洁，分区怎么变掉了？                  Why did the partition changes when finishes a whole house cleaning after quick mapping?

## Answer

（1）快速建图后，再进行一次全屋清洁，地图信息可能会有微小差异，导致某些地图区域变化，建议用户使用手动分区或者设置区域类型进行地图编辑；                                                                                                                                                                                      （1）After quick mapping, then perform a full house cleaning again. There may be slight differences in map information, which may cause changes in certain map areas. It is recommended that users use manual zoning or set area types.
