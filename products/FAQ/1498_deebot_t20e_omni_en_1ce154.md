# How do I upgrade the robot’s firmware?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I upgrade the robot’s firmware?

## 答案 / Answer

D20337

Upgrade

1. DEEBOT must be placed on the station and it must be charging. 
2. DEEBOT needs to be charged up to 50% (it's recommended to upgrade after charging for 3 hours). 
3. Make sure DEEBOT is connected with the ECOVACS HOME App, and the WiFi signal is good.
4. Enter Smart Cleaning ＞Settings ＞ About DEEBOT＞Update DEEBOT

Note: If automatic update is enabled, DEEBOT will automatically update to the latest version.
