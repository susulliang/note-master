# How to install the robot correctly?

## Question

How to install the robot correctly?

## Answer

1. Please open the outer packaging box to remove the robot, station, and accessory packaging boxes, and remove the protective bags on the robot and station.
2. Please place the robot upside down with the bottom facing up.
3. Please take out the side brush from the accessory packaging box, Align it with the card slot, then firmly press it down. Once you hear a 'click,' it indicates that the installation is complete.
4. Please install the removable base, ensuring that you pay attention to the buckles on both sides. Once you hear a 'click,' it indicates that the installation is complete.
5. Please take out the power cord from the accessory packaging box, connect it to the power interface located on the back of the station, then turn on the power. Find a suitable position to place it against the wall.
(Please ensure the Auto-Clean Station is positioned on a flat, hard surface, and there are no items within 5cm to the left and 80cm in front of it.)
Reminder:
1.The station is preferably placed on tiled floors. If you place it on wooden floors, please check regularly whether there are any water stains left on the bottom of the station. If so, please clean them promptly to prevent moisture damage.
2.For machines in the US version, you need to turn on the power located on the back of the station.
