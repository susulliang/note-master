# APP中显示RTK的信号覆盖，地图大面积显示无信号覆盖是什么原因 / Why is there a large area without signal shown through RTK signal quality map on the APP?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

APP中显示RTK的信号覆盖，地图大面积显示无信号覆盖是什么原因 / Why is there a large area without signal shown through RTK signal quality map on the APP?

## 答案 / Answer

RTK Reference Station

GOAT在当前位置无法收到卫星的信号。有树冠树荫遮挡或建筑物遮挡等。

GOAT is unable to receive the satellite's signal at its current location beacause of anopy shade or building shade, etc.
