# 滤篮的拆卸，清洗&安装

> **产品线**：ULTRAMARINE　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

滤篮的拆卸，清洗&安装

## 答案 / Answer

当您发现滤篮里有脏污，或者清洁效果不好的时候，您可以尝试拆卸滤篮并清洗，具体步骤如下：                   
1.按压机器前方按钮，打开机器滤篮仓盖，取出滤篮组件；
2.清空滤篮里的碎屑；
3.拆下内部精细过滤篮，然后用软管彻底冲洗内外滤篮层；
4.清洗完毕后，务必晾干滤网和滤篮，然后将其放回泳池机器人中；
注：                                                                                                                                                      
1.建议在每次工作完毕后都清洗滤篮；                                                                                                       
2.建议3个月更换一次，保证工作正常。 对于3μm的滤篮，在使用30次之后，建议更换，以确保最佳的过滤效果；

When you find dirt in the filter basket or notice that the cleaning effect is not satisfactory, you can try disassembling and cleaning the filter basket by following these steps:
1.Press the button on the front of the robot to open the filter basket cover and remove the filter basket assembly.
2.Empty the debris from the filter basket. 
3.If the 3 μm filter is installed, remove it, then thoroughly rinse the inner and outer filter baskets separaterly.
4.After cleaning, make sure to air-dry the filter mesh and filter basket before placing them back into the pool robot.

Note:
1.It is recommended to clean the filter basket after each use.
2.It is recommended to replace the filter basket every three months to ensure proper operation. For the 3μm filter mesh, it is recommended to replace it after 30 uses to ensure optimal filtering performance."
