# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 什么场景下需要设置低避让区？

## Question

什么场景下需要设置低避让区？

## Answer

Within low-avoidance zones, the mower automatically disables its non-contact obstacle avoidance system, relying solely on physical contact sensors (bumper plates) to detect obstacles. For safety, AI recognition remains active to avoid living beings like humans, hedgehogs, cats, and dogs. This feature is suitable for the following scenarios to prevent visual system misidentification of obstacles, reduce unnecessary detours, and avoid missed mowing:
 1. Flat non-grass areas within the lawn (e.g., stone paths, manhole covers)
 2. Overhanging obstacles (e.g., low-hanging branches)
 3. Soft or irregularly shaped plants (e.g., dandelions, wild grasses)
