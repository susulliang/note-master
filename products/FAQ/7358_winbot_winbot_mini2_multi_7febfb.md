# What should I do if the WINBOT slips (slides laterally/body shakes)?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

What should I do if the WINBOT slips (slides laterally/body shakes)?

## 答案 / Answer

Kind Reminder:
* Too much water on the glass surface will cause slipping. Do not use WINBOT in high humidity (such as rainy days).
* Excessive water on the wiping pad will also cause slipping. Ensure the pad is wrung out until it no longer drips.
1. Flip the WINBOT over and use a clean cloth to wipe any dirt or oil from the driving wheel tracks.
2. If the glass surface is damp, wipe it with a dry cloth before adhering the WINBOT.
