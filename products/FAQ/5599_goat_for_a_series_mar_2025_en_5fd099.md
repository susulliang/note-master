# 阳光棚安装会影响机器的功能吗-LiDAR / Will the garage affect the function of the GOAT?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

阳光棚安装会影响机器的功能吗-LiDAR / Will the garage affect the function of the GOAT?

## 答案 / Answer

（1）如您使用的是Ecovacs提供的配套阳光棚，不会影响机器的功能使用。
（2）如您使用的是DIY/其他品牌的阳光棚，无法评估其影响，请注意不要遮挡GOAT回充的视线和路径，影响GOAT回充。

(1) It is recommended to use the garage provided by Ecovacs.
(2) The impact cannot be accessed if you are using other kind of garage.
(3) Please be careful not to block the white reflective film on the base station.
