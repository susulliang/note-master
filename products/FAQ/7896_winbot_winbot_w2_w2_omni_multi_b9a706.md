# What is the water spray coverage of the WINBOT W2?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the water spray coverage of the WINBOT W2?

## 答案 / Answer

W00089+

When WINBOT W2 walks in a back and forth pattern, the water spray coverage on both sides of the robot can reach the upper edge of the robot and 10 cm below the robot. Please note that water will be sprayed only upwards and not downwards when the robot walks vertically. The water spray interval is about 8 seconds.
