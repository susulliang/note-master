# DEEBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - WINBOT Storage Guide

## Question

WINBOT Storage Guide

## Answer

1.Place the WINBOT into the station with the front facing out and the cable outlet facing up.
2.Short press the station’s cable retract button to quickly retract the cable. Then press and hold the power button for 2 seconds to turn off the station.
3.Place the station near a power outlet and connect it to power. Charging takes about 3 hours at room temperature. The blue indicator breathes while charging and stays solid when fully charged.
