# How to disassemble/clean/install the dust bin correctly?

## Question

How to disassemble/clean/install the dust bin correctly?

## Answer

1. Remove the surface cover of the robot and take out the dust bin.
2. Unlock the dust bin and empty the garbage.
3. Extract the filter element and clean the dust from the primary filter (note: clean both the inside and outside).
4. Gently tap the filter element and rinse the dust bin with water.
5. After cleaning, reinstall the filter cartridge and place it back into the main unit.
Reminder: If cleaned with water, allow it to dry in a well-ventilated area before use.
