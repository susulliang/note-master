# 44

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

44

## 答案 / Answer

1.Slightly lift the side of the robot where the roller mop is installed, release the blue buckle on the water tray, and remove the water tray.
2.Remove the robot's top cover, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, and flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
3.To install the Mop Roller Smart Cover, accurately align it with the gap on the outside of the roller mop compartment.
Note: The end of the mop roller smart cover with the tab should face outward, and the tab should be facing downward. During installation, first ensure the end with the tab is securely in place, then push the entire mop roller smart cover down to its lowest position so that it is level with the roller mop compartment.
4.After completing the above steps, push the blue buckle inward. When you hear a "click" sound, it indicates the buckle is securely fastened.
5.Please reinstall the water tray onto the bottom of the robot. You should hear a "click" sound, which means it's securely in place.
