# 地图出现异常(歪斜、重叠)的情况时如何处理? / How to deal with abnormal situations(skew、overlapping) in the map?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图出现异常(歪斜、重叠)的情况时如何处理? / How to deal with abnormal situations(skew、overlapping) in the map?

## 答案 / Answer

1.可在APP地图管理中使用"恢复地图"功能,恢复出初始保存的地图,继续使用;
2.若初始保存的地图不满足需要,可删除地图,重新建图。

1.You can restore the initially saved map via APP, enter App-click Map-Restore Map.
2.If the initially saved map does not meet your needs, you can delete the map and rebuild it.
