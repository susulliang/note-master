# DEEBOT / T50 降配版 / EN - How to deal with the mopping pad plate not lifting automatically?

## Question

How to deal with the mopping pad plate not lifting automatically?

## Answer

1. Please clean the mopping pad plate of foreign objects.
2. Remove the mopping pad plate and reinstall it.
