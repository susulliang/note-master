# 为什么我的GOAT出了外轮廓或者穿越了禁区？-LiDAR / Why does my GOAT leave the map boundaries or enter the restricted areas?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么我的GOAT出了外轮廓或者穿越了禁区？-LiDAR / Why does my GOAT leave the map boundaries or enter the restricted areas?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

（1）请检查建图时是否光线和天气都满足要求，如果建图时有小雨或者光线暗可能导致建图质量下降，导致定位偏差，机器出界。建议重新建图
（2）基站周围2米内为回充默认通行区域，可能出现出界和穿禁区情况
（3）请确认机器工作过程中是否有拖拽GOAT，拖拽后会造成机器定位偏差，可能造成出界
（4）地图中如果存在鹅卵石路面可能造成GOAT打滑出界
（6）如建图时，边界距离危险区域太近＜10cm，请设置物理围挡

(1) Please check whether the lighting and weather conditions at the time of map creation meet the requirements. In the event of light rain or dark environments at the time of map creation, the map quality may be poor, which may result in positioning errors and cause the GOAT to leave the boundaries. In this case, we recommend that you remap. 
(2) By default, the area within 2 m around the docking station is a free area, so that the GOAT can return and recharge. This may cause the robot to leave the boundaries or enter restricted areas.
(3) Please confirm if there is any dragging of GOAT during the operation of the machine. Dragging may cause positioning deviation of the machine and may cause it to go out of bounds
(4) If there are cobblestone roads on the map, it may cause GOAT to slip out of bounds
(6)When creating the map, if the boundary is too close to the danger zone by less than 10cm, please set up a physical fence
