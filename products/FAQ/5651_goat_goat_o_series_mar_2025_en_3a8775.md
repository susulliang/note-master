# 在机器自动建图时，能否不跟随机器？ / Can I not follow the machine when the GOAT auto mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

在机器自动建图时，能否不跟随机器？ / Can I not follow the machine when the GOAT auto mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

能，但建议跟随，以免机器绘制的地图需要切入到遥控模式。
请确保GOAT在基站上所连接的Wifi信号稳定，地图将通过网络传输至收集APP

Yes, but it is recommended to follow, in case you need to switch into remote control mode or modify the route. 
Please make sure that the WiFi signal connected to GOAT is stable when GOAT back to the docking station, the map will uoload to the APP then.
