# Can I remote control the map into any desired shape?

## Question

Can I remote control the map into any desired shape?

## Answer

A map can be generated normally as long as the map can be closed. However, in order for the GOAT to work well, it is recommended that sharp corners and intersecting areas do not appear on the map, or this may make the GOAT get stuck.
