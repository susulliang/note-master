# GOAT / GX / ZH - Can the GOAT mow at night?

## Question

Can the GOAT mow at night?

## Answer

GOAT is not equipped with night vision and due to weak vision localization ability at night, it is unable to recognize small animals. Therefore, it is recommended that mowing tasks not be initiated after dark.
