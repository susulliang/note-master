# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Why is the GOAT G1 moving in a chaotic route when mowing?

## Question

Why is the GOAT G1 moving in a chaotic route when mowing?

## Answer

(1) If there are too many obstacles in the garden, the robot will avoid these obstacles, resulting in a chaotic route.
(2) On slopes with an inclination of greater than 20° or in narrow areas, the robot is not able to mow in a zigzag pattern
