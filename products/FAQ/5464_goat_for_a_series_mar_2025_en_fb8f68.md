# 自动建图时我可以不跟随机器吗 / Can I not follow the machine when the GOAT auto mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

自动建图时我可以不跟随机器吗 / Can I not follow the machine when the GOAT auto mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

能，但建议跟随，以免机器绘制的地图需要切入到遥控模式。

Yes, but it is recommended to follow, in case you need to switch into remote control mode.
