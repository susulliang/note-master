# Will the water spray onto the window frame?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Will the water spray onto the window frame?

## 答案 / Answer

W00170

The WINBOT W2 has frame distance detection, and will not spray water when the distance to the frame is insufficient.
