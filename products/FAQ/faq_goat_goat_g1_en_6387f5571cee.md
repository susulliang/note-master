# Can I hide the navigation beacon in a concealed location?

## Question

Can I hide the navigation beacon in a concealed location?

## Answer

For accurate positioning, please try to ensure the following:
(1) The front of the navigation beacon is more than 5 m away from any large obstacle more than 60cm in height on the lawn.
(2) The back of the navigation beacon is more than 20 cm away from hedges and metal fencing on the boundary of the lawn.
(3) There are no obstacles between the connections of the navigation beacon, and the distance between the beacons is not greater than 45 m.
(4) The robot is able to receive signal from at least one beacon when it is at the docking station.
