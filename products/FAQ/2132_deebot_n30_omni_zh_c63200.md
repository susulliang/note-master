How to install and use the soild floor cleaner?

1.Remove the clean and dirty water tanks.
2.Unscrew the small cap on the base and insert the cleaner (with the correct orientation).
3.Tighten the cap and reinstall the tanks.
4.Open the APP-Click Robot Controls-Click Station，then tap "Activate Soild Floor Cleaner". The cleaner  will rinsed for 100 seconds, and the dirty water will be drained automatically.
Note: Replace the Soild Floor Cleaner every 30 days or when the core color fades.
