# DEEBOT / X12 PRO OMNI / EN - What is the Off-Peak Charging Function? How do I use it?

## Question

What is the Off-Peak Charging Function? How do I use it?

## Answer

Off-Peak Charging Function supports customized charging periods to avoid peak electricity usage hours and save on electricity costs.

The Off-Peak Charging function is disabled by default. You can enable it via the APP path: Settings -Station Advanced Settings - Off-Peak Charging. Once enabled, you need to set the effective time period. The device will only charge the battery to full during this period. Outside this period, it will only ensure the battery maintains a safe charge level, meaning it will not charge the battery if the remaining charge is above 30%.

To ensure normal operation, the device may still charge the battery outside the Off-Peak Charging period. If there is an unfinished task, the device will charge the battery to the level required for the task outside the Off-Peak Charging period. If no tasks are pending, the device will only maintain the safe charge level. (The default Off-Peak Charging duration is 7 hours, and the duration cannot be set to less than 7 hours.)

Outside the Off-Peak Charging period, the device will charge the battery to the safe charge level of 30%. If the battery level is already at or above 30%, it will maintain the current charge level without charging to full.
