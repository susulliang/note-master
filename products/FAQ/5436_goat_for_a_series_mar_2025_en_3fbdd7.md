# GOAT为何充电很慢？ / Why is the GOAT charging so slowly?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT为何充电很慢？ / Why is the GOAT charging so slowly?

## 答案 / Answer

Charging Station

（1）GOAT在正常情况下,工作中当电量低于15%时会自动回充，达到85%后会继续割草。充电时长如下：
A3000 LiDAR:45±5min
（2）GOAT从0%电量充电到100%充电时长如下：
A3000 LiDAR:90min
（3）当电池温度≤8℃时为了保护电池基站将会切换为小电流充电，充电时间会变长（电池完全充满需3~5个小时）
（4）长期使用后电池老化，可能导致充电时间变长
如非以上情况，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门。

(1) Under normal conditions,  GOAT will automatically recharge when the battery level drops below 15% during operation, and will continue mowing after reaching 85%. The charging duration is as follows:
A3000 LiDAR:45±5min
(2)The charging duration of GOAT  from 0% to 100% is as follows：
A3000 LiDAR:90min
(3) When the temperature of the docking station's battery is ≤8°C, the charging time will become longer as it switches to a low current charge (3–5 hours for a fully charged battery) to protect the battery. 
(4) The battery ages after long-term use which may result in a longer charging time. 
If none of the above scenarios apply, please contact ECOVACS customer service.
