# 我的Lidar传感器工作中会有奇怪的声音，是不是坏了？ / My Lidar sensor makes strange noises while working, is it broken?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的Lidar传感器工作中会有奇怪的声音，是不是坏了？ / My Lidar sensor makes strange noises while working, is it broken?

## 答案 / Answer

LiDAR

没有坏
如果传感器异常会有传感器异常提醒，正常工作下Lidar内部电机转动，会带来一定的声音和振动，

Not bad
If the sensor is abnormal, there will be a sensor abnormality reminder. Under normal operation, the rotation of the Lidar internal motor will cause certain sound and vibration,
