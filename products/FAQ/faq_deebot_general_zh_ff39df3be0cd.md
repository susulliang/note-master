# 机器人未清扫完毕返回充电.The robot has not finished cleaning and is returning to charge

## Question

机器人未清扫完毕返回充电.The robot has not finished cleaning and is returning to charge

## Answer

（1）房间较大或家居环境非常复杂的情况下，单次清洁无法完成，需要中途返回充电，建议在APP中打开“智能断点续扫”功能；
（2）若地图中某个区域房间门关闭，机器多次尝试无法进入时会直接报被阻挡，会继续打扫下一个区域。在下一次清洁时，提前打开所有房间门，重新启动自动清洁即可。                                                                                                                                                             (1) When the room is large or the home environment is very complex, a single cleaning cannot be completed, and it is necessary to return to charging midway. It is recommended to turn on the "Resumed Clean" function in the APP;
(2) If the door of a room in a certain area of the map is closed and the machine cannot enter after multiple attempts, it will directly report being blocked and continue to clean the next area. For the next cleaning, open all room doors in advance and restart the auto cleaning.
