# How do I use the robot's Wi-Fi management function?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I use the robot's Wi-Fi management function?

## 答案 / Answer

D20404

WiFi Connection

1. Confirm that DEEBOT has successfully connected to the app and is online. 
2. Open the app and go to Smart Cleaning ＞ Settings (in the upper-right corner) ＞ DEEBOT Information ＞ Wi-Fi Management and tap "+" to add multiple Wi-Fi connections.
