# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 为什么有部分区域割草方向与设置不符？

## Question

为什么有部分区域割草方向与设置不符？

## Answer

GOAT will use visual sensor to complementary positioning when RTK signal is affected in the shaded area.  In the poor RTK signal area, the GOAT will adjust the mowing direction according to the senario which will be slightly inconsistent with the setting.
