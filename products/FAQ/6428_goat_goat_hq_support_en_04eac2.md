# What is the mode for switching between Wi-Fi and auto-connection module?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the mode for switching between Wi-Fi and auto-connection module?

## 答案 / Answer

G00143

Disable

Additional Connection Module

The Wi-Fi signal is automatically switched when there is a good Wi-Fi signal. It is switched to the module's signal when the Wi-Fi signal is weak.
