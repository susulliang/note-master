# DEEBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - Is It Normal for WINBOT to Be Noisy While Working?

## Question

Is It Normal for WINBOT to Be Noisy While Working?

## Answer

Yes, it’s normal.
WINBOT uses a high-speed fan system to ensure strong suction and stable attachment to the glass.
This design enhances safety but may produce noticeable noise during operation.
