# 17

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

17

## 答案 / Answer

If the robot alerts you that the anti-drop sensors are dusty or abnormal, it's recommended to first turn off the robot and then follow these steps:
1.Slightly lift the side where the roller mop is installed, gently release the blue buckle on the water tray, and remove the water tray.
2.Take off the top cover of the robot, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, then flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
3.Clean the anti-drop sensors on the bottom to remove any dust that might be blocking them.
4.After cleaning the sensors, reinstall the water tray. You should hear a "click" sound, which means it's securely in place.
5.Turn the robot back on and observe if the issue is resolved.
