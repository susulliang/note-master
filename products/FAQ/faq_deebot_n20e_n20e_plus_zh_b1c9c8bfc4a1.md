# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 机器人为何无法将地毯识别完整或将地毯判断为地板？Why can't robots recognize carpets completely or judge carpets as floors?

## Question

机器人为何无法将地毯识别完整或将地毯判断为地板？Why can't robots recognize carpets completely or judge carpets as floors?

## Answer

（1）机器人无法识别部分特殊材质及规格地毯，请检查家中地毯是否拥有以下特征：硬度过高（接近木质地板硬度），毛长过短2mm以下，或PVC等特殊材质。                                                                                                                                                               （2）对于未被识别到的地毯区域，可以通过在地图编辑中的设置虚拟墙，以防机器人打湿地毯；                                                                  （1）The robot cannot recognize some special materials and specifications of carpets. Please check if the carpets in your home have the following characteristics: high hardness (close to the hardness of wooden floors), short hair length below 2mm, or special materials such as PVC.                                                                                                                                                                 (2) For areas of carpets that have not been recognized, virtual walls can be set in the map editing to prevent the robot from wetting the carpet.
