# 集尘袋更换指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

集尘袋更换指导

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 当您的机器出现“尘袋已满提示”等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行操作尝试：
 1. 请从两侧取下集尘仓盖；
 2. 取出尘袋并丢弃；
 3. 用干抹布擦拭集尘仓，安装新尘袋；
 4. 关闭集尘仓盖；
 5.请在APP里面点击“耗材与部件”＞“尘袋”＞“我已更换”

Friendly Reminder:
 When your machine displays alerts like "Dust Bag full," try the following steps:
 1. Remove the Dust Collection Cabin cover from both sides;
 2. Take out and discard the Dust Bag;
 3. Wipe the Dust Bin with a dry cloth and install a new Dust Bag;
 4. Close the Dust Collection Cabin cover;
 5. In the app, tap "Maintenance" ＞ "Dust Bag" ＞ "I've Replaced It"
