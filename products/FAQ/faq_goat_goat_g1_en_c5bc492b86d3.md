# I have returned the abnormal Navigation Beacon to its original position according to the App prompt. Why does the problem persist on the App?

## Question

I have returned the abnormal Navigation Beacon to its original position according to the App prompt. Why does the problem persist on the App?

## Answer

The GOAT cannot detect the status of the Navigation Beacon in real time and can only get the status when the GOAT is working close to a Navigation Beacon. If you have confirmed that the fault has been resolved following the prompts, you can start one task and the faulty status in the App will be updated automatically.
