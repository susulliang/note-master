# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 OMNI / EN - Why does the docking station smell when emptying?

## Question

Why does the docking station smell when emptying?

## Answer

When a robot is used for the first time, the noise-canceling, fireproof cotton may emit a slight odor during emptying, but this will not affect normal use.
