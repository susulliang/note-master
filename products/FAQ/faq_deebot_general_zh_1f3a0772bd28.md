# 如何正确给机器人充电及谷电充电说明

## Question

如何正确给机器人充电及谷电充电说明

## Answer

Robot Charging Guide                                                                                                                         1. Remove the cover and turn on the robot by switching the power toggle to "I".
2. Place the Robot in front of the station; press and hold the "auto button" to let the Robot return to the station automatically and hear the voice prompt "start charging".
Off-peak Charging Instructions
1. Open the app - click enter - Settings -  Station Advanced Settings - Toggle "Off-peak Charging " ON.
2. Default off, when enabled, the battery fully charges only during your set schedule and maintains a safe minimum otherwise.;
3. To ensure the normal operation of the robot, the battery may still be charged during non-Off-peak Charging  hours. If a task is not completed, the robot will be charged to the required level during the non-Off-peak Charging period (the default charging duration is 7 hours, and cannot be set to be less than 7 hours);
4. During non-Off-peak Charging periods, the robot will be charged to a minimum of 30% and then maintained at 30%;
