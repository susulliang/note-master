# Can DEEBOT be damaged if left charging for too long?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can DEEBOT be damaged if left charging for too long?

## 答案 / Answer

D20015

Enable

Charging

No, once DEEBOT has fully charged, it goes to sleep and will not become overcharged. Please do not allow children to play with it. 
When you restart the robot, first press the Auto button to wake it up, then proceed with other operations.
