# 未学习完成GOAT停在原地

## Question

未学习完成GOAT停在原地

## Answer

(1) The GOAT will automatically try to return to the docking station when it detects a weak location signal in the event of heavy rain or weak lighting. If the return attempt is unsuccessful, it will stop.
(2) Check the error message on the App. The GOAT may have encountered some errors and require assistance.
