# Why are W1 PRO's safety tether and power cord attached to the robot?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why are W1 PRO's safety tether and power cord attached to the robot?

## 答案 / Answer

W00087

Product feature

The safety tether is attached to the robot for safety purposes, ensuring that the robot will not directly fall off when it fails to hold due to uncontrollable factors, thus minimizing the damage.
