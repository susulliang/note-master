Disassembly, clean and installation of mopping pad plate and mopping pad

Disassemble and clean the mopping pad plates
 1. Please turn the Robot over.
 2. Remove the mopping pad plates by lifting it upward with a little force and tear the washable mopping pads from the mopping pad plates.
 3. Washable mopping pads can be cleaned and used after air drying.
Mopping pads&mopping pad plates installation
 1. Please flip the Deebot and place it upside down.
 2.  Align the mopping pads with the mopping pad plates, stick it flat, then install the mopping pad plates back to the Deebot and make sure the mopping pad plates are in place.
