# GOAT 使用哪种类型的电池？电池能用多久？ / What type of battery does the GOAT use? How long does the battery last?

>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 使用哪种类型的电池？电池能用多久？ / What type of battery does the GOAT use? How long does the battery last?

## 答案 / Answer

7- 主机其他

GOAT 使用锂电池。这款电池的寿命超过1000次。在1000次完全充电循环下，它仍然可以保留70%以上的原始容量。

The GOAT uses a lithium battery. This battery has a lifespan of more than 1000 cycles. Under 1000 full charge cycles, it can still retain more than 70% of its original capacity.
