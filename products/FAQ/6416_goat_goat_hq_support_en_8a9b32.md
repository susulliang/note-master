# Do I need to move the Navigation Beacons indoors in winter?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Do I need to move the Navigation Beacons indoors in winter?

## 答案 / Answer

G00131

Enable

Maintenance

We recommend that you move the signal transmitting part of the Navigation Beacons indoors for better storage in winter when the GOAT is not in use. The supporting rods can be left outdoors. However, please be sure to match each signal transmitting part with its corresponding supporting rod before using the robot again. Not doing so may lead to positioning errors.
