# The GOAT fails to return and recharge after map learning. Can I just move it back to the docking station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

The GOAT fails to return and recharge after map learning. Can I just move it back to the docking station?

## 答案 / Answer

G00070

Mapping

No. The map learning process is not complete until the GOAT docks itself into the docking station successfully. Please move the GOAT to a location on the map that is clear and away from boundaries and trigger the return again.
