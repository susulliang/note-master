# DEEBOT / T50降配版 / EN - How to deal with the issue of the station not supplying hot water?

## Question

How to deal with the issue of the station not supplying hot water?

## Answer

1.Please fill the clean water tank with water.
2.Turn on the hot water washing function in the APP.
3.Note:Clean water heating is a dynamic process and the temperature will be slightly lower at first.
