# 如何清理清洁槽？ / How to clean the cleaning sink?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何清理清洁槽？ / How to clean the cleaning sink?

## 答案 / Answer

1.取下清洁槽内清洁拖布盘
2.可用清水冲洗清洁拖布盘。
3.清洁完成后重新安装回基站清洁槽内。
注：注意溢水浮球安装完整

1.Remove the mop washing tray.
2.Clean it by rinsing with clean water.
3.After cleaning, reinstall it back into the station.
Note: the overflow float must be installed properly.
