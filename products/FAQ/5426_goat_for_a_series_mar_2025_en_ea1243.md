# 基站指示灯（蓝灯）不亮 / The station indicator light stays off

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

基站指示灯（蓝灯）不亮 / The station indicator light stays off

## 答案 / Answer

Charging Station

（1）检查是否正确连接了电源
（2）电源电压是否在100-240V之间
若以上可能原因均已被排除，但问题仍未解决，请联系科沃斯售后

(1)Check whether the power supply is connected correctly
(2) Check whether the power supply voltage is between 100-240V
If the problem persists, please contact Ecovacs Customer
