# 冬季需要将RTK拿回室内吗？

## Question

冬季需要将RTK拿回室内吗？

## Answer

We recommend that you move the RTK Reference station signal head indoors for better storage in winter when the GOAT is not in use. The mounting pole can be left outdoors. However, when using it again, please ensure that the position of the mounting pole remains the same as before, otherwise the positioning signal will be misaligned.
