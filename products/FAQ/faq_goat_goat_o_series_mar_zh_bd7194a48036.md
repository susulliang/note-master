# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 我可以将RTK基站安装在屋顶或者其他高处吗？

## Question

我可以将RTK基站安装在屋顶或者其他高处吗？

## Answer

(1) The RTK station needs to be placed in an open and unobstructed area. Houses and trees will obstruct the RTK signal, and it is necessary to ensure that there is no obstruction around the RTK station as much as possible.
(2)The RTK reference station should be upright and not tilted.
(3)  If the RTK signal indicator steady light, it means that the position signal is stable (please wait 1min after powering on to see the light effect).                      （4）The installation accessories like wall mount can be purchased from the official website.
The high places can be installed if the above conditions are met.
* If you will install the RTK station at a high place, please pay attention to the safety of operation
