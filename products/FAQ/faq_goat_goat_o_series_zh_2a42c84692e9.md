# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 我的花园中有路能否使用自动建图功能

## Question

我的花园中有路能否使用自动建图功能

## Answer

It is recommended to follow the GOAT and switch to manual mapping if needed, in case the garden paths being identified as the map boundaries during automatic mapping.
