# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Is it possible to remotely control the GOAT in live video mode?

## Question

Is it possible to remotely control the GOAT in live video mode?

## Answer

No. According to safety regulation requirements, remote controlled lawn mowers can only be connected via Bluetooth but not Wi-Fi. Therefore, you can only try to connect the GOAT to the remote control when it is within 20 m.
