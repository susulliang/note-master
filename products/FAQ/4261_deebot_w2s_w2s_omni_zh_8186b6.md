## 答案 / Answer

Side brush cleaning

You can try the following steps:
1.Make sure the WINBOT fan is turned off.
2.Place the WINBOT on the wiping pad, ensuring both side brushes are fully in contact with the pad.
3.Press both bumpers near the side brushes at the same time. When you hear the voice prompt “Start cleaning side brushes,” release your hands. The side brushes will begin rotating for cleaning.
4.When you hear the voice prompt “Side brush cleaning completed,” the process is finished.
