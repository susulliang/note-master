# GOAT 春季重新启用，有哪些注意事项？

## Question

GOAT 春季重新启用，有哪些注意事项？

## Answer

1. Reassemble the charging station with the RTK pole and install it at last year's original location. 2. Inspect wires for damage before connecting. 3. Check blades for rust or damage; replace if necessary. 4. Gently wipe the top camera and front AI fisheye camera. 5. After powering on, verify that the app and machine are functioning normally. If abnormalities occur, recreate the map.
 Note: Wear gloves during operation to prevent cuts from the blades.
