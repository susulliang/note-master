# How to deal with the station not producing hot water?

## Question

How to deal with the station not producing hot water?

## Answer

Do not use hot water when cleaning the cloth
Note: Each time the cleaning task is started, wet the cloth with cold water, and then clean the cloth with hot water.
1. Confirm that the machine is connected to the network.
2. Open the APP > Enter the robot control > Base station function > Confirm that the hot water cleaning has been turned on. If there is no hot water when cleaning the mop when it is turned off.
3. Check the remaining water in the clean water tank. If the water level is too low, please add clean water.
Note:
① The hot water cleaning switch is turned on by default
If you are using the self-cleaning function of the station and the water is cold, you cannot use the hot water cleaning function.
③ Water heating is a dynamic process, and the temperature will be slightly lower before the hot water washing process
④ When the water temperature is too high, this function will be temporarily turned off to avoid damage caused by high temperature
