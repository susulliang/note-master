# GOAT能对蹦床底部的区域进行除草吗？ / Can the GOAT mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT能对蹦床底部的区域进行除草吗？ / Can the GOAT mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

GOAT无法越过高于4cm的障碍物，蹦床底部的横杠可能导致GOAT卡困，建议将蹦床区域以及类似的娱乐设施区域圈为禁区。但您仍可以启用手动除草模式遥控GOAT进入此类区域进行修整

The GOAT is unable to cross obstacles more than 4 cm in height. The horizontal bar at the bottom of the trampoline may make the GOAT become stuck. It is recommended to delimit the trampoline area and similar entertainment facilities as restricted areas. However, you may still initiate manual mowing mode remotely to allow the GOAT to enter such areas for trimming.
