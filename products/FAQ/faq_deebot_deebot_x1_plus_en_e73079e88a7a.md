# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 PLUS / EN - Is there anything to be aware of when using Follow My Voice cleaning command?

## Question

Is there anything to be aware of when using Follow My Voice cleaning command?

## Answer

When you need DEEBOT to come to you for cleaning, make sure that there are no obstructions between you and DEEBOT. You can use the command "OK YIKO, Follow My Voice" to call DEEBOT to clean. DEEBOT can find you via the voice source and AI recognition and go to your location for cleaning (the cleaning range is 1.5 m*1.5 m around you).
