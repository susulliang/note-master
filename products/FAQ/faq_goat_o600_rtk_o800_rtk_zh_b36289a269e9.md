# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 何时标注“齐平边界”（Flush Boundary）

## Question

何时标注“齐平边界”（Flush Boundary）

## Answer

When the mow area boundary is flush with hard surfaces (such as tiles, asphalt, concrete, wooden decks, etc.) and there is no significant height difference, it should be labeled as a “Flush Boundary.” Note:
1. The length of a flush boundary must be at least 2 meters.
2. If the boundary features steps, curbs, or significant height differences, it is recommended not to mark it.
3. If the flush boundary is adjacent to flower beds, walls, or complex structures, it is recommended not to mark it.
