# Why does YIKO respond in English?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why does YIKO respond in English?

## 答案 / Answer

D20308

APP

(1) Currently, YIKO only supports seven languages: CN_TW/EN/DE/FR/IT/KR/JP. Please select a language supported by YIKO in the App. 
(2) If you select a language not supported by YIKO, the commands used will be issued in English.
