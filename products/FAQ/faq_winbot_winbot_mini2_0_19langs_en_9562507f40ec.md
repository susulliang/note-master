# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 .0 19langs / EN - Alert: Insufficient air pressure How to deal with it?

## Question

Alert: Insufficient air pressure
 How to deal with it?

## Answer

Kind Reminder:
 If air pressure is low during use, check the window first. Gaps or low obstacles on the glass can cause pressure loss. For such areas, use manual remote control via the App.
 If it is not an environmental issue, try these steps:
 1. Use the App to remote control the WINBOT to a reachable position.
 2. Grip the handle and hold the Start Button for 2 seconds. Remove the WINBOT once the fan stops.
 3. Ensure the wiping pad is aligned with the wide and narrow edges and is attached securely.
 4. Confirm there are no abnormalities, then hold the Start button to restart the fan. The WINBOT will resume working once it adheres successfully.
