## 答案 / Answer

Robot Charging Guide.

Connect the power cord, keep the area around the Auto-empty station clear of objects ( within 0.05m left and right, 0.8m in front), especially objects with reflective surfaces. Long press the power button.                                                                                                                                   1.Place the Robot in front of the Auto-empty station,press the return button to make the Robot automatically return to the charging station, there will be a voice prompt "start charging".                                                                                                                        2.Please push the Robot into the charging station manually. Then shake it left and right, and when it is successfully docked, it will have a voice prompt 'start charging'.
