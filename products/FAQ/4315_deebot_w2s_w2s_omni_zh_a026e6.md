## 答案 / Answer

Fix WINBOT Compound Cable Twist

Tip: It's normal for the cable to twist slightly during retraction — this happens due to coiling tension.
Please simply pick up the WINBOT and gently rotate it in the opposite direction of the twist.
This will release the tension and allow retraction to continue smoothly.
