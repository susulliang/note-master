# Will the slopes in my garden affect the installation of navigation beacons?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Will the slopes in my garden affect the installation of navigation beacons?

## 答案 / Answer

G00032

Navigation Beacon

(1) When inserting a navigation beacon on a slope, please ensure that it is placed perpendicular to a flat surface. 
 (2) If there is an independent slope that exceeds the height of the navigation beacon and covers a large area, it is recommended to contact after-sales customer service for detailed solutions. It may be necessary to add more navigation beacons to ensure signal coverage at the back of the slope.
