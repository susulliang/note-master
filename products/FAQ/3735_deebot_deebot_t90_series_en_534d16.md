# YIKO语音助手使用指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

YIKO语音助手使用指导

## 答案 / Answer

一、如何使用YIKO功能：
 1.打开APP-点击进入设备-点击右上角YIKO按钮
 2.在YIKO对话框页面说出/输入发送相关问题
 二、YIKO智能体功能使用：
 1..打开APP-点击进入设备--点击开启智能体模式-点击“设置”
 3.在偏好设置在对话框说出/输入清洁偏好
 4.待AI智能体生成相对应方案-点击“采纳”/“刷新”按钮重新生成清洁偏好
 5.点击“采纳”按钮后，偏好将在偏好设置对话框上端列表展示
 6.偏好设置删除：点击偏好设置按钮-点击“√”取消勾选-点击保存即可
 TIPS:YIKO偏好设置仅在机器处于智能体模式工作时生效

I. Using YIKO Features:
 1. Open the app → Tap "Enter" → Tap the YIKO button in the top-right corner
 2. Speak or type your question in the YIKO dialog box
 II. Using YIKO Agent Features:
 1. Open the app → Tap "Devices" → Tap "Enable Agent Mode" → Tap "Settings"
 3. Speak or type your cleaning preferences in the dialog box under "Preferences"
 4. Wait for the AI Agent to generate a corresponding plan - Tap "Accept" / "Refresh" to regenerate cleaning preferences
 5. After tapping "Accept," preferences will display in the top list of the preference settings dialog
 6. To delete preferences: Tap the preference settings button - Tap the "√" to uncheck - Tap Save
 TIPS: YIKO preferences only take effect when the machine operates in Agent Mode
