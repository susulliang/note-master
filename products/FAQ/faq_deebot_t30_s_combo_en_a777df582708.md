# How to open the automatic dust bin/replace the dust bag?

## Question

How to open the automatic dust bin/replace the dust bag?

## Answer

1. Press the center of the dust bin cover to open it.
2. Remove the dust bag and discard it.
3. Wipe the dust bin with a dry cloth and install a new dust bag.
4. Close the dust bin cover.
