# How to maintain my WINBOT W2?

## Question

How to maintain my WINBOT W2?

## Answer

WINBOT W2 Maintenance Tips

1. Water Tank
After the WINBOT W2 has completed its cleaning, please empty the water tank (if the cleaning solution was used, it's suggested to fill a little water to rinse the water tank).
2. Wiping Pad
① When replacing the wiping pad, please attach it correctly with the width of the pad corresponding to the width of the WINBOT W2's bottom.
② Use a clean wiping pad.
③ Please make sure that the wiping pad is cleaned and dried before storage.
3. Driving Threads
Before cleaning the driving threads, please make sure that the WINBOT W2 is connected to the power source and turned on, then use the App control to rotate the driving threads  forward/backward, and clean them.
4. Bottom Components
A dry cloth can be used to wipe the bottom components of the WINBOT W2, including the fan, sensors, side rollers, etc., to keep them clean and free of foreign objects.
