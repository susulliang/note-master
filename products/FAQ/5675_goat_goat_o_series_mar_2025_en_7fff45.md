# 地图学习完成后回充失败，可以直接将GOAT搬回基站吗？ / The GOAT fails to return and recharge after map learning. Can I just move it back to the docking station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图学习完成后回充失败，可以直接将GOAT搬回基站吗？ / The GOAT fails to return and recharge after map learning. Can I just move it back to the docking station?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

可以。但必须重新启动地图学习，地图学习环节中必需让GOAT自行与基站对接成功才能结束此环节。
1. 可以遥控GOAT至基站正前方0.5m处，再次点击尝试自动回充
2. 若仍旧回充失败，请将GOAT手动对接到充电站内，并重新启动地图学习。

Yes，but the map learning process must be restarted after moving the GOAT. The map learning process is not complete until the GOAT docks itself into the charging station successfully. 
1. Please remote control the GOAT to 0.5m in front of the charging station and make the GOAT recharging automaticlly. 
2. You can move the GOAT to the charging station manually if the recharging failed, and restart map learning again.
