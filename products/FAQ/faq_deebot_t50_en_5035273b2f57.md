# DEEBOT / T50降配版 / EN - How to deal with abnormal situations(skew、overlapping) in the map?

## Question

How to deal with abnormal situations(skew、overlapping) in the map?

## Answer

1.You can use the "Restore Map" function in the APP to restore the initially saved map and continue to use it;
2.If the initially saved map does not meet your needs, you can delete the map and rebuild it.
