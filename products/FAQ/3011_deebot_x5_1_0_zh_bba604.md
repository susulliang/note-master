# How do I check the driving wheels if they are stuck? / 1. Check whether the robot's anti-collision protection strips have been removed. If not, please remove them first. 
2. Power off the robot, flip over and gently rest it upside down.
3. Press and rotate the driving wheels to remove any entangled debris.
