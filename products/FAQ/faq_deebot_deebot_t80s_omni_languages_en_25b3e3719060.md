# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T80S OMNI . languages / EN - How to clean the ROBOT's dirty water box?

## Question

How to clean the ROBOT's dirty water box?

## Answer

1.Press the blue release button at the rear of the ROBOT to remove the dirty water box
2. Lift the dirty water box lid upward from the blue latch position, then pour out the dirty water inside.
3. Remove the Filter Net and thoroughly clean both the Filter Net and the dirty water box (use cleaning tools if needed).
4. Reinstall the Filter Net and ensure the dirty water box lid is securely closed.
5. Reinsert the dirty water box into the rear of the ROBOT. A "click" sound indicates proper installation.
