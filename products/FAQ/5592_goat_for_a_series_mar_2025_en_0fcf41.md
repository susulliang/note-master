# 为什么GOAT在充满电后的工作时间会缩短？ / Why does the working time of the GOAT shorten after it has fully charged?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么GOAT在充满电后的工作时间会缩短？ / Why does the working time of the GOAT shorten after it has fully charged?

## 答案 / Answer

（1）草坪较密，草况复杂
（2）使用了高效模式
（3）电池老化

(1) The lawn is dense and the growing conditions of the grass are complex. 
(2) Efficient mode is in use. 
(3) Battery aging.
