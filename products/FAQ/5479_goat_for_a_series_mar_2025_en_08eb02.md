# 建图为何创建失败了？ / Why did I fail to create the map？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图为何创建失败了？ / Why did I fail to create the map？

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

（1）确认建图过程中是否搬动或拖移了机器，搬动机器将会导致建图失败。建议您修整花园中的坑洼区域，减少遥控过程中GOAT被卡困导致的需要搬动或拖移的概率
（2）确认草坪区域是否过于开阔（机器左右60m内无参照物），过于开阔LiDAR没有参照点，无法生成地图。
（3）确认蓝牙中断未超过0.5个小时
（4）确认已按照APP指示在外轮廓遥控完成后正常闭合地图，遥控结束位置距离起点过远，会导致地图无法闭合。

(1) Confirm if the GOAT has been moved or dragged during mapping, as moving the GOAT can cause mapping to fail. It is recommended that you level the garden ground to reduce the chance of moving or dragging the GOAT due to it becoming trapped when being remotely controlled 
(2)Confirm if the lawn area is too open (there are no reference points within 60m of Goat), as there are no reference points for LiDAR and the map cannot be generated.
(3) Confirm that the Bluetooth connection has not failed for more than half an hour 
(4) Confirm that the GOAT is automatically connected to the docking station after the mapping is completed according to the instructions in the app. If you manually control the GOAT to the docking station, or push the GOAT to the docking station, the map cannot be closed properly.
