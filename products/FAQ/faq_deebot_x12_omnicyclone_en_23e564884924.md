# How to Clean the Water Tray?

## Question

How to Clean the Water Tray?

## Answer

1.Remove the robot's top cover, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, and slightly lift the side where the roller mop is installed.
2.Release the blue buckle on the water tank, remove the water tank, then press and rotate the drain button to clean the entire Water Tray (a cleaning tool can be used).
3.Flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up; then, clean the Water Tray on the robot (a cleaning tool can be used).
4.Reinstall the Water Tray onto the bottom of the robot. You should hear a "click" sound, which means it's securely in place.
