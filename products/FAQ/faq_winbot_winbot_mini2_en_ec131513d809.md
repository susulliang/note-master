# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Why are there water streaks after cleaning?

## Question

Why are there water streaks after cleaning?

## Answer

1. The wiping pad may be too wet; ensure it is thoroughly wrung out before use.
2. If the cleaning frequency is low (e.g., once a quarter/half-year), dust buildup is heavy. Wash the pad and corner brushes, and perform multiple cleaning passes for best results.
