# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 机器人有时不能避障？

## Question

机器人有时不能避障？

## Answer

1. Please check whether the obstacle avoidance sensor (located at the center of the front of the robot) is covered with dust. If so, gently wipe it with a dry paper towel.
2. Make sure that the ‘AIVI 3D’ switch in the DEEBOT settings is turned on.
Note: The robot may slow down and lightly touch black and thread-like objects.
