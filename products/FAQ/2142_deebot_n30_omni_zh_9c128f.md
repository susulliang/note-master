How to schedule a cleaning task?

1. Click settings＞Robot advanced settings＞Scheduled Clean, to schedule Auto Clean/Scenario Clean/room clean task.
 2. Click the "+" button at the top right to add a new schedule.
 3.You also can set the cleaning repetition.
