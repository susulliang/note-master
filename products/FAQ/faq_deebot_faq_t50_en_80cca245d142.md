# DEEBOT / FAQ T50 / EN - Disassembly, clean and installation guide of Dust bin

## Question

Disassembly, clean and installation guide of Dust bin

## Answer

Warm Reminder：
When your robot has been used for a period of time, if you find that the dust box is full or the vacuuming effect is not satisfactory, etc., you can follow the following steps to try:
1.Remove the top cover, turn off the robot, press the catch to remove the dust bin;
2.Open the dust bin and empty it;
3.Remove the filter and filter cotton(Prompt：If the dust box is dirty, use water to rinse the dust bin, filter and filter cotton.)
4.After cleaning, reinstall the filter assembly.
5.Put the dust bin into the Robot, When you hear a "click", it indicates that it is properly installed.
Note: If cleaned with water, please shake off the water droplets and need to be placed in a well-ventilated area to dry before use.
