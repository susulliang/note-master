# Do the AI camera and panoramic camera violate the user's privacy?

## Question

Do the AI camera and panoramic camera violate the user's privacy?

## Answer

The GOAT G1 has two cameras, which are mainly used to identify and detect obstacles and lawn boundaries during mowing. If you turn on human detection or garden keeper for spot patrols, the cameras will take photos of anyone within 7 meters of the GOAT and give alarms. You can rest assured that your privacy will not be violated. We always put the privacy of customers first. We also recommend that you turn off human detection in public areas to avoid violating the privacy of others.
