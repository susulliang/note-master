# Disassembly and Cleaning of the Mop Washing Tray

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Disassembly and Cleaning of the Mop Washing Tray

## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
When your robot has been used for a period, if you find some dirt in the tray that cannot be cleaned thoroughly, you can try the following steps:
1.Remove the mop washing tray.
2.Clean it by rinsing with clean water.
3.After cleaning, reinstall it back into the station.
Note: Pay attention to the complete installation of the overflow float.
