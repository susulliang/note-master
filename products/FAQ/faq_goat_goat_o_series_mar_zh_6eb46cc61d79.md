# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 环境中有哪些因素会导致无法使用自动建图

## Question

环境中有哪些因素会导致无法使用自动建图

## Answer

(1) There is no border on the lawn (such as a lawn that borders a neighbor and has no partition)
(2) Two lawns are separated by a road with a width greater than 15 cm (in this scenario, only a map of one lawn can be created)
(3) There are many obstacles on the edge of the lawn
