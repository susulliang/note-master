# What should I do if DEEBOT has lost the map after being powered off?

## Question

What should I do if DEEBOT has lost the map after being powered off?

## Answer

DEEBOT is equipped with a Restore Map function. Set DEEBOT on the charging dock, then follow the prompts in the App to restore the map.
