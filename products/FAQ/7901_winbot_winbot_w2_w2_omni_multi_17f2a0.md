# Can I use WINBOT W2 outdoors in a low temperature?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I use WINBOT W2 outdoors in a low temperature?

## 答案 / Answer

W00128+

It's recommended to use the WINBOT W2 within the temperature range of 0℃ and 40℃. Otherwise, it may cause damage to the robot or affect the robot's life.
