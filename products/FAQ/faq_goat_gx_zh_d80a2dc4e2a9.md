# Can the GOAT mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

## Question

Can the GOAT mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

## Answer

The GOAT is unable to cross obstacles more than 3 cm in height. The horizontal bar at the bottom of the trampoline may make the GOAT become stuck.It is recommended to set up physical enclosures (height greater than 15cm) for trampoline areas and similar entertainment facilities.However, you may still initiate manual mowing mode remotely to allow the GOAT to enter such areas for trimming.
