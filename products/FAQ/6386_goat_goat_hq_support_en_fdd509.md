# Can the GOAT G1 recognize humans or small animals at night when AI recognition is enabled?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT G1 recognize humans or small animals at night when AI recognition is enabled?

## 答案 / Answer

G00101

Disable

Intelligence & Privacy

No. The GOAT G1 is not equipped with night vision and is unable to carry out AI recognition after dark.
