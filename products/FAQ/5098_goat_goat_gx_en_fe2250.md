# Can the GOAT only identify and warn people that are present in the garden?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT only identify and warn people that are present in the garden?

## 答案 / Answer

G00123

Intelligence & Privacy

The GOAT is currently unable to distinguish whether people are in the garden or not. After turning on the relevant functions for human detection, it will sound an alarm to anyone within 7 m of it. To avoid alarming people outside the garden, try to keep a certain distance from the boundary of the garden when setting the cruise points.
