# Should I map along the outline of the lawn or the garden?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Should I map along the outline of the lawn or the garden?

## 答案 / Answer

G00042

Mapping

If there is no height difference between the lawn area and other areas in your garden, you can create a map along the edge of the garden so that the GOAT is able to operate in fixed spots in non-lawn areas. If the height difference between your lawn and other areas is more than 3 cm, it is recommended that you only map around the outline of the lawn. The GOAT is not able to cross over steps more than 3 cm in height.
