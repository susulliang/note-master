# GOAT可以在雨中割草吗？ / Can the GOAT be used in the rain?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT可以在雨中割草吗？ / Can the GOAT be used in the rain?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

GOAT的防水等级为IPX6，无惧雨淋。但是雨中割草刀盘容易被湿草堵塞且机身摄像头上的雨水会一定程度的干扰GOAT进行视觉定位以及避障。因此建议您打开雨淋传感器，GOAT会在草坪干燥后再进行工作

The GOATis IPX6-rated and able to withstand rain. However, it is easy for the blades to become blocked by wet grass, and rain on the camera will also interfere with the GOAT’s visual localization  and obstacle avoidance. It is therefore recommended to turn on the rain sensor, so the GOAT will begin mowing after the lawn has dried.
