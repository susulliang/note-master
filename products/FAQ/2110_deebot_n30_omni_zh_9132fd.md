How to correctly install the robot?

1.Please take out the Robot, station, base and remove protective strip.
2.Turn the Robot over.
3.Take out the side brush, align it with the slot, press it down. A "click" sound indicates it has been installed properly.                                                                                                                4.Connect base with the station, the sound of "Click" indicates proper installation.                                                                                                                                         5.Take out the power cord, plug in the station, then find a suitable position to place it against the wall on a level,hard surface.
