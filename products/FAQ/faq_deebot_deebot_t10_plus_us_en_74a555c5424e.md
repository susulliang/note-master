# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T10 PLUS（US） / EN - Does the camera on the AIVI 3.0 module violate the user's privacy?

## Question

Does the camera on the AIVI 3.0 module violate the user's privacy?

## Answer

The AIVI 3.0 module has one camera, mainly used to identify and detect obstacles during cleaning for better cleaning. Your privacy will not be violated and we always put the privacy of customers first.
