## 答案 / Answer

After quickly creating a map, why did the zoning change during a full house cleaning?

After quickly mapping, perform a full house cleaning again. There may be slight differences in map information, which may cause changes in certain map areas. It is recommended that users use manual zoning or set area types for map editing.
