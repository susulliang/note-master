# 我将GOAT拿出了地图外，为何没有触发超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我将GOAT拿出了地图外，为何没有触发超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

（1）请确认是否在“设置”-“机器人设置”-“安全设置”中打开了“超出地图范围报警”
（2）当前超出地图范围设置为200米，当GOAT远离地图＞200m后将会自动触发报警

(1) Please confirm whether installed 4G Cellular Module，and 4G Cellular Module is turned on in Settings ＞ Robot Settings ＞Net Settings-“4G Cellular Module”. 
(2) Please confirm whether Distance Alarm is turned on in Settings ＞ Robot Settings ＞Net Settings-“4G Cellular Module”. 
(3) The current setting for exceeding the map range is 200m. When GOAT is ＞200 m away from the map, an alarm will be automatically triggered
