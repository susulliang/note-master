# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 机器人在基站/充电座上报下视积灰，无法正常开始工作，怎么处理？When the robot is at the station and starts working, it alarms “Anti-drop sensors have accumulated dust”， what should I do?

## Question

机器人在基站/充电座上报下视积灰，无法正常开始工作，怎么处理？When the robot is at the station and starts working, it alarms “Anti-drop sensors have accumulated dust”， what should I do?

## Answer

1.请将机器人搬出充电座，长按主机上返回键，将机器人召回充电座;                                                                                                              2.确保听到“开始充电”后，重新启动工作；                                                                                                                                                    1. Please move the robot out, then press and hold the return button to make robot return back automatically;                                    2. After "Start charging" is heard，restart the work;
