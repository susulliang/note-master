# DEEBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - What Floor Types Are Compatible with the Station’s Suction Pad? Why Won’t It Stick?

## Question

What Floor Types Are Compatible with the Station’s Suction Pad? Why Won’t It Stick?

## Answer

1.The suction pad uses vacuum suction and only works on smooth, seamless surfaces.
2.Check if your floor has tile gaps, floorboard seams, or uneven areas like carpets.
If so, move the station to a flat, seamless surface and restart — the suction pad will engage automatically.
3.Do not place the station on tables or shelves.
This will prevent proper weight distribution and suction function.
