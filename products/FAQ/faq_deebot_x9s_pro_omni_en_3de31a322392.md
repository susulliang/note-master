# DEEBOT / X9S PRO OMNI / EN - Alarm: Dust accumulation on Anti-drop Sensors

## Question

Alarm: Dust accumulation on Anti-drop Sensors

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
 If you encounter prompts indicating dust accumulation or abnormalities in the Anti-drop Sensors during use, we recommendturning off the machine and attempting the following steps:                                                                                  1.Wipe the bottom Anti-drop Sensors to prevent dust obstruction.                                                                                        2.Observe after powering off and restarting.
