# 边刷的拆卸，清理及安装指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

边刷的拆卸，清理及安装指导

## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
 If, after using your robot for a period, you notice debris tangled in the Side Brush or receive a Side Brush entanglement alert, we recommend first powering off the machine. Then, try the following steps:                                                                              1.Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen the Side Brush screw counterclockwise, then remove the Side Brush. Clean the Side Brush bearings and any tangled debris using the cleaning tool, ensuring thorough removal.
 2.Align the Side Brush with the slot, press down firmly until you hear a "click," then tighten the screw using a Phillips screwdriver.
 Note:Use an M3 Phillips screwdriver (recommended). Gently loosen the screw on the Side Brush (no need to remove it completely) to detach the Side Brush.When reinstalling, ensure the screw holes align. Turn the screwdriver clockwise until the Side Brush is secure and cannot rotate. If the screw continues to spin freely, check that the screw holes are properly aligned. To remove the Side Brush, turn the screwdriver counterclockwise.
