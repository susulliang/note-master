# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - Introduction to Station Button Functions

## Question

Introduction to Station Button Functions

## Answer

The buttons on the top of the station include seven types: "Open Cleaning Compartment," "Power On/Off," "Retract Cable," "Up/Down" "Function Switch," and "Start/Pause/Confirm."
1.Open Cleaning Compartment
Press the "Open Cleaning Compartment" button to open the compartment door when you need to clean the wiping pad.
2.Retract Cable
Press the "Retract Cable" button to retract the cable after cleaning is completed.
3.Mode Selection
Press the "Up/Down" buttons to switch between modes, then press the "Confirm" button to activate the selected mode.
4.Function Switch
Press the "Function Switch" button to toggle between the winbot, station, and user guide displays.
5.Return to Original Position
Press and hold the "Start/Pause/Confirm" button to make the robot return to its starting point.
