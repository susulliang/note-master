## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
If you encounter a Anti-drop sensors dust accumulation or Anti-drop sensors abnormality warning during use, try the following steps:                                                                                                                                  1.Remove the ROBOT's dirty water box and flip the machine upside down. Wipe the bottom Anti-drop sensors to prevent dust obstruction.
2. Reinstall the ROBOT's dirty water box, power off and restart the machine, then observe.
Important note: Cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
