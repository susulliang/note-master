# What should I do if the cleaning solution has been used up?

## Question

What should I do if the cleaning solution has been used up?

## Answer

1. If the cleaning solution has been used up, you can use tap water as a temporary makeshift.
2. It's prohibited to use filtered, purified or pure water for this product. This is because the water level in the WINBOT W2 water tank is gauged via capacitance detection, which will fail if filtered, purified or pure water is used.
3. Please use the ECOVACS cleaning solution for WINBOT W2. If the cleaning solution has been used up, you can use tap water temporarily. However, the cleaning solution have a better cleaning effect than tap water.
