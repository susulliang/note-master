# 如何清理主机污水盒？ / How to Clean the dirty water box?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

如何清理主机污水盒？ / How to Clean the dirty water box?

## 答案 / Answer

1.按压主机尾部蓝色释放键取下污水盒
2.从蓝色卡扣位置向上掀开污水盒上盖，随后倾倒里面的污水。
3.取出过滤网，将过滤网与污水盒清理干净（可使用清理工具辅助清理干净）
4.请重新安装过滤网，并确保污水盒上盖被正确且牢固地重新合上。
5.请重新将污水盒装回主机尾部。听到“咔哒”声音表示安装到位。

1. Press the blue release button and remove the dirty water box.
 2. Open the top cover and dump the waste water inside.
 3. Remove the filter, clean the filter and the dirty water box (you can use the cleaning tool to help clean it).
 4. Reinstall the filter and close the cover.
 5. Put the dirty water box back into the Robot, the "click" sound indicates that the installation is in place.
