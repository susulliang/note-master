## 答案 / Answer

What should be done when the robot voice alarm says 'Station cleaning sink abnormality'?"

1.Check if the detachable tray is properly installed.
2.Verify that the water level sensing floater is correctly installed.                                                                  3.Power off the station, reconnect the power, try again then observe.
