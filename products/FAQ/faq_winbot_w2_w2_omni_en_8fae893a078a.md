# After cleaning, why is there a trace of wiping pad left on the surface where the WINBOT W2 was adsorbed?

## Question

After cleaning, why is there a trace of wiping pad left on the surface where the WINBOT W2 was adsorbed?

## Answer

During cleaning, the wiping pad used to clean the surface will become more and more dirty, and when the WINBOT W2 is removed after use, it may leave traces in its adsorption position.

1.Please replace or wash the wiping pad in a timely manner to ensure a better cleaning performance.
2.When cleaning a vacuum double-layered glass, if the glass has air leakage, please remove the WINBOT W2 immediately after cleaning.
