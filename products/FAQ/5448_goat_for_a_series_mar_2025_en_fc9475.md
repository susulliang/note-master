# RTK基站插歪了影响使用吗？ / Does the RTK Reference Station installed skew affect use?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

RTK基站插歪了影响使用吗？ / Does the RTK Reference Station installed skew affect use?

## 答案 / Answer

RTK Reference Station

RTK基站的倾斜角度不可大于5°，可用手机上的水平仪进行检测。若倾斜角度大于5°将会造成定位偏差。建议定期检查RTK基站是否有倾倒的趋势，可进行扶正。
请务必保证RTK基站安装稳定，避免大风天气或其他原因带来晃动等。

The tilt Angle of the RTK Reference Station should not be greater than 5°, which can be detected by the gradienter APP on the mobile phone. If the tilt Angle is greater than 5°, the positioning errors will be caused. It is recommended to check the condition of RTK regulary in case the positioning affected on account of leaning.
Please make sure that the RTK Reference Station is installed stably to avoid shaking due to strong winds or other situations.
