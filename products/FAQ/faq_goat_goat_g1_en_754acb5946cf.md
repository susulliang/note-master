# I have replaced the Navigation Beacon battery according to the App prompt. Why does the App still display low Navigation Beacon battery or no signal?

## Question

I have replaced the Navigation Beacon battery according to the App prompt. Why does the App still display low Navigation Beacon battery or no signal?

## Answer

The GOAT cannot detect the status of the Navigation Beacon in real time and can only get the status when the GOAT is working close to a Navigation Beacon. If you have confirmed that the battery has been replaced correctly, you can start one task and the Navigation Beacon status in the App will be updated automatically.
