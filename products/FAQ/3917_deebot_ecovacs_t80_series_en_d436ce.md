# Handling Missed Cleaning Areas

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Handling Missed Cleaning Areas

## 答案 / Answer

1.Check for rugs/mats blocking doorways. and check in the app-Map Management-Edit Map-Carpet Settings-Bypass.
2.The robot can climb up to 2cm. If thresholds/furniture exceed 2cm in height/thickness, the area will be skipped.
3.To prevent collisions with door frames or furniture, the robot may intentionally avoid tight spaces, causing minor missed spots.
Friendly Reminder: In cluttered environments, briefly declutter problem areas and observe if cleaning coverage improves.
