# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - GOAT能在雨中割草吗？

## Question

GOAT能在雨中割草吗？

## Answer

GOAT has an IPX6 waterproof rating, protecting it from rain. However, when mowing in rain, the cutting deck may become clogged by wet grass, and water on the camera may interfere with visual positioning. We recommend enabling the rain sensor feature. GOAT will resume operation immediately or after a delay based on your settings once the rain stops.
