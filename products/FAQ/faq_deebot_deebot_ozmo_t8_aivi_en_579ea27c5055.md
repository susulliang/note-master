# DEEBOT / DEEBOT OZMO T8 AIVI / EN - What can I do if the robot quickly runs out of battery and returns to the charging dock?

## Question

What can I do if the robot quickly runs out of battery and returns to the charging dock?

## Answer

(1) We recommend fully charging the robot before using it for the first time to ensure it is fully charged (check the battery level in the App).
(2) For daily use, we recommend leaving the robot on the charging dock.
(3) If the robot will not be used for an extended period of time, we recommend switching it off. The robot's off switch can be found under the unit's cover.
(4) If you live in a larger home, we recommend turning on the Continuous Clean function in the App.
