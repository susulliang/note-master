# 32

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

32

## 答案 / Answer

1.Slightly lift the side where the roller mop is installed, gently release the blue buckle on the water tank, and remove the water tray.
2.Take off the top cover of the robot, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, then flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
3.Press the roller release button on the side of the roller mop, gently lift it up, and pull out the roller mop.
4.Use the cleaning tool to clean the roller mop compartment. After confirming it is clean, turn the bearing inside the compartment to ensure it rotates smoothly.
5.First, fit the roller mop onto the roller motor, then gently push it forward until it is fully in place.
6.After the mop is securely attached, align the roller release button with the groove on the side of the compartment and press it down. You should hear a "click" sound, which means the mop is installed correctly.
7.Restart the robot and observe. If it still shows the "roller mop tangled" alert, please contact Ecovacs after-sales support for assistance.
