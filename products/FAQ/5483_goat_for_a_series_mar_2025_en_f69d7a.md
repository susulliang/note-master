# 禁区-我可以在任意位置创建禁区吗？ / Can I create a No-Entry zone anywhere?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

禁区-我可以在任意位置创建禁区吗？ / Can I create a No-Entry zone anywhere?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

基站周围2m为GOAT回充默认通行区域，建议尽量不要在基站附近建立禁区，在基站前方设置大面积的禁区可能会导致GOAT无法回充。此外若禁区轮廓之间或其与外轮廓之间的间距小于0.8m将会形成狭窄通道，可能导致该区域漏割

The area 2 m around the docking station is the default access area for the GOAT to return to charge. It is recommended that No-Entry zone be set near the docking station. Setting a large No-Entry zone in front of the docking station may cause GOAT to be unable to recharge. In addition, In addition, if the spacing between No-Entry zone is less than 0.8 meters, it will form a narrow passage, which may cause the area to be missed.
