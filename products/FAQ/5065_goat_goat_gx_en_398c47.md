# Why is the GOAT missing some areas when mowing?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the GOAT missing some areas when mowing?

## 答案 / Answer

G00090

Mowing

(1) Please check whether there are temporary obstacles blocking the route of the GOAT. Areas smaller than 1 m in diameter blocked by temporary obstacles will not be mowed by the GOAT. 
 (2) Please check whether AI recognition or Unattended mode is turned on in the app. These may occasionally result in false obstacle avoidances. 
 (3) Please check whether the obstacle avoidance height you set in the App is too low. Lower obstacle avoidance heights may result in missed areas. 
 (4) Please check whether there are passages narrower than 2 m on the map (including narrow areas surrounded by map boundaries and restricted areas or between restricted areas). Currently, the GOAT will mow passages narrower than 2 m only once. 
 (5) The GOAT can clear obstacles with a height of up to 3 cm (based on laboratory data). Steps or roads with a greater height are inaccessible to the GOAT. 
 (6) The GOAT is unable to mow grass higher than 12 cm. Higher grass may be recognized as an obstacle, so please make sure that grass has been trimmed to lower than 10 cm. 
 (7) The GOAT is unable to climb slopes with an inclination of greater than 25°. We recommend that you set these slopes as restricted areas. 
 If an area is missed due to temporary obstacles, it will be mowed next time after the obstacle has been removed. You can also use the App to manually control the GOAT and mow the missed areas
