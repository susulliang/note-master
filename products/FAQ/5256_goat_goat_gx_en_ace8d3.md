# Can the GOAT give alarms when it is in the docking station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT give alarms when it is in the docking station?

## 答案 / Answer

G00122

Intelligence & Privacy

You can go to Settings and turn on garden keeper. In this mode, as long as the GOAT is in the docking station, it will give alarms if anyone is detected within 7 meters.
