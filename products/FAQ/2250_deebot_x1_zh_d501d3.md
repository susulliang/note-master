# Shorter battery life

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Shorter battery life

## 答案 / Answer

Shorter battery life

1. Ask the users about the recharging state of DEEBOT, whether the red light is always on to recharging or other indicators to recharging.
2. If the white light is always on or blue light is always on to recharging, it means that the robot has completed the task before recharging. It is necessary to explain it to the user, and observe the running route in the app to observe whether the cleaning is completed.
3. If the red light is always on to recharging, please instruct the user to charge it fully, and observe that the power in the app is 100%.
4. Check the suction in the app, please instruct the user to set the standard suction in the app, and click cleaning again to observe.
