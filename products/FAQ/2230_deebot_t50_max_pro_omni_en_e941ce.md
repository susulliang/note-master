# 如何正确使用视频管家功能？ / How to use the Video Manager function correctly?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何正确使用视频管家功能？ / How to use the Video Manager function correctly?

## 答案 / Answer

使用指导：
打开APP-进入设备-左上角“视频图标”进入视频管家进行相关操作
1.点击相机-拍照-可实时抓拍。
2.点击相机-录像-可录制无声视频（最长15S）
3.点击相机-图库-进行相关照片/视频管理设置
4.点击语音通话-可进行单项视频实时通话
5.点击全屋巡航-机器会前往您设置好的每个巡航点东西南北自动各拍一张照片，完成自动返回基站。
6.点击精准达到-可以在地图上标注需机器前往的地点，点击前往机器即可自动行走到指定位置。

I. Video Manager Functions
Notes on the use of video manager:
1. Video Manager Functions can not be used at the same time, but can be freely switched between the two.
2. The video manager supports photo taking, video recording, two-way voice, whole-house patrol, precise arrival, and album management.
Usage guide:
Open the APP - enter the device - “video icon” in the upper left corner to enter the video manager to carry out related operations.
1. Click the camera to take a picture - you can take a picture in real time.
2. Click camera-video-record silent video (up to 15S).
3. Click Camera-Gallery to manage photo/video settings.
4. Click Voice Call - can make a single video real-time call
5. Click whole-house patrol- the machine will go to each cruise point you set up, automatically take a photo in each of the four directions of east, west, south, north and south, and return to the base station automatically when finished.
6. Click Precise Reach - you can mark the location on the map where you need the machine to go, click to go and the machine will automatically walk to the specified location.
