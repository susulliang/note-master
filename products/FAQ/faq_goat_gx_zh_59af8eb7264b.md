# If I use a SIM card, how much data will I consume in a month?

## Question

If I use a SIM card, how much data will I consume in a month?

## Answer

If you just use WiFi data to watch live video and OTA, the data should be less than 150M a month.
(1) The data of 1 hour of real-time video with a SIM card is about 100M
(2) An OTA upgrade package is about 210M
In order to reduce data fee, it is recommended that you use WiFi to watch live video and perform OTA upgrades
