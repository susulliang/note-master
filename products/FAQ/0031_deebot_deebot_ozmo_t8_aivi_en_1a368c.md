# Why does DEEBOT become loud and stop cleaning well after cleaning for a while?

## 问题 / Question

Why does DEEBOT become loud and stop cleaning well after cleaning for a while?

## 答案 / Answer

D20027

Enable

Usage advice

(1) Remove and empty the dust bin. 
(2) Check and clean off the main brush (including any hair that may be tangled in the bearings at both ends of the main brush). 
(3) Check and clean the side brushes. 
(4) Clean or replace the filter.
