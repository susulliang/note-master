# Mop Washing Tray Maintenance Guide

## Question

Mop Washing Tray Maintenance Guide

## Answer

1. Remove the mop washing tray.
2. Wash the tray with water; then dry thoroughly to remove water stains.
3. Reinstall the tray back into the station.
Note: Ensure the overflow float (water level sensor) is properly secured.
