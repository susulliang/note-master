# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Charge Guidance

## Question

Charge Guidance

## Answer

1. Connect the power cord to the adapter and plug it in. A voice prompt will indicate a successful startup.
 2. Long press the Start Button to start the fan. Long press again to turn off the fan. Check the indicator status:
 ① A blinking blue light indicates that the device is currently charging. Use after a full charge is recommended.
 Note: After fully charged, the blue light will remain solid as the device enters standby mode. It will shut down automatically after approximately 10 minutes.
 ② A solid blue light indicates that it is ready for use.
