# After the Cellullar Module being installed to another GOAT, will the Auto-Connection service still be available?

## Question

After the Cellullar Module being installed to another GOAT, will the Auto-Connection service still be available?

## Answer

Auto-Connection service is bound to the module. If you purchase three years of service and the module is installed on another Goat after one year of use, it will still be available for two years.
