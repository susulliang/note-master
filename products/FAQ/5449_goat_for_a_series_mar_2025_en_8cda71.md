# 我的花园中有斜坡影响RTK基站的安装吗？ / Will having slopes in my yard affect installing the RTK base station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的花园中有斜坡影响RTK基站的安装吗？ / Will having slopes in my yard affect installing the RTK base station?

## 答案 / Answer

RTK Reference Station

（1）在斜坡上插RTK基站杆时需要将杆垂直于水平地面

When inserting the RTK base station pole on the slope, the pole needs to be perpendicular to the horizontal ground.
