## 答案 / Answer

Friendly reminder:
After prolonged use, if you notice clicking noises during operation, receive warnings about Main Brush entanglement, or experience poor dust suction, try the following steps:                                                            1.Remove the cover of the ROBOT. Turn the Power Switch to "O" to power off. Remove the ROBOT's waste water tank. Flip the machine over.
2. Simultaneously press and hold the two clips on the center of the Main Brush cover plate to remove it, then take out the Main Brush assembly;
3. Remove any tangled debris from the Main Brush;
4. Reinstall the Main Brush and manually rotate it to ensure smooth movement;
5. Align the Main Brush cover with the machine's slots and install it. A "click" sound indicates proper installation.
6. Reinstall the ROBOT's waste water tank.
Friendly reminder: Cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
