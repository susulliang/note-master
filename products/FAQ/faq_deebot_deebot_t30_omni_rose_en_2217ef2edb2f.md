# The cleaning route of the robot is messy, why and how to deal with it?

## Question

The cleaning route of the robot is messy, why and how to deal with it?

## Answer

1. Please check whether the TrueDetect 3D component is stained with dust. If there is dust, please wipe it gently with a dry paper towel;
2. Check whether there are many obstacles indoors. Because high-precision obstacle avoidance may make the route look messy, it is recommended to sort it out a little;
3. Check whether the driving wheel is dirty. If so, please wipe it clean with a dry rag;
Note: Do not add laundry detergent and other non-Ecovacs-specific cleaning solution into the clean water tank, as this may cause the machine to slip;
