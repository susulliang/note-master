# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 建图时APP显示RTK基站未添加是什么原因?

## Question

建图时APP显示RTK基站未添加是什么原因?

## Answer

(1) Confirm whether the RTK station has been successfully powered on, and the indicator will steady light if the RTK station is successfully powered on and the signal is good.
(2) If the RTK station is powered on and the signal is good, scan the code on the APP to match the RTK. Setting - RTK - Add RTK Station.
* If it cannot be resolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales Service
