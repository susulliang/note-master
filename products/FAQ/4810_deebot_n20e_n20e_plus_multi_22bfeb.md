## 答案 / Answer

Will the robot shake when walking on the carpet?

The robot will experience slight shaking when walking against the hair on the carpet.
When the robot walks on the edge of the carpet (with one driving wheel on the floor and the other on the carpet), there will be obvious shaking. Don't worry, the robot is trying to clean the dust and garbage on the edge of the carpet. When it returns to a flat ground, the robot will return to normal.
