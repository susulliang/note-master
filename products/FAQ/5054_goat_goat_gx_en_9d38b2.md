# What factors in the environment will prevent automatic mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What factors in the environment will prevent automatic mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

G00079

Mapping

(1) The base station is not in the lawn
(2) There are steps with a height difference greater than 3 cm on the edge of the lawn
(3) There is no border on the lawn (such as a lawn that borders a neighbor and has no partition)
(4) Two lawns are separated by a road with a width greater than 15 cm (in this scenario, only a map of one lawn can be created)
(5) There are many obstacles on the edge of the lawn
