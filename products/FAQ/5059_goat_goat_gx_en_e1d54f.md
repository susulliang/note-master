# Can the GOAT mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

## 答案 / Answer

G00084

Mowing

The GOAT is unable to cross obstacles more than 3 cm in height. The horizontal bar at the bottom of the trampoline may make the GOAT become stuck. It is recommended to delimit the trampoline area and similar entertainment facilities as restricted areas. However, you may still initiate manual mowing mode remotely to allow the GOAT to enter such areas for trimming.
