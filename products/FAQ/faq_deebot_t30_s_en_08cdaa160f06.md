# DEEBOT / T30 S / EN - How to quickly map?

## Question

How to quickly map?

## Answer

1. Prepare before mapping
1.1 Please open doors and remove floor debris, ensuring sufficient lighting for complete home environment coverage.
1.2 Press and hold the "Start" button on the robot to power it on，and place it in front of the station.
1.3 Press the "Back" button on the robot to initiate automatic return to charging. Charging begins upon successful docking.
2. Quick mapping
Reminder: Ensure your robot is connected to the network and online.
2.1 Using voice assistant:
You can instruct the robot: 'OK YIKO, Quick Mapping'.
2.2 Using the app:
Open ECOVACS HOME > Enter to Smart Cleaning > Quick Mapping.
2.3 Upon completion of mapping, the robot will announce, 'Mapping complete. Please check in the app.' The map is now successfully created.
Reminder:
① When using voice assistant, the robot's three buttons will simultaneously flash white during voice interaction.
② If the robot is offline, the quick mapping function cannot be used temporarily. However, you can manually start the robot by pressing the "start" button on the machine, and it will work while mapping. After completion, there will be a voice prompt saying, 'Cleaning complete, return to charging.' The map is then successfully created.
