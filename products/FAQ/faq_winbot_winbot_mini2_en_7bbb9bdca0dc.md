# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Alert: Low water level How to deal with it?

## Question

Alert: Low water level
 How to deal with it?

## Answer

1. Long press the Start Button for over 2 seconds until the fan stops working, then take down the WINBOT.
2. Open the rubber plug and check if the cleaning solution is sufficient. If not, refill the tank with ECOVACS WINBOT cleaning solutionor tap water, then close it tightly.
3. Power the WINBOT off and on again. Adhere it to the glass and observe if the spraying function works normally.
