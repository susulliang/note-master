# 我应该沿草坪轮廓还是花园轮廓建图？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我应该沿草坪轮廓还是花园轮廓建图？

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

1. 若花园内草坪与其他区域平坦无高差，可沿花园边缘建图，使GOAT能在非草坪区域行走。
2. 若草坪与其他区域高差超过3cm，建议仅沿草坪轮廓建图，GOAT无法跨越高于3cm的台阶。                                                                             温馨提醒： 因GOAT存在一定定位误差，建议遥控时与落差超3cm的边界保持10cm以上安全距离；泳池、悬崖等危险区域，建议与边界保持30cm以上安全距离，以防因定位偏差导致跌落或卡困。

1. If the lawn and other areas within the garden are level with no elevation difference, map along the garden perimeter to enable GOAT to navigate non-lawn areas.
 2. If the elevation difference between the lawn and other areas exceeds 3cm, map only along the lawn's outline. GOAT cannot traverse steps higher than 3cm. Friendly Reminder: Due to GOAT's positioning inaccuracies, maintain a minimum 10cm safety distance from edges with over 3cm elevation changes during remote operation. For hazardous areas like pools or cliffs, keep at least 30cm clearance to prevent falls or entrapment caused by positioning errors.
