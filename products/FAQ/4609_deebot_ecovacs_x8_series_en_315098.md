# 1.Press the blue release button at the rear of the robot to remove the dirty water box;
2.Open the dirty water box and dump the sewage;
3.Take out the filter and clean the dirty water box (can use cleaning tools to assist in cleaning);
4.Please reinstall the filter and close the blue cover tightly;
5.Please reinstall the dirty water box. A "click" sound indicates that it is installed in place.
