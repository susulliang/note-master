# 为什么设备会长时间停留在泳池排水口上？

> **产品线**：ULTRAMARINE　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么设备会长时间停留在泳池排水口上？

## 答案 / Answer

ULTRAMARINE P1 可通过直径小于 5.5cm 的常规排水口盖。清洁过程中请勿打开泳池排水口，避免设备被吸附。
若排水口吸力将设备吸住无法清洁，机身指示灯会红灯常亮，设备会在 3 分钟内自动调整推力尝试脱困。
若脱困失败，指示灯保持常亮，请您手动将设备挪离排水口：
1.挪离后能继续工作，说明问题已解除；
2.若仍不工作，请将设备捞出水面后重启，并再次确认排水口已关闭，重新启动清洁任务。
 给您带来不便非常抱歉，按以上步骤操作即可恢复正常使用。

ULTRAMARINE P1 can pass over standard drain covers with a diameter smaller than 5.5 cm. Do NOT open the pool drain while cleaning to prevent the device from being stuck by suction.
If the device is stuck due to drain suction, the indicator will show a solid red light, and it will automatically adjust its thrust to escape within 3 minutes.
If it fails to escape and the indicator remains red, manually move the device away from the drain:
1.If it resumes working after being moved away, the issue is resolved.
2.If it still does not work, take the device out of the water, restart it, make sure the drain is closed, then start a new cleaning task.
