Why Are There Water Streaks After Cleaning the Glass?

Water streaks after cleaning are usually caused by the following:
1.wiping pad is too wet:
If the wiping pad isn’t wrung out properly, excess moisture may evaporate unevenly and leave streaks.
Tip: Wring out the wiping pad before cleaning.
2.Too much dust on the glass:
If cleaning is infrequent, dust can build up.
When mixed with water, it may create a muddy residue that dries into streaks.
Tip: Rinse the wiping pad when it gets dirty and wipe the glass 2–3 times. Regular cleaning helps reduce buildup.
