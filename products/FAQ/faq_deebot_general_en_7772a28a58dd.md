# How to create multi-floor maps?

## Question

How to create multi-floor maps?

## Answer

1. Open the app > Enter > tap “Map” > tap “+” >Follow the on-screen instructions to enable this feature.
2. Move the DEEBOT to the new environment where you want to create a map. Then tap “Map” > “+” > “Add New” > tap “Quick Mapping”.
Note: After mapping is complete, the robot will automatically return to the starting point.
