# 新增区域时覆盖了原来创建的通道，通道会保留吗？ / When a new area is created, the original path is overwritten. Will the path remain?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

新增区域时覆盖了原来创建的通道，通道会保留吗？ / When a new area is created, the original path is overwritten. Will the path remain?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

不会
草坪中间不会有通道存在，会根据新增区域的情况，尽量保留草坪外区域联通的部分。

No, there will be no draw-path in the middle of the lawn, according to the situation of the new area, as far as possible to retain the part of the draw-path outside the area.
