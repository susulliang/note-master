# 清扫线路乱如何处理？ / How to deal with cluttered cleaning routes?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

清扫线路乱如何处理？ / How to deal with cluttered cleaning routes?

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
当您在使用过程中若您有遇到机器人原地转圈,行走轨迹异常等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
1.请检查激光模组(在机器人正前方撞板中心位置)是否沾灰,如有沾灰请用干纸巾轻轻擦拭;
2.检查室内环境障碍物是否较多，因高精度避障可能会让路线看起来较乱，建议稍作整理；
3.检查驱动轮轮是否有脏污，若有请用干的抹布擦拭干净。

Warm Reminder：
During use, If you encounter situations where the robot is spinning in circles or has abnormal walking trajectory, etc. You can follow the following steps to try:
1.Please check the laser module(In the center of the bumper directly) for dust, If yes, please gently wipe it with a dry tissue;
2.Check if there are many obstacles in the environment. It is recommended to tidy up a bit;
3.Check if the drive wheels are dirty, if so, please wipe them with a dry cloth.
