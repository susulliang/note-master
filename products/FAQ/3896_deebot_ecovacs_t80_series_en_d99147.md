# How to disassemble/install the dust collection cabin filter cotton？

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to disassemble/install the dust collection cabin filter cotton？

## 答案 / Answer

1. Remove dust collection cabin cover;
2. remove the dust bag;
3. slide the filter foam holder to the right and remove it.
4. Remove the filter pad from the back and clean it.
5. Reinstall the filter pad back into the holder.
6. reinstall the filter pad holder back into the dust collection cabin.
7. Install the dust bag in place and close the dust collection cabin lid.
(Note: If washed, please air dry before use)
