# GOAT 可以割高草吗？ / Can the GOAT cut tall grass?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 可以割高草吗？ / Can the GOAT cut tall grass?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

（1）GOAT 无法良好切割高于12cm的草，且过高的草可能被识别为障碍物，请确保草坪中的草已修整至10cm以下
（2）如果GOAT在切割较高较密的草时，实际的切割高度会受草况的影响，从而留下的草比设置的切割高度高1-2cm，为保证切割效果，请提前处理高草，并且定期让GOAT工作。

(1) The GOAT is unable to cut grass higher than 12 cm well. Taller grass may be recognized as an obstacle, so please make sure that grass has been trimmed to 10 cm or lower.
(2) When the GOAT is cutting tall and dense grass, the actual cutting height may be influenced by the condition of the grass, resulting in leaving grass higher by 1-2cm than the set cutting height. To ensure optimal cutting results, it is recommended to pre-cut tall grass and regularly schedule GOAT for work.
