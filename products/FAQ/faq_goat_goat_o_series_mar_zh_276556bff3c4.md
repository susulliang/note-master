# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - APP上的RTK信号强度地图，为什么大面积显示无信号覆盖？

## Question

APP上的RTK信号强度地图，为什么大面积显示无信号覆盖？

## Answer

GOAT is unable to receive satellite signals at its current location. This may be due to obstruction by tree canopies, shadows, or buildings.
If within an area without signal coverage, with map boundary or no-go zone boundary included, it is recommended to add physical boundaries such as fences with a height of >20cm to prevent the machine from going out of boundary or entering no-go zone.
