# Disassembly, clean and installation guide of mopping pad assembly

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Disassembly, clean and installation guide of mopping pad assembly

## 答案 / Answer

I. Mopping Pad Assembly Removal and Cleaning
1.Press and hold the AUTO button to turn off the Robot. Flip the Robot over.
2.Lift the mopping pad assembly upwards with slight force to remove it. Peel off the washable mopping pads.
3.Wash the removed washable mopping pads and air dry it before reuse.
II. Mopping Pad Assembly Installation
1.Flip the Robot over.
2.Align the mopping pad with the mopping pad plate and attach it smoothly. Reinstall the mopping pad assembly and ensure it is properly installed.
