# Can the GOAT detect possible intruders while in the middle of mowing?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT detect possible intruders while in the middle of mowing?

## 答案 / Answer

G00121

Intelligence & Privacy

The GOAT is currently unable to perform long-distance human recognition for security purposes while in motion.
