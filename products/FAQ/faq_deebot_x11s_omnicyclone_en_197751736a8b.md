# Robot Packing Instruction Video

## Question

Robot Packing Instruction Video

## Answer

Before packing, please ensure the following steps are completed:
1. Empty the clean water tank, dirty water tank, and cleaning solution box.
Open the app, tap "Settings" > "Device Relocation" > "Empty Deebot Clean Water Tank" to drain the internal water tank of the machine, and allow the damp roller mop to dry.
2. Remove the top cover of the robot and slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off.
3. Unplug the power cord from the station and remove the station base.
4. Place the station flat at the bottom of the packing box, then cover it with the foam board. Place the robot into the space on the side of the packing box.
5. Return the side brush, cleaning solution, power cord, and station base to their designated positions on top of the foam protective board.
