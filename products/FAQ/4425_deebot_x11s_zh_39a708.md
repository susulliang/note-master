# 27

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

27

## 答案 / Answer

1. Open the app → Enter → Tap "Map" →  Tap "Threshold Ramp" → Add a "Threshold Ramp" at the the threshold location.
2. Remove the front cover of the ROBOT, slide the Power Switch to "O"'to turn off the ROBOT, then flip it over and place it with the bottom facing up;
3. Clean dust and debris from the Driving Wheels. Check if foreign objects are stuck in the Levering wheels; remove any if found.
3. Press the trigger above the Driving Wheels while slowly rotating them clockwise. Observe if the Levering wheels rotate synchronously. If they rotate normally, continue using the robot while monitoring its performance. If they fail to rotate properly, contact after-sales support.
