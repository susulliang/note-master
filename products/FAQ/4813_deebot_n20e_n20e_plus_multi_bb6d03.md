## 答案 / Answer

(1) Confirm whether there are any foreign objects in the roller brush. When the dustbin is full or there are foreign objects stuck in the roller brush, there will be a "da da da" sound. It is recommended to remove the foreign objects in time. (3) If there are obvious gaps between the floor or tiles, the robot may make a slight sound when passing by. In order to provide better whole-house cleaning, the cover of the robot roller brush is floating. It is normal for the robot to make a slight sound when passing through the threshold or baseplate gap.
