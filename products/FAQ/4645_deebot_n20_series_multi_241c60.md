What to do if the OZMO Pro 2.0 Vibrating Mopping System doesn't seep？(standard verison)

1.Open the water inlet to check if there is any water inside.                                                        2.Wipe the metal plates of the water tank and the Robot with a dry cloth.                                                          3.Wipe the water outlet.                                                                                                                    4.Wipe the water filling nozzle.
