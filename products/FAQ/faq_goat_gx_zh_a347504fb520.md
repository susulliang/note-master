# Will the blades of the GOAT continue turning when passing through non-grass areas?

## Question

Will the blades of the GOAT continue turning when passing through non-grass areas?

## Answer

When mowing, GOAT will consider a small non-grass area as a passable area and will always keep the cutterhead rotating. If it is a large non-grass area, GOAT will not pass.
