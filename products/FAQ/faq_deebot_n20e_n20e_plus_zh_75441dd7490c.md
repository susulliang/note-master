# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 边刷拆卸，清理及安装指导 Disassembly, clean and installation Guide of side brush

## Question

边刷拆卸，清理及安装指导                                                            Disassembly, clean and installation Guide of side brush

## Answer

1.长按启动键将主机关机，把机器翻转后，底部朝上放置；
2.取下边刷，使用清理工具清理边刷轴承，边刷上的缠绕物等，并确认清理干净。
3.把边刷对准卡槽后向下按压，听到“嗒”一声表示安装到位。                                                                                                            1.Long press the power button to turn off the Robot, turn it over.
2.Remove the side brush, clean the side brush, remove any entangled materials with cleaning tools.
3.Align the side brush with the installation port and press down. Make sure that you hear a "click" sound.
