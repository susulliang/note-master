# Does the "Breakpoint Resume" feature work after a power outage?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Does the "Breakpoint Resume" feature work after a power outage?

## 答案 / Answer

This feature supports "Pause and Resume" only, not resume after a complete power disconnection.
