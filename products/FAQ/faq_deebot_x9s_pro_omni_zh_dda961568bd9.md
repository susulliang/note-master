# DEEBOT / X9S PRO OMNI / ZH - Robot Installation Guide

## Question

Robot Installation Guide

## Answer

1. Open the outer packaging and remove the ROBOT, base, station, and power cord in sequence. Remove the protective material from the ROBOT and the protective bag from the station.
 2. Install the base by aligning it with the slots beneath the station. Press firmly until you hear a "click" to confirm proper installation.
 3. Use the included Power Cord to connect to the Power Socket on the back of the station. Plug in the power and place the station against a wall in a suitable location.
 (Place the station on a flat, hard surface. Keep the area within 5cm on either side and 80cm in front clear of objects.)
 Friendly Reminder:
 1. Prioritize placing the station on tile surfaces. If placed on flooring, regularly check for water residue beneath the station and wipe it dry promptly to prevent moisture damage.
 2. Ensure the base is installed properly.
 3. If reflective objects like mirrors or reflective baseboards are near the OMNI Station, cover the bottom 14cm section of these objects.
 4. Avoid placing the OMNI Station in direct sunlight.
 5. Position it in an area with strong Wi-Fi signal for optimal performance.
