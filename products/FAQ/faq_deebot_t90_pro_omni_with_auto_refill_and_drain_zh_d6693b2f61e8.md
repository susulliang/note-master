# DEEBOT / T90 PRO OMNI With Auto Refill And Drain / ZH - How to disassemble and reassemble the station's guide wheels?

## Question

How to disassemble and reassemble the station's guide wheels?

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
 If you encounter foreign objects in the station guide wheels during use that require cleaning, follow these steps:
 1. Use a flathead screwdriver to gently pry off the small wheel, then thoroughly clean it.
 2. After cleaning, reinstall the base guide wheel. Gently rotate the small wheel; if it turns smoothly without resistance, it is properly installed.
