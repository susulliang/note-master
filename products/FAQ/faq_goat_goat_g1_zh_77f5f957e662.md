# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Why is the Navigation Beacon making a sound?

## Question

Why is the Navigation Beacon making a sound?

## Answer

(1) The Navigation Beacon will buzz when it senses being powered on.
 (2) After the Navigation Beacon is upgraded, the beacon within communication range will restart, and the beacon will buzz after restart.
 *If the Navigation Beacon keeps on buzzing, try to reinstall the battery. If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
