# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 建图时APP显示“RTK基站未添加”是什么原因？

## Question

建图时APP显示“RTK基站未添加”是什么原因？

## Answer

1. Verify the RTK station is powered on with the blue light illuminated.
 2. Scan the QR code to pair the RTK: APP - Settings - RTK - Add RTK Station
 *If the issue persists, contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
