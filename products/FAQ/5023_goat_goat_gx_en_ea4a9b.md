# Can I remotely control the GOAT for mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I remotely control the GOAT for mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

G00048

Mapping

The GOAT and the App are connected by Bluetooth only. When your phone is more than 20 m away from the GOAT, the Bluetooth connection will be automatically interrupted. For safety reasons, we recommend that you follow the GOAT for remote mapping.
