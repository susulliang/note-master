# Can the real-time positioning function be used after installing the auto-connection module?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the real-time positioning function be used after installing the auto-connection module?

## 答案 / Answer

G00136

Disable

Additional Connection Module

Yes, after the auto-connection module is installed, you can turn on the real-time positioning switch in Settings ＞ More Settings ＞ Network Settings. After the GOAT is turned on and it has detected that it is far away from the map range (about 200 m), it will push Google Maps to your phone to mark its current location.
