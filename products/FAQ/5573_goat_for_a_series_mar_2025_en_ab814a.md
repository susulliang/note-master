# GOAT春季重新拿出来使用，有何注意事项？ / What are the precautions before the first use when spring coming?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT春季重新拿出来使用，有何注意事项？ / What are the precautions before the first use when spring coming?

## 答案 / Answer

Maintain

（1）将充电站和RTK杆重新组装，并且安装在去年的原址
（2）在连接电线前检查电线是否有损坏；
（3）检查刀片是否有生锈损坏，若有，请及时更换刀片；
（4）开启机器，查看APP和机器是否工作正常，如不正常，请重新创建地图。
请注意：操作过程中佩戴手套，以免被刀片割伤

(1) Assemble docking stations and RTK Reference Station, putting them back to where they were in the last working season.
(2) Check the cables for any damage or breaks and repair or replace as needed before connecting.
(3) Check the Blades for sharpness and replace them if necessary.
(4) Power on your GOAT and test if it is connected to the App and works properly. If not, please rebulid the map for working properly.

Tips: always wear protective gloves to avoid being injured by the blades.
