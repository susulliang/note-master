# How to maintain/care for/clean WINBOT?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How to maintain/care for/clean WINBOT?

## 答案 / Answer

1. Once cleaning is complete, if no longer in use, pour out any remaining liquid from the water tank.
 2. Remove the dirty wiping pad. Wash it with water and wring it out, then allow it to air dry to prevent odors and bacterial growth.
 3. Use a dry cloth to wipe the fan, spherical sensors, side rollers, and corner brushes to keep the surface clean.
