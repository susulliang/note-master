# 更换刀片时，LiDAR镜头划伤怎么办？ / What about LiDAR lens scratchers when replaing blades？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

更换刀片时，LiDAR镜头划伤怎么办？ / What about LiDAR lens scratchers when replaing blades？

## 答案 / Answer

Maintain

1.更换刀片前，请务必带好防护手套，并盖好LiDAR保护盖。
2.机器翻转过来后，请勿拖动机器，避免LiDAR镜头划伤
3.如果LiDAR镜头划伤，可能影响定位，请联系售后更换新的LiDAR镜头罩

1.Wear protective gloves and cover the LiDAR cover before replacing the blades.
2.After GOAT is turned over, do not drag the GOAT to avoid LiDAR Lens scratches.
