How to Connect WINBOT to the App?

1.Scan the QR code on the battery to download and install the ECOVACS HOME App. Or download it directly the from your App Store 
2.Open ECOVACS HOME App to scan the QR code on the battery and follow the on-screen prompts to connect WINBOT.
