# How to Use Base Station Features?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to Use Base Station Features?

## 答案 / Answer

1.Open the app and click "Enter."
2.Click "Station" to access the control panel.
3.Select dust collection, drying, or mop washing based on the robot's current task.
Note: You can check the base station's real-time status in the app.
