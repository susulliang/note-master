# 地图上GOAT 的位置和实际和位置不符？ / The location of GOAT on the map is not the actual location.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图上GOAT 的位置和实际和位置不符？ / The location of GOAT on the map is not the actual location.

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

请确认是否搬动、拖动了GOAT，APP地图上将仍显示GOAT被搬动前的位置。请将机器遥控或搬回基站，重新启动任务。

如已排除以上情况，请检查
（1）GOAT的摄像头是否有脏污
（2）当前天气是否晴好无雨
如果以上操作GOAT始终在地图上显示的位置不正确，可删除地图后重新建图

(1) Please confirm whether GOAT has been moved or dragged. The GOAT will not be relocated, the position of GOAT before it is moved will still be displayed on the APP map. Please remote or carry the GOAT to return to the docking station and restart the task.

If the above situation has been eliminated, please check
(1) Whether the camera of GOAT is dirty
(2) Whether the current weather is fine.
If GOAT consistently appears incorrectly on the map, you can delete the map and rebuild it.
