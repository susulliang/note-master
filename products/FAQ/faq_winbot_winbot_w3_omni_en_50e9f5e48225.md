# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to connect winbot to the ECOVACS Home App?

## Question

How to connect winbot to the ECOVACS Home App?

## Answer

1.Remove WINBOT from its hook.
2.Use your phone to scan the QR code inside the station. Alternatively, search for "ECOVACS HOME" in your app store, then download and register an account.
3.Bluetooth Connection:
① Click the "+" (Add) button on the app homepage and scan the QR code on the back of the station. Press and hold the power button on the station for 2 seconds to turn it on, and wait until the startup sound finishes. Follow the prompts in the app: click "Powered On", then "Skip Connection for Now", and finally "Start Using" to complete the Bluetooth connection.
4.Wi‑Fi Connection:
① Click the "+" (Add) button on the app homepage and scan the QR code on the back of the station. Press and hold the power button on the station for 2 seconds to turn it on, and wait until the startup sound finishes. Click "Powered On", then select "Choose Home Wi‑Fi Network" , and enter your Wi‑Fi password. Press and hold the function switch button on the station for 3 seconds. After hearing the sound prompt "Entering Wi‑Fi pairing mode", click "Completed" in the app and wait for the Wi‑Fi setup to finish successfully.
Tips:
If you are unable to scan the QR code, you can also click "Select Model" on the scanning interface. Choose the corresponding winbot model and follow the on‑screen instructions to complete the connection.
