## 答案 / Answer

How to deal with the Robot gives an alarm "Difficulty in running, please check the drive wheels"?

1. Long press the power button to turn off the Robot, turn the Robot over.
2. Press and turn the drive wheel to clear tangles;
3. Wipe surface stains with a semi-dry mop;
