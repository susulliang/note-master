# 任务未完成，GOAT 为何自动结束了割草任务？

## Question

任务未完成，GOAT 为何自动结束了割草任务？

## Answer

(1) In order not to conflict with the scheduled mowing tasks, the GOAT will automatically end the mowing task when it has been paused for more than 48 hours.
(2) If GOAT triggers movement or tipping, which may cause a change in its position, it will automatically end the current task.
(3) The GOAT will deem the mowing task complete if certain areas are inaccessible due to obstacles.
(4) The task will be automatically ended if the GOAT has a power outage during the task.
