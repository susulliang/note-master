# 为什么GOAT充满电后，工作时间还是缩短了？

## Question

为什么GOAT充满电后，工作时间还是缩短了？

## Answer

Possible reasons:
 (1) Dense grass and complex terrain
 (2) High-efficiency mode in use
 (3) Battery degradation
