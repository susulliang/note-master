# Why can't DEEBOT recognize carpet completely or recognize a carpet as floor sometimes?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why can't DEEBOT recognize carpet completely or recognize a carpet as floor sometimes?

## 答案 / Answer

D20425

Cleaning

Currently,  some special materials and specifications of carpets may disturb the robot's recognition. Such as:
1. Carpets with extremely hardness (similar to wooden floor hardness)
2. Carpets with hair length less than 2mm
3. PVC or other special materials

Additionally, you can open the app, tap Map Management-Map Edit-Add Carpet to make relevant settings.
