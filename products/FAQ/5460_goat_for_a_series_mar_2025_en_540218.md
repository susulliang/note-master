# 我的Lidar传感器工作时特别烫，会烧坏吗？ / My Lidar sensor is particularly hot when working, will it burn out?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的Lidar传感器工作时特别烫，会烧坏吗？ / My Lidar sensor is particularly hot when working, will it burn out?

## 答案 / Answer

LiDAR

不会
（1）Lidar工作时，lidar传感器会有热量释放出来，不会影响极其正常使用
（2）工作中请勿主动触碰Lidar镜头，避免烫伤。

Can't
(1) When Lidar is working, the Lidar sensor will release heat, which will not affect its normal use
(2) Please do not touch the Lidar lens actively during work to avoid burns.
