# 割草

## Question

割草

## Answer

If you turn off the Do not disturb mode in the Settings, GOAT will work all day, but the GOAT vision module will not work properly due to the influence of light. Therefore, the area with weak RTK signal as well as lawn boundary will not be able to mow at night. By default, GOAT will actively trigger mowing thiese areas at 8:00 am the next day.
