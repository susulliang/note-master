# DEEBOT / T50 MAX PRO OMNI / EN - Anti-drop sensors have accumulated dust, please wipe it clean.

## Question

Anti-drop sensors have accumulated dust, please wipe it clean.

## Answer

Warm Reminder：
When you got a prompt that the  Anti-drop sensors have accumulated dust or Anti-drop sensor abnormal, you can follow the following steps to try:
1.Turn the Robot over and wipe the anti-drop sensors to prevent dust blockage.
2.Shutdown and restart.
