# WINBOT / W2 / EN - How long does it take to charge the safety battery?

## Question

How long does it take to charge the safety battery?

## Answer

The safety battery of W2 has a capacity of 2600mAh. If the safety battery is exhausted, it needs about 4 hours of charging to go from battery shortage to a state without warnings.
