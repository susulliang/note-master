# DEEBOT 601 FAQ

DEEBOT 601 FAQ List

__1\. Using DEEBOT 601__

__1\.1 What are the corresponding cleaning patterns in DEEBOT 601 working mode? How to switch the cleaning patterns? When should I switch it?__

1\. DEEBOT 601 has two cleaning patterns\. Random Cleaning Pattern \(Default Pattern\) and Programmed Cleaning Pattern\.

2\. Cleaning Patterns are selected using the Remote Control or ECOVACS APP or DEEBOT AUTO button\. Double\-click the AUTO button on the robot or press cleaning mode switch button on the Remote Control when robot is paused\. Cleaning pattern will be switched and cleaning mode switch indicator will be lighted on or off\. You can switch the cleaning pattern   using the ECOVACS APP \(Cleaning pattern can be switched immediately by using the ECOVACS APP\)\.

3\. Random Cleaning Pattern should be set when cleaning carpet; Programmed Cleaning Pattern should be set when cleaning bare floors\.

__1\.2 What does DEEBOT 601 do if it meets an obstacle, like furniture? __

DEEBOT has an active obstacle detection system to helps it avoid contact your furniture, walls and household items\. Anti\-Collision Sensors are on\-board the robot\. When the sensors detect an obstacle, a signal will be sent to DEEBOT’s “brain” to slow down, change direction and select a new cleaning path\. If the object is very low to the ground, 1\.5” or smaller in height, DEEBOT may gently touch the obstacle with its mechanical cushion bumper before turning around\.

__1\.3 When I move my DEEBOT 601 to another place and restart it to work, why does it repeat the cleaning the area that already cleaned?__

When DEEBOT601 is working in Programmed Cleaning Pattern, DEEBOT will start a new cleaning cycle once you move it to another place or the cleaning pattern is switched\.

__1\.4 Why does the AUTO indicator light off when I paused my DEEBOT601 for a while? why does it repeat the cleaning the area that already cleaned when restarting?__

1\. After a pause of more than 10min, DEEBOT 601 will fall in sleep state and all the indicators will be off\.

When pressing the AUTO button to wake up the robot, all the indicators will be on\. Press the AUTO button again on the robot to start cleaning \(DEEBOT 601 can start work after one pressing AUTO button\)\.

2\. After waking the DEEBOT 601 up, the previous working memory will be wiped and DEEBOT 601 will start a new cleaning cycle\.

__1\.5 I paused DEEBOT 601 to clean the Dust Bin while it is working, why does it repeat the cleaning the area that already cleaned when restarting? How to avoid this problem?__

1\. When you pick up the DEEBOT, the previous working memory will be wiped and DEEBOT 601 will start a new cleaning cycle\.

2\. We suggest you not to pick up the DEEBOT when cleaning the Dust Bin\. You can hold the DEEBOT and draw out the Dust Bin\. 

__1\.6 DEEBOT 601 is cleaning with a louder noise and/or lower suction than normal\. What should I do?__

The suction intake may be blocked or the Main Brush/ Side Brushes may be tangled with dirt and debris\. For optimal performance, the full bin sensors and filter should be regularly cleaned of debris\. 

1\. Power OFF\. Remove the Dust Bin, empty it and reinstall\. 

2\. Turn the DEEBOT upside down\. Remove the Main Brush Cover\. Remove the Main Brush\. Use the Multi\-Function Cleaning Tool to cut off and remove any hair tangled around the Main Brush\. Reinstall the Main Brush\. 

3\. Remove the Side Brushes\. Wipe the Side Brushes and their slots with a dry cloth and reinstall\.

4\. If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service\.

__1\.7 I can't charge my DEEBOT 601\. What should I do?__

1\. DEEBOT can be charged only when the power switch is ON\.

2\. Check if the socket is functioning \(if not, replace the socket\)\.

3\. Check if the DEEBOT is properly connected to the Charging Dock \(the best way is to allow the DEEBOT to be recharged automatically\)\.

4\. Check to see if the Charging Dock Pins and Charging Contacts are receding \(if yes, please contact the ECOVACS service center\)\.

__1\.8 What should I do if my DEEBOT 601 cannot return to the Charging Dock?__

__Condition 1__: DEEBOT cannot find the Charging Dock

1\. Put your Charging Dock in a more open area

2\. Start the DEEBOT from the Charging Dock so that it will be easier to find the Charging Dock again afterwards\.

3\. Check whether there is any signal interference from the area surrounding the Charging Dock\.

__Condition 2__: DEEBOT cannot connect the Charging Dock

1\. Check if the Charging Dock Pins is receding\. If yes, please contact service center

2\. Check if the Charging Contacts and Dock Pins are oxidized\. If yes, clean them or contact our service center\.

__1\.9 Why does DEEBOT 601 move around in circles and backwards after starting up?__

1\. The Anti\-Drop Sensors on the bottom of DEEBOT 601 may be covered with dust\. Wipe the Anti\-Drop Sensors with provided Cleaning Tool to maintain their effectiveness\.

2\. The Driving Wheels may be tangled with hair or debris\. Use the Multi\-function Cleaning Tool to cut off and remove any hair/debris tangled around the Driving Wheels\.

3\. The Bumper rail may be stuck\. Press each side of the bumper rail to see if it rebounds automatically\.

If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service\.

<a id="OLE_LINK10"></a><a id="OLE_LINK11"></a>__1\.10 DEEBOT 601 did not clean automatically at the scheduled time\. What should I do?__

1\. Ensure the DEEBOT 601’s Power Switch is ON\.

2\. Ensure the robot is charged\.

3\. The scheduled cleaning time may not have been programmed correctly or was cancelled\. Set the scheduled cleaning time again making sure it is saved\.

__1\.11 What should be done if DEEBOT 601 accidentally vacuums up liquid?    __

Please turn off DEEBOT 601 immediately\. Remove the Dust Bin, Filters, Main Brush and allow them to dry completely before reinstalling them\. If the DEEBOT does not function normally after You’ve completed these steps, please contact Customer Service\.

__1\.12 What should I do if my DEEBOT 601's Remote Control doesn't work?__

1\. Check if the Remote Control has power \(Please replace the batteries if no power\)\.

2\. Check if the DEEBOT has power \(please charge the DEEBOT by manual if no power\)

3\. Check if it is within range \(the Remote Control has a max distance of 5 meters\)\.

4\. Check if there is any interference near the DEEBOT \(please remove them if there is\)

5\. Check whether the signal transmitter and receiver are dirty \(wipe the transmitter on the remote and the receiver on the machine\)\.

__1\.13 Why does my DEEBOT 601 always have a short pause then start?__

The DEEBOT is working and processing at the same time\. it may stop for a while and then go on after a few seconds\. The pause times depend on the home environment complexity\.

__2\. APP FAQ,__

<a id="OLE_LINK17"></a><a id="OLE_LINK18"></a>__2\.1 Why my APP can’t connect to DEEBOT 601? What can I do?__

1\. Ensure you have downloaded and login in ECOVACS APP successfully\.

2\. Ensure your Wi\-Fi is okay and stable, using 2\.4G Wi\-Fi not using 5G Wi\-Fi\.

3\. Ensure the unit is not far away from router \(less than 2 meters\) when setting up connection\.

4\. Ensure you connect the APP according to the APP guide step by step, not miss or lose any step\. 

5\. You can try to reset machine and use mobile's hotspot to connect to check if it’s home router problem\.

If the problem persists, please contact Customer Service__\.__

__2\.2 Battery indicator inaccurate in APP\. What should I do? __

If the network environment is not well, APP will take some time to identify the power value\. Please ensure the network environment is smooth\.

__3\. Maintenance FAQ__

__3\.1 Will DEEBOT disturb my pet? __

Normally, no\. DEEBOT works quietly and the noise should not upset your pets\. We recommend that you supervise DEEBOT during its first cleaning and assist should there be a problem\.

__3\.2 Can I keep DEEBOT 601 on the Charging Dock for an extended period of time? __

Yes\. After DEEBOT’s batteries are fully charged, the battery management system will reduce electricity to a trickle charge and DEEBOT will not be overcharged\. We recommend keeping DEEBOT powered ON and on the Charging Dock so it is ready to clean at any time\.

__3\.3 Items that need to be cleaned and maintained on a regular basis __

For optimal performance, please keep the following items regularly cleaned\. 

Front Wheel 

Side Brush 

Main Brush 

Anti\-fall sensor 

Charging Contacts 

Dust Bin

__3\.4 Can DEEBOT 601 clean when nobody is home? __

Yes, you can schedule DEEBOT to automatically clean once or twice per day, even if no one is at home\.
