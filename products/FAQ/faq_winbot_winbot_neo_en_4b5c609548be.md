# What should I do if WINBOT slips (sliding sideways or shuddering)?

## Question

What should I do if WINBOT slips (sliding sideways or shuddering)?

## Answer

1) Slight slippage is normal and does not affect the normal operation.
2) Check whether the mopping pad is overly wet.
3) If the robot is tainted with stains, such as oil, please follow the instruction manual to clean the driving threads.
