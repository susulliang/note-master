# DEEBOT / T90 PRO OMNI / ZH - Warning: Roller mop is tangled

## Question

Warning: Roller mop is tangled

## Answer

1.Double-click the AUTO button on the ROBOT. The roller mop will automatically extend outward.
2. Press the release button on the inner side of the roller bracket with your finger, then gently pull the roller end cover outward; remove the roller mop. Apply slight force to pull out the Roller End Cover; clean the roller mop.
3. Flip the ROBOT over, so its bottom faces upward; then lift the roller motor upward.
4. Use a cleaning tool or flathead screwdriver to clean the roller cavity. After ensuring it is clean, rotate the roller cavity bearing to confirm it turns smoothly.
5. Reattach the Roller End Cover to the roller mop;
6. Align the roller mop with the roller motor and gently push it inward. A "click" sound indicates proper installation. The Roller End Cover should be flush with the top of the roller mounting bracket.
7. Double-click the AUTO button on the ROBOT to automatically retract the roller mop.
8. Restart the machine and observe. If the "Roller mop is tangled" error persists, contact Ecovacs after-sales service for assistance.
 TIPS: The cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
