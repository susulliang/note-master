# Can the GOAT G1 mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

## Question

Can the GOAT G1 mow the area at the bottom of a trampoline?

## Answer

The GOAT G1 ​is unable to cross obstacles more than 3 cm in height. The horizontal bar at the bottom of the trampoline may make the GOAT become stuck. It is recommended to delimit the trampoline area and similar entertainment facilities as restricted areas. However, you may still initiate manual mowing mode remotely to allow the GOAT to enter such areas for trimming.
