# 若将RTK基站头取下后未按原位置放回，GOAT还能正常使用吗？

## Question

若将RTK基站头取下后未按原位置放回，GOAT还能正常使用吗？

## Answer

If the RTK station is moved, GOAT will not function normally. Movement may cause positioning errors, leading to risks such as boundary breaches or falls, and may even cause it to stop working. You may try returning the RTK station to its original position, or relocate and secure it before rebuilding the map.
