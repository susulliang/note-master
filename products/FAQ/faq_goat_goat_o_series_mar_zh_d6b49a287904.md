# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - GOAT春季重新拿出来使用，有何注意事项？

## Question

GOAT春季重新拿出来使用，有何注意事项？

## Answer

(1) Assemble charging station and RTK station, putting them back to where they were in the last working season.
(2) Check the cables for any damage or breaks and repair or replace as needed before connecting.
(3) Check the Blades for sharpness and replace them if necessary.
(4) Gently wipe the front AI camera.
(5) Power on your GOAT and test if it is connected to the App and works properly. If not, please rebulid the map for working properly.

Tips: always wear protective gloves to avoid being injured by the blades.
