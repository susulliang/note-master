# Warm Reminder：
During use, if you encounter a prompt robot stuck, etc. You can follow the following steps to try:
1.Open the top cover, flip the power switch to "O", turn the robot over;
2.Press and turn the drive wheel to clear tangles;
3.Use a semi-dry cloth to wipe or use a brush to remove surface stains.
