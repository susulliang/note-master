How to correctly install the robot?

1. Please remove protective strip.                                                                                                   2.Turn the Robot over.                                                                                                                  3.Take out the side brush, align it with the slot, press it down. A "click" sound indicates it has been installed properly.                                                                                                                4.Take out the docking station and remove the protection film.                                                                        5.Take out two plugs and remove protective sticker.                                                                    6. Insert the sticky end into the installation holes and find a suitable position to place it against the wall.
