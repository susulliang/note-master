# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 机器人断点续扫时，返回充电后为何没有继续未完成的任务？Why doesn't the robot continue the unfinished task after returning to station and finishing charging?

## Question

机器人断点续扫时，返回充电后为何没有继续未完成的任务？Why doesn't the robot continue the unfinished task after returning to station and finishing charging?

## Answer

（1）请确保过程中没有重置主机；
（2）请确保在APP上开启智能断点续扫功能；
（3）请确保机器人在正常充电中，充至所需电量后将继续未完成任务。                                                                                                    （1）Please ensure that the host is not reset during the process.​
（2）Please make sure to enable the intelligent continuous cleaning function on the APP.​
（3）Please ensure that the robot is charging normally and will continue to perform unfinished tasks after reaching the required battery level.
