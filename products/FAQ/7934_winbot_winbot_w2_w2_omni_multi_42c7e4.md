# Is there a range of window area that WINBOT W2 can clean?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Is there a range of window area that WINBOT W2 can clean?

## 答案 / Answer

W00172

The working area of the WINBOT W2 mainly depends on the length of the power cord/safety rope. 
Minimum applicable area: 30*40cm
Maximum applicable area (calculated by the length of 5.5m compound cable):
external single glass: width ≤ 2.5m and length ≤ 4m
internal single glass: width ≤ 2.5m and length ≤ 8m
