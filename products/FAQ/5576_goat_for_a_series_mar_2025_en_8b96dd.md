# 我可以关掉APP保养提醒吗 / Can I turn off APP Maintain alerts？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我可以关掉APP保养提醒吗 / Can I turn off APP Maintain alerts？

## 答案 / Answer

Maintain

关掉GOAT消息提醒，您将收不到任何关于GOAT的消息提醒，建议您打开，以便我们及时将GOAT的状态通知到您。

Turn off GOAT message reminders, you will not receive any message reminders about GOAT. We suggest that you turn them on so that we can notify you of GOAT's status in a timely manner.
