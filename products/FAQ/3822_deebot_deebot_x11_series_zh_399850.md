# 如何更换集尘袋/机器提示尘袋已满如何处理？ / How to Replace the Dust Bag / Alert: Dust Bag Full

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

如何更换集尘袋/机器提示尘袋已满如何处理？ / How to Replace the Dust Bag / Alert: Dust Bag Full

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 当您的机器出现“尘袋已满提示”等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行操作尝试：
 1. 请从两侧取下集尘仓盖；
 2. 取出尘袋并丢弃；
 3. 用干抹布擦拭集尘仓，安装新尘袋；
 4. 关闭集尘仓盖；
 5.请在APP里面点击“耗材与部件”＞“尘袋”＞“我已更换”

Friendly Reminder:
 If your device displays a "Dust bag Full" alert or similar notifications, follow these steps to resolve the issue: 1. Remove the dust collection cabin cover;
 2. Remove and discard the dust bag;
 3. Wipe the dust collection cabin with a dry rag and install a new dust bag;
 4. Close the dust collection cabin cover;
 5. In the app, please click Settings -Maintenance -Dust Bags-I've Replaced.
