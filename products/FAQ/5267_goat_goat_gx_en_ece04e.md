# In addition to using APP to control GOAT, are there any other smart ways?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

In addition to using APP to control GOAT, are there any other smart ways?

## 答案 / Answer

G00133

Intelligence & Privacy

In addition to the APP, GOAT also supports using Google Home and Alexa to command it to perform some simple instructions, such as starting automatic lawn mowing, etc.
