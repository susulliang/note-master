# DEEBOT / X12 OmniCyclone / EN - How to Use the Mopping Function？

## Question

How to Use the Mopping Function？

## Answer

1.Remove the clean water tank, open its lid, and fill it with water (fill until it is full but not overflowing). Then, securely close the lid and place the tank back into the station.
2.Remove the dust collection compartment cover and pull out the cleaning solution cartridge. Place it on a hard, level surface.
3.Open the lid of the cleaning solution box, pour in Ecovacs official cleaning solution, then close the lid and push the box back into the station. (The box is properly installed when it is pushed in all the way to the deepest point.)
4.Close the dust collection compartment cover securely to ensure a complete seal.
5.Ensure the machine is powered on, then place it directly in front of the station and press and hold the "Auto" button to have the machine return to the station. Once successfully docked, charging will begin.
6.Press the "Auto" button on the machine to start a vacuum and mop cleaning task.
