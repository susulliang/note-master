# It keeps prompting that upgrade failed during upgrade to a new version?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

It keeps prompting that upgrade failed during upgrade to a new version?

## 答案 / Answer

D20304

Intelligence

(1) Please ensure that DEEBOT is charging at the docking station and the battery has at least 20% capacity remaining. 
(2) If you purchased a dust-collecting docking station separately, please make sure that DEEBOT is in the docking station and upgrade after pairing them.
