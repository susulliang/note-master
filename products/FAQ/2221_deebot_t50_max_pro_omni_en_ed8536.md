# 如何升级固件版本？ / How to upgrade the firmware version?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何升级固件版本？ / How to upgrade the firmware version?

## 答案 / Answer

点击页面右上角“设置”＞点击“地宝高级设置”＞点击“机器人信息”＞点击“地宝升级”根据提示升级即可。

Click "Enter Device"- click "Settings"-Click on "DEEBOT Advanced Settings"-Click on "DEEBOT Information"-Click "DEEBOT Upgrade" , then follow the prompts to upgrade.
