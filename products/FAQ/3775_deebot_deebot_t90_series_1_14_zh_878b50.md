# 无法充电，如何处理？

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

无法充电，如何处理？

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 当您的机器人若出现无法充电或者无法开机等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行操作尝试：
 1.请先确认家里电源插座有电
 2.取下主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“I”处确认开机，长按“启动按键”让机器人自动返回基站对接充电
 3.若主机无法开机，可手动直接对接，待启动键灯闪烁表示正在充电，也可以在APP中查看地宝充电状态;充电状态会有闪电图标显示;
 4.若手动或自动无法对接充电，您可以这样操作
 ①请清理擦试基站充电极片确认回弹正常
 ②请清理擦试地宝尾部的电极片；
 以上操作完成后，请重新对接后再观察，仍就无效，请联系售后客服处理。

Friendly Reminder:
If your robot fails to charge or power on, try the following steps:
1.First, make sure the household power outlet is working. Check that the power cable connector on the back of the station is firmly inserted, and that the station’s power plug is securely connected to the outlet. The front indicator on the station should light up.
2. Remove the cover of the ROBOT. Slide the Power Switch to the "I" position to confirm power-on. Press and hold the "Start Button" to have the robot automatically return to the station for docking and charging.
3. If the ROBOT fails to power on, manually dock it. When the auto button light flashes, charging is in progress. You can also check the charging status in the app; a lightning icon will appear during charging
4. If manual or automatic docking fails, follow these steps:
① Clean and wipe the charging contacts on the station to ensure proper contact.
② Clean and wipe the charging contacts on the robot's rear.
After completing the above steps, try docking again and observe whether charging starts. If the issue persists, contact after-sales customer service for assistance.
