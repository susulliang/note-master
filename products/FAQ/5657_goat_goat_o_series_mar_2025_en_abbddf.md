# 建图为何创建失败了？ / Why did the map setting fail?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图为何创建失败了？ / Why did the map setting fail?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

（1）确认草坪区域是否过于开阔，距离GOAT 25m距离内若无建筑或较高的植物，无法生成地图。
（2）确认建图过程中是否搬动或拖移了机器，搬动机器将会导致建图失败。建议您修整花园中的坑洼区域，减少遥控过程中GOAT被卡困导致的需要搬动或拖移的概率
（3）确认建图时光线充足且无雨
（4）确认已按照APP指示在遥控完成后回到基站，才可以完成建图，开启地图学习。

(1) If the lawn area is too open wihtout any construction or any tall plants within a 25-meter radius, the  GOAT is not suitable for this kind of senario.
(2) Confirm if the GOAT has been moved or dragged during mapping, as moving the GOAT can cause mapping to fail. It is recommended that you level the garden ground to reduce the chance of moving or dragging the GOAT due to it becoming trapped when being remotely controlled 
(3) Make sure that there is sufficient light and no rain when mapping 
(4) Make sure GOAT goes back to the base station on its own after finishing using the remote control. This is done according to the instructions in the app. Then, start the map learning process to make a complete map.
