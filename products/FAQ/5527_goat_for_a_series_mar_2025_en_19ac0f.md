# 我在APP上暂停了割草，为何GOAT 自动返回了基站？ / Why does the GOAT automatically return to the docking station after I pause mowing on the app?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我在APP上暂停了割草，为何GOAT 自动返回了基站？ / Why does the GOAT automatically return to the docking station after I pause mowing on the app?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

为了防止GOAT在外停留时间过长导致电量耗尽自动关机，APP上触发的割草暂停超过30分钟后GOAT将会自动返回基站暂停。您仍然可以继续任务，GOAT将会自动前往任务暂停位置继续工作

To prevent the GOAT from automatically shutting down due to a dead battery caused by extended stays outside of the docking station, if a mowing task triggered on the App has been paused for more than 30 minutes, the GOAT will automatically return to the docking station. However, you will still be able to resume the task, and the GOAT will automatically go to the pause location and resume work.
