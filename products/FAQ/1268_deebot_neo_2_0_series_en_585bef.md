# How to deal with the robot reminder "The Anti-drop Sensor is covered with dust" or "Deebot is off the floor"?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to deal with the robot reminder "The Anti-drop Sensor is covered with dust" or "Deebot is off the floor"?

## 答案 / Answer

1. Turn the machine over and wipe the anti-drop sensors with a dry paper towel/dry towel;
2. Start and observe the machine after placing it back on the ground.
