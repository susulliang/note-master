# How do I clean the robot driving wheels when they are entangled or dirty on the surface?

## Question

How do I clean the robot driving wheels when they are entangled or dirty on the surface?

## Answer

1. Power off the robot, then flip the robot over and place it with the bottom facing up.
2. Press and rotate the driving wheels to remove any entangling debris.
3. Press and rotate the driving wheels and wipe the surfaces with a semi-dry cloth.
