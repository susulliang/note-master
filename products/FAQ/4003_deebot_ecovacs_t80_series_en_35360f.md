# How to properly charge a robot and instructions for energy energy-saving charging?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to properly charge a robot and instructions for energy energy-saving charging?

## 答案 / Answer

1. Remove the cover and turn on the Robot by flicking the power switch to "I". 
2. Place the Robot in front of the station; long press the "start button" to let the Robot return to the station automatically and hear the voice prompt "start charging".
 Instructions for charging the robot after starting energy-saving charging:
1. Open the app - click  enter - Settings -  Station Advanced Settings - energy-saving charging, turn on the function.
Energy saving charging instructions:
1. Default off, when it is on, the battery will be full only in the set period, and keep at the safe charge in other periods;
2. To ensure the normal operation of the robot, the battery may still be charged during non-energy-saving charging hours. If a task is not completed, the robot will be charged to the required level during the non-energy-saving charging period (the default charging duration is 7 hours, and cannot be set to be less than 7 hours);
3. During non-energy-saving charging periods, the robot will be charged to a minimum of 30% and then maintained at 30%;
