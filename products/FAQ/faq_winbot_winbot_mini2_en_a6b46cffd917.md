# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Alert: Drive wheel stuck How to deal with it?

## Question

Alert: Drive wheel stuck
 How to deal with it?

## Answer

1. Use the App to remote control the WINBOT to a reachable position.
 2. Grip the handle and hold the Start button for 2 seconds. Remove the WINBOT once the fan stops.
 3. Check if any foreign objects are jammed in the driving wheel tracks. If so, clear them with a tool, then restart the WINBOT to continue cleaning.
