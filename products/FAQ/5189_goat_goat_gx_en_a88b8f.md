# The generated map outline is not consistent with outline I actually create remotely, and part of the boundary is missing.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

The generated map outline is not consistent with outline I actually create remotely, and part of the boundary is missing.

## 答案 / Answer

G00055

Mapping

Please confirm whether the missing part is an extremely narrow area. Areas that are too narrow are sometimes not recognized due to positioning errors. Try to create no-go zones in areas where the boundary disappears
