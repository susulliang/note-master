# 冬季需要将基站、RTK、GOAT 都移至室内吗？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

冬季需要将基站、RTK、GOAT 都移至室内吗？

## 答案 / Answer

8- 保养维护

为了避免设备受冻损坏，冬季停用 GOAT 时建议这样操作：
基站：整体搬到室内（防止信号线、电子元件冻坏）；可在原来安装的位置做个标记，开春装回去时对准原位，避免定位出问题。
RTK：把基站拆下来拿回室内，支撑杆可以留在室外；下次用的时候，确保支撑杆和原来位置一致，不然定位信号容易错位。
GOAT：直接放到室内存放，放之前记得充满电、关机

To prevent freezing damage, follow these steps when decommissioning GOAT during winter:
 Station: Move the entire unit indoors (to protect signal cables and electronic components from freezing). Mark the original installation location for precise reinstallation in spring to avoid positioning errors.
 RTK: Dismantle the station and bring it indoors; the support pole may remain outdoors. When reinstalling, ensure the support pole aligns with its original position to prevent signal misalignment.
 GOAT: Store it directly indoors. Before storing, ensure it is Fully Charged and powered off.
