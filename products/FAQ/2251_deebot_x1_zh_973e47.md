# Water accumulated/overflowed/leaked in the cleaning tank

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Water accumulated/overflowed/leaked in the cleaning tank

## 答案 / Answer

Water accumulated/overflowed/leaked in the cleaning tank

1. Please check whether the snap-in position of the waste water bucket is fastened, and press the sealing plug downward to ensure it is installed properly.
2. Please press and hold the "Start/Pause" button on the base station for about 3 seconds and end the current task after hearing the voice prompt.
3. Short press the [Add/Pump water in the cleaning tank] button on the base station, and after hearing the voice prompt, short press the [Add/Pump water in the cleaning tank] button again to confirm whether the waste water can be recycled normally.
Waste water can be recycled normally, please wipe the water level sensor on both sides with a dry cloth (Below Picture), shut down the robot and restart it, and then use and observe it.
If the waste water cannot be recycled, please check the waste water recycling port (Below picture) to see whether it is blocked by foreign objects. If it is, please clean it up. If not, it is recommended to follow the procedure.
