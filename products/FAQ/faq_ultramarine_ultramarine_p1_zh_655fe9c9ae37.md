# ULTRAMARINE / ULTRAMARINE P1 / ZH - What should I do if the pool robot sinks to the bottom but fails to work, with water discharging from the impeller port and the blue light flashing continuously?

## Question

What should I do if the pool robot sinks to the bottom but fails to work, with water discharging from the impeller port and the blue light flashing continuously?

## Answer

1. Take the robot completely out of the water and place it back into the pool gently.
2. Wait until the robot fully sinks to the bottom, and check whether the blue light turns steady on and works normally.
3. If the blue light still flashes and the robot does not work properly, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
