Clean dirty water  tank and clean water tank guide

1. Remove the dirty water tank, open the cover, and pour out the dirty water. (If dirt remains at the bottom, you can use a cleaning brush to assist in cleaning it thoroughly.)
2. Remove the clean water tank, open the cover, take out and clean the cotton filter , then put it back in place and make sure the water tank is closed properly.
