# 1.Take out the clean water tank, add water up to the MAX line, then close it and put it back to the station.
2.Remove the dust collection cabin baffle, take out the cleaning solution unit.
3.Open it and add ECOVACS cleaning  solution. Close it and reinstall it. Make sure you hear the "click" sound to ensure that it is installed in place.
4.Then close the dust cabin baffle.
5.Install the roller mop, then double click Auto button to retract.
6.Turn on the robot, place it in front of the station, long press the power button to make the robot  automatically return for charging.
7. Short press the power button to start cleaning task.
