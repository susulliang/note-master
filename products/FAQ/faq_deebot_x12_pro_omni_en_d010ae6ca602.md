# How do I set up a cleaning schedule?

## Question

How do I set up a cleaning schedule?

## Answer

1. Use the app to schedule whole-house cleaning, custom cleaning, area cleaning, or whole-house patrol tasks.
2. Click ‘Schedule’ to add a new task.
3. You can select the frequency of repetition by setting the ‘Repeat’ frequency.
