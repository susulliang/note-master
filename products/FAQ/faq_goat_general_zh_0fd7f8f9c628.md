# 我的花园中WiFi信号较差，是否影响建图？

## Question

我的花园中WiFi信号较差，是否影响建图？

## Answer

During the mapping process, the GOAT and the App are only connected via Bluetooth. The strength of Wi-Fi will not affect the mapping process.
