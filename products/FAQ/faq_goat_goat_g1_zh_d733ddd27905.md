# Can the GOAT mow at night?

## Question

Can the GOAT mow at night?

## Answer

If you turn off the animal protection mode in the settings, the GOAT will work around the clock. However, the GOAT is not equipped with night vision and due to weak vision localization ability at night, it is unable to recognize small animals. Therefore, it is recommended that mowing tasks not be initiated after dark.
