# How to deal with the Robot alarm "Difficulty in running, please check the drive wheels"?

## Question

How to deal with the Robot alarm "Difficulty in running, please check the drive wheels"?

## Answer

Warm Reminder：
During use, if you encounter a prompt operation difficulty or slip, etc. You can follow the following steps to try:
1.Please turn the robot over;
2.Press and turn the drive wheel to clear tangles;
3.Use a semi-dry cloth to wipe or use a brush to remove surface stains.
