# 为什么泳池机器人的清洁效果不佳？

> **产品线**：ULTRAMARINE　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么泳池机器人的清洁效果不佳？

## 答案 / Answer

当你发现机器人的清洁效果不佳时，可能是由于以下原因：                                                                        
1.过滤篮可能被堵塞。建议每次使用后清洗它们，以防止杂物堵塞您的设备，从而影响性能，必要时需更换新过滤篮。
 1）清洁滤篮：
  a.按压机器前方按钮，打开机器滤篮仓盖，取出滤篮组件；
  b.清空滤篮里的碎屑；
  c.如果安装了3μm过滤网，请将其拆除，然后分别冲洗内外过滤篮；
  d.清洗完毕后，务必晾干滤网和滤篮，然后将其放回泳池机器人中；
2.叶轮可能存在堵塞情况，观察机器人是否在水面上产生气泡或波浪，请检查并清洗叶轮，在检查清洗叶轮前，请先关闭机器
 1）清洗叶轮，打开过滤篮面盖，并取出过滤篮，找到桨叶罩对应卡扣，从两侧挤压取出桨叶罩，观察叶轮是否被异物堵塞，并使用工具取下叶轮，清洗完异物后，将叶轮及桨叶罩安装到位，安装过滤篮并扣紧过滤篮面盖
3.若叶轮中没有发现杂物，请尝试使用工具旋转叶轮，查看是否无法旋转。如果是，在这种情况下，或因密封舱内的水泄漏损坏了泵电机，请联系客服寻求支持与帮助。
4.滚刷或存在老化或损坏情况，请检查并更换滚刷
 1）更换滚刷：
  a.将机器关机
  b.找到机器滚刷的卡扣，一一解开，取下滚刷；
  c.取出新的滚筒刷，将卡扣与槽对齐，然后将滚筒刷固定在滚筒上。重复相同的步骤更换另一个滚筒刷。
5.请查看机器电量是否过低导致清洁效率及效果下降

When you notice that the robot's cleaning performance is not satisfactory, it may be due to the following reasons:
1.The filter basket may be clogged. It is recommended to clean it after each use to prevent debris from blocking the device and affecting performance. Replace the filter basket with a new one if necessary.
 1).To clean the filter basket:
  a.Press the button on the front of the robot to open the filter basket cover and remove the filter basket assembly.
  b.Empty the debris from the filter basket. 
  c.If the 3 μm filter is installed, remove it, then thoroughly rinse the inner and outer filter baskets separaterly.
  d.After cleaning, make sure to air-dry the filter mesh and filter basket before placing them back into the pool robot.
2.The impeller may be blocked. Put the robot into the water, observe whether bubbles and ripples will form on the water surface. If not, please check and clean the impeller. Before checking and cleaning the impeller, turn off the robot.
 1).To clean the impeller, open the filter basket cover and remove the filter basket. Locate the snap fasteners on the impeller cover, pinch both sides to remove it, and check if the impeller is blocked by debris. Use a tool to remove the impeller. After cleaning out any debris, reinstall the impeller and its cover securely. Reinstall the filter basket and close the cover tightly.
3.If no debris is found in the impeller, try using a tool to rotate the impeller to see if it cannot turn. If it is stuck, or if the pump motor has been damaged due to water leakage in the Waterproof Chamber Assembly, please contact customer service for support and assistance.
4.The brush roller may be aging or damaged. Please inspect and replace the brush roller.
 1).To replace the brush roller:
  a.Turn off the robot.
  b.Locate the silicone clips on the brush roller, release them one by one, and remove the brush roller.
  c.Take the new roller brush and align the clips with the slots, then secure the roller brush onto the roller. Repeat the same steps to replace the other roller brush.
5.Please check if the robot's battery level is too low, which can reduce cleaning efficiency and effectiveness.
