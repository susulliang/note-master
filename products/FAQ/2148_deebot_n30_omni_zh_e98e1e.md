How to turn on Child Lock?

1.Long press the spot cleaning button until the robot says 'Child Lock is on'.                              2.Or Turn it on via APP. Enter App-Click Robot Controls-Click Settings-Click Robot Advanced Settings-Child Lock                                                                                                                                            Note: Once Child lock is on, all robot buttons are disabled.
