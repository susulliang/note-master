# 地图

## Question

地图

## Answer

During remote mapping, GOAT will enable AI function to recognize boundaries. When remote GOAT is near clear boundaries, you can enable automatic mapping. If AI finds that it cannot recognize clear grass-non-grass boundaries, it will stop exploring the map.
