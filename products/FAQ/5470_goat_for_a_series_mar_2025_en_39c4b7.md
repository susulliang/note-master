# 自动建图过程中蓝牙断掉或退出了APP会影响建图吗？ / Will the automatic mapping be affected if the Bluetooth is disconnected or the APP is exited during the automatic mapping process?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

自动建图过程中蓝牙断掉或退出了APP会影响建图吗？ / Will the automatic mapping be affected if the Bluetooth is disconnected or the APP is exited during the automatic mapping process?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

不会，在自动建图环节可以不需要蓝牙连接。
但是建议跟随GOAT保持蓝牙连接，以免随时需要切换到遥控模式。

No.
Bluetooth connection is not required during the automatic mapping process.
But it is recommended to follow GOAT and keep Bluetooth connected, in case you need to switch into remote control mode.
