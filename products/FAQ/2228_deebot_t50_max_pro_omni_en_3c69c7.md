# V型梳齿拆卸清理及安装 / Disassembly, cleaning and installation guide of V-shaped comb teeth

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

V型梳齿拆卸清理及安装 / Disassembly, cleaning and installation guide of V-shaped comb teeth

## 答案 / Answer

1.把机器翻转后，底部朝上放置；
2.同时往中间按住滚刷盖板上两个卡扣，取下滚刷盖板，取出滚刷组件；
3.取下V型梳齿，清洗晾干后清理（温馨提示：可用水冲洗，冲洗后请务必晾干使用）
4.重新装回V型梳齿
5.装回滚刷后；拨动滚刷确认滚刷转动顺畅；
6.将滚刷盖板对准机器卡槽，稍用力往下压，听到“咔”一声表示安装到位。

1.Please turn the robot over;
2.Press the two buckles towards the middle, remove the cover, take out the main brush;
3.Remove the V-shaped comb teeth, clean up after washing and drying(Tips: Can rinse it with water, please make sure to dry it after rinsing);
4.Reinstall the V-shaped comb teeth;
5.Reinstall the main brush, move the main brush to make sure it rotates smoothly.
6.Align the cover with the mounting slot, then press it down with a little force, when you hear the "click" sound, the installation is in place.
