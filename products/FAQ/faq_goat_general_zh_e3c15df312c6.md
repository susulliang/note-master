# 如果日常使用中有RTK基站丢失或者坏了，还能正常使用GOAT吗？

## Question

如果日常使用中有RTK基站丢失或者坏了，还能正常使用GOAT吗？

## Answer

The GOAT cannot be used if the RTK reference station is lost or broken.
GOAT will not receive the positioning signal, then it cannot working when it detects the signal loss.
