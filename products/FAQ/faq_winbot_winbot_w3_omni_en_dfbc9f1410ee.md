# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to Install/Remove the wiping pad assembly?

## Question

How to Install/Remove the wiping pad assembly?

## Answer

Installation Steps:
1.Hold the wiping pad plate and identify its narrow side and wide side. Align the plate precisely with the mounting position on the machine, ensuring the correct orientation to avoid incorrect installation.
2.Once all four mounting points are aligned, gently press down on the plate with your hand. When you hear a "click", it indicates the plate is securely installed.
Removal Steps:
1.Turn Winbot upside down. Gently pinch the pull tabs on both sides of the wiping pad plate with both hands and slowly lift it upward to detach the plate.
