# Can I place the navigation beacon against a wall?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I place the navigation beacon against a wall?

## 答案 / Answer

G00025

Disable

Navigation Beacon

The navigation beacon may be placed against a wall if it is non-metallic and there are no plants climbing over it. Otherwise, please ensure a 20 cm distance between the beacon and the wall.
