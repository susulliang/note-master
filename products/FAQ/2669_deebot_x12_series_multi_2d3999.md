# 25

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

25

## 答案 / Answer

1.Open the APP, tap on "Map" - tap on "Threshold" - add a "Threshold" at the door threshold location.
2.Slightly lift the side of the robot where the roller mop is installed, release the blue buckle on the water tray, and remove the water tray.
3.Remove the robot's top cover, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, and flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
4.First, clean any dust or dirt from the surface of the drive wheel. Also, check if there is any debris stuck near the blue auxiliary wheel; if so, clean it promptly.
5.Press the trigger button located above the drive wheel and slowly turn the drive wheel clockwise. Observe if the blue auxiliary wheel turns simultaneously. If the auxiliary wheel turns normally, continue using the robot and monitor its operation. If it does not turn normally, please contact after-sales support for assistance.
