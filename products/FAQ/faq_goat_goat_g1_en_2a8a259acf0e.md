# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - My garden has a poor Wi-Fi signal. Will this affect mapping?

## Question

My garden has a poor Wi-Fi signal. Will this affect mapping?

## Answer

During the mapping process, the GOAT and the App are only connected via Bluetooth. The strength of Wi-Fi will not affect the mapping process.
