# Installation and Use Guidance

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Installation and Use Guidance

## 答案 / Answer

Preparation:
 1. Open the box, take out the product, accessories, and manual. Read the manual carefully before use.
 2. Dampen the wiping pad and wring it out until it stops dripping.
 3. Align the wide and narrow edges. Stick the wiping pad onto the velcro and press to secure it.
 4. Open the rubber plug and fill the tank with up to 50ml of tap water or ECOVACS WINBOT cleaning solution. Close tightly.
 Kind Reminder：
 - Do not add excessive cleaning solution to overflow the water tank.
 - Do not use purified water or filtered water.
 - The ECOVACS WINBOT cleaning solution is recommended; others may reduce cleaning performance or damage the product. 
 - If the ECOVACS WINBOT cleaning solution runs out, tap water can be used temporarily.
 5. Secure the safety rope to a stable object. Lock the carabiner to prevent accidents.
 6. Connect the power cord to the adapter and plug it in. 
 
 Operating WINBOT：
 Important Tips:
 - Fully charge before use (approximately 3 hours). A solid blue light indicates full power.
 - When cleaning frameless glass, ensure the adhering position is at least 10cm away from the edges.
 - Do not start the WINBOT near window handles.
 - Only for flat glass ≥3mm thick.
 - Once powered on, keep the fan away from hair or small debris to avoid blockage.
 - Inspect the attachment area and clear any particles before adhering to avoid scratching the glass. 
 - Dry the spray nozzles before starting.
 1. Hold Start Button for 2 seconds.Once the fan starts, place WINBOT on the glass, and then automatically adsorb and start the cleaning.
 2. Short press the button or use the App to pause and resume cleaning. 
 3. After cleaning is complete, the WINBOT will return to near the starting position. Hold the handle tightly and long press the Start Button until the fan stops working,then take down the WINBOT.
 4. If you no longer use it, please pour out the cleaning solution and remove the wiping pad in time.
