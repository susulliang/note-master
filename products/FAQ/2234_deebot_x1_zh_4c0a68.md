# Firmware cannot be upgraded.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Firmware cannot be upgraded.

## 答案 / Answer

Firmware cannot be upgraded.

1. Check whether DEEBOT is charging.
(Open the app to confirm that there is a lightning symbol near the battery icon or the robot’s AUTO button flashes white light, indicating that the robot is charging.)
2. Check DEEBOT electricity. When the battery is low or no power, the firmware cannot be upgraded. It is recommended to upgrade after charging for 3 hours.

Verbal trick guidance can solve the problem.
