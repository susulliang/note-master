# Can the GOAT recognize humans or small animals at night when AI recognition is enabled?

## Question

Can the GOAT recognize humans or small animals at night when AI recognition is enabled?

## Answer

No. The GOAT is not equipped with night vision and is unable to carry out AI recognition after dark.
