# 新增区域与已有区域重叠怎么办？ / Can a new mowing area overlaps with an existing mowing area?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

新增区域与已有区域重叠怎么办？ / Can a new mowing area overlaps with an existing mowing area?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

如果新增区域与已有区域重叠，则新增区域并入原有区域生成一个更大的区域。

The new area will be merged into the existing region to be a whole area.
