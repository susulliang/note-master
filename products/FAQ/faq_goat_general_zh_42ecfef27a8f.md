# GOAT 为何未按预约时间自动执行割草工作？

## Question

GOAT 为何未按预约时间自动执行割草工作？

## Answer

Possible scenarios causing this issue:
 1. GOAT was not powered on
 2. GOAT is in animal protection mode and ignores scheduled tasks
 3. GOAT is in rain protection mode and ignores scheduled tasks
 4. GOAT has insufficient battery power to operate
 5. GOAT is performing another manually triggered temporary task
 6. Scheduled settings may be incorrectly configured or saved; check in the app
 7. Verify in the app that GOAT's time zone setting matches its current location
