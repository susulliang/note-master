# DEEBOT / ECOVACS X9 PRO OMNI&YEEDI S16 PLUS 中英文版 / EN - ALERT: Dirty Water Box Abnormal

## Question

ALERT: Dirty Water Box Abnormal

## Answer

1.Remove the dirty water box, empty it, and clean the filter. Reinstall the lid firmly to ensure a tight seal.
2.Press the drain port to check for blockages. If the tank has water, pressing it normally should allow water to flow out. Clean the port with a brush.
3.Press the charging contacts on the base’s right side for 1-2 seconds, release and check if the drain rod moves up/down smoothly.
