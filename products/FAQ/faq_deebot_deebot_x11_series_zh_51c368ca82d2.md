# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 机器人连接网络指导

## Question

机器人连接网络指导

## Answer

Download and Log In
 1.Place the robot near station and scan the QR code on the robot with your phone.
 (Tip: You can also download the ECOVACS HOME app from the App Store.)
 2.Install the app, register, and log in to the main interface.
 Wi-Fi Setup
 1.Scan the robot's QR code, enter your Wi-Fi password, and click to connect.
 2.Follow the app prompts: Open the robot's top cover, switch the power to "I," and wait for the sound. Select "Next," press the network button briefly, confirm you hear "Bluetooth hotspot released," then select "Next" to start the hotspot connection. Wait for network access.
 3.Once connected, you can name your DEEBOT.                                                                                                             Friendly Reminder：Please perform network configuration in a location with strong WIFI signal coverage.
