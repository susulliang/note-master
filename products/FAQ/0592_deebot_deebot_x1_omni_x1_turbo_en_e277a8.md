# While DEEBOT is cleaning, why does it become louder when moving to the carpet?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

While DEEBOT is cleaning, why does it become louder when moving to the carpet?

## 答案 / Answer

D20036

Cleaning

Once you have enabled Auto-Boost Suction in the App, DEEBOT will automatically increase its suction power in carpeted areas to give the carpet a deeper cleaning.
