# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 如何清理污水箱及清水箱？

## Question

如何清理污水箱及清水箱？

## Answer

1. Remove the dirty water tank and open the lid to pour out wastewater.
 (Use the cleaning brush for stubborn stains.)
 2. Refill the clean water tank to the max line, close the lid, and reinstall both tanks.                                   Note: After cleaning the dirty water tank, close the lid tightly.
