# 摄像头是否会侵犯用户隐私？

## Question

摄像头是否会侵犯用户隐私？

## Answer

GOAT's camera is primarily used for obstacle recognition and lawn boundary detection during mow. Rest assured, GOAT does not infringe on your privacy—we always prioritize consumer privacy rights. All human images are blurred and pixelated.
