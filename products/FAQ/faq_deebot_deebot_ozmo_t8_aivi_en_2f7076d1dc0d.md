# DEEBOT did not detect a drop off, like stairs. What should I do?

## Question

DEEBOT did not detect a drop off, like stairs.  What should I do?

## Answer

The anti-Drop Sensors may be too dirty. Wipe the Anti-Drop Sensors with a dry mop to maintain their effectiveness.
