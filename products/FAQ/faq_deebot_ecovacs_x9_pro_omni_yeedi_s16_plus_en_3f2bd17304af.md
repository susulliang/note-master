# DEEBOT / ECOVACS X9 PRO OMNI&YEEDI S16 PLUS 中英文版 / EN - ALERT: Laser Sensor Abnormal

## Question

ALERT: Laser Sensor Abnormal

## Answer

1.Gently rotate the laser unit to face the radar lens forward. Wipe the lens with a soft brush or clean cloth.
2.Power off the robot, wait a few seconds, then turn it back on.
