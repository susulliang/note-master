# Why does the firmware upgrade have a low speed or even fail?

## Question

Why does the firmware upgrade have a low speed or even fail?

## Answer

Firmware upgrades require a Wi-Fi connection. If the upgrade is very slow or fails, you can move the GOAT to an indoor location with good Wi-Fi coverage and try upgrading again
