# 尘盒拆卸清理安装指导 / Disassembly, clean and installation guide of Dust bin

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

尘盒拆卸清理安装指导 / Disassembly, clean and installation guide of Dust bin

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
当您的机器人使用一段时间后，若您发现尘盒已满或吸尘效果不理想时等，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
1.取下主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“O”关机，按下卡扣以释放并取出尘盒
2.打开尘盒盖，倾倒垃圾。
3.打开初级滤网后，取下滤芯（温馨提示：若尘盒较脏，可用水冲洗尘盒、滤芯）
4.清理完成后，重新安装滤芯，
5.将尘盒装入主机，听到“咔哒”的声音表示安装到位，装回主机面盖
注：若用水清理后请甩干水珠,需放置通风处晾干后再使用

Warm Reminder：
When your robot has been used for a period of time, if you find that the dust bin is full or the vacuuming effect is not satisfactory, etc., you can follow the following steps to try:
1.Remove the top cover, turn off the robot, press the catch to remove the dust bin;
2.Open the dust bin and empty it;
3.Remove the filter (Prompt：If the dust box is dirty, use water to rinse the dust bin, filter)
4.After cleaning, reinstall the filter assembly.
5.Put the dust bin into the Robot, When you hear a "click", it indicates that it is properly installed.
Note: If cleaned with water, please shake off the water droplets and need to be placed in a well-ventilated area to dry before use.
