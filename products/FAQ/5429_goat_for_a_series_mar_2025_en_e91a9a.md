# 必需要用地钉固定基站吗？ / Do you have to nail down the docking station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

必需要用地钉固定基站吗？ / Do you have to nail down the docking station?

## 答案 / Answer

Charging Station

如不用地钉固定基站可能导致基站移动，基站位置的移动将会造成定位信号的偏移，当移动距离超过10cm时需要您重新创建地图。且长期不对基站进行固定可能导致底板变形，影响回充

The docking station might move if it is not nailed to the ground. The movement of the station will cause the positioning signal to shift and you will need to recreate the map if the moved distance exceeds 10 cm. Moreover, failure to secure the docking station for an extended period of time may cause the bottom plate to deform and affect its ability to recharge.
