## 问题 / Question

40

## 答案 / Answer

There are two primary reasons for dampness at the station bottorn, along with corresponding solutions:
1. Frequent cleaning of the station bottom, combined with the DEEBOT pad leaving the station with high moisture content, may cause minor water seepage into the station bottom.
We recommend periodically moving the station to clean accumulated water from the bottom, or purchasing a dedicated Ecovacs water-absorbing mat to reduce residual moisture.
2. The base may not be fully installed, with latches not securely locked, potentially causing poor sealing and moisture leakage.
We recommend checking and confirming that all base latches are properhy engaged to ensure the base fits tightly against the station.
