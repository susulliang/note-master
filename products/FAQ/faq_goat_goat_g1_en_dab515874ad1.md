# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Is the use of the robot affected if the navigation beacon is crooked (not placed upright)?

## Question

Is the use of the robot affected if the navigation beacon is crooked (not placed upright)?

## Answer

The angle at which the navigation beacon is tilted should not be greater than 10°. The spirit level on a phone may be used to check this. A tilted angle greater than 10° will result in a misalignment. It is recommended that the navigation beacon is checked regularly for any signs of tipping over and realigned if necessary.
