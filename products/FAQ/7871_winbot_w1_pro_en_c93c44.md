# Will the suction power decrease when the robot is unplugged?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Will the suction power decrease when the robot is unplugged?

## 答案 / Answer

W00097

Product feature

The mentioned suction power refers to the value in normal circumstances and under proper working conditions. 
When the robot is powered off, the suction power can only hold the robot itself and cannot bear any loads. Please operate as per the robot's instruction manual.
