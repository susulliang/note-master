# Why can't I edit a wall?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why can't I edit a wall?

## 答案 / Answer

D20151-10

3D Map

(1) Editing of internal walls is not supported at this time. 
(2) To avoid affecting DEEBOT's work, it is not possible to edit walls that affect the house type.
