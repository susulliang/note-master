# 我的花园中有路应该如何建图？

## Question

我的花园中有路应该如何建图？

## Answer

If your road surface is flat and flush with the surrounding area, you can include the road surface directly in the map. If your road is higher or lower than the surrounding area by over 3 cm and divides the lawn into two areas, you will need to create a man-made access wider than 2 m to connect the two areas. When mapping, you need to map only along the edge of the created access, and delimit other road areas
