# Does WINBOT W1/W1 PRO have an increase in walking speed compared to W920?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Does WINBOT W1/W1 PRO have an increase in walking speed compared to W920?

## 答案 / Answer

W00090

Product feature

W1/W1 PRO can complete a fast cleaning in approximately 2-3 minutes and a deep cleaning in approximately 4-6 minutes, which is equivalent to W920 (based on the cleaning of 1m*1m glass in the Ecovacs lab).
