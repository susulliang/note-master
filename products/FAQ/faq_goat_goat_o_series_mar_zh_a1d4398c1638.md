# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 新增区域时覆盖了原来创建的通道，通道会保留吗？

## Question

新增区域时覆盖了原来创建的通道，通道会保留吗？

## Answer

No, there will be no paths in the middle of the lawn. Depending on the new area, the parts of the lawn that connect to the outside will be retained as much as possible.
