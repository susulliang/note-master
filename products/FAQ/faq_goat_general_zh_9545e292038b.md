# GOAT电量充满后为何未自动继续未完成的任务？

## Question

GOAT电量充满后为何未自动继续未完成的任务？

## Answer

1. Ensure GOAT is not reset or powered off during operation
 2. Verify GOAT charges properly; it will resume tasks after full charge
 3. Check for malfunctions; manually restart tasks after resolving issues
