## 答案 / Answer

How to deal with abnormal situations (skewed, overlapping) on the map in the APP?

You can use the "Restore Map" function in the APP map management to restore the initially saved map and continue to use it. The restored map will lose the reservation, manual partition, virtual wall and other information saved in the current map, and needs to be reset.
If the initially saved map does not meet the needs, you can delete the map and create a new one.
