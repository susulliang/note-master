# What is the spraying method of the WINBOT mini 2?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the spraying method of the WINBOT mini 2?

## 答案 / Answer

The mini 2 uses ultrasonic spraying. It is normal for a small amount of water mist to be produced during operation.
