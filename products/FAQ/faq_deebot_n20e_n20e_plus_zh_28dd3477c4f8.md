# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 主机建图不完整/没有生成分区,怎么办? The mapping is incomplete/no partition is generated, what should I do?

## Question

主机建图不完整/没有生成分区,怎么办?                                       The mapping is incomplete/no partition is generated, what should I do?

## Answer

（1）主机建图清扫时尽量把房门打开到最大，避免在建图过程中出现遗漏房间；
（2）建图时建议清理下地面杂物，确保机器人可通行至各个区域；
（3）建议在APP中删除地图进行重新建图；                                                                                                                                                （1）Try to open the door as much as possible to avoid missing rooms during the mapping process.
（2）It is recommended to clean up ground debris during mapping to ensure that the robot can travel to various areas.
（3）Suggest deleting the map in the app and creating a new one.
