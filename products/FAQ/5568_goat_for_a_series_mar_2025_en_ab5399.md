# 可以用洗涤剂进行清洁吗？ / Can I clean the mower with cleaning solution?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

可以用洗涤剂进行清洁吗？ / Can I clean the mower with cleaning solution?

## 答案 / Answer

Maintain

避免使用清洁喷剂或洗涤剂，以免损坏机身

Avoid using cleaning sprays or cleaning solution, to prevent damage to the mower.
