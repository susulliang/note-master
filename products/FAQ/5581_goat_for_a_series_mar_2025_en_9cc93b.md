# 4G  Cellullar Module和SIM卡的关系？ / What is the relationship between 4G Cellullar Module  and SIM cards?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

4G  Cellullar Module和SIM卡的关系？ / What is the relationship between 4G Cellullar Module  and SIM cards?

## 答案 / Answer

4G  Cellullar Module

1.4G Cellullar Module是ECOVACS提供的网络模块，如果您家无Wi Fi或者Wi Fi信号不好，可以通过4G Cellullar Module实现远程控制。
2.4G  Cellullar Module需要自购SIM卡激活后插入4G  Cellullar Module使用SIM卡

1.4G Cellullar Module is a kind of remote sollution provided by ECOVACS that is built into the Cellullar Module. If your home has no Wi-Fi or the Wi-Fi signal is poor, you can achieve remote control from 4G Cellullar Module . 
2.You must buy a SIM card and insert the Cellullar Module after activation it.
