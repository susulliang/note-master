# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 TURBO / EN - Why does the base of the docking station get wet?

## Question

Why does the base of the docking station get wet?

## Answer

When DEEBOT's mopping pads moves out of the docking station, it contains more water and some may wet the base of the docking station. It is recommended to move the docking station for a while to clean the base or to purchase a special ECOVACS suction pad to solve the problem completely.
