# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Do I need to move the GOAT indoors in winter?

## Question

Do I need to move the GOAT indoors in winter?

## Answer

We recommend that you move the GOAT indoors for better storage in winter when it is not in use. Before storage, please give the GOAT a full charge and shut it down.
