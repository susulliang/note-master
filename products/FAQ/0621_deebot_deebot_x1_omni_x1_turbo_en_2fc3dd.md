# Can I disable DEEBOT X1's obstacle detection function and how do I do it?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I disable DEEBOT X1's obstacle detection function and how do I do it?

## 答案 / Answer

D20267

Intelligence

(1) After completing network setup for your DEEBOT, open the App and go to Settings ＞ Smart Cleaning Settings ＞ AIVI 3D to disable. 
(2) When the AIVI 3D Smart Detection function is disabled, DEEBOT will not avoid low objects or detect steps lower than 2–6 cm when cleaning. When it encounters obstacles during cleaning, DEEBOT will slow down and bump into the obstacle gently at low speed, and use the DTOF sensor to avoid obstacles of about the same height as the robot.
