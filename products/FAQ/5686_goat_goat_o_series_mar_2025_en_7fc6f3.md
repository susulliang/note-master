# 新增区域时覆盖了原来创建的通道，通道会保留吗？ / When a new area is added, it overwrites the previously created path. Will the path be retained?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

新增区域时覆盖了原来创建的通道，通道会保留吗？ / When a new area is added, it overwrites the previously created path. Will the path be retained?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

不会，草坪中间不会有通道存在，会根据新增区域的情况，尽量保留草坪外区域联通的部分。

No, there will be no paths in the middle of the lawn. Depending on the new area, the parts of the lawn that connect to the outside will be retained as much as possible.
