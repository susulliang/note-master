# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Why can sounds be heard from the docking station?

## Question

Why can sounds be heard from the docking station?

## Answer

The docking station is installed with a motor to drive the GOAT's lens cleaning brush. It is normal for the docking station to make some noise when the lens is being cleaned.
