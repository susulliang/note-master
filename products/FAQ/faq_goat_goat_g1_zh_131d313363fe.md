# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - How do I change winter and daylight saving time?

## Question

How do I change winter and daylight saving time?

## Answer

(1) You can select your time zone in Settings > More Settings > Robot Info > Time Zone
 (2) You can also set the time zone for your phone system to match the time zone where you live, then go to Settings > More Settings > Robot Info > Time Zone in the ECOVACS HOME App and sync up with your phone.
 (3) When the time zone is modified, changes to times are synchronized to schedules and logs.
