# 冬季需要将RTK基站拿回室内吗？ / Do I need to move the RTK Reference Station indoors in winter?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

冬季需要将RTK基站拿回室内吗？ / Do I need to move the RTK Reference Station indoors in winter?

## 答案 / Answer

Maintain

建议在您不使用GOAT时将RTK基站部分取下放入室内存储，支撑杆部分可以留在室外。但是再次使用时请注意支撑杆位置与原来保持一致，否则会造成定位信号的错位

We recommend that you move the signal transmitting part of the RTK indoors for better storage in winter when the GOAT is not in use. The supporting rods can be left outdoors. However, please be sure to match each signal transmitting part with its corresponding supporting rod before using the robot again. Not doing so may lead to positioning errors.
