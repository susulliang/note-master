# 遥控建图-我可以完全贴着花园或草坪边界进行遥控吗？ / Can I remotely control it completely against the boundaries of the garden or lawn?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

遥控建图-我可以完全贴着花园或草坪边界进行遥控吗？ / Can I remotely control it completely against the boundaries of the garden or lawn?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

由于GOAT的定位存在一定的误差，建议您遥控时与落差大于4cm的边界保持10cm以上的安全距离，个别危险区域如：泳池、悬崖等，建议与其边界保持25cm以上的安全距离，避免GOAT定位偏差导致跌落或卡困

Due to the limited positioning accuracy of GOAT, it is recommended that you maintain a safe distance of at least 10cm from the boundary with a drop greater than 4cm when remotely controlling. For some dangerous areas such as swimming pools, cliffs, etc., it is recommended to maintain a safe distance of at least 25cm from the boundary to avoid GOAT positioning deviation causing falls or getting stuck
