# 45

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

45

## 答案 / Answer

1.Please confirm that the "AI Stain Detection" and "Auto Stain Spray" functions are turned on.
2.Check if the water spray nozzle is blocked by debris. If so, please clean it.
Note:
1.Environmental Impact: When there are obstacles near a stain or if there is a carpet on the floor, the robot will automatically pause its water spray cleaning for that area to protect objects and prevent water stains from spreading.
2.Type of Stain: The water spray function of the robot is specifically designed for stains like coffee, soy sauce, etc. If the detected stain is not in this category, the water spray function will not be triggered.
3.Station Placement Issue: If the base station is not placed on the same floor where the robot is currently located, the robot will not be able to return to the base station to refill with clean water, which will prevent it from performing the water spray cleaning operation normally.
