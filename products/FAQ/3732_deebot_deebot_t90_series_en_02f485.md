# 机器人充电及谷电充电说明

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人充电及谷电充电说明

## 答案 / Answer

1.取下主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“I”开机，等待开机提示音结束即可。。
 2. 将主机放置基站正前方；长按下“auto”键，让机器人自动返回基站，听到语音提示“开始充电”
 二 . 开始谷点充电后的机器人充电说明：
 1.打开App-点击“进入设备”-设置-基站高级设定-谷点充电开关打开。
 谷点充电说明：
 1.开关默认关闭, 开启后,仅在设置的时段内充满电池,其他时段除保证安全电量外不对电池充电
 2.为保证机器人正常运行,非谷点时段,仍可能向电池充电。如有任务未完成,机器人会在非谷点时段充至所需电量,如无任务,机器人会在非谷点时段充电至安全电量。（默认充电时长7小时，不可设置小于7小时时长）
 3.非充电时间段,需要将电池充至最低电量30%,大于等于30%时维持当前电量

1. Remove the front cover of the ROBOT. Slide the Power Switch to "I" to turn on. Wait until the startup chime completes.
 2. Position the ROBOT directly in front of the station. Press and hold the "auto" button to guide the ROBOT back to the station. Listen for the voice prompt "Begin charging."
 II. Off-Peak Charging Instructions:
 1. Open the App → Tap "Enter Device" → Settings → Station Advanced Settings → Enable Off-Peak Charging.
 Off-Peak Charging Notes:
 1. Default setting is off. When enabled, the battery will only charge fully during scheduled time slots. Outside these periods, charging occurs only to maintain a safe battery level.
 2. To ensure normal operation, charging may occur during non-off-peak hours. If tasks remain unfinished, the robot will charge to the required level during non-off-peak hours. If no tasks exist, it will charge to a safe level.(Default charging duration: 7 hours. Duration cannot be set below 7 hours.)
 3. During non-charging periods, the battery must be charged to a minimum of 30% capacity. When the battery level is at or above 30%, it will maintain the current charge level.
