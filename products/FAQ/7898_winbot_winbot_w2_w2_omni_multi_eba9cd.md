# Can I use WINBOT W2 directly on the glass with water?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I use WINBOT W2 directly on the glass with water?

## 答案 / Answer

W00120+

Please try to avoid cleaning the glass immediately after a shower or when there are a lot of water droplets on the surface. It will increase the slippage and cause the falls.
