# How to remove/install the Dust Collection Cabin filter cotton?

## Question

How to remove/install the Dust Collection Cabin filter cotton?

## Answer

Friendly reminder:
If your station emits an unusual odor, try the following steps:
1. Remove the Dust Collection Cabin cover;
2. Remove the dust bag;
3. Slide the filter cotton holder and remove it.
4. Remove the filter cotton and clean it thoroughly.
5. Reinstall the filter cotton into the holder.
6. Reinstall the filter cotton holder.
7. Securely reinstall the dust bag and close the Dust Collection Cabin cover.(Note: If washed, allow to air dry completely before reuse)
