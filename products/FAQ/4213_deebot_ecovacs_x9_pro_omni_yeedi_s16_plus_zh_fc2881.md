# Robotic Crating Guidelines

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Robotic Crating Guidelines

## 答案 / Answer

Warm Reminder:                                                                                                                                                                           Before packing:
Empty all water tanks (robot’s internal tank, station’s water tanks, and cleaning solution container). Air-dry the damp mop roller.                                                                                                                       1. Remove the cover of the Robot, turn the power switch to "O" .
2. Remove the Robot dirty water box and empty it, remove two water tanks and empty them, then put them back.
3. Unplug the power cord from the station and remove the base.
4.Place the station in the box first, cover with protective plastic. 
5. Then carefully set the Robot on top. Add another layer of plastic for cushioning. Put the removed accessories such as power cord and base back into the packing box in order.
