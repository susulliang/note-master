## 答案 / Answer

Disassembly, clean and installation guide of dust bin

1.Please remove the Robot cover and take out the dust bin;
2.Remove the filter and empty the dust bin;
3.Tap the filter gently and clean the dust on both sides;
4. Dust bin and filter can be washed with water, need to be placed in a well-ventilated area to dry before use.                                                                                                                                5.After cleaning, install the filter and put dust bin back in the Robot，make sure that a "click" sound is heard.
