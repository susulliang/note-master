# After beacon installation is complete, and the App detects that the Navigation Beacon communication is abnormal, can the GOAT be used normally?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

After beacon installation is complete, and the App detects that the Navigation Beacon communication is abnormal, can the GOAT be used normally?

## 答案 / Answer

G00034

Navigation Beacon

(1) If the detection result is "Failure," and none of the beacons are communicating properly, you will not be able to use the GOAT for mapping. Follow the prompts in the App to adjust the position of Navigation Beacons. 
 (2) If the detection result is "Medium" or "Poor," it means that there is only no direct communication between the beacons listed on the app. If your garden is a U or O shape, it is normal for the beacons in the back yard to be unable to communicate directly with those in the front yard. You can still map as normal. Alternatively, follow the prompts in the App to adjust the Navigation Beacon position and detect again.
