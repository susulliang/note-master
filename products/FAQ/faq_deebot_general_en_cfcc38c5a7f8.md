# What should I do if the clean water tank isn't dispensing clean water?

## Question

What should I do if the clean water tank isn't dispensing clean water?

## Answer

During mop cleaning and self-cleaning, the station does not dispense clean water.
1. Remove the water tank and clear any obstructions between it and the station. Ensure the water tank is placed level.
2. Verify the clean water tank's sealing plug on the station is properly installed and tightly secured.
3. Open the water tank, remove the filter component, disconnect its attached hose, and clean the filter with a cleaning brush. After cleaning, reconnect the hose, reposition the filter component, and reinstall the water tank (alternatively, rinse the filter with clean water).
Friendly reminder: Cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
