# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T30 PRO OMNI Silver / EN - What should I do if hot water does not come out when cleaning the mops?

## Question

What should I do if hot water does not come out when cleaning the mops?

## Answer

1. Confirm that the main unit has connected  to a network;
2. Open the APP - click "enter" - click "station" - confirm whether hot water washing is turned on;
3. Check the remaining water in the clean water tank. If the water is too low, please add clean water;
Note:
①Hot water washing function is on by default;
②When performing self-cleaning, the water is cold water;
③ Heating of clean water is a dynamic process, and the temperature in the early stage  will be slightly lower;
④When the temperature of the clean water is too high, the machine will automatically stop heating to avoid damage caused by excessive temperature;
⑤Every time the cleaning task is started, cold water is used to moisten the mops. When cleaning, hot water is used when returning to the station to clean the mops;
