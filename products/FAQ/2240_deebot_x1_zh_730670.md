# There is a harsh sound on the robot or mobile phone during the voice call.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

There is a harsh sound on the robot or mobile phone during the voice call.

## 答案 / Answer

There is a harsh sound on the robot or mobile phone during the voice call.

In the voice call, there is a harsh sound on the robot or the mobile phone. During the voice call, there is a whistling when the robot and the mobile phone are in the same room.
When using it, it is recommended to separate the robot and the mobile phone as far as possible from the wall or to communicate at a long distance.
