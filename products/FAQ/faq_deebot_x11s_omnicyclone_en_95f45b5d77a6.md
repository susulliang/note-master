# DEEBOT / X11S Omnicyclone / EN - How to set mop-only mode？

## Question

How to set mop-only mode？

## Answer

Opne APP > Click Enter > Click Zone icon > Click yellow icon, mop only, to start cleaning.
Note: In mop-only mode, only one zone can be drawn, and the area must be less than or equal to 2m*2m.
