# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 .0 19langs / EN - Why does a new machine prompt "Low battery, please charge before use"?

## Question

Why does a new machine prompt "Low battery, please charge before use"?

## Answer

The safety battery ensures the WINBOT can maintain suction for at least 30 minutes during a power outage to prevent falling. Fully charge the battery (approx. 3h) before the first use.
