# 我可以将RTK杆插在植物丛中吗？ / Can I install the RTK station in the plant?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我可以将RTK杆插在植物丛中吗？ / Can I install the RTK station in the plant?

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

RTK基站受高大的植物遮挡，可能无法收到卫星信号，建议基于以下原则，可以根据实际情况判断是否适合。
（1）RTK杆远离墙体放置，大型树木，建议距离＞2m。
（2）RTK基站上方无遮挡物，保证卫星信号接收。
（3）RTK信号头需要竖直向上，不可倾斜，建议不要放在松软地面上，以免角度产生偏移；
（4）安装位置RTK基站信号良好，通电1min后，观察到基站蓝色灯常亮即可

如果草丛低于RTK基站高度，您可以将RTK杆置于其中。请定期清理杂乱的植物以免植物长高或者缠绕了RTK基站杆。

The positioning signal will be blocked by tall plants. It is recommended to install the RTK station location based on the following principles.
(1) Place the RTK station away from the wall, large trees, the recommended distance is ＞2m.
(2) There is no shelter or shadow above the RTK base station.
(3) The RTK reference station should be upright and not tilted. It cannot be placed on soft ground to avoid Angle deviation. 
(4) You can confirm the signal of the RTK station good if the indicator on the RTK is steady on after the power on for 1 minute. 

If the grass is lower than the RTK base station height, you can place the RTK station in it. Please clean up messy plant regulary if the plant is growing tall or winding the RTK station.
