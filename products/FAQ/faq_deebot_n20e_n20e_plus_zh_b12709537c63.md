# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 机器人有时不能避障？Why can't the robots avoid obstacles sometimes?

## Question

机器人有时不能避障？Why can't the robots avoid obstacles sometimes?

## Answer

（1）红外模组可能沾灰，建议用干的轻柔的纸巾擦拭
（2）为了提升清洁覆盖率，偶尔会有轻碰，属于正常现象                                                                                                                           (1) The infrared module may be dusty, it is recommended to wipe it with a dry and gentle tissue
(2) In order to improve the cleaning coverage, occasional light touches are normal
