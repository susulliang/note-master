# 地图上标注的障碍物位置和形状和实际不符？ / Why is the position and shape of an obstacle marked on the map different from real life?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图上标注的障碍物位置和形状和实际不符？ / Why is the position and shape of an obstacle marked on the map different from real life?

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

（1）APP不会实时更新障碍物情况，只有当开启割草任务后APP才会对地图上的障碍物位置进行刷新
（2）地图上标注的障碍物仅为工作中TOF识别到的障碍物，AI识别到的障碍物不会在地图上进行标注
（3）TOF识别到的直径小于40cm的障碍物，地图上不会进行标注
（4）GOAT存在一定的误识别概率，无法完全精准的绘制出障碍物轮廓

(1) The App does not update obstacles and their positions in real time. The position of the obstacles on the map will only be updated by the App when the mowing task has started. 
(2) The obstacles marked on the map are only recognized by the ToF sensor during operation. The obstacles recognized by AI will not be marked on the map. 
(3) Obstacles with a diameter of less than 40 cm that are recognized by the ToF sensor will not be marked on the map. 
(4) The GOAT has a certain probability of misrecognition and is not able to completely and accurately draw the outlines of obstacles.
