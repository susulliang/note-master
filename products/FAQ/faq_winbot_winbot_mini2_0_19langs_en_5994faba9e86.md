# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 .0 19langs / EN - Should I wring out the wiping pad? What is the ideal dampness?

## Question

Should I wring out the wiping pad? What is the ideal dampness?

## Answer

Excessive water on the pad will affect cleaning results, causing streaks or leading to slipping and potential damage. Before use, manually dampen the pad and wring it out until it no longer drips. Attach it flat to the bottom of the WINBOT.
