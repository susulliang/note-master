# Why didn't DEEBOT start cleaning automatically at the scheduled time?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why didn't DEEBOT start cleaning automatically at the scheduled time?

## 答案 / Answer

D20026

Cleaning

(1) DEEBOT will not carry out scheduled cleaning while in Do Not Disturb mode.
(2) DEEBOT is not powered on. 
(3) DEEBOT does not have enough battery power to clean.
(4) If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
