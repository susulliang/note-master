# 如何给机器人充电？ Robot Charging Guide.

## Question

如何给机器人充电？                                                             Robot Charging Guide.

## Answer

1.请将充电座靠墙放置在地面，连接电源线，左右0.5米和前方1.5m范围内不放置物品；                                                                                                                                                                2.长按主机“启动”键开机；
3.将主机放置在集尘座/充电座正前方，短按主机“返回键”让机器自主返回充电，成功对接后机器会提示“开始充电"；                                 1.Connect the power cord, keep the area around the docking station clear of objects ( within 0.5m left and right, 1.5m in front), especially objects with reflective surfaces.                                                                                                                                        2.Long press the power button.                                                                                                                                                                3.Place the Robot in front of the docking station, press the return button to make the Robot automatically return for charging, there will be a voice prompt "start charging".
