# 固件升级流程是否可以中途取消？

## Question

固件升级流程是否可以中途取消？

## Answer

In order to ensure the stability of the OTA upgrade, after entering the upgrade process, the upgrade process cannot be canceled unless the robot's power has been turned off. If the upgrade is not successful within 60 minutes, it will be automatically terminated. It is recommended that the Silent Upgrade function is enabled, so that when a new version is detected, it will automatically upgrade your GOAT.
