# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T30 PRO OMNI Silver / EN - How do I remove/clean the Dust Bin?

## Question

How do I remove/clean the Dust Bin?

## Answer

1. Remove the main unit’s top cover and take out the Dust Bin.
2. Open the dust bin  and empty the contents into the trash.
3. Remove the filter element assembly.
4. Clean the dust from the filter net. (Note: Both inside and outside need to be cleaned)
5. Tap the filter element assembly lightly to clean out the dust then wash the dust bin with water.
6. After the cleaning is completed, re-install the filter elements and then insert them back into the robot.
Note: If cleaned with water, leave it in a ventilated area to dry before use.
