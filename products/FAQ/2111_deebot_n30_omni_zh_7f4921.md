Robot Charging Guide

1.Connect the power cord, keep the area around the station clear of objects ( within 0.05m left and right, 0.8m in front), especially objects with reflective surfaces.                              2.Long press the power button to turn it on.                                                                                                                                   3.Place the Robot in front of the station, press the return button to make the Robot automatically return for charging, there will be a voice prompt "start charging".
