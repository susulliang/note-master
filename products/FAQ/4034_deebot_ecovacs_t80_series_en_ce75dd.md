# No Hot Water from Station

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

No Hot Water from Station

## 答案 / Answer

1.Fill the clean water tank.
2.Open the app ＞ click enter＞ click settings＞station advanced settings＞ wash method, select "Standard/Deep" .
Note: Water heating is dynamic. Initial wash cycles may have slightly lower temperatures.
