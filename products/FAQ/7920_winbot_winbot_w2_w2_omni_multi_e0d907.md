# How many times can a 60ml water tank spray?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How many times can a 60ml water tank spray?

## 答案 / Answer

W00158

Under the laboratory conditions of ECOVACS, using a 1m*1m size glass as the test standard, a full water tank can continuously clean 30 square meters.
