# Guidelines for proper removal of mopping pad plate and mop cleaning

## Question

Guidelines for proper removal of mopping pad plate and mop cleaning

## Answer

Mopping pad plate disassembly and cleaning
1. Remove 2 in 1 module.
2. Lift the mopping pad plate slightly upward, remove the mop pad, and tear the washable mop from the mopping pad plate.
3. Wash the washable mop, then dry it and use it, frequent cleaning is not recommended.

Install the mop and mopping pad plate
1. Stick the mop evenly onto the mopping pad plate (with Velcro side).
2. Align the mopping pad plate with the installation port of 2 in 1 module, press slightly harder to confirm that you hear a click.
