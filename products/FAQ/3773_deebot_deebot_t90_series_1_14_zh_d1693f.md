# 机器人装箱指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人装箱指导

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 在装箱之前，请确保完成以下步骤：
 1.请清空清/污水箱中的水、清洁液盒中的清洁液；打开app设置-设备搬家-点击“清空地宝清水箱”可清空内置小水箱的水，并将潮湿的滚筒拖布晾干（上下水版本还需“清空基站水箱”中的水）
 2.取下主机正面上盖，将电源开关至“O”关机。
 3.拔下基站电源线，取下基站斜坡底座。
 4.先将基站平稳地放入包装箱内。在基站上方放入下纸塑保护层。
 5.轻轻放置主机于下纸塑之上，再放入上纸塑保护层，以保护主机不受损伤。依次将拆下的边刷、清洁液、电源线、基站斜坡底座等配件放回包装箱内。

Friendly Reminder:
 Before packing, ensure the following steps are completed:
 1. Empty water from the clean/dirty water tanks and Cleaning Solution from the Cleaning Solution cartridge. Open the app, Settings ＞ Device Relocation ＞ tap "Empty DEEBOT Water Tank" to drain the internal water tank. Air-dry the damp roller mop. (For models with Auto Refill and Drain Module version, also drain water from the "Station Water Tank").
 2. Remove the front cover of the robot and switch the Power Switch to "O" to turn it off.
 3. Unplug the Power Cord and remove the Base.
 4. Place the station securely into the Packaging first. Place the lower paper/plastic protective layer on top of the station.
 5. Gently place the ROBOT on the lower protective layer, then cover it with the upper protective layer to prevent damage. Return the removed Side Brush, Cleaning Solution, Power Cord, Base, and other accessories to the Packaging in order.
