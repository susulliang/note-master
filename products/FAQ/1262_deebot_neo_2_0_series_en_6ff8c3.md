# The bottom of the reservior is leaking, how to deal with it?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

The bottom of the reservior is leaking, how to deal with it?

## 答案 / Answer

In order to keep the mop wet, there will be water seeping out from the bottom of the reservoir when the machine is running. After filling the reservoir with water, please cover the plug tightly in time and install the mopping pad plate, a small amount of water dripping is normal.
