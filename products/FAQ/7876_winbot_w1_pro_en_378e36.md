# What is the default startup mode of WINBOT? How many back and forth cleaning sessions will be performed? How long will it take?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the default startup mode of WINBOT? How many back and forth cleaning sessions will be performed? How long will it take?

## 答案 / Answer

W00108

Work logic

1. By default, the robot will enter Fast Cleaning Mode when powered on for the first time. 
2. During normal use (for non-narrow glass), one cleaning cycle will include one back and forth cleaning session. However, you can choose to perform one or two cleaning cycles in the app. 
3. During normal use, cleaning an area of 1 square meter takes approximately 2 minutes and 50 seconds.
