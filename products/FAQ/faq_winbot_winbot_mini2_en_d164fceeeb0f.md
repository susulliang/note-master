# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 / EN - Alert: Please avoid the window corners. How to deal with it?

## Question

Alert: Please avoid the window corners.
 How to deal with it?

## Answer

Kind Reminder:
 If the adhering position is too close to the window frame, WINBOT will trigger an alarm due to the inability to detect the frame properly, accompanied by a voice prompt. Please try the following:

 1. Hold the handle, press and hold the Start Button for 2 seconds, and remove the WINBOT.
 2. Reattach WINBOT at least 10cm away from the window edges.
