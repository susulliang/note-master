# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 割完草之后，边缘留的草有点多。

## Question

割完草之后，边缘留的草有点多。

## Answer

1. GOAT uses AI to precisely identify lawn edges and employs differentiated edge-mowing strategies for various edge types to minimize grass residue.
 1. GOAT positioning has inherent inaccuracies. For safety, it maintains a safety buffer when mowing along edges near cliffs, pools, and similar areas.
 3. Due to clearance between the cutting deck and the outer edge of the GOAT unit, minor grass residue may remain near walls or other edges. We recommend using a trim machine for finishing touches.
 4. Check if the charging station has shifted. If moved, please return it to its original position.
