# DEEBOT / ECOVACS T80 Series / EN - How to Replace the Dust Bag / Alert: Dust Bag Full

## Question

How to Replace the Dust Bag / Alert: Dust Bag Full

## Answer

1. Remove the dust collection cabin cover;
2. Remove and discard the dust bag;
3. Wipe the dust collection cabin with a dry rag and install a new dust bag;
4. Close the dust collection cabin cover;
5. In the app, please click Settings -Maintenance -Dust Bags-I've Replaced.
