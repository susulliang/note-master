## 问题 / Question

19

## 答案 / Answer

Removal Steps:
1.Press the "Open Cleaning Compartment" button on the station to open the door.
2.Pull all 4 washing brush plates outward to remove them. Use a cleaning tool to clear any debris from the plates bearings and the plates, ensuring they are thoroughly clean.
Installation Steps:
1.Align each washing brush plate with its bearing and press down firmly until you hear a "click", indicating it is securely installed.
Tips: All 4 washing brush plates are universal and can be installed in any position; there is no specific placement requirement.
