# Unable to control Furniture Area Cleaning with YIKO voice assistant.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Unable to control Furniture Area Cleaning with YIKO voice assistant.

## 答案 / Answer

D20282

YIKO Voice Assitant

Please do a full mapping or cleaning to generate a complete map of your home, then match the home furniture on the 3D map with your actual home environment. Next, use the command, "OK YIKO, go vacuum {variable},” where {variable} is the name you gave the furniture area.
