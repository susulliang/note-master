# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 污水箱异常？

## Question

污水箱异常？

## Answer

1. Remove the tank and dump the sewage. (If dirt remains in the bottom of the tank, a cleaning brush may be used to assist in cleaning it out.)
 2. Check the float in the Dirty Water Tank to ensure that it is pivoted to return properly.
 3. Reclose the top lid of the Dirty Water Tank and ensure that the clips are securely fitted in place.
 4. Reinstall the Dirty Water Tank back into the station.
