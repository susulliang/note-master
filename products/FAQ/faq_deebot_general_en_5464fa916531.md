# Replacing the Dust Bag / Machine indicates dust bag is full?

## Question

Replacing the Dust Bag / Machine indicates dust bag is full?

## Answer

Friendly reminder:
When your device displays a "dust bag full" alert, follow these steps:
1. Remove the Dust Collection Cabin cover;
2. Remove the dust bag and discard it;
3. Wipe the Dust Bin with a dry cloth and install a new dust bag;
4. Close the Dust Collection Cabin cover;
5. In the app, tap "Maintenance" > "Dust Bag" > "I've Replaced It"
