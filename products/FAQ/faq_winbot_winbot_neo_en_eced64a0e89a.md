# WINBOT / WINBOT NEO / EN - Can I use WINBOT outdoors in northern winter with a low temperature?

## Question

Can I use WINBOT outdoors in northern winter with a low temperature?

## Answer

It's recommended to use the robot within the normal temperature range of 0℃ and 40℃. Otherwise, it may cause damage to the robot or affect the robot's life.
