## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
After using your robot for a period, if you notice the dustbin is full or vacuuming performance is unsatisfactory, try the following steps:                                                                                                                                1.Remove the cover of the ROBOT, slide the Power Switch to "O" to turn off the device, press the dustbin release button, and remove the dustbin.
2. Gently press the latches on the side of the dustbin to remove the top cover, then empty the contents.
3. Remove the filter. (Note: If the dustbin is heavily soiled, it's OK to rinse the dustbin and filter with water.)
4. After cleaning, reinstall the filter assembly.
5. Reattach the dustbin lid to the dustbin. Insert the dustbin into the ROBOT and press until you hear a "click" to confirm proper installation. Finally, replace the ROBOT's Top Cover.
Note: Components may be rinsed with water. Ensure they are completely dry before reuse.
