# APP上的RTK信号强度地图显示大面积无信号覆盖，还能割草吗？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

APP上的RTK信号强度地图显示大面积无信号覆盖，还能割草吗？

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

GOAT是可以工作的。
1.GOAT在无RTK信号区域会通过视觉传感器定位，确保割草效果。
2.无信号区域机器会自动调整割草方向，可能与设置略有偏差。
3.大面积区域建议长宽不超过10米，超出可能导致定位精度下降。
4.若地图边界或禁区边界位于无信号覆盖范围内，建议增设高度＞20cm的物理栅栏，防止机器出界或进入禁区。

GOAT can still operate.
 1. In areas without RTK signals, GOAT uses visual sensors for positioning to ensure mow effectiveness.
 2. In signal-free zones, the machine will automatically adjust its mow direction, which may slightly deviate from the set path. 
 3. For large areas, it is recommended that the length and width do not exceed 10 meters. Exceeding this may result in reduced positioning accuracy. 
 4. If map boundaries or No-Entry zone boundaries are located within areas without signal coverage, it is recommended to add a physical fence with a height greater than 20cm to prevent the machine from going out of bounds or entering the No-Entry zone.
