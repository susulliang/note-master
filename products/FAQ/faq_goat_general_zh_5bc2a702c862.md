# 我的花园中有路，应该如何建图？

## Question

我的花园中有路，应该如何建图？

## Answer

1. If the road surface is level with no elevation difference from surrounding areas, it can be directly included in the map.
 2. If the elevation difference between the road and surrounding areas exceeds 3cm, dividing the lawn into two sections, a path wider than 2m must be constructed to connect the two areas. The path slope must not exceed 24°. When mapping, only follow the edge of the path; other road areas should be excluded.
