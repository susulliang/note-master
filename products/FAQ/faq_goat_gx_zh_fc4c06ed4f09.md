# Can the GOAT cut tall grass?

## Question

Can the GOAT cut tall grass?

## Answer

The GOAT is unable to cut grass higher than 12 cm well. Taller grass may be recognized as an obstacle, so please make sure that grass has been trimmed to 10 cm or lower.
