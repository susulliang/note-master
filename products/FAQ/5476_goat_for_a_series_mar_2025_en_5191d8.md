# 遥控建图-我的花园里有狭窄通道，应该如何建图？ / How should I map if there is a narrow aisle in my garden?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

遥控建图-我的花园里有狭窄通道，应该如何建图？ / How should I map if there is a narrow aisle in my garden?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

宽度小于1m且有高墙遮挡的狭长通道中定位信号较弱，GOAT无法在其中进行弓字型割草且通过时易产生卡困，请尽量贴边遥控。

The positioning signal is weak in narrow aisles with a width of less than 1 m and a blocking high wall. The GOAT cannot mow in zigzags and is likely to get stuck when passing. Try to control the GOAT to be close to the boundary.
