# Why is there an unusual noise during auto-empty collection?

## Question

Why is there an unusual noise during auto-empty collection?

## Answer

There is a low probability that the robot may not align perfectly with the dust collection port when it returns to the base station. If you hear an unusual noise during the auto-empty cycle, please manually move the robot away from the base station and then command it to return again. You can also initiate another auto-empty cycle by tapping the corresponding button in the App.
