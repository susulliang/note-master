# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

## Question

I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

## Answer

（1）G1&G1-2000 are equiped with GPS Module for anti-theft alarm(G1-800 got no GPS Module) . (2) Please confirm whether Out of Map Alarm is turned on in Settings > More Settings > Security Settings.
 (3) The GPS positioning accuracy deviation is 200 m. When the GOAT is far away from the map, it will automatically trigger the alarm.
