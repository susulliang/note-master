# 遥控建图-我应该沿草坪轮廓还是花园轮廓进行建图？ / Should I map along the outline of the lawn or the garden?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

遥控建图-我应该沿草坪轮廓还是花园轮廓进行建图？ / Should I map along the outline of the lawn or the garden?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

如果您的花园中草坪区域和其他区域无高低落差非常平坦，您可以沿花园的边缘进行建图。如果您的草坪和其他区域的高低落差高于4cm，建议您仅围绕草坪轮廓进行建图，GOAT无法越过高于4cm的台阶

If there is no height difference between the lawn area and other areas in your garden, you can create a map along the edge of the garden. If the height difference between your lawn and other areas is more than 4 cm, it is recommended that you only map around the outline of the lawn. The GOAT is not able to cross over steps more than 4 cm in height.
