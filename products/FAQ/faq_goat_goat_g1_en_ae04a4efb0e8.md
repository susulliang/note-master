# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Can I rinse the mower with a pressure washer?

## Question

Can I rinse the mower with a pressure washer?

## Answer

Do not use a pressure washer, as the water will enter the seals and damage mechanical components.
Tips: Before cleaning the GOAT, turn off the mower's power, put down the signal receiver on the mower, and place the protective cover over the panoramic camera. During cleaning, always wear protective gloves to avoid being injured by the blades.
