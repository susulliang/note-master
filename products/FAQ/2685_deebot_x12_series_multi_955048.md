## 问题 / Question

41

## 答案 / Answer

If you encounter situations like the robot frequently returning to the station to wash its roller mop, or returning to wash the roller mop after only a few minutes of operation, try the following steps:
1.Slightly lift the side where the roller mop is installed, gently release the blue buckle on the water tank, and remove the water tank.
2.Take off the top cover of the robot, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, then flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
3.Press the drain port on the bottom of the robot to check if it is stuck or blocked by debris. If there is any blockage, please clean it. Under normal conditions, water should come out when pressed.
4.Reinstall the water tray onto the bottom of the robot. You should hear a "click" sound, which means it's securely in place.
5.Simultaneously press the charging contacts on both sides of the station for 1-2 seconds, then release and observe the station's drain rod. Under normal conditions, the rod should rise and fall smoothly.
6.Check if AI stain recognition is enabled in the app. Disable this feature and attempt the operation.
7. Verify if the "Instant Supercharge" function is set to "Adaptive" mode in the app. If so, switch it to "Standard" mode.
