# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - In unattended mode, will areas that haven't been mowed due to the blades being stopped be re-mowed?

## Question

In unattended mode, will areas that haven't been mowed due to the blades being stopped be re-mowed?

## Answer

In this mowing session, the areas missed due to the blades being stopped will not be re-mowed. The grass removal process will be carried out normally in the next mowing task.
