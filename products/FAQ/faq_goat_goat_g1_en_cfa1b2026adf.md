# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Can the GOAT G1 only identify and warn people that are present in the garden?

## Question

Can the GOAT G1 only identify and warn people that are present in the garden?

## Answer

The GOAT G1 is currently unable to distinguish whether people are in the garden or not. After turning on the relevant functions for human detection, it will sound an alarm to anyone within 7 m of it. To avoid alarming people outside the garden, try to keep a certain distance from the boundary of the garden when setting the patrol spots.
