# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 OMNI / EN - The child lock on the docking station fails to activate.

## Question

The child lock on the docking station fails to activate.

## Answer

If the docking station loses communication with the robot (due to distance or walls), move the robot nearer the docking station to ensure that it is connected to the docking station before activating the child lock.
