# GOAT / GX / ZH - Does the docking station need to be paired with the GOAT?

## Question

Does the docking station need to be paired with the GOAT?

## Answer

Pairing is not required. The charging contacts is used by the station and mower for simple communication. There is no corresponding relationship between them.
