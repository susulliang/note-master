# DEEBOT / DEEBOT OZMO T8 AIVI / EN - Can I download version updates for the robot directly from the Internet?

## Question

Can I download version updates for the robot directly from the Internet?

## Answer

Once connected to a WiFi network, DEEBOT can receive updates (OTA). The App automatically pushes the latest firmware package. Just follow the steps in the App to complete the updates.
