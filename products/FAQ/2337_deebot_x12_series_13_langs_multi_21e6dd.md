## 问题 / Question

35

## 答案 / Answer

1. Make sure that DEEBOT has been successfully connected to the ECOVACS HOME App and is online；
2.Open APP - click Enter- create a home environment map first- click Customize-click Edit;
3.Can add default scenarios, such as "Daily Clean/Deep Clean/..." directly on the page
4.Directly added scenarios also support setting the area to be cleaned and setting cleaning preferences (suction power adjustment/water volume adjustment/frequency selection).
