# Tips for opening the front cover of the station.

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Tips for opening the front cover of the station.

## 答案 / Answer

W00145

The front cover of the station is magnetically attached. To open the front cover, please use your hand to apply outward force to release the magnetic attraction. If the front cover still cannot be opened, there may be foreign objects stuck. Check around the front cover and remove the foreign objects if any before trying to open it.
