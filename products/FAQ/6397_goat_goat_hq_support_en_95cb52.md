# I removed the GOAT G1 from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

I removed the GOAT G1 from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

## 答案 / Answer

G00112

Disable

Intelligence & Privacy

(1) Please confirm whether Out of Map Alarm is turned on in Settings ＞ More Settings ＞ Security Settings. 
(2) The GOAT G1's GPS positioning accuracy deviation is 200 m. When the GOAT is far away from the map, it will automatically trigger the alarm.
