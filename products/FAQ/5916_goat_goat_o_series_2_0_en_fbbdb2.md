# 为什么无法配网？ / Why the GOAT cannot be connected to the network?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么无法配网？ / Why the GOAT cannot be connected to the network?

## 答案 / Answer

6- APP其他

（1）检查GOAT显示屏WiFi icon是否亮起，确认Wi-Fi信号强度是否足够，若GOAT显示屏无WiFi icon显示，则GOAT已与路由器断连。若GOAT显示屏上有WiFi icon显示，则路由器可能故障
（2）在配网环节，必须在机器显示屏上长按“CHARGING”键，待屏幕上倒计时结束，才可以进行配网的动作。

(1) Check whether the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up to ensure the signal avilable. If not, it means the GOAT is disconnected from the router. If the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up, the router may be malfunctioning. 
(2) During the network configuration process, you must press and hold the "CHARGING" button on the GOAT's display screen. Wait for the countdown on the screen to finish before proceeding with the network configuration.
