## 答案 / Answer

Why didn't the robot start collecting dust when it returned to the station?

(1) Please check whether the power cord is correctly plugged into the socket and connected to the station;
(2) Please confirm that the auto-empty function is turned on, specific path: Settings-Station Advanced Settings;
(3) Make sure that the dust container is installed properly;
(4) Make sure that the top cover is correctly installed;
(5) Confirm whether it is during the do not disturb period;
(6) If the robot is manually moved back, it will not start. Only when the robot automatically goes back will the auto-empty be triggered;
(7) Confirm whether the transparent window in the station is dirty. Please clean it with a dry paper towel and try again.
