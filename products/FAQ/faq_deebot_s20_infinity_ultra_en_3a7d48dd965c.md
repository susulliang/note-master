# How to solve the issue of Water Tray Not Installed？

## Question

How to solve the issue of Water Tray Not Installed？

## Answer

1.Remove the top cover and flip the power switch to "O" (off).
2.Lift the side of the robot where the roller mop is installed.
3.Release the blue latch to remove the tray. Inspect the tray for missing magnets.
4.If the magnet is present: Reattach the tray until you hear a "click". Restart the robot.
5.If the magnet is missing: Contact customer service for assistance.
