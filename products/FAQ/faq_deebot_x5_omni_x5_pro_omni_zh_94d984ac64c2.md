# How do I use the robot's Child Lock function?

## Question

How do I use the robot's Child Lock function?

## Answer

If your robot is connected with ECOVACS Home App, open the app and go to Smart Cleaning > Settings > Child Lock to turn it on or off.

Note: When Child Lock is turned on, the station buttons and the robot’s start/pause buttons are locked and the button light is off.
