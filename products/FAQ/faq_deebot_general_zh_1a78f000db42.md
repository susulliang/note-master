# 语音提示撞板被卡住如何处理？ What to do if the Robot gives an alarm "The bumper is stuck"?

## Question

语音提示撞板被卡住如何处理？                                                                   What to do if the Robot gives an alarm "The bumper is stuck"?

## Answer

1.确保主机防撞保护条已移除；
2.轻拍撞板左中右部位确认回弹正常；
3.上下晃动LDS上盖组件；                                                                                                                                                                                                           1.Please check if the protection strip is removed.                                                                                                                                                                            2.Tap the bumper in the left, middle and right side, make sure it can bounce back.                                                                                                                     3.Gently shake the LDS module.
