# My front yard and backyard are not connected. How should I map them?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

My front yard and backyard are not connected. How should I map them?

## 答案 / Answer

G00044

Mapping

The GOAT cannot mow grass in a uniform manner in passageways less than 2 m in width. It is also likely to get stuck when trying to pass through. It is recommended that you only create a map for the front yard or backyard, or build a passageway greater than 2 m in width for the front yard and backyard.
