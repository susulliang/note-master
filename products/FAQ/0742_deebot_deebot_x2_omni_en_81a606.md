# How do I update the robot’s cleaning modes?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I update the robot’s cleaning modes?

## 答案 / Answer

D20403

Usage advice

Please make sure that the robot is connected with the ECOVACS HOME App. Open the app to set the relevant settings: 

Go to Smart Cleaning, tap on cleaning preferences, and choose from Vacuum, Mopping, Vacuum and Mopping, or Mop after Vacuum . You can also use the OK YIKO Voice Assistant to instruct the robot which tasks to perform. 

Gentle Reminders: 
1. You can set the frequency for Vacuum, Mop, and Vacuum and Mop tasks. 
2. For Mop after Vacuum tasks, a frequency cannot be set and the cleaning will follow the same set route.
