## 答案 / Answer

How to deal with the situation that robot misses cleaning some areas?

1.If there is a carpet or floor mat placed at the door of the room; please select "Adaptive Carpet Mode" in the app.
2.The robot can overcome obstacles up to 2cm high; otherwise, it will skip the area.
3.The robot can avoid obstacles intelligently but may miss some areas to prevent collisions with furniture or thresholds at room entrances.
