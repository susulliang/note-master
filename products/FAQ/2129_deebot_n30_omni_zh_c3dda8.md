## 答案 / Answer

What can I do if the voice prompts that the cleaning sink is full?

1. Open the dirty water tank, check whether there are foreign objects on the sealing ring and if it is in place;
2. Check the dirty water tube is installed properly, then close the tank tightly;                                   3. Remove and clean the sealing plug, then reinstall it; check for no foreign objects between the tank and the station; put it back and press it down to ensure it is properly installed.
4. Long press the return button, observe whether the dirty water is drained;                                                                                                 5. If not,remove the detachable tray, pour the water out, clean the filter，then put back the detachable tray; 
6.Disconnect and reconnect the power supply of the station. When the robot is docked and in charging mode, execute self-cleaning function again to see if it is normal.
