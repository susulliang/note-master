# Robot shakes when walking on carpet, is this normal?

## Question

Robot shakes when walking on carpet, is this normal?

## Answer

It is normal for a robot to experience slight shaking when walking against the direction of the carpet fibers. However, if the shaking is too noticeable, it's recommended to create a no-entry zone for the carpeted area during cleaning.
