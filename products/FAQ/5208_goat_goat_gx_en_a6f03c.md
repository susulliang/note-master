# My front and back yards are not connected. Can I use the automatic mapping function?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

My front and back yards are not connected. Can I use the automatic mapping function?

## 答案 / Answer

G00074

Mapping

No, in this scenario, GOAT can only create a map of the area where the station is located.
