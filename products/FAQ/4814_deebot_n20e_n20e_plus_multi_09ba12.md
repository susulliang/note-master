>

## 问题 / Question

15

## 答案 / Answer

The robot has a path distortion when performing edge cleaning tasks and does not walk in a straight line

Please confirm whether there is a skirting board next to the wall and confirm whether the height of the skirting board is less than 5 cm (if the skirting board is less than 5 cm, it will cause the robot to misrecognize the edge sensor, resulting in the robot hitting the board and hitting the skirting board, triggering obstacle avoidance and causing the path to be crooked).
