# What to do if the robot isn't resuming cleaning from the last stopping point?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What to do if the robot isn't resuming cleaning from the last stopping point?

## 答案 / Answer

1. Ensure the "Resume Cleaning" feature is enabled.
2. The "Resume Cleaning" feature won't activate during "Do Not Disturb" hours.
3. If the robot returns to charge and triggers an error alarm, "Resume Cleaning" won't activate.
4. Don't restart or reset the robot after activating "Resume Cleaning."
Note: "Resume Cleaning" only works in auto mode.
