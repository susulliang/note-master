# Why is the anti-theft alarm triggered when the mower is inside the map?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the anti-theft alarm triggered when the mower is inside the map?

## 答案 / Answer

G00113

Disable

Intelligence & Privacy

The GPS module occasionally has signal drift which may lead to a certain probability of false alarms. If there are frequent false alarms, please contact ECOVACS customer service.
