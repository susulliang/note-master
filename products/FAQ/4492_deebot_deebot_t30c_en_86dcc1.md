# Robot Charging Guide & Energy-saving Charging Instructions

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Robot Charging Guide & Energy-saving Charging Instructions

## 答案 / Answer

Robot Charging Guide                                                                                                                                                                             1. Press and hold the AUTO button to turn on the Robot.
2. Place the Robot in front of the station; press the recharge button to let the Robot return to the station automatically and hear the voice prompt "start charging".
Energy-saving Charging Instructions
1. Open the app - click enter - Settings -  Station Advanced Settings - Toggle "Energy-saving charging" ON.
2. Default off, when enabled, the battery fully charges only during your set schedule and maintains a safe minimum otherwise.;
3. To ensure the normal operation of the robot, the battery may still be charged during non-energy-saving charging hours. If a task is not completed, the robot will be charged to the required level during the non-energy-saving charging period (the default charging duration is 7 hours, and cannot be set to be less than 7 hours);
4. During non-energy-saving charging periods, the robot will be charged to a minimum of 30% and then maintained at 30%;
