# Some water remains when the clean water tank and waste water tank are removed. / This is because the entire principle of cleaning the mopping pads involves drawing water from the cleaning water tank through a water pump and a suction pipe. After cleaning, the water is pumped through a water pump and a suction pipe to the waste water tank. There may be some water residue at the connection, which will not affect operation and performance. Please wipe before use.
