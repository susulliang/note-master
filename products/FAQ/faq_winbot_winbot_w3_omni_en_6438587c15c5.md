# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to store winbot after cleaning task is completed？

## Question

How to store winbot after cleaning task is completed？

## Answer

1.Unlock the hook on the back of the station.
2.Position the WINBOT with the front facing outward, the compound cord outlet end pointing upward, and the colored side of the WINBOT hooked onto the hook.
3.Short press the "Retract Cable" button on the station. It will immediately start retracting the cable quickly.
4.If the power cord becomes twisted during retraction, straighten it gently and press the "Retract Cable" button again to complete the retraction.
5.Unlock the safety rope, place it back in its original position, and close the rear flip cover of the station.
6.Pour out any remaining water from the clean water tank and dirty water tank of the station.
7.Press and hold the power button on the station for more than 2 seconds to shut down Winbot.
Tips:
When storing Winbot, remove the damp wiping pad and allow it to dry completely before storage to prevent odor.
