# AI识别开启后GOAT 能在夜间对人形或者小动物进行识别吗？ / Can the GOAT recognize humans or small animals at night when AI recognition is enabled?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

AI识别开启后GOAT 能在夜间对人形或者小动物进行识别吗？ / Can the GOAT recognize humans or small animals at night when AI recognition is enabled?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

不可以。GOAT 没有配备夜视，天黑后无法进行AI识别

No. The GOAT is not equipped with night vision and is unable to carry out AI recognition after dark.
