## 答案 / Answer

The robot has not finished cleaning and is returning to charge

(1) When the room is large or the home environment is very complex, a (1) When the room is large or the home environment is very complex, a single cleaning cannot be completed, and it is necessary to return to charging midway. It is recommended to turn on the "Smart Breakpoint Continuation Sweeping" function in the APP;
(2) If the door of a room in a certain area of the map is closed and the machine cannot enter after multiple attempts, it will directly report being blocked and continue to clean the next area. For the next cleaning, open all room doors in advance and restart the automatic cleaning.
