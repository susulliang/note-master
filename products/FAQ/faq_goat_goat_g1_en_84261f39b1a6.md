# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Is auto-connection module still available after robot replacement?

## Question

Is auto-connection module still available after robot replacement?

## Answer

The auto-connetcion module is bound to the module. If you purchase a three-year service, use it on robot A for one year, then plug it into robot B, you will still have two years of use remaining.
