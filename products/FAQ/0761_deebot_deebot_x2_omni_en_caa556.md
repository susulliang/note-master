# There is a strange sound when drawing water during the mopping pads cleaning.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

There is a strange sound when drawing water during the mopping pads cleaning.

## 答案 / Answer

D20254

Station

After cleaning, it is normal for the water to be pumped through a water pump and a suction pipe as it is pumped into the dirty water tank. As the process draws to a finish, the small amount of water left over results in water and air mixing together, causing a gurgling sound. This is normal.
