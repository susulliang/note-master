# 为什么GOAT充电很慢？

## Question

为什么GOAT充电很慢？

## Answer

(1) Normal charging (0%-100%) for GOAT O600/O800 takes approximately 80/150 minutes. When operating with Low Battery (less than 15%), it automatically returns to charge. After reaching 80%, it resumes mowing. This phase (15%-80%) takes about 55/100 minutes.
 (2) When battery temperature ≤8°C, the station automatically switches to low-current charging to protect the battery, extending charging time (full charge requires 3-5 hours).
 (3) Battery aging from prolonged use may also cause longer charging times.
 If none of the above apply, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
