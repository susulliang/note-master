## 问题 / Question

47

## 答案 / Answer

You can troubleshoot by following these steps:
1. Confirm the DEEBOT is powered on;
2. Check if Do Not Disturb mode is enabled in the app. If within the Do Not Disturb timeframe, the robot will not respond to scheduled tasks;
3. Check the battery level in the app to ensure sufficient power;
4. Review scheduled tasks in the app settings to confirm they are correctly set and saved.
Additionally, note that if the current map uses the area merging feature, scheduled tasks for associated areas will not execute.
