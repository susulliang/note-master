>

## 问题 / Question

通道-我可以自己增加一条通道吗？最多可以有几条通道？ / Can I add path for the map？
How many channels can there be between each two zones?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

可以
可以在地图编辑-通道-增加通道功能中新增通道，每两个区域之间最多可以有3条通道。

sure
You can add paths in the map editing, with a maximum of 3 channels between every two areas.
