# Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

## Question

Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

## Answer

It is recommended that the docking station is placed on a lawn with a flat area and wide visibility, and secured to the ground with the provided nails to prevent the station from moving. For details, please refer to the installation instruction manual. Note：Please keep the side with the label close to the lawn. If Install the Station on the hard surface besides the Iawn，provided that the lawn and the hard surface are at the same height (no
 steps and gaps)，please refer to the installation instruction manual for details.
