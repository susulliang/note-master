# GOAT为什么配网后提示机器被锁定，无法使用？ / Why does GOAT prompt that the machine is locked and cannot be used after distribution network?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT为什么配网后提示机器被锁定，无法使用？ / Why does GOAT prompt that the machine is locked and cannot be used after distribution network?

## 答案 / Answer

APP

GOAT 机器不能支持跨区使用，如果您购买的机器非当前区域销售版本，配对后会锁定您的使用权限。
可在回到销售区域后，重新使用时，联系售后处理。

GOAT  cannot support cross regional use. If the GOAT  you purchased is not the current regional sales version, your usage rights will be locked.
After returning to the sales area, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
