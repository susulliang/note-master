# WINBOT / W2/W2 OMNI / EN - The button on the WINBOT W2 does not respond.

## Question

The button on the WINBOT W2 does not respond.

## Answer

1. If the WINBOT W2 is working and there is no response to the button, it will automatically stop and keep adsorption. Please remove the WINBOT W2 in time.
2. Hold the reset button at the bottom of the WINBOT W2 to reset it.
