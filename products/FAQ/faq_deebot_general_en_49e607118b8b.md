# How to Charge the Station

## Question

How to Charge the Station

## Answer

1.Open the small cover at the lower back of the station and take out the charging cable.
Plug the cable into the power port on the back of the station.
2.Connect the other end to a household outlet.
The indicator light will breathe while charging and stay solid when fully charged. Charging takes about 3 hours at room temperature.                      Notes:
① Low temperatures may slow down charging.
② WINBOT is designed for use between 0°C and 40°C.
Do not charge it outside this temperature range.
③ If not used for a long time, charge the station periodically to maintain battery health.
