## 答案 / Answer

How to use?

1.Open the WINBOT box and take out the accessories. Tear off the protective film and protective strip. 
2.Before use, open the instruction manual and read the precautions carefully.
3.Connect the power cord to the adapter and plug it into the power source. 
4.Open the Filling Opening on the WINBOT, take out the ECOVACS WINBOT Cleaning Solution. Pour the solution into the opening, being careful not to overfill.
5.Before cleaning,manually moisten the Wiping Pad and wring it dry. Attach the Wiping Pad to the velcro at the bottom of WINBOT, then press it firmly to ensure it sticks securely.
6.Before use,  securely fasten the safety rope to an immovable and stable object. Also, lock the Safety Rope to prevent any potential risks.
7.Press and hold START button on WINBOT for 2 seconds to start the fan, then place WINBOT on the glass. WINBOT will automatically attach itself and start cleaning. 
Note: 
1) WINBOT is only suitable for flat surfaces without gaps. 
2) Ensure the glass thickness is not less than 3mm.
3) Do not place WINBOT too close to the edge of the window. keep a distance of at least 10cm from the edge of the window. 
8.To pause cleaning, short press the Power button on the WINBOT. Short press the Power button again to resume and continue the unfinished cleaning task.
9.After finishing the cleaning task, WINBOT will automatically return to its Initial Spot and wait to be removed. 
Press and hold the power button for 2 seconds or more to stop the fan. Then, take WINBOT off according to voice prompts.
