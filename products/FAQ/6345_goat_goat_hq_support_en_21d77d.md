# Can the GOAT be moved during map learning?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT be moved during map learning?

## 答案 / Answer

G00060

Enable

Mapping

If the GOAT gets trapped during map learning, it can still continue learning after being lifted and moved. However, do not drag the GOAT directly, as dragging the GOAT will not trigger it to reposition and will result in abnormal positioning.
