# Why does the device automatically shut down after being turned on?

## Question

Why does the device automatically shut down after being turned on?

## Answer

After turning on, the device activates the water pump every 10 seconds to detect the water level. If the device is not placed into water within 20 minutes, it will automatically power off for self-protection.
