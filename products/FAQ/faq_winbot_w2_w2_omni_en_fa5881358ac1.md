# WINBOT / W2/W2 OMNI / EN - How often should the wiping pad be replaced or cleaned?

## Question

How often should the wiping pad be replaced or cleaned?

## Answer

If the wiping pad is dirty or there are marks left on the surface during the cleaning, please replace the wiping pad in time to avoid affecting the cleaning performance.

After cleaning, please remove the wiping pad, clean and air dry it.
