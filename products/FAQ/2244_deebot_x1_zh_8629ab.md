# Excessive water stains on the ground after mopping.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Excessive water stains on the ground after mopping.

## 答案 / Answer

Excessive water stains on the ground after mopping.

1. Instruct users to reduce the amount of water in the app.
2. Instruct the user to upgrade the firmware.
3. Dry the mopping pads before use.

Guide users for maintenance
