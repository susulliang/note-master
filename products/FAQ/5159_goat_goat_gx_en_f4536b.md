# What should I do if I don't have enough Navigation Beacons?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What should I do if I don't have enough Navigation Beacons?

## 答案 / Answer

G00025

Navigation Beacon

You can map and use in the area covered by the current Navigation Beacon signal, based on the principles of Navigation Beacon placement. Remap after purchasing the required Navigation Beacon.
