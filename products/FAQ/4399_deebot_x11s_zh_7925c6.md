## 答案 / Answer

1. Open the packaging, take out the robot, station base, station, and power cord, and remove the Anti-collision protective sponges, tapes, protective bags, etc.
2. Install the station base by aligning the base with the slot at the bottom of the station and pressing down firmly until you hear a "click" sound, indicating proper installation. Remove the foam filler from above the cleaning sink.
3. Use the power cord from the packaging to connect to the power socket on the back of the station. Find a suitable location to place it against the wall and connect it to the power supply.
(Place the station on a flat, hard surface, ensuring that there are no objects within 5 cm on either side and 80 cm in front of it.)
Tips:
1. The station should preferably be placed on a tiled floor. If placed on a wooden floor, regularly check the bottom of the station for any residual water stains and clean them promptly to prevent moisture damage.
2. Ensure the base of the station is installed and remove the foam filler.
3. If there are reflective objects such as mirrors or metal baseboards near the station, cover the bottom 14 cm of these objects.
4. Do not place the station in direct sunlight.
5. It is recommended to place the station in an area with a strong Wi-Fi signal for a better user experience.
