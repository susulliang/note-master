# How to add more Wi-Fi signals?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to add more Wi-Fi signals?

## 答案 / Answer

1. Make sure Robot has been successfully connected to the APP and is online.
2. Open APP＞click "Enter"＞click "Settings"＞ click "Robot Advanced Settings" ＞ click "About Robot" ＞ Wi-Fi ＞ click "+" to add multiple Wi-Fi signals.
