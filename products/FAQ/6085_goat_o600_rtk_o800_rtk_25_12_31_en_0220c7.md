# 如何选择GOAT的切割效率？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何选择GOAT的切割效率？

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

O600 RTK割草默认行走速度为0.35m/s，空旷环境下每小时可切割约100㎡，暂不支持调节。

The default walking speed for O600 RTK mow is 0.35m/s, achieving approximately 100 square meters per hour in open environments. Adjustment is not currently supported.
