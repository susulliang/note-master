# GOAT 割草时经过非草区域刀盘会保持转动吗？ / Will the blades of the GOAT continue turning when passing through non-grass areas?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 割草时经过非草区域刀盘会保持转动吗？ / Will the blades of the GOAT continue turning when passing through non-grass areas?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

GOAT在割草时目前不会区分草与非草区域，GOAT在地图内的区域进行工作时将始终保持刀盘转动。

The GOAT currently does not accurately distinguish between grass and non-grass areas when mowing. The blades of the GOAT will continue to keep rotating when working in areas within the map.
