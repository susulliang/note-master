# 当前室外无雨，为何GOAT未解除雨淋保护？

## Question

当前室外无雨，为何GOAT未解除雨淋保护？

## Answer

(1) Please confirm whether the rain delay setting has been enabled in the App. By default, if the rain protection mode is enabled, it will be lifted three hours after the rain stops. You can modify the time in the App.
(2) Please confirm whether the rain sensor on the GOAT is dry.
(3) In order to protect the lawn, it is not recommended to cut the grass immediately after the rain stops. The rain sensor is sensitive. After the rain stops, it takes a certain amount of time to sense the humidity of the environment. If you need to mow, turn off the rain sensor.
