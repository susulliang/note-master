# Why do the mopping pads plates sometimes lift up and sometimes not when DEEBOT is cleaning?

## Question

Why do the mopping pads plates sometimes lift up and sometimes not when DEEBOT is cleaning?

## Answer

1. When DEEBOT is cleaning, DEEBOT will lift up the mopping pads when navigating across zones and back to wash and recharge to avoid secondary pollution.
2. DEEBOT will lift up the mopping pads by default when walking on the carpet, crossing obstacles or getting out of trouble.
3. DEEBOT will lift up the mopping pads when creating a map.
