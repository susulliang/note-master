# DEEBOT / T90 PRO OMNI With Auto Refill And Drain / ZH - Machine Not Producing Clean Water / Machine Alarms Water Supply Abnormal? (Auto Refill and Drain Module version)

## Question

Machine Not Producing Clean Water / Machine Alarms Water Supply Abnormal? (Auto Refill and Drain Module version)

## Answer

You can try troubleshooting with the following steps:
1. Power off and restart the station. Place the DEEBOT back on the station and confirm it is charging. Double-click the start button to trigger the water system self-check function. Observe if water appears in the mop washing tray.
2. If clean water appears in the mop washing tray, the fault is resolved.
3. If the mop washing tray remains empty, proceed with the following steps.
Inspect the water inlet hose for kinks or leaks.
Verify the water inlet valve is fully open. If closed, open it immediately.
Confirm your home's water supply is active. Attempt operation again after water service resumes.
If water pressure is low, a 120-second water intake delay triggering an alarm is normal. Wait 5 minutes before attempting operation again.
