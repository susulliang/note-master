# 如何关闭补光灯？ / How to turn off the night vision supplementary Light?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

如何关闭补光灯？ / How to turn off the night vision supplementary Light?

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
“夜视补光”灯功能默认打开，当您需要关闭时可按如下步骤进行操作：
1.点击右上角”设置“＞选择”地宝高级设定”＞选择“夜视补光”可开启或关闭。

Friendly reminder:
 The night vision supplementary light function is enabled by default. If you need to turn it off, please follow these steps:
 1.Tap "Settings" in the upper right corner-Select Robot Advanced Settings-Choose Night Vision Supplementary Light，to turn it on or off.
