# GOAT除了用APP操控还有其他的智能触发方式吗？ / Does GOAT have any other smart triggers besides APP control?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT除了用APP操控还有其他的智能触发方式吗？ / Does GOAT have any other smart triggers besides APP control?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

GOAT在APP外还支持使用Google Home和Alexa命令其执行一些简单的指令，如：开始自动割草等

GOAT also supports the use of Google Home and Alexa commands outside the APP to perform some simple commands, such as: start mowing and so on
