# 我已经根据APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回了原位，为何APP上的故障未解除？ / I have installed the abnormal RTK station back in place according to the APP prompt, why the fault on the APP has not been removed?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我已经根据APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回了原位，为何APP上的故障未解除？ / I have installed the abnormal RTK station back in place according to the APP prompt, why the fault on the APP has not been removed?

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

可尝试重启RTK基站，APP - Setting - RTK - 详情（三个点，详见右图）- 重启。
*若RTK基站持续蓝灯闪烁，无法解决需联系科沃斯售后服务部门

Please reset the RTK on the APP, Setting - RTK - Detail（icon）- Reset.
* If it cannot be resolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales Service
