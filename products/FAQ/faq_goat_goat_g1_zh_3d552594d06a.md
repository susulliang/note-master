# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Why can't the GOAT connect to the App even after completing network setup?

## Question

Why can't the GOAT connect to the App even after completing network setup?

## Answer

(1) Please confirm that the region you select when logging in to the APP is consistent with the region where GOAT is currently located. Cross-region network connection may cause the APP to not work properly.          （2）Check whether the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up. If not, it means the GOAT is disconnected from the router. If the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up, the router may be malfunctioning.
 (3) Check whether the Wi-Fi signal is strong enough. Try completing network setup again after turning on Bluetooth.
