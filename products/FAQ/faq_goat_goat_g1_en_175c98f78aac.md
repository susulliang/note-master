# Why does the GOAT G1 automatically end the mowing task when it is not complete?

## Question

Why does the GOAT G1 automatically end the mowing task when it is not complete?

## Answer

(1) In order not to conflict with the scheduled mowing tasks, the GOAT will automatically end the mowing task when it has been paused for more than 24 hours.
(2) For safety reasons, the GOAT will automatically end the current mowing tasks if it detects abnormal positioning signals.
(3) The GOAT will deem the mowing task complete if certain areas are inaccessible due to obstacles.
(4) The task will be automatically ended if the GOAT has a power outage during the task.
