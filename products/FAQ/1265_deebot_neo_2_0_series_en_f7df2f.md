# Why can't a robot cross the 20mm threshold?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why can't a robot cross the 20mm threshold?

## 答案 / Answer

1. In real-life scenarios, factors such as the shape of the thresholds, their width, and the angle of the robot while climbing can impact the robot's climbing efficiency. To overcome such issues, you can purchase an auxiliary climbing plate from the Ecovacs online store.(20mm data collected from Ecovacs lab);
2. The drive wheels may be stained with pet and human hair during operation, and it is recommended to clean them regularly, otherwise it will also affect the robot's climbing performance.
