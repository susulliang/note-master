# Robot occasionally fails to avoid obstacles?

## Question

Robot occasionally fails to avoid obstacles?

## Answer

1. Check if the line laser module (located at the center of the front bumper) is dusty. If so, gently wipe it with a dry paper towel.
2. Verify that the "AIVI 3D" switch in the robot's settings is turned on.
Note: The robot may slow down and lightly touch black or linear objects.
