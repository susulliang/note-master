# 建图后为什么提示我面积过大？ / Why does APP remind me that the area is too large?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图后为什么提示我面积过大？ / Why does APP remind me that the area is too large?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

GOAT针对不同面积推出了不同的型号，推荐根据用户家的实际切割面积进行选购。

The GOAT series have the many models for different lawn size, it is recommended to choose GOAT model based on your actual area.
