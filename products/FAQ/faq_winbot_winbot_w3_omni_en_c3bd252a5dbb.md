# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - Scrubber cleaning

## Question

Scrubber cleaning

## Answer

You can try the following steps:
 1.Make sure the WINBOT fan is turned off.
 2.Place the WINBOT on the wiping pad, ensuring both scrubbers are fully in contact with the pad.
 3.Press both bumpers near the scrubbers at the same time. When you hear the voice prompt “Start cleaning scrubbers,” release your hands. The scrubbers will begin rotating for cleaning.
 4.When you hear the voice prompt “scrubber cleaning completed,” the process is finished.
