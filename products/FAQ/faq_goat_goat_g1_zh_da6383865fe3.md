# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Why did automatic mapping fail?

## Question

Why did automatic mapping fail?

## Answer

(1) Confirm that you have installed enough UWB poles and placed them correctly as instructed
(2) Confirm that the UWB poles are in normal condition and have not moved during the map building process
(3) Confirm whether the machine has been moved or dragged during the map building process. Moving the machine will cause the map building to fail. It is recommended that you trim the potholes in the garden to reduce the probability of GOAT being stuck and needing to be moved or dragged during the map building process
(4) Confirm that there is sufficient light and no rain during the map building process
(5) Confirm that the Bluetooth interruption has not exceeded 1 hour
(6) Confirm that the GOAT has been automatically docked with the base station after the automatic edge is completed according to the instructions of the APP. Manual remote control of GOAT docking with the base station, or pushing GOAT into the base station cannot close the map normally
(7) Confirm whether there are obstacles that prevent the machine from moving smoothly during the map building process
