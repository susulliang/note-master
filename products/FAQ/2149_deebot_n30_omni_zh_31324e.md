## 答案 / Answer

How to handle when no clean water comes out?

1.Check for obstructions between the clean water tank and station. If found, remove them.
2.Ensure the seal plug of the clean water tank on the station is properly installed and tightly fitted.
3.Open the clean water tank to inspect and clear any blockage at the outlet and filter cotton (detachable for cleaning).
4.Close the tank, place it evenly, and retry.
