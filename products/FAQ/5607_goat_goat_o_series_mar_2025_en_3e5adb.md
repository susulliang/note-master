# 基站指示灯一直显示蓝灯闪烁 / The indicator light on the docking station keeps flashing blue.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

基站指示灯一直显示蓝灯闪烁 / The indicator light on the docking station keeps flashing blue.

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

当GOAT与基站对接成功充电，基站指示灯将会一直蓝灯闪烁

When the GOAT is successfully connected to the docking station and charging, the  indicator light will continue to flash blue.
