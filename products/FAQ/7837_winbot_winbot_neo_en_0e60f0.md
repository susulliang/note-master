# What is the W1 PRO water spray angle? What is the water spray interval?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the W1 PRO water spray angle? What is the water spray interval?

## 答案 / Answer

1. When WINBOT walks in a back and forth pattern, the water spray coverage on both sides of the robot can reach the upper edge of the robot and 10 cm below the robot. Please note that water will be sprayed only upwards and not downwards when the robot walks vertically. 
2. Water is sprayed at an 8-second interval.
