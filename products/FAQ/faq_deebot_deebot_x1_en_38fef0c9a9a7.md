# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 / EN - Unable to control Area Cleaning with YIKO voice assistant.

## Question

Unable to control Area Cleaning with YIKO voice assistant.

## Answer

Please do a full mapping or cleaning to generate a complete map of your home, before matching the names and types of each area of your home to the actual home environment. Next, use the command, "OK YIKO, go vacuum {variable},” where {variable} is the name you gave the area.
