# 可以用水枪冲洗割草机吗？ / Can I rinse the GOAT with a pressure washer?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

可以用水枪冲洗割草机吗？ / Can I rinse the GOAT with a pressure washer?

## 答案 / Answer

8- 保养维护

切勿使用高压水枪，高压水流将进入密封件内、并损坏电子机械部件
Tips:在清洁GOAT前，请先关闭割草机电源，并套上全景摄像头的保护罩；清洁过程中，请始终佩戴防护手套以免刀片划伤

Do not use a pressure washer, as the water will enter the seals and damage mechanical components. 
Tips: Before cleaning the GOAT, turn off the GOAT's power, and place the protective cover over the panoramic camera. During cleaning, always wear protective gloves to avoid being injured by the blades.
