# 环境中有哪些因素会导致无法使用自动建图？ / What factors in the environment will prevent automatic mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

环境中有哪些因素会导致无法使用自动建图？ / What factors in the environment will prevent automatic mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

（1）草坪边界有落差高度大于4cm的台阶（台阶下还是草）
（2）无边界的草坪（如和邻居接壤的草坪无隔断）
（3）草坪边缘障碍物比较多
（4）两块草坪被一条宽度大于1m的路隔开（此情景仅能创建一块草坪的地图）
（5）触发自动建图时得位置不在草坪边缘

(1) There are steps with a height difference greater than 4 cm on the edge of the lawn
(2) There is no border on the lawn (such as a lawn that borders a neighbor and has no partition)
(3) There are many obstacles on the edge of the lawn
(4) Two lawns are separated by a road with a width greater than 1m (in this scenario, only a map of one lawn can be created)
(5)When triggering auto  Mapping, the position is not at the edge of the lawn
