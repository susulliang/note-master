# 如何将GOAT A Series恢复出厂设置？ / How do I restore the GOAT to default settings?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何将GOAT A Series恢复出厂设置？ / How do I restore the GOAT to default settings?

## 答案 / Answer

（1）在GOAT上同时长按“开始+回充” 2s
（2）跳出PIN码输入页面，输入PIN码，点击OK确认后，主机将恢复出厂设置

(1) Simultaneously long press "Start" and "Recharge" at the same time on the GOAT for 2 seconds until the display show the PIN code input interface.
(2) Enter the right PIN code, then press "OK", the GOAT will be reset.
