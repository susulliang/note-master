# How long can GOAT's battery last in standby mode?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How long can GOAT's battery last in standby mode?

## 答案 / Answer

G00141

Others

GOAT's battery can support about 20 hours when there is no task and in standby mode. If the video is turned on, battery consumption will increase.
