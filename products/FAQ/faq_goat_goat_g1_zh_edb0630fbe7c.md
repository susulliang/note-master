# Can I change the position of an Navigation Beacon after it is installed?

## Question

Can I change the position of an Navigation Beacon after it is installed?

## Answer

You can adjust the location of a Navigation Beacon at your discretion before mapping. Changing the location of a Navigation Beacon after the map is generated will cause the positioning signal to move and will require remapping.
