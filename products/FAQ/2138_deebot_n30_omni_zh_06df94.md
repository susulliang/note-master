## 答案 / Answer

How to upgrade firmware version ?

1. The Robot should be placed at the station and in a charging state.
2. Make sure Robot is connected to the APP and is on line. 
3. Enter App-Click robot controls-Click settings-Robot Advanced Settings＞Robot Details＞Update.
