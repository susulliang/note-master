# Some water remains when the clean water tank and waste water tank are removed.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Some water remains when the clean water tank and waste water tank are removed.

## 答案 / Answer

D20253

Station

This is because the entire principle of cleaning the mopping pads involves drawing water from the cleaning water tank through a water pump and a suction pipe. After cleaning, the water is pumped through a water pump and a suction pipe to the waste water tank. There may be some water residue at the connection, which will not affect operation and performance. Please wipe before use.
