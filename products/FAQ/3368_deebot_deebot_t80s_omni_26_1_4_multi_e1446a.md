>

## 问题 / Question

23

## 答案 / Answer

1. Remove the detachable mop washing tray.
2. First attach the longer end of the float to the mounting bracket, then install the shorter end. After installation, gently push the overflow float up and down. If it returns to its original position naturally, the installation is correct.
Note: After installation, the magnet should always remain at the bottom of the float.
