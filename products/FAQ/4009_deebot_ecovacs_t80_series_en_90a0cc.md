# How to Use Mopping Function (Water Tank Version)

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to Use Mopping Function (Water Tank Version)

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the clean water tank and fill to the maximum water level line.
2.Measure ~20ml of cleaning solution into the cap (dilution ratio 1:200), then pour into the tank.
3.Securely attach the lid and return the tank to the Station.
4.Power on the robot, place it directly in front of the Station, press and hold the "Auto" button to initiate charging. Charging starts after successful docking.
5.Press the "Auto" button to begin simultaneous vacuuming and mopping.
