# 开启“瞬时超充”功能后，机器人仍然无法完成全屋清洁，如何处理？

## Question

开启“瞬时超充”功能后，机器人仍然无法完成全屋清洁，如何处理？

## Answer

1. In the app, swipe up on the control page and set suction to Standard mode.
2. Go to Settings > Station Advanced Settings > set Mop Washing Frequency to by time，10 min.
   If the issue persists, please contact customer support.
