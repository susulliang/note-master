# Why is the GOAT not mowing exactly to the selected edges?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the GOAT not mowing exactly to the selected edges?

## 答案 / Answer

G00103

Mowing

Due to the limitation of positioning accuracy, if there is an overlap between the contours of the restricted area or between the restricted area and the outer contour, all overlapping contours will be cut together by default. If the distance between the contours is less than 1 m, the GOAT will likely consider that the contours overlap and cut the grass together.
