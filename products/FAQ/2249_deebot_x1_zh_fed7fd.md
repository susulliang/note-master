# Robot cannot charge or cannot be fully charged.

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Robot cannot charge or cannot be fully charged.

## 答案 / Answer

Robot cannot charge or cannot be fully charged.

1. Check whether the power socket is powered on, replace the power socket to test, and confirm that the power line interface on the back of the base station is installed in place.
2. Remove the top cover of the robot and confirm that the power switch has been pushed to the "丨" position to turn on the robot.
3. Confirm whether DEEBOT can be started up normally
     ①If it can be turned on: Please place the robot directly in front of the base station, click the "robot recall/exit" button of the base station, and let the machine automatically return to the base station for charging. After successfully connected, the Auto button of the robot displays white light. Observe and use it after charging 4-5 hours.
     ②If it cannot be turned on: please push the tail of the robot to the cleaning tank of the base station. The voice plays that it starts to charge after successfully connected, and the Auto button of the robot displays white light. Observe and use it after charging 4-5 hours.
4. Check whether the red light is blinking on DEEBOT during work, and if so, follow the voice prompts to troubleshooting.
5. Please check the firmware version of the robot and upgrade it to the latest.
