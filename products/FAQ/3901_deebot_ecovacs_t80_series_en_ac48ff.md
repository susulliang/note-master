# Side Brush Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Side Brush Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
If debris becomes tangled in the side brush or a "brush entangled" alert appears, follow these steps:
1.Power off the robot by switching the power toggle to "O", remove the dirty water box, and flip the robot upside down.
2.Detach the side brush and clean it using a cleaning tool (e.g., a small brush or tweezers).
3.Reattach the side brush by aligning it with the slot and pressing downward until a "click" sound confirms proper installation.
4.Reinstall the dirty water box.
