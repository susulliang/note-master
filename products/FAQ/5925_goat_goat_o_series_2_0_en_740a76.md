# 固件升级很慢或升级失败？ / Why does the firmware upgrade have a low speed or even fail?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

固件升级很慢或升级失败？ / Why does the firmware upgrade have a low speed or even fail?

## 答案 / Answer

7- 主机其他

固件升级依赖WiFi连接，如果升级很慢或升级失败可将GOAT搬至WiFi信号强的地方进行升级

Firmware upgrades require a Wi-Fi connection. If the upgrade is very slow or fails, you can move the GOAT to the location with good Wi-Fi coverage and try upgrading again
