# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Why is the GOAT G1 charging so slowly?

## Question

Why is the GOAT G1 charging so slowly?

## Answer

(1) Under normal conditions, the G1 can charge to 80% in about 1.5 hours before the docking station switches to low current charging (which will take about 2–2.5 hours before the battery is fully charged).
(2) When the temperature of the docking station's battery is ≤8°C, the charging time will become longer as it switches to a low current charge (3–5 hours for a fully charged battery) to protect the battery.
(3) The battery ages after long-term use which may result in a longer charging time.
If none of the above scenarios apply, please contact ECOVACS customer service.
