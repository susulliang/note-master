# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - RTK基站的信号覆盖范围有多大？

## Question

RTK基站的信号覆盖范围有多大？

## Answer

In the case of no shaows, the signal coverage of the RTK is a circular area about 300-400m centered on the station.
