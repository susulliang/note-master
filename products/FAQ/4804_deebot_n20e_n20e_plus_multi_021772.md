How to set the cleaning parameters of a robot?

Suitable cleaning parameters can be selected through APP operation.
Cleaning mode: single sweep (need to remove the cloth holder), sweep and mop (default mode).
Mopping water volume: low, medium, high.
(3) Suction: Silent, standard, strong, super strong; (The battery life of the robot may be correspondingly reduced under strong/super strong suction); (4) Cleaning frequency: 1 time, 2 times.
