# Robot Packing Guide

## Question

Robot Packing Guide

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
Before packing, please ensure the following steps are completed: Empty the water in the clean/dirty water tank, empty the cleaning solution in the cleaning solution box, drain the water from the built-in small water tank of the robot, and remove the damp mopping pad to air-dry.
1.Press and hold the AUTO button to turn off the robot.
2.Remove the clean/dirty water tank from the base station, empty the water, and reinstall it.
3.Unplug the station power cord and remove the base.
4.Place the station steadily into the packaging box. Place a cardboard-plastic protective layer on top of the base station.
5.Gently place the robot on top of the cardboard-plastic layer, then add another cardboard-plastic protective layer to protect the robot from damage. Place the removed side brush, power cord, base, and other accessories back into the packaging box in order.
