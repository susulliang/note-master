# How to disassemble and clean the Handheld Vaccum Cleaner's Dustbin and Mesh Filter?

## Question

How to  disassemble and clean the Handheld Vaccum Cleaner's Dustbin and Mesh Filter?

## Answer

1. After the handheld vacuum cleaner stops working, press the button on the side of the dustbin to open the dustbin cover.
2. Take the mesh filter down and use a cleaning tool to sweep away the dust on the inner wall of the mesh filter.
3. Please use a wrung-out wet cloth to wipe the dustbin and rinse the mesh filter with room temperature water.
4. After cleaning, please dry all parts thoroughly and put them back together.
5. Cover the dustbin cover and hear a "click" to indicate that the installation is in place.
