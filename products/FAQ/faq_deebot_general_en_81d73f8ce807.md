# Video Manager Usage Guide

## Question

Video Manager Usage Guide

## Answer

1. Open the app →Click Enter → Tap the "Video Icon" in the top-right corner to access Video Manager. Press and hold the "Power Button" for 3 seconds to activate Video Manager.
Note:
1. Video Manager and Smart Cleaning cannot be used simultaneously, but you can switch freely between them.
2. Video Manager supports photo capture, video recording, one-way audio, whole-home patrol, precise navigation, and photo album management.
