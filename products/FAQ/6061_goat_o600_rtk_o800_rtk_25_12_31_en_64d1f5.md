# 我的花园里有狭窄通道，该如何绘制地图？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的花园里有狭窄通道，该如何绘制地图？

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

GOAT的狭窄通道通过能力为0.7米。在宽度小于1米且有高墙遮挡的狭长通道中，定位信号较弱，GOAT易发生卡困，请尽量贴边遥控。

GOAT's narrow path capability is 0.7 meters. In narrow paths less than 1 meter wide with high walls obstructing the view, positioning signals are weak, and GOAT is prone to getting stuck. Please operate it remotely while keeping it close to the edge.
