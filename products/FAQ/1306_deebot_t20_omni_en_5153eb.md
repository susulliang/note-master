# How do I charge my robot?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I charge my robot?

## 答案 / Answer

D20399

Charging

Robot charging instructions 
Remove the robot’s cover and power on the robot ("o" is off, "I" is on). 
1. Automatic robot docking and charging: 
Place the robot in front of the station, press and hold the robot’s AUTO button for 3 seconds to make the robot return to the station where it will dock and start charging. 
2. Manual docking and charging: 
When the battery level is low after power-on and the robot is unable to automatically return to the station to charge, you can manually dock the robot to charge it. 
If the robot cannot power on, manually push the rear end of the robot into the station cleaning sink. Once docked, a voice prompt will be broadcast and the robot will start charging. The robot’s AUTO Button will flash white. Check after 4–5 hours of charging. 
Note: It is recommended that the robot be fully charged before being used for the first time.
