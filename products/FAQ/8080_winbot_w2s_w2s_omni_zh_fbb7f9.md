## 答案 / Answer

How to use?

1.Open the product box, take out the robot and accessories in order, and remove all protective films and strips.
2.Before use, be sure to open the user manual and read the safety instructions carefully.
3.Take out the station and place it on the floor near an openable window to prevent the compound cable from being pulled.
4.Press and hold the station’s power button for more than 2 seconds until the power indicator lights up.
5.Slide the latch on the station to open the L-shaped cover. Remove the WINBOT from the station, peel off its protective films and strips, and take out the ECOVACS WINBOT cleaning solution.
6.Open the seal on the WINBOT's top water tank. Pour an appropriate amount of ECOVACS WINBOT cleaning solution into the tank, then tighten the seal.
Tip: Avoid overfilling the tank to prevent spillage.
7.Before cleaning, slide the switch and open the rear cover of the station to take out the wiping pad. It's recommended to wet the pad manually, wring it out, then attach it to the velcro at the bottom of WINBOT. Press it flat by hand.
8.To ensure safety, always secure the safety rope before cleaning to prevent accidents.
9.To start cleaning, press and hold the WINBOT’s power button for 2 seconds. Once the fan starts and the robot successfully attaches to the glass, cleaning will begin.
During use, please note:
1）WINBOT can only clean flat surfaces.
2）Make sure the glass thickness is not less than 3 mm.
3）For frameless glass, keep the attachment point at least 10 cm from the edge.
10.To pause cleaning, short press the station’s power button. WINBOT will stop and remain in place. Press again to resume the task.
11.After cleaning, WINBOT will automatically return to the starting point and emit a sound prompt.
12.To end cleaning manually, press and hold the station’s cable retract button for more than 2 seconds. WINBOT will stop and return to the starting point.
13.To remove WINBOT from the glass, firmly hold the handle, press and hold the power button for over 2 seconds, and follow the voice prompts to detach it safely.
