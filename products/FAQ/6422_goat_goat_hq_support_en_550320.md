# What type of SIM card do I need to purchase?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What type of SIM card do I need to purchase?

## 答案 / Answer

G00137

Disable

Additional Connection Module

The auto-connection module is compatible with regular SIM cards, so you are free to select the SIM card of your choice. However, when purchasing, please confirm with the operator whether the purchased SIM card needs to be set with an APN, and whether there is a PIN code. Please activate the SIM card before inserting the module and disable its PIN code. After inserting it, make sure to pay regular attention to the data usage.
