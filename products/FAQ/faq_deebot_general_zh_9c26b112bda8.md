# 机器人为什么没有按预约时间自动执行清洁工作？ Why didn't the robot automatically perform the cleaning work according to the scheduled time?

## Question

机器人为什么没有按预约时间自动执行清洁工作？                      Why didn't the robot automatically perform the cleaning work according to the scheduled time?

## Answer

可能有以下场景导致该问题：
（1）机器人没有处于开机状态；
（2）机器人处于勿扰模式，不响应预约；
（3）机器人电量不足，不执行工作；
（4）预约设定时，可能没有正确设置或者保存，请在APP中检查下；
如使用以上情况均已排除，但问题还未解决，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门。                                                                                               The following scenarios may cause this problem:
（1）The robot is not in the startup state.
（2）The robot is in Do Not Disturb Mode and does not respond to appointments.
（3）The robot has insufficient battery and is not performing its work.
  (4) When making an appointment, it may not be set correctly or saved. Please check it in the APP.
If all the above situations have been ruled out, but the problem has not been solved, please contact the Ecovacs after-sales services department.
