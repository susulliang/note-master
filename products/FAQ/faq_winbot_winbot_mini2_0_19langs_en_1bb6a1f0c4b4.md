# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 .0 19langs / EN - What is the charging status indicator?

## Question

What is the charging status indicator?

## Answer

When connected to the adapter, the mini 2 powers on and charges automatically. The indicator stays solid blue. It enters sleep mode and the light turns off after 20 minutes.
