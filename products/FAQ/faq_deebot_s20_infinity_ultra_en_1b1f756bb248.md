# DEEBOT / S20 Infinity Ultra / EN - How to solve the issue of drive wheels stuck？

## Question

How to solve the issue of drive wheels stuck？

## Answer

During cleaning process, the robot alarms running difficulties or slippery, etc., you can try as follows:
1.Slightly lift the side of the robot where the roller mop is installed, release the blue buckle on the water tray, and remove the water tray.
2.Remove the top cover of Robot, toggle the power switch to "O", remove the dirty water box and turn the robot over;
3. Press and turn the drive wheels to clear any tangles; 4. Wipe with a dampened and wrung out cloth or use a brush to remove surface stains.
5.Please reinstall the water tray onto the bottom of the robot. You should hear a "click" sound, which means it's securely in place.
