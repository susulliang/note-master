# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Why does the GOAT G1 fail to reposition and trigger mowing after I move it?

## Question

Why does the GOAT G1 fail to reposition and trigger mowing after I move it?

## Answer

Triggering a task after moving the GOAT requires repositioning first. For safety reasons, the GOAT G1 cannot be repositioned within 1.5 m from the map boundaries and restricted areas. You can move the GOAT to an open area far from the boundaries and try again
