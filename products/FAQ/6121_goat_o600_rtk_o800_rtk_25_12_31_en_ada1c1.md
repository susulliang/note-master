# 为何GOAT经常触发撞板异常故障？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为何GOAT经常触发撞板异常故障？

## 答案 / Answer

7- 主机其他

1.检查GOAT是否在基站内。若是，可能因回充时未及时停止、顶到基站导致撞板持续触发，只需将GOAT向后拉出一点即可恢复正常。
2.GOAT可能在工作中顶到障碍物无法脱困，导致撞板持续触发，请协助其脱困。
若以上情况均已排除但问题仍未解决，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门。

1. Verify if the GOAT is inside the station. If so, it may be continuously triggering collisions due to failing to stop promptly during recharge and bumping into the station. Simply pull the GOAT back slightly to restore normal operation.
 2. The GOAT may have collided with an obstacle while running and become stuck, causing continuous collision triggers. Assist it in escaping the obstacle.
 If the issue persists after ruling out the above scenarios, contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
