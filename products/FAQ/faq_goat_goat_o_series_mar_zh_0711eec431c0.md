# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - GOAT有工作时长限制吗？

## Question

GOAT有工作时长限制吗？

## Answer

There is no limit to the working hours set in the program of the GOAT. In theory, you could set the GOAT to mow 24 hours a day. When power is less than 15%, the GOAT will automatically return to the docking station to charge. When power reaches 80%, the GOAT will automatically head to the breakpoint location to continue mowing. However, in order to ensure better positioning and mowing results, as well as animal safety,  it's not recommended to mow in the rain or after dark.
