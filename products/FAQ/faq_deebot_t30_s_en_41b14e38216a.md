# How to correctly disassemble the mopping pad plate and clean the mopping Pad?

## Question

How to correctly disassemble the mopping pad plate and clean the mopping Pad?

## Answer

1. Disassembly and cleaning of the mopping pad plate
1.1 Please turn the main unit upside down and place it on a soft floor mat with the bottom facing up.
1.2 Hold the bottom of the mopping pad plate with both hands, lift it up slightly, remove the mopping pad plate, and tear off the washable mopping pad from the mopping pad plate.
1.3 The torn washable mopping pad can be cleaned and dried before reuse.
2. Mopping pad and plates installation
2.1 Please turn the main unit upside down and place it on a soft floor mat with the bottom facing up.
2.2 Align the mopping pad with the mopping pad plate and paste it flat.
2.3 Then install themopping pad back to the main unit, and be sure to hear a 'click,' it indicates that the installation is complete.
