# 14

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi

## 问题 / Question

14

## 答案 / Answer

If the robot's side brush gets tangled or you receive a "tangled side brush" alert after some use, try these steps:
1.Gently lift the side where the roller mop is installed, release the blue latch of the water tray, and remove the water tray.
2.Remove the robot cover, switch the power to "O" to turn it off, take out the dirty water box, flip the robot upside down.
3.Loosen the side brush screws with a small screwdriver, remove the brush, and clean the brush bearings and any debris using a cleaning tool. Ensure everything is cleaned thoroughly.
4.Align the brush with the slot, press down until you hear a "click," and then tighten the screws with the small screwdriver.
5.Reinstall the dirty water box.
 Note:
 Use an M3 Phillips screwdriver (recommended) to gently loosen the screws on the side brush (no need to remove them completely) to take off the brush. When reinstalling, make sure to align the brush with the screw holes and tighten the screws clockwise until the brush is secure and cannot turn, indicating it's properly installed. If the screws keep spinning without tightening, check if the screw holes are aligned correctly. Turn the screwdriver counterclockwise to remove the brush.
