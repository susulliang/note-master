# 启动拖地任务指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

启动拖地任务指导

## 答案 / Answer

1. 取下清水箱，打开水箱上盖并加水（清水箱加满不溢出即可）加完水后，盖紧水箱上盖并将清水箱装回基站。
 2.打开集尘仓盖，向外拉出清洁液盒。
 3.打开清洁液盒，然后将科沃斯专用清洁液适量倒入盒内。接着，紧密地合上上盖，并将其装入基站内（需推到底。确认安装到位）
 4.装回集尘仓盖，以确保完全密封。
 5. 确认主机开机后，放置基站正前方；长按主机“auto”键，让机器自主返回充电，对接成功后开始充电。
 6. 短按主机“启动/暂停“键启用边扫边拖任务。

1. Remove the clean water tank, open the tank lid, and add water (fill to capacity without overflowing). After filling, securely close the lid and reinstall the clean water tank onto the station.
2. Open the Dust Collection Cabin cover and pull out the Cleaning Solution box.
 3. Open the Cleaning Solution box and pour an appropriate amount of Ecovacs-specific Cleaning Solution into it. Then, securely close the lid and insert it into the station (push it all the way in. Ensure it is properly seated).
4. Replace the Dust Collection Cabin cover to ensure a complete seal.
 5. After confirming the ROBOT is powered on, position it directly in front of the station. Press and hold the “auto” button on the ROBOT to initiate autonomous return to charge. Charging will commence upon successful docking.
6. Briefly Press the “Start/Pause” button on the ROBOT to activate the simultaneous vacuuming and mopping task.
