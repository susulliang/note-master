# Why are new maps slanted?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why are new maps slanted?

## 答案 / Answer

D20268

Intelligence

(1) There are a lot of household items around the home. We recommend that you remove the items when creating maps. 
(2) It is recommended to start from the docking station (charging dock) when creating maps. 
(3) Try to delete the map and mapping again after moving the docking station (charging dock).
