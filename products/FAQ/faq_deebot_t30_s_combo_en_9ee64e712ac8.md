# DEEBOT / T30 S Combo / EN - How to disassemble/clean/install the side brush correctly?

## Question

How to disassemble/clean/install the side brush correctly?

## Answer

1. Press and hold the AUTO button to power off the robot. Turn the robot upside down, with the bottom facing upwards.
2. Remove the side brush and clean the side brush bearings and any debris entangled on the side brush using a cleaning tool. Ensure they are thoroughly cleaned.
3. Align the side brush with the slot and firmly press it down.Once you hear a 'click,' it indicates that the installation is complete.
