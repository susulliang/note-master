# GOAT 为何找不到基站或未正确对接基站？ / Why can't the GOAT find or be connected to the docking station correctly?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 为何找不到基站或未正确对接基站？ / Why can't the GOAT find or be connected to the docking station correctly?

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

（1）基站是否未通电或中途被断电，或基站是否故障：基站灯是否不亮
  *基站故障需联系科沃斯售后服务部门
（2）基站是否被移动了位置
（3）回充路线是否被阻断，基站周围2米内是否有障碍物
（4）基站内的白色反光膜是否被遮挡或损坏
（5）基站或GOAT上的充电极片是否有铜锈，若有请给基站断电，GOAT关机后擦拭
（6）基站附近是否存在与基站内白色反光膜类似的反光物体（如：打开的雨伞、反光强烈的金属栏杆、反光带、荧光材质的鞋子和鞋带、反光的镜子、带反光材质的路标等），对GOAT形成了误导
（7）GOAT主机的顶部环视摄像头以及前部的AI鱼眼摄像头是否清洁

如使用以上情况均已排除，但问题还未解决，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门

(1) Confirm if the docking station is faulty: Whether the docking station indicator is off. 
*In case of a docking station fault, ECOVACS Customer Care should be contacted. 
(2)  Check whether the docking station has been moved to another position. 
(3) Check whether the route for returning to charge has been blocked or there are obstacles within 2 meters of the docking station. 
(4) Check whether the white reflective film inside the docking station is covered or damaged. 
(5) Check if there is copper rust on the charging contacts on the docking station or GOAT. If there is, wipe it off. 
(6) Check if there are reflective objects near the docking station similar to the white reflective film inside the docking station (for example: deployed umbrellas, highly reflective metal railings, reflective straps, shoes and laces of fluorescent material, reflective mirrors, reflective signposts, etc.) that are misleading the GOAT. 
(7) Check if the Panorama camera or the AI camera at the front of GOAT is clean. 

If the issue persists,  please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
