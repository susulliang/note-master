# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T80S OMNI . languages / EN - Robot Network Connection Guide

## Question

Robot Network Connection Guide

## Answer

Download, register, and log in to the ECOVACS HOME App
1.Place the ROBOT near the station. Use your phone to scan the QR code on the ROBOT (Note: You can also download the ECOVACS HOME app from the App Store).
2. Install the app, register, then log in to the app's main interface.
Network Setup
1. Scan the ROBOT's QR code, enter your Wi-Fi password, and tap to connect.
2. Follow the app prompts: Lift the cover of the ROBOT, slide the Power Switch to "I" to turn on, and wait for the startup sound. Select "Next," briefly press the reset button, confirm you hear the "Bluetooth hotspot released" voice prompt, then select "Next" to initiate hotspot connection. Wait for network connection.
3. After connection is complete, you may proceed to name your DEEBOT.
Friendly reminder:
Ensure both your robot and the phone used for pairing are in an area with strong WiFi signal coverage during pairing.
