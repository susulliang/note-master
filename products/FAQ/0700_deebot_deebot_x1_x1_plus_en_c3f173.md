# What do I do if I don't want to enable YIKO?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What do I do if I don't want to enable YIKO?

## 答案 / Answer

D20279

YIKO Voice Assitant

Please disable the voice wake-up function in App ＞ Robot Control Interface ＞ Settings ＞ YIKO Voice Assistant ＞ Turn Off Robot Voice Wake-up. The Auto light will turn orange. The robot will be muted and unable to respond to YIKO commands.
