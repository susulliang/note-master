# How many sprays are in a 60 ml water tank? How big a house area can it serve?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How many sprays are in a 60 ml water tank? How big a house area can it serve?

## 答案 / Answer

W00096

Product feature

Using a glass pane of 1m x 1m as an example, a full reservoir can be used continuously to clean an areas of 20 square meter as per Ecovacs lab data( 
1. Approximately 60 minutes of continuous water spray on a 1x1 glass pane 
2. Approximately 3 minutes of cleaning on a 1x1 glass pane in Fast Cleaning Mode).
