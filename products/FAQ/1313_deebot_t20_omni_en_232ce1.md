# How to install my robot?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to install my robot?

## 答案 / Answer

D20346

Installation and Disassembly

1. Please open the packaging box, take out the robot, accessories and the station. 
2. Take off the DEEBOT cover, remove the DEEBOT anti-collision protection strips, and the protective things for the station. 
3. Flip DEEBOT over and place it bottom up. Remove the red and green side brushes from the accessory box, align the brushes with their mounting slots and push them in. You’ll hear a click when they are correctly attached. 
4. Remove the power cord from the accessory box, connect it to the power connector on the back of the station, switch on the power, and find a suitable location to place it against a wall. 
 (Place the station against a wall on a flat surface, leaving a clear space of 0.5m on the sides and 1.5m in front of the station.)
 
Tips: 
The station is preferred to be placed on tile floor when possible. If you place it on wood floor, it is suggested to regularly check whether there is residual water at the bottom of the station. If so, please clean it in time to keep it dry.
