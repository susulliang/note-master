# The voice prompt says that LDS component malfunction. What can I do?

## Question

The voice prompt says that LDS component
malfunction. What can I do?

## Answer

1.Flick the LDS assembly; observe if the LDS assembly rotates
2.Shut down and restart
Note: If it is still invalid, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
