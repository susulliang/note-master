# About the function of the robot's power button

## Question

About the function of the robot's power button

## Answer

1. Short press: start cleaning or pause/stop cleaning
2. Long press: 3 seconds and above, the robot switches on/off
3. Low-power mode: when the robot is on the AES/charging dock, long press for 20 seconds and above will trigger the low-power mode; the robot will enter the low-power mode after hibernating with full power, the indicator light will be off, WiFi will be disconnected, APP can not be manipulated, and it can be woken up by any button of the robot.
