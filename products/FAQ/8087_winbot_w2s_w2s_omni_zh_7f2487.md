## 答案 / Answer

Alert: Not enough air pressure

Warm Reminder:
When using the WINBOT, if you encounter a low air pressure warning, please first check the window environment. Gaps or low obstacles on the window surface may lead to insufficient air pressure. If you need to clean glass surfaces with large gaps or many obstacles, we recommend using the ECOVACS HOME App to manually control the WINBOT.
If the issue is not caused by the environment, you may try the following steps:
1.Use the ECOVACS HOME App to remotely move the WINBOT to a position where it can be safely retrieved.
2.Firmly hold the handle of the WINBOT, then press and hold the power button for 2 seconds until the fan stops. Carefully remove the WINBOT.
3.Make sure the cleaning cloth is properly aligned with the wide and narrow edges of the cloth bracket, and that it is securely attached.
4.After confirming everything is normal, press and hold the power button for 2 seconds again to restart the fan. Once successfully adhered to the surface, the WINBOT will start operation.
