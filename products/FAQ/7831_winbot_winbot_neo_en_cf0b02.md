# Do I need to differentiate between the front and back of the mopping pad? Can both sides be used for cleaning?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Do I need to differentiate between the front and back of the mopping pad? Can both sides be used for cleaning?

## 答案 / Answer

Both the front and back of the mopping pad have ultra-fine fibers attached to them and therefore can be used for cleaning. You do not need to differentiate between the front and back.
