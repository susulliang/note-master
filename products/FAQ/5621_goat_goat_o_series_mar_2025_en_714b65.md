# 为什么GOAT 在基站内自动关机？ / Why is GOAT shut down automatically when in the station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么GOAT 在基站内自动关机？ / Why is GOAT shut down automatically when in the station?

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

可能触发了“静默模式”，连续点击GOAT 主机上的开关键5次，机器将会进入静默模式，10s内自动关机。重新开机后自动解除

如非以上情况，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门。

The ECO mode may be triggered. Click the power button on the GOAT 5 times, and GOAT will enter silent mode and automatically shut down within 10 seconds. It will be automatically released after restarting.                                                                                                                           If the above situation does not apply,please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
