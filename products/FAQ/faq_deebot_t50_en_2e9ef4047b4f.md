# DEEBOT / T50 降配版 / EN - Will the camera lead to privacy leaks? (For high-end models only)

## Question

Will the camera lead to privacy leaks? (For high-end models only)

## Answer

The AIVI 3D module camera is designed specifically to identify and detect obstacles during the cleaning process, enabling more efficient and intelligent navigation. Please rest assured that your privacy is fully protected. We prioritize consumer privacy rights above all else and have implemented strict measures to ensure all data captured is used solely for improving cleaning performance.
