# 为什么我的GOAT出了外轮廓或者穿越了禁区？ / Why does my GOAT leave the map boundaries or enter the No-Entry zones?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么我的GOAT出了外轮廓或者穿越了禁区？ / Why does my GOAT leave the map boundaries or enter the No-Entry zones?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

（1）请检查RTK基站是否正确安装，如果RTK基站未按要求安装可能导致定位偏差，机器出界。
（2）如果工作时有小雨或者光线暗可能导致定位偏差，机器出界。建议在天气晴好时启动工作。
（3）基站周围2米内为回充默认通行区域，可能出现出界和穿禁区情况
（4）地图中如果存在鹅卵石路面可能造成GOAT打滑出界
（5）请确认充电基站位置是否产生移动，若产生移动，请恢复原位。
（6）请确认在无信号覆盖的范围内，是否地图边界或禁区边界在内，建议增加物理边界如高度＞20cm的栅栏，以防止机器出界或进入禁区。

A1) Please check whether the RTK have been properly installed. Incorrectly installed RTK may result in positioning errors. You can check the installation and then remap. 
(2) If there is light rain or low light conditions while working, it may cause localization errors. It is recommended to start the work in clear weather.
(3) By default, the area within 2 m around the docking station is a free area, so that the GOAT can return and recharge. This may cause the robot to leave the boundaries or enter No-Entry zones.
(4) The presence of cobblestone in the lawn could cause the GOAT to slip out of boundary.
(5) Please confirm if the charging station location has been moved. If it has been moved, please restore it to its original position or remapping.
(6) If within an area without signal coverage, with map boundary or no-go zone boundary included, it is recommended to add physical boundaries such as fences with a height of ＞20cm to prevent the machine from going out of boundary or entering no-go zone.
