# 机器人无法越过门槛/台阶，如何处理？ / What to do if the robot cannot cross thresholds or steps?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人无法越过门槛/台阶，如何处理？ / What to do if the robot cannot cross thresholds or steps?

## 答案 / Answer

1.请打开app-进入设备-点击“地图”-点击“门槛条”-在门槛位置添加“门槛条”
2.取下主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“O”关机，取下主机污水盒，将机器人翻转后，底部朝上放置；
3.请先清理驱动轮表面的灰尘污渍，同时留意蓝色攀岩爪处是否有异物卡住，若有请及时清理。
3.请按压驱动轮上方的触发扳机，同时顺时针缓慢转动驱动轮，观察蓝色攀岩爪是否能同步转动，若攀岩爪可正常转动，建议继续使用并观察运行情况；如无法正常转动，请联系售后处理。

1. Open the app ＞ Enter ＞ tap “Map” ＞ tap “Threshold Ramp” ＞ add a threshold ramp at the corresponding location.
 2.Remove the top cover, switch the power button to “O”, take out the dirty water box, and turn the robot over.
 3.Clean any dust or dirt on the drive wheels, and check whether there is any foreign object stuck around the blue climbing claws. If so, remove it promptly.
 4.Press the trigger above the drive wheel while slowly rotating the wheel clockwise. Observe whether the blue climbing claw rotates along with it.
 If the claw rotates normally, you can continue using the robot and monitor its performance.
 If it does not rotate properly, please contact after-sales service for assistance.
