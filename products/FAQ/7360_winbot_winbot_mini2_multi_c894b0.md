# How to Use the Reset Button?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How to Use the Reset Button?

## 答案 / Answer

Tip: 
 If the WINBOT freezes, use the Reset button to recover. Follow these steps:
 
 1. In standby mode, flip the WINBOT over and find the small “Reset” hole.
 2. Use a pointed tool, such as a toothpick, to press and hold the Reset button. Release it once the indicator turns off.
 3. Power the WINBOT off and on again to complete the reset.
