# Can I use WINBOT W2 on a coated surface?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I use WINBOT W2 on a coated surface?

## 答案 / Answer

W00163

WINBOT W2 is not suitable for use on coated surface as it may scratch the coating layer.
