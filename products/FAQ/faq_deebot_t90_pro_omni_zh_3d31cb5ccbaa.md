# Cleaning Guide for dirty water tank and clean water tanks

## Question

Cleaning Guide for dirty water tank and clean water tanks

## Answer

1. Remove the dirty water tank, open the tank lid, and empty the dirty water. (If residue remains at the bottom of the tank, use a cleaning brush to assist in thorough cleaning.) Replace the tank lid.
2. Remove the clean water tank, open the tank lid, and clean any dirt or residue if present. Replace the tank lid after cleaning.
 Note: After cleaning the dirty water tank, ensure the tank lid is securely closed.
