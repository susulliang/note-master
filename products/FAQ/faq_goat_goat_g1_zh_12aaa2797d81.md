# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - The indicator light on the docking station keeps flashing blue.

## Question

The indicator light on the docking station keeps flashing blue.

## Answer

When the GOAT is successfully connected to the docking station, the indicator light will continue to flash blue.
