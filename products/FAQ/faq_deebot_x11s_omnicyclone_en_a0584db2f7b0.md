# Alarm: Dirty water box abnormality

## Question

Alarm: Dirty water box
abnormality

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
If you encounter frequent returns to the station during operation, we recommend first powering off the robot, flipping it over, and then attempting he following steps:
1. Press the drain port at the bottom of the ROBOT to check for jamming or blockage by foreign objects. If present, clear them. Water should flow out when pressed under normal conditions.
2. Simultaneously press the charging contacts on both sides of the station for 1-2 seconds, then release and observe the station's drainage rod. Under normal conditions, the rod should move up and down smoothly.
