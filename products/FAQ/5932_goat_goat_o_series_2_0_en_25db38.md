# 冬季需要将基站拿回室内吗？ / Do I need to move the docking station indoors in winter?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

冬季需要将基站拿回室内吗？ / Do I need to move the docking station indoors in winter?

## 答案 / Answer

8- 保养维护

为了防止基站信号线和一些电子元器件被冻坏，建议冬天不使用GOAT时将其基站整体移入室内储存。
*基站的位置移动会导致机器定位异常，需要重新建图。冬季若将基站拿回室内保存，可以在摆放位置处做个标记，以待春季可以再次安装在原址。

To prevent the signal cable and certain electronic components of the docking station from getting frozen, we recommend that you move the entire docking station indoors for better storage in winter when the GOAT is not in use. 
*Moving the charging station may cause positioning errors and require re-mapping. In winter, if the charging station is brought indoors for storage, it is recommended to mark the placement location for reinstalling it in the same spot in spring.
