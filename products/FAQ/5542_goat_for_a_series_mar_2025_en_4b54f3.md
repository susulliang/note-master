# 我已将雨淋延时调整为雨停后立即开始割草，当前室外无雨为何GOAT未工作？ / I have adjusted the rain sensor delay time to start mowing immediately after the rain stops. Why is GOAT not working when there is no rain outside?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我已将雨淋延时调整为雨停后立即开始割草，当前室外无雨为何GOAT未工作？ / I have adjusted the rain sensor delay time to start mowing immediately after the rain stops. Why is GOAT not working when there is no rain outside?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

修改雨淋延时时间将在GOAT下一次触发雨淋时自动生效。如要立即生效可将雨淋关闭再打开，打开后将按最新设置的时间进行工作

Rain sensor delay time will automatically take effect the next time GOAT rain sensor works. To take effect immediately, turn the rain senser function off and on again on the APP. After opening, it will work according to the latest schedule.
