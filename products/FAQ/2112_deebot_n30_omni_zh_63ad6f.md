Robot Network Connection Guide

Download and register to log in ECOVACS HOME App.                                                                 1.Make sure the Robot is near the station. Scan the QR code to download and install the ECOVACS HOME App. You can also search it on the app market.                                            2.Register an account, then log in to the main interface of the app.                                                                                               Network Connection                               
1. Scan the QR code, input WIFI code, click to enter;
2.Follow the App prompts, long press the power button until the startup music starts, confirm and click NEXT;                                                                                                                                           3. Short press the spot cleaning button and return button at the same time . After hearing a voice prompt "Entering network setup mode", it starts the network connection;                      4.After it is completed, you can name the Robot.
