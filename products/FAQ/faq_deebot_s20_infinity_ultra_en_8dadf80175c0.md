# DEEBOT / S20 Infinity Ultra / EN - How to Properly Charge the Robot？

## Question

How to Properly Charge the Robot？

## Answer

1.Remove the robot's top cover and toggle the power switch to the "I" position to turn it on. You will hear the startup music and a voice prompt.
2.Place the robot directly in front of the station. Press and hold the "Auto button" to allow the robot to autonomously return to the base station. Once the robot has docked, a voice prompt will announce "Start charging".
