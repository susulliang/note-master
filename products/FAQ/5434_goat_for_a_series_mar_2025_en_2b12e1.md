# GOAT为什么充不进电？ / Why can't the GOAT charge?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT为什么充不进电？ / Why can't the GOAT charge?

## 答案 / Answer

Charging Station

（1）确保基站插座有电，GOAT正确放置在基站上，且处于充电状态：基站蓝灯常亮；语音播报充电中；显示屏显示充电中
（2）将GOAT再次推入基站；等待基站蓝灯常亮，显示屏上电池标记闪烁；等待GOAT语音反馈“开始充电”；
（3）检查充电接触片是否清洁。若充电接触片有铜锈可能导致无法充电
（4）当电池温度≤0℃和温度≥55°时将会触发电池高低温保护，无法进行充电；当电池温度恢复到3~55℃时将会解除故障继续工作（传感器读取存在一定的误差）
（5）电池充电环温范围 5-45°， 放电环温 0-45°,温度检测有±3°的误差
如使用以上方法仍不能排除故障，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门。

(1) Make sure that the docking station is powered on, the GOAT is placed correctly on the docking station, and is charging: The blue light on the docking station light up; The battery icon on the display flashs.
(2) Push the GOAT back into the docking station; Wait until the blue indicator on the docking station light up, and the battery icon on the display starts flashing；Wait until the GOAT says, "Start Charging"
(3) Check whether the charging contacts are clean. Copper rust on the charging contacts may cause charging failure.
(4) When the battery temperature is ≤ 0°C or ≥ 55°C, the battery will trigger high/low temperature protection and charging will fail; When the battery temperature returns to 3–55°C, the fault will be cleared and charging will continue (there is a certain degree of error in the sensor reading) 
(5)The range of ambient temperature for charging  is 5-45 ° C, and for  mowing is 0-45 ° C. There is an error of ± 3 ° in temperature detection.
If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
