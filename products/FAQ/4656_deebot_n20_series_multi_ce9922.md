How to clean dust container?

1.Press the container release button to take it out.                                                                      2.Open the cover,take out the cotton filter.                                                                            3.Press dust release toggle.                                                                                                           4.Rotate the multi-cyclone module to take it off ( it's not recommded to wash inside with water)                                                                                                                                               5.reinstall the dust container and put it back.
