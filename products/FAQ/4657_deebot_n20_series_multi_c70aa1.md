## 答案 / Answer

Why does the dust container of the station smell bad?

1. Due to the humid weather or inhaling too much wet garbage, the garbage in the container will become moldy and smelly after long-term storage, which does not affect the machine function. It is recommended that you dump the garbage in time after cleaning to ensure that the dust in the container is below the MAX line.
