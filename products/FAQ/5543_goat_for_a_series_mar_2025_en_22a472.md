## 问题 / Question

鱼眼AI摄像头是否会侵犯用户隐私？ / Do the AI camera violate the user's privacy?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

- GOAT 的鱼眼摄像头，主要用于割草过程中识别、检测障碍物以及草坪边界。请您放心，GOAT不会侵犯您的隐私，我们始终将消费者的隐私权利放在首位。同时也希望您不要在公共区域使用GOAT的视频和拍照功能，避免侵犯他人的隐私

The AI camera of Goat , which are mainly used to identify and detect obstacles and lawn boundaries during mowing.  You can rest assured that your privacy will not be violated. We always put the privacy of customers first. We also recommend that you do not use video & photo feature in public area to avoid violating the privacy of others.
