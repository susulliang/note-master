# If the Bluetooth connection fails or you exit the App while controlling the GOAT, will mapping be affected?

## Question

If the Bluetooth connection fails or you exit the App while controlling the GOAT, will mapping be affected?

## Answer

If the Bluetooth connection has failed for less than one hour during controlling, mapping can continue after re-entering the App to connect the GOAT using Bluetooth. If the disconnection exceeds one hour, the mapping task will be terminated.
