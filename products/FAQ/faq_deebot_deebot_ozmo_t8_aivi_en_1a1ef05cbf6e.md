# Why didn't DEEBOT start cleaning automatically at the scheduled time?

## Question

Why didn't DEEBOT start cleaning automatically at the scheduled time?

## Answer

(1) DEEBOT is not powered on.
     DEEBOT does not have enough battery power to clean.
     Schedule settings were not saved (DEEBOT can save up to 10 schedule entries). Please follow the instructions to reset the scheduled time.
(2) DEEBOT does not carry out scheduled cleaning while in Do Not Disturb mode.
(3) If the problem persists after following the steps above, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
