# 建图指导

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图指导

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

创建地图方式                                                                                                                                                                                                1、自动建图：GOAT会开启AI功能识别草-非草边界，自动沿着边界进行建图，过程中，可根据实际环境，随时切换为手动遥控模式，自定义地图边界进行建图；                                                                                                                                                                                                    2、手动建图：GOAT与APP通过蓝牙连接，请紧随GOAT行走，确保处于其6米半径范围内。若手机与GOAT的距离超出蓝牙连接范围（不同设备蓝牙能力不同），蓝牙将自动断开。                                                                                                                                                                               注意事项：                                                                                                                                                                                                   1.确认RTK信号状态正常，基站正确放置。
2.确认建图过程中不搬动或拖移机器，搬动机器会导致建图失败。建议修整花园中的坑洼区域，减少遥控过程中GOAT被卡困而需搬动或拖移的概率。
3.确认建图时光线充足且无雨。
4.确认已按照APP指示在遥控完成后正常闭合地图，遥控结束位置距离起点过远会导致地图无法闭合。

Mapping Methods 1. Automatic Mapping: GOAT activates AI to identify grass-non-grass boundaries and automatically maps along them. During this process, you may switch to manual remote control mode at any time based on the actual environment to customize the map boundary. 2.Manual Mapping: Connect GOAT to the app via Bluetooth. Follow GOAT closely while it moves, ensuring you remain within its 6-meter radius. If the distance between your phone and GOAT exceeds the Bluetooth range (which varies by device), the connection will automatically disconnect. Important Notes: 1. Verify the RTK signal State is normal and the station is positioned correctly.
 2.Ensure the machine is not moved or dragged during mapping, as this will cause mapping failure. We recommend leveling uneven areas in the garden to reduce the likelihood of GOAT getting stuck and needing to be moved or dragged during remote control.
 3. Ensure sufficient lighting and no rain during mapping.
 4. Confirm the map is properly closed after remote control as instructed by the app. Ending the remote control too far from the starting point may prevent the map from closing.
