# Should I use tap water or purified water?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Should I use tap water or purified water?

## 答案 / Answer

Do not use filtered or purified water, as the sensors cannot detect the water level. We recommend ECOVACS WINBOT Cleaning Solution. Tap water can be a temporary backup, but the specific solution provides superior cleaning results.
