# How do I install and remove the station base?

## Question

How do I install and remove the  station base?

## Answer

1. Align the base with the station and press both sides evenly with both hands until you hear a "click" sound to indicate that it is in place.
2. Slightly lift the entire base and gently lift the base upwards to remove the base.
