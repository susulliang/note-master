# Tips for the station placement of WINBOT W2 OMNI

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Tips for the station placement of WINBOT W2 OMNI

## 答案 / Answer

W00143

Tips for the placement of WINBOT W2 OMNI Station
1. Please try to place the station in a position close to the window.
2. If a larger floor-to-ceiling window needs to be cleaned, please make sure to place the station on the ground near the window opening to prevent the compound cable from being pulled tightly (the effective length of the compound cable is about 5.5 meters).
3. Please place the station on a flat surface (such as flat seamless floors, tiles, marble, etc.) to facilitate the suction power of the suction cup at the bottom of the station.
4. Do not place the station on uneven surfaces such as carpets, seamed tiles/floors, etc.
5. Do not place the station on a high surface such as a table, stand or shelf, etc., otherwise the counterweight and suction cup function of the station will be ineffective.
