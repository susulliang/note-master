# GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？ / Can the GOAT mow at night?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？ / Can the GOAT mow at night?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

（1）如果您在APP设置中关闭动物保护模式GOAT将会全天候工作，夜间割草GOAT视觉模块受光线影响无法正常工作，因此RTK信号弱的位置、草坪边界等危险区域将无法夜间割草，系统默认GOAT第二天早上8:00会主动触发补割。
（2）如需在夜间手动割草，也可开启遥控割草功能。

(1) If you turn off the Do not disturb mode in the Settings, GOAT will work all day, but the GOAT vision module will not work properly due to the influence of light. Therefore, the area with weak RTK signal as well as lawn boundary will not be able to mow at night. By default, GOAT will actively trigger mowing thiese areas at 8:00 am the next day. 
(2) If you need to manually mow at night, you can also enable the remote control mowing function.
