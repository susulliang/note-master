# The GOAT stopped in its original location without completing its learning.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

The GOAT stopped in its original location without completing its learning.

## 答案 / Answer

G00067

Mapping

(1) The GOAT will automatically return to the docking station when it detects a weak location signal in the event of heavy rain or weak lighting. 
 (2) Check the error message on the App. The GOAT may have encountered some errors and require assistance.
