# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 机器人在清扫状态时，上地毯为什么声音变大？Why does the sound become louder when the robot is cleaning the carpet?

## Question

机器人在清扫状态时，上地毯为什么声音变大？Why does the sound become louder when the robot is cleaning the carpet?

## Answer

（1）机器人默认开启“地毯自动增压功能”后，当机器人进入地毯区域时，会自动增大吸力，以达到更深度的地毯清洁。                                 After the robot defaults to enabling the "carpet automatic pressure boost function", when the robot enters the carpet area, it will automatically increase suction to achieve deeper carpet cleaning.
