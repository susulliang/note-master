## 答案 / Answer

What can I do if the mopping pad plates can't be lifted automatically?

1.Please remove foreign objects from the mopping pad plates.
2.Please refer to the APP carpet cleaning mode; set it to adaptive carpet mode.
3.Remove the mopping pad plates and reinstall them.
