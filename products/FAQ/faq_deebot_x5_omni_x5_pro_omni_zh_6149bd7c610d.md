# DEEBOT / X5 OMNI/X5 PRO OMNI / ZH - The driving wheels are stuck or not clean.

## Question

The driving wheels are stuck or not clean.

## Answer

1. Power off the robot and turn it over gently.
2. Press and rotate the driving wheels to clean the entanglement.
3. Press and rotate the driving wheels, and use the semi-dry mop to clean it.
