## 问题 / Question

在地图内触发了超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / Why is the GOAT alarms it goes beyond the srange of map when the mower is inside the courtyard?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

GPS模块偶尔存在信号漂移，可能导致一定概率的误报警。如果频繁误报警，请联系科沃斯售后

The GPS module occasionally experiences signal drift, which may lead to a certain probability of false alarms. If there are frequent false alarms, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service
