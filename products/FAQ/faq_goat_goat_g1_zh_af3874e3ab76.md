# Why does the GOAT not get out of the docking station and start mowing immediately after I tap Start?

## Question

Why does the GOAT not get out of the docking station and start mowing immediately after I tap Start?

## Answer

For better positioning, the GOAT will automatically clean the camera lens in the docking station before starting mowing. This will take approximately 20 seconds.
