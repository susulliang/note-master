# 地图出现异常(歪斜、重叠)的情况时如何处理? / What should I do if the map appears abnormal (skewed, overlapping)?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

地图出现异常(歪斜、重叠)的情况时如何处理? / What should I do if the map appears abnormal (skewed, overlapping)?

## 答案 / Answer

1.进入APP-地图管理-恢复地图,恢复至初始保存的地图,继续使用;
2.若初始保存的地图不满足需要,可删除地图,重新建图。

1. Enter the app, go to Map Management, select Restore Map, and restore the map to its initial saved version. Continue using the map.
2. If the initially saved map does not meet your needs, you can delete the map and create a new one.
