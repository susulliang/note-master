## 答案 / Answer

Why can't robots recognize carpets completely or judge carpets as floors?

（The robot cannot recognize some special materials and specifications of carpets. Please check if the carpets in your home have the following characteristics: high hardness (close to the hardness of wooden floors), short hair length below 2mm, or special materials such as PVC. (2) For areas of carpets that have not been recognized, virtual walls can be set in the map editing to prevent the robot from wetting the carpet.
