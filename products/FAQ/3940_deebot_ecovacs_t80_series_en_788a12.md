# How do I use the robot child lock feature?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I use the robot child lock feature?

## 答案 / Answer

1. If your robot has been connected to the network, you can open ECOVACS HOME＞Enter＞Settings＞Robot Advanced Settings＞Child Lock＞Can be turned on or off.
Note: When DEEBOT is in standby mode in the station, you can also turn on or off the child lock function through "YIKO voice assistant".
