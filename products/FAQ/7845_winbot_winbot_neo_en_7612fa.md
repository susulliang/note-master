# What is the walking route of WINBOT?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the walking route of WINBOT?

## 答案 / Answer

Fast mode: 
When the width of the window is more than 2 times that of the robot, WINBOT will complete a Z-shape route (twice in Deep Cleaning mode). 
When the width of the window is less than 1.5 times that of the robot, WINBOT will complete an N-shape route (twice in Deep Cleaning mode). 
When the width of the window is between 1.5 and 2 times that of the robot, the walking route will be depending on how the robot is placed.
