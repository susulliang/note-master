# Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

## Question

Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

## Answer

For better positioning and better security when the GOAT is at the docking station, do not place the docking station in a narrow passageway. There should be no obstacles within 2 m from the left, right and front of the station. It is recommended that the docking station is placed on a lawn with a flat area and wide visibility, and secured to the ground with the provided nails to prevent the station from moving.
