## 答案 / Answer

Why didn't the robot automatically perform the cleaning work according to the scheduled time?

The following scenarios may cause this problem:
The robot is not in the startup state.
The robot is in Do Not Disturb Mode and does not respond to appointments.
The robot has insufficient battery and is not performing its work.
(4) When making an appointment, it may not be set correctly or saved. Please check it in the APP.
If all the above situations have been ruled out, but the problem has not been solved, please contact the Ecovacs after-sales services department.
