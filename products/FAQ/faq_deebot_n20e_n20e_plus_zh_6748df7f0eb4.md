## Question

运行困难，请检查驱动轮如何处理?How to deal with the Robot gives an alarm "Difficulty in running, please check the drive wheels"?

## Answer

1. 长按启动键将主机关机，将机器翻转后，底部朝上放置；
2. 按压并转动驱动轮清理缠绕物；
3. 使用半干湿的抹布擦拭表面污渍。                                                                                                                                                1. Long press the power button to turn off the Robot, turn the Robot over.
2. Press and turn the drive wheel to clear tangles;
3. Wipe surface stains with a semi-dry mop;
