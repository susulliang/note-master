# 为什么我分割不成功？ / Why was my segmentation unsuccessful?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么我分割不成功？ / Why was my segmentation unsuccessful?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

为了保证割草效果：
（1）请你确保分割的区域长宽大于2m
（2）分割线的起点需要与明确的边界相交
（3）如果您使用遥控分割，请保证分割出来的区域都是闭合的

To ensure the effectiveness of mowing:
(1) Please ensure that the length and width of the segmented area are greater than 2m
(2) The starting point of the dividing line needs to intersect with a clear boundary
(3) If you use remote control segmentation, please ensure that the segmented areas has complete boundaries.
