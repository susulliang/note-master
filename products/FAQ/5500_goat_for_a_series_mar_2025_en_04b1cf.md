# 我可以远程遥控GOAT进行建图吗？ / Can I remotely control the GOAT for mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我可以远程遥控GOAT进行建图吗？ / Can I remotely control the GOAT for mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

在建图环节GOAT与APP仅靠蓝牙进行连接，当您的手机与GOAT的距离超过20m,蓝牙将会自动断开。为了保证安全，建议您跟随在GOAT后方6m进行遥控建图

The GOAT and the App are connected by Bluetooth only. When your phone is more than 20 m away from the GOAT, the Bluetooth connection will be automatically interrupted. For safety reasons, we recommend that you follow the GOAT within 6m for remote mapping.
