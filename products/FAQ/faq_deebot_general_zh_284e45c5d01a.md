# 在APP上地图出现异常（歪斜、重叠）的情况时如何处理？How to deal with abnormal situations (skewed, overlapping) on the map in the APP?

## Question

在APP上地图出现异常（歪斜、重叠）的情况时如何处理？How to deal with abnormal situations (skewed, overlapping) on the map in the APP?

## Answer

（1）可在APP地图管理中使用“恢复地图”功能，恢复出初始保存的地图，继续使用；恢复后的地图将丢失当前地图保存的预约、手动分区、虚拟墙等信息，需要重新设置；
（2）若初始保存的地图不满足需要，可删除地图，重新建图。                                                                                                                      （1）You can use the "Restore Map" function in the APP map management to restore the initially saved map and continue to use it. The restored map will lose the reservation, manual partition, virtual wall and other information saved in the current map, and needs to be reset.
（2）If the initially saved map does not meet the needs, you can delete the map and create a new one.
