# 我庭院中有部分区域割草方向与设置不符，是因为什么？ / Why is the mowing direction in some areas different from the setting?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我庭院中有部分区域割草方向与设置不符，是因为什么？ / Why is the mowing direction in some areas different from the setting?

## 答案 / Answer

RTK Reference Station

（1）Goat在无RTK信号的位置会用视觉传感器进行定位，保证割草效果
（2）在无信号区域会自行调整割草方向和割草策略。

(1) GOAT will use visual sensor to complementary positioning when RTK signal is affected in the shaded area.  
(2) In the poor RTK signal area, the GOAT will adjust the mowing direction according to the senario which will be slightly inconsistent with the setting.
