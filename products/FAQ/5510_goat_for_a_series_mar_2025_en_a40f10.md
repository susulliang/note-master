# GOAT有工作时长限制吗？ / Is there a limit on how long the GOAT can work?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT有工作时长限制吗？ / Is there a limit on how long the GOAT can work?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

GOAT的程序中没有设置工作时长的限制。理论上您可以设置GOAT进行24小时除草。当电量低于15%时GOAT将会自动返回充电，当电量达到85%时GOAT会自动前往断点位置继续割草。
但是为了保证更良好的定位和割草效果以及动物安全，我们建议您尽量不要在雨中或者在天黑后进行割草任务

There is no limit to the working hours set in the program of the GOAT. In theory, you could set the GOAT to mow 24 hours a day. When power is less than 15%, the GOAT will automatically return to the docking station to charge. When power reaches 85%, the GOAT will automatically head to the breakpoint location to continue mowing. 
However, in order to ensure better positioning and mowing results, as well as animal safety,  it's not recommended to mow in the rain or after dark.
