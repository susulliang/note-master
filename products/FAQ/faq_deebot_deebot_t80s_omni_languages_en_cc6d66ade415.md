# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T80S OMNI . languages / EN - How to clean the mop washing tray?

## Question

How to clean the mop washing tray?

## Answer

1.Remove the mop washing tray and take out the roller soaking tray.
2.Rinse the mop washing tray with water. After washing, wipe off any remaining water. Use running water to flush away dirt from the mop washing tray and the roller soaking tray. A cleaning tool can be used to help remove stubborn dirt. After cleaning, use a cloth to dry the water thoroughly.
3.Reinstall the roller soaking tray onto the mop washing tray and place it back into the station.
Note: Make sure the overflow float is properly installed.                                                                                    Friendly reminder: Cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
