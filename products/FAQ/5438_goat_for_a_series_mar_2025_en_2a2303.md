# RTK基站指示灯为什么会闪烁？ / Why does the RTK Reference Station light flash?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

RTK基站指示灯为什么会闪烁？ / Why does the RTK Reference Station light flash?

## 答案 / Answer

RTK Reference Station

（1）RTK基站是依赖GNSS/GPS信号，传递给主机进行定位的，指示灯闪烁表示当前位置GPS信号不佳，请确认是否有环境变化对RTK基站有遮挡？（房屋、树木、其他遮挡）。
（2）若无以上因素干扰，RTK基站持续蓝灯闪烁，可尝试重新给RTK基站上电。
若无法解决需联系科沃斯售后服务部门

RTK Re stations rely on GNSS/GPS signals to transmit to the host for positioning,
(1) The flash indicator indicates that the GPS signal of the current position is not good. Please confirm whether the RTK Reference Stationis blocked by houses, trees or other kind of shadows.
(2) If there is no interference from the above factors and the RTK Reference Stationcontinues to flash blue light, you can try to power on the RTK Reference Stationagain.
* If it cannot be resolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales Service
