# 搬动GOAT后触发割草，报重定位失败? / Why did the grass cutting report fail to locate after moving GOAT?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

搬动GOAT后触发割草，报重定位失败? / Why did the grass cutting report fail to locate after moving GOAT?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

1.搬动GOAT后触发工作需要先进行重定位
2.出于安规要求，GOAT 在外轮廓及禁区边界附近1.5m内无法进行重定位。
3.搬动机器后，请远离树木，房屋＞2m重新触发工作
*建议您可将GOAT搬至地图中远离边界的空旷处再次尝试

1.Triggering a task after moving the GOAT requires repositioning first.
2. For safety reasons, the GOAT cannot be repositioned within 1.5 m from the map boundaries and restricted areas.
3.After moving the GOAT, please stay away from trees and restart the work if the house is more than 2 meters away
* You can move the GOAT to an open area and try again
