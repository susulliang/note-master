# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T30 PRO OMNI Black / EN - How do I open the Dust Collection Cabin and replace the Dust Bag?

## Question

How do I open the Dust Collection Cabin and replace the Dust Bag?

## Answer

1.Remove dust collection cabin baffle;
2. Remove dust bag and discard;
3. Wipe the dust collection cabin with a dry cloth and install a new dust bag;
4. Install the dust bin baffle;
