# 实时视频无法打开或没有画面 / The live video is unable to launch or there is no image.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

实时视频无法打开或没有画面 / The live video is unable to launch or there is no image.

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

（1）wifi信号差，请检查主机的wifi连接情况，主机需要在wifi信号良好的地方才能开启实时视频
（2）当前为蓝牙模式，蓝牙模式下不支持开启实时视频
（3）鱼眼摄像头被遮挡，请移除遮挡物
若以上可能原因均已被排除，但问题仍未解决，请联系科沃斯售后

(1) The Wi-Fi signal may be poor. Please check the robot's Wi-Fi connection. The robot must have a good Wi-Fi connection to be able to launch the live video.
 (2) The robot may be in Bluetooth mode, which does not support live video. 
(3) The AI camera may be blocked. Please remove anything blocking the cameras. If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
