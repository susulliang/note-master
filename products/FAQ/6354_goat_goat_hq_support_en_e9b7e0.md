# Can the GOAT G1 be used during cold winters?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the GOAT G1 be used during cold winters?

## 答案 / Answer

G00069

Enable

Mowing

It is not recommended to mow frosted lawns, so we advise against mowing in winter. In addition, to ensure battery life, the GOAT G1 comes with high/low temperature protection, which will be triggered when the detected battery temperature is lower than 0° or higher than 55°. The robot will not be able to work under such conditions.
