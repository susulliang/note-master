# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 计划表中的割草进程被自动清空了？

## Question

计划表中的割草进程被自动清空了？

## Answer

(1) After the GOAT has been turned off and restarted, the mowing process in the schedule will be cleared.
(2) The GOAT has performed a firmware upgrade (check whether Silent Upgrade is turned on in the settings). It will automatically restart during the upgrade, which causes the mowing process in the schedule to be cleared.
