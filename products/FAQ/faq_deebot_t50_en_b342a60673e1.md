# DEEBOT / T50 降配版 / EN - Disassembly, Cleaning, and Installation of the Anti-Tangle Comb

## Question

Disassembly, Cleaning, and Installation of the Anti-Tangle Comb

## Answer

1.Remove the robot's top cover, toggle the power switch to the "O" position to turn it off, and then turn the robot over so its bottom faces upwards.
2.Simultaneously push the two release button on the main brush cover inward to release it. Remove the cover and take out the main brush.
3.Remove the anti-tangle comb. (Helpful Tip: The anti-tangle comb is washable. If you wash it with water, please ensure it is completely dried before reinstalling it.)
4.Reinstall the anti-tangle comb back into its original position.
5.After reinstalling the main brush, spin it gently with your fingers to ensure it rotates smoothly and freely.
6.Align the main brush cover with its slots and press down firmly until you hear a "click" sound, confirming it is securely attached.
Note: After installing the main brush cover, you can gently pull up on it to double-check that it is correctly and firmly in place.
