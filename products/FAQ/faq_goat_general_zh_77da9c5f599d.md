# GOAT 能对蹦床底部的区域进行除草吗？

## Question

GOAT 能对蹦床底部的区域进行除草吗？

## Answer

The GOAT is unable to cross obstacles more than 3 cm in height. The horizontal bar at the bottom of the trampoline may make the GOAT become stuck. It is recommended to delimit the trampoline area and similar entertainment facilities as No-Entry zones. However, you may still initiate manual mowing mode remotely to allow the GOAT to enter such areas for trimming.
