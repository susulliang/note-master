# WINBOT does not spray water.

## Question

WINBOT does not spray water.

## Answer

1. Please check whether there is cleaning solution in the water tank. Refill with special WINBOT cleaning solution in necessary.
2. Please check whether "Auto Spray" is turned off in the app.
3. If "Auto Spray" is turned on in the app and the water tank has cleaning solution, but the robot still does not spray water, it may be because water has been used up due to cleaning solution is not added timely when WINBOT works with a low water level. Please follow the auto water spray guidance in the app for troubleshooting.
