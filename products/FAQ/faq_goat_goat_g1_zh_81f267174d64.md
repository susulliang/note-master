# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Will the narrow passageways in my garden affect the installation of the navigation beacons?

## Question

Will the narrow passageways in my garden affect the installation of the navigation beacons?

## Answer

The positioning signal is weak in narrow passages with a width of less than 1.5 m that are blocked by high walls. It is also difficult for the GOAT to pass through. The navigation beacon should be placed at the entrance and exit of the narrow passageway instead.
