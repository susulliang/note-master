# 建图后为什么提示我面积过大？ / Why does it prompt me that the area is too large after creating the map?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图后为什么提示我面积过大？ / Why does it prompt me that the area is too large after creating the map?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

为了保证割草体验，增加了最大割草面积限制：
A1600 RTK：1920㎡
A2500 RTK :2750㎡
A3000 LiDAR :3300㎡

To ensure the mowing experience, we added a maximum mowing area limit:
A1600 RTK：1920㎡
A2500 RTK :2750㎡
A3000 LiDAR :3300㎡
