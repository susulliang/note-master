# GOAT 能否对蹦床底部区域进行除草？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 能否对蹦床底部区域进行除草？

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

GOAT无法越过高于3厘米的障碍物，蹦床底部的横杠可能导致其卡困。建议将蹦床及类似娱乐设施区域设为禁区，但您仍可启用手动除草模式遥控GOAT进入此类区域割草。

GOAT cannot overcome obstacles higher than 3 cm, and the crossbars beneath trampolines may cause it to become stuck. We recommend designating trampolines and similar recreational equipment as No-Entry zones. However, you may still manually control GOAT to enter such areas for mowing.
