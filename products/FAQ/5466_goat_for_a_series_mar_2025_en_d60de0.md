# 为什么自动建图无法创建整个草坪？ / Why can't auto mapping create the entire lawn?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么自动建图无法创建整个草坪？ / Why can't auto mapping create the entire lawn?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

（1）庭院中有多块草坪(两块草坪间隔之间非草＞1m、被障碍物、建筑物分隔)，自动建图时机器识别距离有限，仅识别当前草坪边界。
（2）可以当前草坪建完后，遥控到下一片草坪区域继续创建。

(1) There are multiple lawns in the courtyard (with a spacing of more than 1m between non lawns, separated by obstacles or buildings), and the GOAT only recognizing the current lawn boundary.
(2) After the current lawn is built, you can remotely controll GOAT to another lawn to continue mapping.
