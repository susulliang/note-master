# Why is the AIVI 3D module sometimes unable to avoid obstacles?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the AIVI 3D module sometimes unable to avoid obstacles?

## 答案 / Answer

D20266

Intelligence

(1) Dust may have accumulated in the AIVI 3D module. We recommend wiping it with a soft, dry tissue. 
(2) The AIVI 3D Smart Detection function is not enabled in the App. 
(3) It will occasionally lightly touch obstacles in order to improve coverage. This is normal. 
If DEEBOT bumps into furniture a lot, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
