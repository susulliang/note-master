# 机器人语音提示“基站清洁槽异常”如何处理？ / How to deal with the robot's voice prompting “Abnormal station cleaning sink”?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人语音提示“基站清洁槽异常”如何处理？ / How to deal with the robot's voice prompting “Abnormal station cleaning sink”?

## 答案 / Answer

1.检查溢水浮球安装到位
2.检查清洁槽可拆底座安装到位
3.重新插拔基站电源开关，再次使用观察

1.Check that the float is installed properly. 
2.Check that the mop washing tray is in place;
3.Reconnect power to the station and start a cleaning task to observe.
