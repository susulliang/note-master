# Robotic Crating Guidelines

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Robotic Crating Guidelines

## 答案 / Answer

1. Remove the top cover of the Robot, turn the power switch to "O" , and remove the side brush.
2. Remove the Robot dirty water box and empty it, remove two water tanks and empty them, then put them back.
3. Unplug the power cord from the base station and remove the base.
4.Smoothly place the Station into the packing box, then place a paper-moulded protective layer on top of the Station.
5. Gently place the Robot on top of the paper-mould, and then place the paper-mould protective layer to protect the Robot from damage. Put the removed accessories such as side brush, power cord and base back into the packing box in order.
