# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Why is the anti-theft alarm triggered when the mower is inside the map?

## Question

Why is the anti-theft alarm triggered when the mower is inside the map?

## Answer

G1&G1-2000 are equiped with GPS Module. It occasionally has signal drift which may lead to a certain probability of false alarms. If there are frequent false alarms, please contact ECOVACS customer service.
