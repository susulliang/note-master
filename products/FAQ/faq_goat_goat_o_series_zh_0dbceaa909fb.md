# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 工作日志显示总割草面积与单次割草面积相加值不一致？

## Question

工作日志显示总割草面积与单次割草面积相加值不一致？

## Answer

The work log shows that the calculation rule of the mowing area is rounded down, and the number after the decimal point is omitted, so the sum of the single mowing area and the total mowing area will be slightly different, about 1 to 2㎡.
