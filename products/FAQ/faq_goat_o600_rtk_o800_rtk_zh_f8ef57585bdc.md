# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 使用计划表割草时，若到结束时间，草未割完或未到结束时间时已完成，该如何处理？

## Question

使用计划表割草时，若到结束时间，草未割完或未到结束时间时已完成，该如何处理？

## Answer

1. Schedule tasks are weekly recurring tasks; mow automatically ends when the scheduled time is reached.
 1. If mow completes before the scheduled end time, GOAT will stop early and resume mow at the next scheduled start time.
