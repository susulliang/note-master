# 我的花园中WiFi信号较差，是否影响建图？ / My garden has a poor Wi-Fi signal. Will this affect mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的花园中WiFi信号较差，是否影响建图？ / My garden has a poor Wi-Fi signal. Will this affect mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

- 在建图环节GOAT与APP仅靠蓝牙进行连接，WiFi的强弱不会影响到建图

During the mapping process, the GOAT and the App are only connected via Bluetooth. The strength of Wi-Fi will not affect the mapping process.
