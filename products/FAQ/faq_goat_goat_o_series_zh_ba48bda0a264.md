# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 什么时候可以切换成自动建图？

## Question

什么时候可以切换成自动建图？

## Answer

During the remote mapping, GOAT will enable AI function to identify the boundary. If the AI finds that the boundary is a clear grass-non-grass boundary when remote control mapping for a while, it will prompt you to enable the auto mapping mode. You can choose whether to enable it.
