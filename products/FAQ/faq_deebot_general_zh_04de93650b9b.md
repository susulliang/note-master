# 机器人出现某些房间漏清洁的情况？ Did the robot leak cleaning in some rooms?

## Question

机器人出现某些房间漏清洁的情况？                                                    Did the robot leak cleaning in some rooms?

## Answer

（1）请确认房间门口是否放置了地毯或者地垫，机器人在扫拖模式下如果识别到地毯/地垫，会智能规避以防打湿地毯或者地垫，可能导致部分房间漏扫；
（2）机器人的越障高度为2cm（基于实验室数据），为了能够在家里顺畅通行，超过该越障高度的门槛或者台阶，机器人不会尝试通过；
（3）机器人拥有智能避障能力，有时为避免撞到走动中的人或者小动物，可能会导致部分区域漏扫。                                                              （1）Please confirm whether there is a carpet or floor mat placed at the door of the room. If the robot recognizes the carpet/floor mat in the sweeping and mopping mode, it will intelligently avoid wetting the carpet or floor mat, which may cause some rooms to miss cleaning.
（2）The obstacle-crossing height of the robot is 2cm (based on laboratory data). In order to pass smoothly at home, the robot will not attempt to pass through thresholds or steps that exceed this obstacle-crossing height.
（3）Robots have intelligent obstacle avoidance capabilities. Sometimes, in order to avoid hitting moving people or small animals, some areas may be missed.
