# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 PLUS / EN - Does the camera on the AIVI 3D module violate the user's privacy?

## Question

Does the camera on the AIVI 3D module violate the user's privacy?

## Answer

The AIVI 3D module has two cameras, which are mainly used to identify and detect obstacles for better cleaning. Your privacy will not be violated and we always put the privacy of customers first.
