## 问题 / Question

GOAT工作时，顶部的LiDAR 发出激光，这个LiDAR是哪种波段和频率，会对人的眼睛和身体造成伤害吗？ / When the GOAT is working, the LiDAR at the top emits laser. What is the wavelength and frequency of this LiDAR? Will it cause harm to human eyes and body?

## 答案 / Answer

不会
顶部的360° LiDAR是一类激光，不会对身体造成任何伤害

Will not cause any harm to the body
The laser level of the 360° LiDAR on the top is Class 1, will not cause any harm to the body.
