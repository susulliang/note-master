# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - I have installed the additional Connection Module. Why can't it be recognized?

## Question

I have installed the additional Connection Module. Why can't it be recognized?

## Answer

If you have correctly installed the module following the steps in the Instruction Manual, please try restarting the robot. If it still cannot be recognized after multiple restarts, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
