# How do I add cleaning solution?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I add cleaning solution?

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the dust collection cabin cover;
2.Pull out the entire cleaning solution unit;
3.Open the top lid, add cleaning solution (Use only Ecovacs Deebot cleaning solution and ensure the fill level does not exceed the dispensing outlet), securely close the lid, and reinstall the unit into the station;
4.Close the dust collection cabin cover;
5.Open the app and enable the "Auto Cleaning Solution Adding" feature;
Note: Cleaning solution is automatically dispensed. Do NOT add cleaning solution directly to the water tank.
