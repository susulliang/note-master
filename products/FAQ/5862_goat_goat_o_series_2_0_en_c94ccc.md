# GOAT 可以在寒冷的冬天使用吗？ / Can the GOAT be used during cold winters?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 可以在寒冷的冬天使用吗？ / Can the GOAT be used during cold winters?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

结霜后的草坪不适宜进行割草，因此我们不建议您在冬天进行割草。此外，为了保障电池寿命，GOAT有电池高低温保护，当检测到电池温度＜0°或＞55°时将会触发保护，无发继续工作。

It is not recommended to mow frosted lawns, so we advise against mowing in winter. In addition, to ensure battery life, the GOAT comes with high/low temperature protection, which will be triggered when the detected battery temperature is lower than 0° or higher than 55°. The robot will not be able to work under such conditions.
