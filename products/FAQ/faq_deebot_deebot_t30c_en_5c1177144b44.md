# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T30C / EN - Alarm: Difficulty running, check drive wheels

## Question

Alarm: Difficulty running, check drive wheels

## Answer

Warm Reminder：
During use, if you encounter a prompt operation difficulty or slip, etc. You can follow the following steps to try:
1.Please power off the Robot and turn it over;
2.Press and turn the drive wheels to clear tangles;
3.Use a semi-dry cloth to wipe or use a brush to remove surface stains.
