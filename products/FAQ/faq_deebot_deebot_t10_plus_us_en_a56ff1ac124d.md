# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T10 PLUS（US） / EN - Why are the walls of my home not showing up correctly on the 3D map?

## Question

Why are the walls of my home not showing up correctly on the 3D map?

## Answer

1. Non-standard walls (oblique, rounded or curved) cannot currently be displayed in the App. These may be displayed as parallel or perpendicular in the 3D map.
2. If you have finished mapping your entire home, but the internal walls are not being displayed correctly, this may be caused by location errors during mapping or other interference that affected mapping functionality, resulting in the walls being displayed incorrectly.
3. If the outer wall is displayed too small, you can edit the outer wall by going to the "Home Furnishing" page on the 3D map page and adjusting the walls that DEEBOT has mapped too small to the correct dimensions.
4. The internal walls cannot be edited.
5. You can choose to delete the map and recreate it.
