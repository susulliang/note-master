# DEEBOT / T30 S Combo / EN - How to charge a handheld vacuum cleaner?

## Question

How to charge a handheld vacuum cleaner?

## Answer

1. Press the fastening button to remove the suction head (such as gap brush, flat head brush, etc.)
2. Align the handheld vacuum cleaner with the station handheld base and start charging after inserting it
Kind reminder: The green battery grid flashes during charging, and the green light stays on when fully charged before going out.
