# WINBOT / WINBOT W1/W1 Pro / EN - How to clean the self-cleaning brush of the drving threads?

## Question

How to clean the self-cleaning brush of the drving threads?

## Answer

W1 PRO has drving threads self-cleaning feature. The cleaning brush does not need to be replaced after use. Ecovacs lab data show that the product can be used for more than 300 hours.
