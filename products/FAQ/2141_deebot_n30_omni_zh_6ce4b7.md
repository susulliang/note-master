## 答案 / Answer

How do I set up a virtual wall?

1. Click Map-Click Edit Map-Click Virtual Boundary. 
2. Two types of Virtual Boundary: No Entry Area & Mop-Only.      
3. While working, Robot will bypass the virtual boundary area.
