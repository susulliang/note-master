# 漏扫同一区域

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

漏扫同一区域

## 答案 / Answer

漏扫同一区域

① Check whether there is a carpet or floor pad at the door of the area. When the robot is in vacuum and mopping mode, in order not to wet the carpet or floor mat, it may give up cleaning the area.
②  Check whether the height of the threshold in the area exceeds 2cm, if there is a threshold or step that exceeds that height, the machine will not pass

If the verbal trick cannot solve the problem, please upgrade to HQ.
