# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 我可以在晚上或是雨天进行建图吗？

## Question

我可以在晚上或是雨天进行建图吗？

## Answer

The GOAT uses a vision localization system. Poor light or rain covering the camera may cause large misalignments. It is recommended that mapping is done on a bright, sunny day.
