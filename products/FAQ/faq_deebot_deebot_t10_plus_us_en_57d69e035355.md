# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T10 PLUS（US） / EN - DEEBOT's real-time cleaning route is chaotic.

## Question

DEEBOT's real-time cleaning route is chaotic.

## Answer

(1) Please check whether there is dust on the top lens cover of DEEBOT. If yes, please clean it.
(2) Please keep the room as bright as possible during the robot cleaning.
(3) It is recommended to  tidy the obstacles on the ground before cleaning.
(4) The cleaning route may be chaotic during the 3-5 times of map training because DEEBOT is exploring and learning the environemnt . After the training map is is successfully saved, the cleaning route will be more clear.
