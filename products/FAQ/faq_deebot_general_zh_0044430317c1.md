# 为什么有"哒哒哒"的异音? Why is there a strange sound of "da da da"

## Question

为什么有"哒哒哒"的异音?                                                            Why is there a strange sound of "da da da"

## Answer

(1)确认滚刷内是否有异物,当尘盒较满或者有异物卡在滚刷处,会有"哒哒哒"异音,建议及时将异物取出。                                                 （2）如果地板或地砖之间有较为明显的缝隙，机器人经过时可能会产生轻微的声音。为了提供更好的全屋清洁，机器人滚刷盖板是浮动的，机器人过门槛或底板缝隙时，产生轻微声音属于正常现象。                                                                                                                    (1) Confirm whether there are any foreign objects in the roller brush. When the dustbin is full or there are foreign objects stuck in the roller brush, there will be a "da da da" sound. It is recommended to remove the foreign objects in time. (2) If there are obvious gaps between the floor or tiles, the robot may make a slight sound when passing by. In order to provide better whole-house cleaning, the cover of the robot roller brush is floating. It is normal for the robot to make a slight sound when passing through the threshold or baseplate gap.
