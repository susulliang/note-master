## 问题 / Question

5

## 答案 / Answer

If your robot is unable to charge or power on, please follow these steps to attempt a recovery:
1.Verify that the power outlet is functioning correctly and that the station power cord is securely connected. The indicator light on the front of the station should be illuminated.
2.Remove the robot's top cover and toggle the power switch to the "I" position to ensure it is turned on. Press and hold the "Auto button" to allow the robot to autonomously return to and dock with the station.
3.If the robot cannot be powered on, you can manually place it into the station for docking. Once docked, if the "Auto button"  light begins to flash, it indicates that the robot is charging. You can also check the robot's status in the App; a lightning bolt icon will appear when it is charging.
4.If both automatic and manual attempts to dock and charge fail, please try the following steps:
① Clean the charging contacts on the station with a dry cloth, and gently press them to ensure they spring back properly.
② Clean the charging contacts on the robot.
After completing these steps, attempt to dock the robot again and observe if charging begins. If the issue persists, please contact our after-sales customer service for further assistance.
