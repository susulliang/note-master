# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to Charge the Station?

## Question

How to Charge the Station?

## Answer

1. Open the rear flip cover at the bottom of the station and remove the charging cable. Take out the black power plug, align the battery pack connector with the black socket, and connect it securely. Then place the connected battery pack steadily into the corresponding slot on the charging dock.
2. Next, plug the power cord into a household outlet. During charging, the small screen will display a charging animation.
