# 环境中哪些因素会导致无法使用自动建图？

## Question

环境中哪些因素会导致无法使用自动建图？

## Answer

1. Boundary-less lawns (e.g., lawns adjoining neighbors without separation)
 2. Two lawns separated by a path wider than 15cm (this scenario only supports creating a single lawn map)
 3. Lawns with numerous obstacles along the edges
