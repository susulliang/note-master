# About the function of the robot's charge button

## Question

About the function of the robot's charge button

## Answer

1. Short press: charge and pause charge;
2. Long press: when the robot is on the AES/charging dock, long press for 15 seconds to 25 seconds to restore the robot to the factory settings;
3. Long press: long press for 25 seconds or more while the robot is on the AES/charging dock to force the robot to shut down.
