# 地图学习中可以搬动GOAT吗？ / Can the GOAT be moved during map learning?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图学习中可以搬动GOAT吗？ / Can the GOAT be moved during map learning?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

地图学习时如果GOAT发生了卡困等故障，需要人为排障，但搬动会导致定位异常，需要回到充电站后重新启动学习。
（1）可遥控GOAT至基站正前方0.5m处，再次点击尝试自动回充
（2）若回充失败，请将GOAT手动对接到充电站内，并重新启动地图学习。
（3）建议将卡困区域清理障碍物或者设置禁区
（4）准备完成后，重新启动地图学习

If the GOAT gets stuck during map learning, manual troubleshooting may be necessary. However, moving the GOAT can lead to positioning irregularities, so it should be returned to the charging station and the learning process restarted.
(1) Remote control the GOAT to a position 0.5m in front of the base station, then try automatic recharging. 
(2) If automatic recharging fails, manually dock the GOAT into the charging station and restart map learning. 
(3) It is advisable to clear obstacles in the area where the GOAT got stuck or set up no-go zones. 
(4) Once ready, restart the map learning process.
