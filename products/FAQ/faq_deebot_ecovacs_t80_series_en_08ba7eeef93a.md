# DEEBOT / ECOVACS T80 Series / EN - How do I set cleaning sequence？

## Question

How do I set cleaning sequence？

## Answer

1. If your Robot has been equipped with a network, you can open Yeedi app>Enter>Settings>Robot Advanced Settings>Sequence>Click on the areas in order to set the sequence.
