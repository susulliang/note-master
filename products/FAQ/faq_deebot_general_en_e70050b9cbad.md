# Robot Packaging Guide

## Question

Robot Packaging Guide

## Answer

Important Note:
Before unboxing, make sure to complete the following steps: empty the dirty water tank and the cleaning solution tank, drain water from the ROBOT’s internal reservoir, and air-dry the damp roller mop.
1.Remove the ROBOT’s cover and switch the Power Switch to “O” to turn it off.
2.Remove the ROBOT’s dirty water tank and empty it. Remove the station’s clean and dirty water tanks, empty them, and reinstall them in their original positions.
3.Unplug the Power Cord and remove the base.
4.Place the station securely into the packaging box, then position the paper-plastic protective layer on top of the station.
5.Gently place the ROBOT on the protective layer, cover it with the upper protective layer to prevent damage, and return the removed side brushe,power cord, base, and other accessories to the packaging box in order.
