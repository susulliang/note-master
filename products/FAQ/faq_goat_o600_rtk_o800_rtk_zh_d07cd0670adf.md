# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 我已按APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回原位，为何APP上的故障仍未解除？

## Question

我已按APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回原位，为何APP上的故障仍未解除？

## Answer

You may attempt to restart the RTK station: simply power it off and then back on.
 *If the RTK station's blue light continues to flash persistently and the issue cannot be resolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
