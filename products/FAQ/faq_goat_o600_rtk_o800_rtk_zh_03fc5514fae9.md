# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - RTK基站指示灯为何闪烁？

## Question

RTK基站指示灯为何闪烁？

## Answer

1. Flashing Indicators indicate weak GPS signal at the current location. Check for new obstructions (e.g., buildings, trees) affecting the station.
 2. Ensure the RTK station is not tilted or displaced.
 3. Adverse weather (e.g., overcast conditions) may cause signal degradation. We recommend resuming observations after clear weather.
 4. Try restarting the RTK station: simply power it off and back on.
 5. Attempt to reset the RTK station and rebind it.
 *If the blue light on the RTK station continues to flash persistently and the issue remains unresolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
