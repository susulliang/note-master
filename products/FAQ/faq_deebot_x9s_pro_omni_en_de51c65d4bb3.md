# DEEBOT / X9S PRO OMNI / EN - What to do if the clean water tank does not dispense water?

## Question

What to do if the clean water tank does not dispense water?

## Answer

During mop cleaning and self-cleaning, the station does not dispense clean water
 1. Remove the clean water tank, clear any obstructions between it and the station, and ensure the tank is placed level.
 2. Verify the clean water tank seal cap is correctly installed and tightly secured.
 3. Open the clean water tank, remove the filter assembly, disconnect its connected water hose, and clean the filter assembly with a cleaning brush. After cleaning, reconnect the water hose, reinstall the filter assembly, and finally reinsert the clean water tank (alternatively, rinse the filter assembly with clean water).
