# DEEBOT / FAQ T50 / EN - How to deal with a robot that doesn't perform Auto Resume?

## Question

How to deal with a robot that doesn't perform Auto Resume?

## Answer

1.Check whether the Auto Resume is enabled;
2.Auto Resume won't be triggered during the Do not disturb mode time;
Note: Auto Resume function only takes effect in the auto cleaning mode;
