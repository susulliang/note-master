# Robot can't be charged, what can I do?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Robot can't be charged, what can I do?

## 答案 / Answer

Warm Reminder：
When your robot fails to charge, you can follow the following steps to try:
1.Please first make sure that the power outlet is powered. The indicator in front of the station will light up.
2.Press and hold the Auto button to turn it on, then press the recharge button to make it automatically return to the station to dock and charge.
3.If the Robot can not be turned on, you can manually move the robot to the station to dock directly, power button light blinking white indicates that it is charging
4.If manual or automatic charging is not possible, you can do so
①Please clean and wipe the charging contacts of the station and confirm that the rebound is normal.
②Please clean and wipe the charging contacts of the Robot.
After the above operations are completed, reconnect and observe. If the connection is still invalid, contact after-sales service.
