# 安装和拆卸回充托盘指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

安装和拆卸回充托盘指导

## 答案 / Answer

1.请将基站爬坡放置在基站所需的安装位置上，然后用双手均匀按压爬坡板的两边，直到听到“哒”的声音代表安装到位。
 2.请轻微抬起整个基站爬坡板，向上轻提爬坡板，可将爬坡板从基站上取下。

1. Position the station base at the desired installation location. Press evenly on both sides of the base with both hands until you hear a "click" sound, indicating it is securely in place.
2. Gently lift the entire base station, then carefully pull the ramp base upward to remove it from the station.
