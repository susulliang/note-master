# 禁区-哪些区域我需要创建禁区？ / 哪些区域我需要创建禁区？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

禁区-哪些区域我需要创建禁区？ / 哪些区域我需要创建禁区？

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

（1）泳池、沙坑等容易跌落的区域
（2）低于20cm，无围挡，可能造成误割的花圃菜地
（3）有凸出树根的大树，或根部处有斜坡的大树
（4）倾斜超过27°（50%）的斜坡或凹坑
（5）蹦床、秋千、滑梯等娱乐设置区域
（6）黑色的桌椅等户外家具
（7）易碰碎的透明物体
（8）地面上裸露的电线
**最小禁区限制长宽至少一边大于0.5m

(1) Areas where the GOAT is likely to fall, such as swimming pools and sandpits 
(2) Flower beds and vegetable plots less than 20 cm in height without containment, where the GOAT may mow by mistake 
(3) Areas where there are large trees with protruding roots or a slope at the root 
(4) Slopes or pits inclined more than 25° (45%) 
(5) Areas with recreational equipment, such as trampolines, swings, slides, etc. 
(6) Areas with outdoor furniture such as black tables and chairs 
(7) Areas with fragile, transparent objects 
(8) Areas with exposed wires on the ground

*The minimum no-go zone has a length and width of at least one side greater than 0.5m
