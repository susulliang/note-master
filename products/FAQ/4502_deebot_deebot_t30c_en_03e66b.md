# Dustbin Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation Guide

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Dustbin Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation Guide

## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
When your robot has been used for a period, if you find that the dustbin is full or the suction effect is not ideal, etc., you can try the following steps:
1.Remove the top cover of the main unit. Press the dustbin release button and remove the dustbin.
2.Remove the filter from the side.
3.Gently press the buckle on the side of the dustbin to open the filter net and empty the trash.(Tip: If the dustbin is dirty, rinse it and the filter with water.)
4.Reinstall the filter onto the filter net after cleaning. Press the buckle firmly until you hear a "click" sound, indicating proper installation.
5.Place the dustbin back into the Robot. Press until you hear a "click" sound, indicating proper installation. Reattach the top cover.
te: If cleaned with water, please shake off excess water and place it in a ventilated area to dry completely before use.
