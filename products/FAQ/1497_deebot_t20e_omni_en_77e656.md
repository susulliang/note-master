# Robot prompts that please gently tap the D-ToF Sensor. What should I do?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Robot prompts that please gently tap the D-ToF Sensor. What should I do?

## 答案 / Answer

D20387

Alarm

Robot prompts: "Please gently tap the D-ToF Sensor" 
1. Gently shake the D-ToF assembly. 
2. Shut down and restart. 
Note: If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
