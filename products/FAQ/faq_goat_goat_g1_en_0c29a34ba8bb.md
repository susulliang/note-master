# What type of battery does the GOAT G1 use? How long does the battery last?

## Question

What type of battery does the GOAT G1 use? How long does the battery last?

## Answer

The GOAT G1 uses a lithium battery. This battery has a lifespan of more than 1000 cycles. Under 1000 full charge cycles, it can still retain more than 70% of its original capacity.
