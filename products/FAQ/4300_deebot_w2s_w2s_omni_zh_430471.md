How to Connect WINBOT to the App?

1.Please slide the station latch to open the L-shaped cover, and take out the WINBOT.
2.Open your phone’s scanner to scan the pairing QR code inside the station, or search for “ECOVACS HOME” in your app store to download and register.
3.On the app’s home screen, tap “Add Robot” and scan the QR code again. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the WINBOT connection.
