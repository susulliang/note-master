# Why doesn't DEEBOT follow the set cleaning mode while cleaning?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why doesn't DEEBOT follow the set cleaning mode while cleaning?

## 答案 / Answer

D20041

Enable

Schedule

(1) When DEEBOT's battery level is low, it enters mode of Returning to the Charging Dock. Please check that you have enabled Continuous Clean in the App. 
(2) If enabled, DEEBOT will continue the task after charging completion. If disabled, DEEBOT will remain on the charging dock in Standby mode.
(3) If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
