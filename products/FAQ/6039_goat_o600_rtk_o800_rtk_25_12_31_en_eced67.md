# GOAT 为何找不到基站或未正确连接基站？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT 为何找不到基站或未正确连接基站？

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

（1）确认基站通电，没有中途断电或发生故障，基站指示灯是亮的  
*基站故障需联系科沃斯售后服务部门  
（2）检查基站位置是否被移动  
（3）检查回充路线是否被阻断，基站周围2米内是否有障碍物  
（4）检查基站上的白色反光膜是否被遮挡或损坏                                                          （5）检查基站或GOAT充电极片是否有铜锈。如有，请给基站断电，GOAT关机后擦拭  
（6）检查基站附近是否存在类似白色反光膜的反光物体（如：打开的雨伞、反光金属栏杆、反光带、荧光材质鞋/鞋带、反光镜子、带反光材质的路标等），对GOAT形成误导               （7）检查GOAT前部的AI鱼眼摄像头是否清洁                                                              （8）检查基站附近是否阴影区域过多；若是，建议将充电基站移至空旷区域，且尽量接近RTK信号良好的草坪区域（距离建议＜7米）  
（9）如您的机器为O600 RTK，请观察机器从基站前1米处开始回充时，补光灯是否亮起；若不亮，请直接联系售后  
   如以上情况均已排除但问题未解决，请联系科沃斯售后服务部门

（1）Verify the station is powered on, has not experienced power interruptions or malfunctions, and its Indicator is illuminated. 
 *For station malfunctions, contact Ecovacs after-sales service. 
 (2) Check if the station has been moved. 
 (3) Verify the return-to-charge path is unobstructed. Ensure no obstacles within 2 meters of the station. 
 (4) Inspect the white reflective film on the station for coverage or damage. (5) Check for copper corrosion on the station or GOAT charging pads. If present, power off the station and shut down GOAT before wiping clean. 
 (6) Check for reflective objects near the station that resemble the white reflective film (e.g., open umbrellas, reflective metal railings, reflective tape, fluorescent shoes/laces, reflective mirrors, road signs with reflective materials), which may mislead the GOAT (7) Check if the AI fisheye camera on the front of the GOAT is clean. (8) Check if there are excessive shaded areas near the station. If so, relocate the charging station to an open area, preferably close to a grassy area with good RTK signal reception (recommended distance ＜7 meters). 
 (9) If your unit is an O600 RTK, observe whether the Supplementary Light activates when the robot begins recharging from 1 meter in front of the station. If it does not illuminate, contact after-sales support directly. 
  If all above conditions are met yet the issue persists, please contact Ecovacs after-sales service.
