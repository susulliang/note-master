# 我已经根据APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回了原位，为何APP上的故障未解除？ / I have installed the abnormal RTK Reference Station back in place according to the APP prompt, why the fault on the APP has not been removed?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我已经根据APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回了原位，为何APP上的故障未解除？ / I have installed the abnormal RTK Reference Station back in place according to the APP prompt, why the fault on the APP has not been removed?

## 答案 / Answer

RTK Reference Station

请重新上电RTK基站后查看，RTK基站位置会在重启后更新。

Please disconnect the power supply of the RTK base station, and then check again after it is powered on. The position of the RTK base station will be updated.
