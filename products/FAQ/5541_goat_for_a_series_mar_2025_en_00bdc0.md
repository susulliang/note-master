# 当前室外无雨，为何GOAT未解除雨淋保护？ / There is currently no rain outside. Why is the rain protection mode still on?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

当前室外无雨，为何GOAT未解除雨淋保护？ / There is currently no rain outside. Why is the rain protection mode still on?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

（1）请确认APP中是否开启了雨淋延迟，GOAT默认雨淋保护打开后会在雨停三个小时后解除保护，您可以APP中修改时间
（2）请确认GOAT机身上的雨淋传感器是否干燥
（3）为了保护草坪，不建议雨停后立即割草。雨淋传感器比较灵敏，雨停后需要一定的时间去感应环境湿度情况，如果有割草需求，请关闭雨淋传感器，手动触发继续割草

(1) Please confirm whether the rain delay setting has been enabled in the App. By default, if the rain protection mode is enabled, it will be lifted three hours after the rain stops. You can modify the time in the App. 
(2) Please confirm whether the rain sensor on the GOAT is dry.
(3) In order to protect the lawn, it is not recommended to cut the grass immediately after the rain stops. The rain sensor is sensitive. After the rain stops, it takes a certain amount of time to sense the humidity of the environment. If you need to mow, turn off the rain sensor and manually trigger to continue mowing
