# Why does the device leak garbage after being removed from the pool?

## Question

Why does the device leak garbage after being removed from the pool?

## Answer

Please check if the inlet at the bottom of the filter basket is blocked by objects, as this may cause debris to leak out from the bottom. We recommend cleaning the device thoroughly after each use to prevent clogging. We apologize for any inconvenience this may have caused you; it will work smoothly again after proper cleaning.
