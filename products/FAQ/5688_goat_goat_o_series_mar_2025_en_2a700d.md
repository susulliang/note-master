# 建图中为什么没有自动生成通道？ / Why doesn't the channel generate automatically on the map during mapping?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图中为什么没有自动生成通道？ / Why doesn't the channel generate automatically on the map during mapping?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

1. 新增的区域与老区域相连会直接融合
2. 未从已知区域出发
3. 出发的区域已经有15条通道

1. The new area is directly integrated with the old area  
2. Not departing from a known area  
3. There are already 15 passageways in the departure area
