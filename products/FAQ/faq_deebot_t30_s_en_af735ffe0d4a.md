# DEEBOT / T30 S / EN - How to clean the cleaning sink?

## Question

How to clean the cleaning sink?

## Answer

1. Press the filter assembly release button to remove the filter assembly.
2. Wash the filter assembly thoroughly; after cleaning, ensure the filter assembly is completely dry.
3. Install the filter assembly back into the station cleaning sink; press the release button of the filter assembly downward, and listen for the 'click' sound to confirm that the installation is secure.
