How to correctly install the robot?

1.Please take out the Robot, Auto-Empty station,base, and remove protective strip.
2.Turn the Robot over.
3.Take out the side brush, align it with the slot, press it down. A "click" sound indicates it has been installed properly.                                                                        4.Connect Auto-Empty station with the base.                                                                                                                                            5.Find a suitable position to place it against the wall and plug in.
