# What should I do if the robot misses cleaning?

## Question

What should I do if the robot misses cleaning?

## Answer

1. How can I tell if the machine has missed cleaning some area?
1-1: Same area:
① Please first confirm whether the carpet cleaning strategy in the APP is set to avoid carpet mode. If so, the machine will give up cleaning the room with carpets or floor mats at the door. Please change the mode to adaptive carpet mode and try;
② Check whether there is a thick carpet or floor mat placed at the door of the area, or a high threshold. If the height or thickness exceeds 2cm, the machine will not try to pass through and will give up cleaning the area. It is recommended to move it;
1-2: Different areas:
① Please do not follow the machine when it is working to prevent the machine from missing cleaning due to avoiding obstacles;
② If there are many items in the environment, it is recommended that you sort them out before cleaning;
2. The machine can’t get into a low space or a narrow space, resulting in missed cleaning. How to handle it ?
The height of the robot is 10.4cm, and the actual minimum height range that can be passed is between 10.4 and 15.5cm. Different home environments may be affected. In order to reduce collisions for your robot, the robot adopts the TureDetect 3D obstacle avoidance technology . It can detect and avoid obstacles on the ground, and sense the height of the furniture from the ground and the width of the aisle. If it is larger, it will enter cleaning, if it is smaller than the size, it will change the movement . It is recommended that you tidy up a little before cleaning;
