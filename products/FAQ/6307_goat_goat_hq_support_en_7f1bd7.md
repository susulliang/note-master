# My garden is not big. Why are several navigation beacons recommended?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

My garden is not big. Why are several navigation beacons recommended?

## 答案 / Answer

G00022

Disable

Navigation Beacon

The GOAT needs to work in an area with double coverage of the navigation beacon antenna signal to ensure good positioning accuracy. Plants, metal, walls and other objects more than 60 cm in height may cause signal blockage. You may have a U-shaped/O-shaped garden or a large number of obstructions blocking the signal, so additional navigation beacon antennas are required to meet the signal coverage.
