## 答案 / Answer

Robots cannot cross the 20mm threshold

The obstacle-crossing height of 20mm was measured under the standard conditions of the Ecovacs laboratory. Due to differences in the home environment of different users, factors such as the shape, width, and angle of the threshold will have a certain impact on the obstacle-crossing effect of the robot. If it cannot be solved, please purchase the threshold strip on the Ecovacs official store.
(2) The driving wheel may come into contact with pet and human hair during operation, which will affect the obstacle crossing performance. It is recommended to use a wet cloth/wet wipe to clean the driving wheel when the obstacle crossing fails; it is recommended to clean it regularly in daily use to achieve better obstacle crossing performance.
