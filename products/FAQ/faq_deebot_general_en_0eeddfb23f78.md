# No water coming out of the clean water tank, what should I do?

## Question

No water coming out of the clean water tank, what should I do?

## Answer

1.Please check whether there are any foreign objects between the clean water tank and the station. If so, please remove them and put the clean water tank back and make sure that it is placed flat.
2.Please make sure that the cleaning tank sealing plug  is installed correctly and fits tightly.
3.Please open the water tank and check whether the water inlet filter is dirty or blocked. If so, remove it and rinse it with water. After cleaning, please make sure to put the filter back in place.
