# 机器人在地毯上行走会有抖动的情况？Will the robot shake when walking on the carpet?

## Question

机器人在地毯上行走会有抖动的情况？Will the robot shake when walking on the carpet?

## Answer

（1）机器人在地毯上逆毛行走时会出现轻微的抖动；
（2）机器人在地毯边缘行走时（一个驱动轮在地板上，另外一个驱动轮在地毯上），会出现较为明显的抖动。不用担心，此时机器人是在努力清洁地毯边缘的灰尘和垃圾，回到平坦地面时，机器人会恢复正常。                                                                                                          （1）The robot will experience slight shaking when walking against the hair on the carpet.
（2）When the robot walks on the edge of the carpet (with one driving wheel on the floor and the other on the carpet), there will be obvious shaking. Don't worry, the robot is trying to clean the dust and garbage on the edge of the carpet. When it returns to a flat ground, the robot will return to normal.
