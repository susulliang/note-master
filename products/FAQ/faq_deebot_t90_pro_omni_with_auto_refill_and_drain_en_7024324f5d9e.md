# DEEBOT / T90 PRO OMNI With Auto Refill And Drain / EN - Dust Bin Removal, Cleaning, and Installation Guide

## Question

Dust Bin Removal, Cleaning, and Installation Guide

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
After using your robot for a period of time, if you notice the Dust Box is full or the vacuuming performance is unsatisfactory, we recommend turning off the device first, then following these steps:
1. Press the Dust Bin Release Button and remove the Dust Bin.
2. Gently press the latch on the side of the Dust Bin to remove the top cover, then empty the contents.
3. Remove the Filter Net and Filter (Note: If the Dust bin is heavily soiled, rinse the Dust bin and Filter with water).
 4. After cleaning, reinstall the Filter onto the Filter Net first. Then place the Filter Net assembly back into the Dust Bin. Press down until you hear a “click” to confirm proper installation.
5. Replace the Dust Bin lid. Insert the Dust Bin into the ROBOT and press until you hear a “click” to confirm it's securely in place. Finally, reattach the ROBOT's Top Cover.
 Note: Components can be rinsed with water. Ensure they are completely dry before reuse.
