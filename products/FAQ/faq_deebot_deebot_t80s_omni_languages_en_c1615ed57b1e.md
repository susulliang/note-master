# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T80S OMNI . languages / EN - Side Brush removal, cleaning, and installation

## Question

Side Brush removal, cleaning, and installation

## Answer

Friendly Reminder:
If you notice debris tangled in the Side Brush or receive a “Side Brush Entanglement” alert after using your robot for some time, please follow the steps below:
1.Remove the cover of the ROBOT and switch the Power Switch to “O” to power it off. Remove the dirty water box, then flip the ROBOT over.
2.Use a Phillips screwdriver to loosen and remove the Side Brush screw. Clean the brush bearings and remove any tangled debris using the cleaning tool.
3.Align the Side Brush with the mounting slot, press it down until you hear a “click,” and then tighten it securely with a Phillips screwdriver.
4.Reinstall the dirty water box.

Note:
It is recommended to use an M3 Phillips screwdriver. Gently loosen the Side Brush screw (no need to remove it completely) before taking off the brush.
When reinstalling, make sure the screw holes are properly aligned. Turn the screwdriver clockwise until the Side Brush is firmly secured and no longer rotates freely.
If the screw keeps spinning without tightening, check the screw alignment and adjust as needed by turning the screwdriver counterclockwise to remove and reinstall the brush.
