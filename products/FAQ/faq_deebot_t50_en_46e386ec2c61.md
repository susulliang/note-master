# DEEBOT / T50降配版 / EN - Why is my carpet not displayed on the 3D map?

## Question

Why is my carpet not displayed on the 3D map?

## Answer

1.The robot defaults to the Adaptive Carpet Detection strategy. The carpet will be detected and displayed on the map after it has completed one full cleaning cycle of your home.

2.In Quick Mapping mode, the robot prioritizes speed and does not traverse the entire home environment in detail. To ensure carpet detection, please tidy away any clutter from the floor. With the robot's default Adaptive Carpet Detection strategy, the carpet will be displayed after it completes one full cleaning pass.
