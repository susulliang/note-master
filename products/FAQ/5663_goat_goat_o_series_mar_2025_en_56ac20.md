# 我可以在任意位置创建禁区吗？ / Can I create a restricted area anywhere?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我可以在任意位置创建禁区吗？ / Can I create a restricted area anywhere?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

基站周围2m为GOAT回充默认通行区域，建议尽量不要在基站附近建立禁区，在基站前方设置大面积的禁区可能会导致GOAT无法回充。此外若禁区轮廓之间或其与外轮廓之间的间距小于0.7m将会形成狭窄通道，可能导致该区域漏割

1.The area 2 m around the docking station is the default access area for the GOAT to return to charge. It is recommended that no restricted area be set near the docking station. Setting a large restricted area in front of the docking station may prevent the GOAT from returning to charge.                                                                 2. In addition, if the distance between the restricted area outlines or the restricted area outline and the outer outline is less than 0.7 m, a narrow passage will be created, which may lead to missed mowing in that area.
