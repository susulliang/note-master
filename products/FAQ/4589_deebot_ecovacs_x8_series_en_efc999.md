2.Remove the top cover, slide the power switch to “I” , and long press the power button to make it automatically return to the station to dock and charge.
3.If it can not be turned on, you can manually move the robot to the station to dock directly, power button light blinking white indicates that it is charging，and there will be a lightning icon in the app.
