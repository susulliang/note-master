# The GOAT returns to the charging dock before it has finished learning.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

The GOAT returns to the charging dock before it has finished learning.

## 答案 / Answer

G00066

Mapping

(1) The map is large and the GOAT cannot go through the whole area in one learning session. The GOAT needs to return to charge halfway and will continue to learn when the battery level is sufficient. 
 (2) Check the App and the notifications to see if the GOAT is experiencing any abnormalities or faults. 
 (3) The GOAT will automatically return to the docking station when it detects a weak location signal in the event of heavy rain or weak light.
