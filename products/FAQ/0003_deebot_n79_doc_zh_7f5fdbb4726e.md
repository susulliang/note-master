# N79系列区别

1\.  N79 和 N79s的区别  
The DEEBOT N79S is an upgraded version of the DEEBOT N79\. The N79S features a Max Mode suction option which allows you to increase its suction power by 50% based on your cleaning needs\.

2\. N79S 和 N79SE的区别  
Absolutely nothing\! The N79S is sold on Amazon\. The N79SE is sold at Best Buy\.

3\.    N79型号和DN型号的对应查询  
N79S or N79SE is labeled as DN622\.11  
N79 is labeled as the DN622

4\. N79w 和 N79s 的区别  
Aesthetics – The most profound difference between the two vacuums is color\. N79S has been finished in black while the N79W is white\. Clean button – While the interface remains the same with the Deebot and Ecovacs inscriptions, the Clean button is different
