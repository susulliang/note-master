# GOAT可以割高草吗？ / Can the GOAT cut tall grass?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT可以割高草吗？ / Can the GOAT cut tall grass?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

GOAT无法良好切割高于15cm的草，且过高的草可能被识别为障碍物，请确保草坪中的草已修整至10cm以下进行日常割草维护，如果＞12cm，可以尝试使用强劲割草模式。

The GOAT is unable to cut grass higher than 15 cm well. Taller grass may be recognized as an obstacle, so please make sure that grass has been trimmed to 10 cm or lower.
if grass higher than 12 cm，you can try Enhanced Mowing.
