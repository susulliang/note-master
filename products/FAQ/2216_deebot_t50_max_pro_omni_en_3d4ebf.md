# 清水箱不出水，该如何处理？ / No water coming out of the clean water tank, what should I do?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

清水箱不出水，该如何处理？ / No water coming out of the clean water tank, what should I do?

## 答案 / Answer

1.请检查清水箱与清洁座之间是否有散落物品掉落，若有，请清理后重新放置清水箱，以确保放置平整。
2.请确保清洁座上的清水箱密封塞已正确安装且紧密贴合。
3.请打开清水箱，检查进水口过滤件是否附有脏污或堵塞物。若有，可将其拆卸并清理，清洗完毕后，请确保将过滤件归置回原位，并装回清水箱。

1.Please check whether there are any foreign objects between the clean water tank and the station. If so, please remove them, then put it back, make sure that it is placed flat.
2.Check if the cleaning tank sealing plug  is installed correctly and fits tightly.
3.Please open the water tank and check whether the water inlet filter is dirty or blocked. If so, remove it and clean it. After cleaning, please make sure to put the filter back in place.
