# 我可以晚上使用自动建图吗 / Can I use automatic mapping at night or on rainy days?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我可以晚上使用自动建图吗 / Can I use automatic mapping at night or on rainy days?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

不可以，自动建图依赖视觉融合定位技术，需保证光线较好，下雨天或晚上会产生定位偏差导致建图失败

No, automatic mapping relies on visual fusion positioning technology, which requires good lighting. Positioning deviations may occur on rainy days or at night, causing mapping failure.
