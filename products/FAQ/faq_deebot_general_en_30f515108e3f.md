# V-shaped Comb Teeth Removal, Cleaning, and Installation

## Question

V-shaped Comb Teeth Removal, Cleaning, and Installation

## Answer

1.Remove the cover of the ROBOT. Turn the Power Switch to "O" to power off. Remove the ROBOT's dirty water box. Turn the machine upside down.
2. Simultaneously press both clips on the main brush cover toward the center, remove the cover, and take out the main brush assembly;
3. Remove the V-shaped comb teeth (Note: Can be rinsed with water; ensure it's completely dry before reuse).
4. Reinstall the V-shaped comb teeth.
5. After reinstalling the Main Brush, manually rotate it to ensure smooth movement.
6. Align the Main Brush cover with the machine's slots and install it. A "click" sound indicates proper installation.
7. Reinstall the ROBOT's dirty water box.
Note: The cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
