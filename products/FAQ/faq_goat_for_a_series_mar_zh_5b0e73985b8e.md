# GOAT / for A series Mar. / ZH - 其他APP相关

## Question

其他APP相关

## Answer

(1) Ensure that the region you select when logging in to the APP is the same as the region where the host currently resides. The APP may be unavailable after the network is configured across regions.
(2) Check whether the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up. If not, it means the GOAT is disconnected from the router. If the Wi-Fi icon on the GOAT display is lit up, the router may be malfunctioning.
(3) Check whether the Wi-Fi signal is strong enough. Try completing network setup again after turning on Bluetooth.
