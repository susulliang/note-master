# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 如何切换冬令时与夏令时？

## Question

如何切换冬令时与夏令时？

## Answer

1. You can select your time zone in "Settings" > "More Settings" > "Robot Info" > "Time Zone".
 2. Alternatively, set your phone's system time zone to your local time zone, then select "Sync Phone Time Zone" in ECOVACS HOME App under "Settings" > "More Settings" > "Robot Info" > "Time Zone".
 3. After modifying the time zone, times in schedules and logs will update accordingly.
