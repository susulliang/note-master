# 我为何无法闭合禁区？ / Why am I unable to close a No-Entry zone?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我为何无法闭合禁区？ / Why am I unable to close a No-Entry zone?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

禁区支持的最小直径为50cm，请检查遥控的禁区是否太小

The minimum diameter of a No-Entry zone is 50 cm. Please check whether the remote control No-Entry zone is too small.
