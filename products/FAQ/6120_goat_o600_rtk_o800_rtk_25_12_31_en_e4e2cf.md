# 为什么无法配网？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么无法配网？

## 答案 / Answer

6- APP其他

1.检查GOAT显示屏WiFi图标是否亮起，确认Wi-Fi信号强度是否足够。若无WiFi图标显示，说明GOAT已与路由器断连；若有WiFi图标显示，可能是路由器故障。
2.配网时，需在机器显示屏上长按“CHARGING”键，待屏幕倒计时结束后，方可进行配网操作。

1. Check if the WiFi icon on the GOAT display is lit to confirm sufficient Wi-Fi signal strength. If no WiFi icon appears, the GOAT has disconnected from the router; if the icon is present, the router may be malfunctioning.
 2. During network setup, press and hold the "CHARGING" button on the machine's display until the countdown ends before proceeding with configuration.
