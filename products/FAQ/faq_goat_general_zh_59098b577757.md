# 我花园里WiFi信号较差，会影响建图吗？

## Question

我花园里WiFi信号较差，会影响建图吗？

## Answer

During the mapping process, GOAT communicates with the app solely via Bluetooth. The strength of the WiFi signal does not affect mapping.
