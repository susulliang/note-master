# 边刷拆卸，清理及安装？ / Side Brush Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

边刷拆卸，清理及安装？ / Side Brush Removal, Cleaning, and Reinstallation

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
当您的机器人使用一段时间后，若您发现边刷上被缠绕东西或提示边刷被缠绕时等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
1.取下主机正面上盖，将电源开关至“O”关机，取下主机污水盒，将机器翻转，其底部朝上放置；
2.使用十字小螺丝刀将边刷螺丝拧松后取下边刷，使用清理工具清理边刷轴承，边刷上的缠绕物等，并确认清理干净。
3.把边刷对准卡槽后，向下按压听到“咔”的声音，再使用十字小螺丝刀拧紧。
4.重新装回主机污水盒。
注：
使用M3十字螺丝刀（推荐），轻轻拧松边刷上的螺丝（无需完全拧下），即可将边刷取下。在安装时，请确保边刷螺丝口对准，顺时针旋转螺丝刀直至边刷稳固无法转动，即表示安装到位。若螺丝持续空转，请检查螺丝口是否已正确对准。逆时针转动螺丝刀可拧下边刷。

Friendly Reminder:
 If your robot's side brush gets tangled or you receive a "tangled side brush" alert after some use, try these steps:
 1.Remove the robot cover, switch the power to "O" to turn it off, take out the dirty water box, flip the robot upside down.
 2.Loosen the side brush screws with a small screwdriver, remove the brush, and clean the brush bearings and any debris using a cleaning tool. Ensure everything is cleaned thoroughly.
 3.Align the brush with the slot, press down until you hear a "click," and then tighten the screws with the small screwdriver.
 4.Reinstall the dirty water box.
 Note:
 Use an M3 Phillips screwdriver (recommended) to gently loosen the screws on the side brush (no need to remove them completely) to take off the brush. When reinstalling, make sure to align the brush with the screw holes and tighten the screws clockwise until the brush is secure and cannot turn, indicating it's properly installed. If the screws keep spinning without tightening, check if the screw holes are aligned correctly. Turn the screwdriver counterclockwise to remove the brush.
