# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T10 PLUS（US） / EN - Why is the AIVI module sometimes unable to avoid obstacles?

## Question

Why is the AIVI module sometimes unable to avoid obstacles?

## Answer

(1) Dust may have accumulated in the AIVI  module. We recommend wiping it with a soft, dry tissue.
(2) The AIVI Smart Detection function is not enabled in the App.
(3) It will occasionally lightly touch obstacles in order to improve coverage. This is normal.
If DEEBOT bumps into furniture a lot, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
