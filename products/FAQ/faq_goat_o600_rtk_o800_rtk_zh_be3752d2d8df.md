# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？

## Question

GOAT 可以在夜间割草吗？

## Answer

You may disable Animal Protection Mode to enable GOAT for 24/7 operation. However, insufficient nighttime lighting may impair visual recognition, and RTK signal strength weakens. Consequently, GOAT will not perform automatic mowing tasks at night in areas with weak RTK signals or near hazardous zones like lawn boundaries. The system defaults to automatically initiating a catch-up mowing program at 8:00 AM the following day.
 2. If nighttime mowing is required, you may manually activate remote control mow.
