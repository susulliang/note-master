# How to clean the self-cleaning brush of the drving threads?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to clean the self-cleaning brush of the drving threads?

## 答案 / Answer

Winbot neo has drving threads self-cleaning feature. The cleaning brush does not need to be replaced after use. Ecovacs lab data show that the product can be used for more than 300 hours.
