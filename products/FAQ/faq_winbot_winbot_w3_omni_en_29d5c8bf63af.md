# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - Alert: Cleaning compartment wastewater overflow. Please clean up.

## Question

Alert: Cleaning compartment wastewater overflow.
Please clean up.

## Answer

1.Remove the Dirty Water Tank and open its lid. Check whether there is any debris around the seal ring; if present, wipe it clean. If none is found, ensure the lid clip is securely fastened.
2.Remove the sealing plug, clean it, and then reinstall it, making sure no foreign objects are present between the Dirty Water Tank and the station. Place the Dirty Water Tank back in position and press down firmly to ensure it is properly installed.
3.Press the "Open Cleaning Compartment" button on the station. Check whether the drain outlet below the cleaning compartment is blocked by any debris, and clean it if necessary.
