# After the WINBOT battery is low, connect the power cord to charge. After a while, all the indicator lights go out. What is the status of the robot at this time?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

After the WINBOT battery is low, connect the power cord to charge. After a while, all the indicator lights go out. What is the status of the robot at this time?

## 答案 / Answer

If you wonder the current charging status, you can power on the WINBOT. A blinking blue indicator means that WINBOT is charging. A solid blue indicator means that WINBOT is fully charged.
