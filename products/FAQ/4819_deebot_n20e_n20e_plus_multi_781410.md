## 答案 / Answer

The host mapping is incomplete/no partition is generated, what should I do?

Try to open the door as much as possible when cleaning the mainframe map to avoid missing rooms during the mapping process.
It is recommended to clean up ground debris during mapping to ensure that the robot can travel to various areas.
Suggest deleting the map in the app and creating a new one.
