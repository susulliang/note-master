# Robot Charging Guide

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Robot Charging Guide

## 答案 / Answer

Robot charging instruction
Connect the adapter, keep the area around the charging dock/AES clear of objects ( within 1m left and right and 2m in front) especially objects with reflective surfaces, long press the power button for 3 seconds to turn on the robot.
1. Automatic docking charging:
Place the robot right in front of the charging dock/AES, short press the charging button to make robot return for charging automatically;
2. Manual docking charging:
If the power is low after switching on and cannot return automatically, you can use manual docking charging.
Please move the robot to the charging dock/AES and make sure that the charging contacts at the bottom/on the back of the machine(AES) is successfully docked.
