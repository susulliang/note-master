# GOAT / GX / ZH - How can I do when the Auto-Connection service expires?

## Question

How can I do when the Auto-Connection service expires?

## Answer

Ecovacs provides three years of Auto-Connection service. We will launch the renewal service in the future. At that time, you can check the relevant information on the official website to renew the service.
