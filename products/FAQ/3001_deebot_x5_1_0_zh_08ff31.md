# When cleaning, why does the mopping pad not always extend? / 1. When DEEBOT is in Mop Only mode or Vac and Mop mode, the mopping pad will normally extend outwards when cleaning along edges or in a straight line to achieve full coverage (it will not extend when zigzagging). 2. The mopping pad plate will retract when it is lifted, including in the following scenarios: quick mapping, DEEBOT navigation, crossing rooms, returning for mop washing/charging, avoiding obstacles, moving onto carpets, suspending/ending task and moving machine;

## 问题 / Question

When cleaning, why does the mopping pad not always extend? / 1. When DEEBOT is in Mop Only mode or Vac and Mop mode, the mopping pad will normally extend outwards when cleaning along edges or in a straight line to achieve full coverage (it will not extend when zigzagging). 2. The mopping pad plate will retract when it is lifted, including in the following scenarios: quick mapping, DEEBOT navigation, crossing rooms, returning for mop washing/charging, avoiding obstacles, moving onto carpets, suspending/ending task and moving machine;

## 答案 / Answer

1. When DEEBOT is in Mop Only mode or Vac and Mop mode, the mopping pad will normally extend outwards when cleaning along edges or in a straight line to achieve full coverage (it will not extend when zigzagging). 2. The mopping pad plate will retract when it is lifted, including in the following scenarios: quick mapping, DEEBOT navigation, crossing rooms, returning for mop washing/charging, avoiding obstacles, moving onto carpets, suspending/ending task and moving machine;
