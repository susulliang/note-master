# Voice prompt: Bumper is stuck. How to resolve?

## Question

Voice prompt: Bumper is stuck. How to resolve?

## Answer

1. Remove the ROBOT's collision protection strip.
2. Gently tap the left, center, and right areas of the bumper to confirm normal rebound.
3. Power off and restart the unit, then observe.
