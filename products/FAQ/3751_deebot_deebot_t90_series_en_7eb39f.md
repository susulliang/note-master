# 报警：污水箱异常

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

报警：污水箱异常

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 当您在使用过程中若您有遇到提示污水箱未安装或水满时，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
 1. 打开水箱上盖，倾倒污水。（若水箱底部残留污垢，可使用清理刷，辅助清理干净）
 2. 检查污水箱内的浮漂在位，确保其被拨动后能正常回落，表明状态正常。
 3. 将污水箱的上盖重新合上，并确保卡扣已牢固安装到位。
 4. 将污水箱重新安装回基站内。

Friendly Reminder:
If you encounter a prompt indicating the dirty water tank is not installed or full, please follow these steps to resolve the issue:
1. Open the dirty water tank lid and empty the contents. (If residue remains at the bottom, use a cleaning brush to assist in thorough cleaning)
2. Check that the float inside the dirty water tank is properly positioned and can return to its normal position after being moved.
3. Replace the dirty water tank lid and ensure the latches are securely fastened.
4. Reinstall the dirty water tank into the station.
