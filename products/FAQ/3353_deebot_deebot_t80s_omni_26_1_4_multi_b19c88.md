# 8

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

8

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the Clean Water Tank. Open the tank lid and fill it with water up to the maximum fill line.
2.Open the Cleaning Solution package, measure approximately 20ml of Cleaning Solution into the cap, then add it to the clean water tank.
3. Securely close the water tank lid and reinstall the Clean Water Tank into the station.
4. Ensure the ROBOT is powered on and place it directly in front of the station. Press and hold the "auto" button on the ROBOT to initiate autonomous return for charging. Charging will begin upon successful docking.
5. Briefly press the "auto" button on the ROBOT to activate the simultaneous sweeping and mopping task.
Friendly Reminder:
Use only Ecovacs Robot-branded Cleaning Solution
