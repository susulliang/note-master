# 自动建图过程中，我可以按照我的想法修改自动建图的轨迹吗？ / Can I modify the trajectory of automatic mapping according to my own ideas during the process?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

自动建图过程中，我可以按照我的想法修改自动建图的轨迹吗？ / Can I modify the trajectory of automatic mapping according to my own ideas during the process?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

可以
（1）自动建图过程中，您可以根据实际情况，随时中断自动建图，使用遥控继续绘制边界
（2）已经绘制的边界轨迹部分，您可以使用橡皮擦功能回退

Yes
(1) During the automatic mapping process, you can interrupt the automatic mapping at any time according to the actual situation and continue drawing boundaries using the remote control
(2) You can use the eraser function to rewind the boundary trajectory that has already been drawn
