# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 基站安装后还可以更改摆放位置吗？

## Question

基站安装后还可以更改摆放位置吗？

## Answer

You can adjust the docking station location at your discretion before mapping. Changing the docking station location after the map is generated will cause the positioning signal to move and will require remapping.
