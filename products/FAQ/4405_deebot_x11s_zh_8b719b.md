> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

7

## 答案 / Answer

1. Open the APP - Click "Enter" - Click "Agent Hosting" - Click "Edit".
2. In the chat box, tell Agent YIKO your cleaning preferences via voice or text input.
3. Wait for the AI to generate a corresponding plan. You can click the "Accept" or "Refresh" button to save or regenerate the plan.
4. After clicking the "Accept" button, the corresponding plan will be displayed as a preference tag in the list at the top of the chat box.
To delete a preference tag: Click the edit button - Uncheck the "√" - Click "Save".
TIPS: Cleaning preferences set using Agent YIKO are only effective in Agent Hosting mode.
