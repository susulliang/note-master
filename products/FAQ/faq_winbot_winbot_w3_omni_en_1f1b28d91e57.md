# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to Remove and Wash the Wiping Pad?

## Question

How to Remove and Wash the Wiping Pad?

## Answer

1.Turn the winbot over to expose the wiping pad. Pinch the clips on both sides of the pad, pull downward, then lift upward to remove the wiping pad.
2.Press the "Open Cleaning Compartment" button on the station and place the wiping pad into the corresponding slot.
3.Close the compartment door, then select "Standard Wash" or "Deep Wash" on the station to wet the wiping pad.
4.After washing, remove the wiping pad and reinstall it by aligning the wide edge and the grooves for the driving wheel tracks.
Tips: The wiping pad and the wiping pad plate are an integrated design; the pad cannot be removed separately.
