# DEEBOT / T50 降配版 / EN - How to Charge the Robot？

## Question

How to Charge the Robot？

## Answer

When charging the robot, please ensure the base station is connected to a power source. Remove the robot's top cover and check that the power switch is set to the correct position ("O" for off, "I" for on).

Automatic Docking for Charging:
Place the robot directly in front of the base station. Press and hold the "Auto" button on the robot for 3 seconds. The robot will autonomously return to the base station. Charging will begin once docking is successful.

Manual Docking for Charging:
If the robot's battery is too low to power on and automatically return to the base station, you can manually dock it for charging.
Carefully push the rear of the robot into the base station's washing tray until it makes contact with the charging terminals (you may gently wiggle it side to side to ensure proper alignment). Once successfully docked, a voice prompt will announce "Charging started." The "Auto" button light on the robot will flash white during charging. For optimal performance after a low battery manual dock, please allow the robot to charge continuously for 4-5 hours before use.

Note: Before using the robot for the first time, it is recommended to fully charge it. A full charge takes approximately 5.5 hours.

Charging Status: White light flashes.

Charging Complete: White light stays on briefly, then turns off.
