## 答案 / Answer

Tip: The omni station suction pad is mechanically linked to the handle.
It locks and releases automatically — no manual operation needed. Before use, make sure there are no walls or furniture within 10 cm of the station. The handle must drop fully to ensure proper suction. Obstructions may weaken suction.
1.Place the station down and release the handle. The suction pad will engage automatically.
2.To move the station, lift the handle. The suction pad will release on its own.
