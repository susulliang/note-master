# 我抬起了GOAT ，为何GOAT 没有被立即锁定并告警？ / I lifted up my GOAT. Why did it not immediately lock and sound the alarm?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我抬起了GOAT ，为何GOAT 没有被立即锁定并告警？ / I lifted up my GOAT. Why did it not immediately lock and sound the alarm?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

（1）请确认是否在“设置”-“机器人设置”-“安全设置”中打开了“抬起报警”，若没有打开则当机器在待机或者任务暂停时被抬起不会触发报警
（2）若工作中被抬起，为了避免误触发，GOAT会进行10s的自主脱困，10s后会自动锁定机器并告警

(1) Please confirm whether the related alarm is turned on in Settings ＞ Robot Settings ＞ Security Settings. If it is not turned on, the alarm will not be triggered when the robot is on standby or the task is suspended. 
(2) If it is lifted during operation, the GOAT will automatically self-escape for 10 seconds to avoid any false triggering. Then it will automatically lock and sound the alarm after 10 seconds.
