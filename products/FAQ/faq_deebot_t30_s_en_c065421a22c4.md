# How to deal with a robot wetting the carpet while mopping the floor?

## Question

How to deal with a robot wetting the carpet while mopping the floor?

## Answer

The robot washes the carpet while mopping the floor
During the cleaning process, if there is a medium-length carpet in the house, it may get wet.
1. You can try to open the APP > enter Smart Cleaning > Map > Map Edit > Virtual Wall to make relevant settings.
Why does the carpet get wet?
When the machine is cleaning, it will only perform the rag lifting action after the ultrasonic wave recognizes the carpet. At this time, the rag lifting requires a response time, but the machine will not stop walking, so the machine will walk a short section on the carpet before you see the rag lifting. This may cause the carpet to get wet.
