# What is the signal coverage of the Navigation Beacon?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the signal coverage of the Navigation Beacon?

## 答案 / Answer

G00018

Enable

Navigation Beacon

The signal coverage of a single Navigation Beacon is a circle with a radius of 45 m with the beacon at the center. Objects above 60 cm in height, such as plants, metal, walls, etc., may cause the signal to be blocked.
