# How to download the ECOVACS HOME App?

## Question

How to download the ECOVACS HOME App?

## Answer

Scan the QR code on the robot or search the ECOVACS HOME App in Google Play or Apple App Store, and follow the instructions to download and install the App.

Tips:
* Please follow the guidance in the App to complete the registration, login and WiFi connection.
* Make sure your phone is connected with your home WiFi before adding a robot.
