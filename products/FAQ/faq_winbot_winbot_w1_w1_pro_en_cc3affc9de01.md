# WINBOT / WINBOT W1/W1 Pro / EN - Does the mopping pad have to be wrung? What is an acceptable level of moisture?

## Question

Does the mopping pad have to be wrung? What is an acceptable level of moisture?

## Answer

Too much moisture in the mopping pad will affect the window cleaning effect, may leaving water marks or water stains or even causing WINBOT to slip or get damaged. It's suggested to manually wet the WINBOT mopping pad, wring it dry as much as possible, attach it to the bottom of the robot and smooth it out before beginning cleaning.
