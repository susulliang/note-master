# How do I get my windows cleaner?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How do I get my windows cleaner?

## 答案 / Answer

W00147

The cleanliness of your windows depends on the frequency you wipe them. If you clean the windows infrequently or the surface is heavily soiled, using a window cleaning product may not get them completely clean. Here are some tips:

1. Use the deep cleaning mode of WINBOT W2 to clean heavily soiled surface and repeat the process if necessary.
2. Replace and wash the wiping pad as soon as it becomes dirty to ensure effective cleaning.
3. For external windows, it's recommended to use deep cleaning mode for a more fine and thorough cleaning. For internal windows that are relatively clean, the fast mode is recommended for a more time-efficient and effective cleaning.
