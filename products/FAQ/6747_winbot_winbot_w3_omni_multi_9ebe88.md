## 答案 / Answer

Reminders:
Before boxing, please ensure you have completed the following steps:
Empty the clean and dirty water tanks.Drain any remaining cleaning solution from the Winbot and allow the wiping pad to dry.
1.Press and hold power button on the station for more than 2 seconds until the winbot shuts down.                                                                                                                              2.Open the rear flip cover of station, take out the battery pack, loosen the interface of the battery pack, and pull out the black power cord plug.
3.Place a foam pad of suitable size flat at the bottom of the packaging box. Then, carefully hold the station with both hands and place it inside the box, ensuring it sits stably.
4.Put the battery pack into the side of the packing box.                                                        5.Place the winbot and cleaning solution into the specially designed foam insert on the side of the packaging box. Then, take another wiping pad and lay it flat against the side of the foam insert, ensuring one edge of the pad fits snugly against the foam.
6.Next, cover it with the other half of the foam top. Pay attention to the reserved notch on the foam cover, which is designed to accommodate the Winbot’s power cord.
7.Finally, place another suitable foam pad on top of the station to provide cushioning and prevent the device from being damaged during transport.
