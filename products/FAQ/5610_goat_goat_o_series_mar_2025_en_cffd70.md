# 基站能否放置在雨棚底下、车库中等非草坪区域？ / Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

基站能否放置在雨棚底下、车库中等非草坪区域？ / Can the docking station be placed in a non-lawn area, such as under an awning or in a garage?

## 答案 / Answer

1- 充电基站

可以不用在草坪上
1. 为了更好的回充效果，请勿将基站放置在狭窄通道中，充电基站左右各预留40cm的平坦区域，并且前方2m内不要有障碍物遮挡；
2. 建议可以根据您家的房子楼层选择最佳的基站摆放位置：
a. 一层楼：基站放在距离房子3.5m处
b. 二层楼：基站建议放在距离房子7m处
c. 三层楼：基站建议放在距离房子10m处
3. 建议用附件的地钉将其固定在地上避免基站移动

You can install the docking station on the non-grassy area. There are some tips when you select the location of docking station:
1. For better positioning, do not place the docking station in a narrow passageway. Ensure that there are no objects (e.g. trees or houses) within 40 cm on either side and 2 m in front of the docking station.
2. It is recommended that you choose the optimal charging station location based on the number of floors in your house:
a. One floor: better to place the charging station at a distance of 3.5m from the house.
b. Two floors: better to place the charging station at a distance of 7m from the house. 
c. Three floors: better to place the charging station at a distance of 10m from the house.
3. It is recommended to secure the docking station to the ground with the provided nails to prevent the station from moving.
