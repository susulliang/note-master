# Are there any requirements on the tilt angle of the glass?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Are there any requirements on the tilt angle of the glass?

## 答案 / Answer

WINBOT is able to clean glass panes at an angle. The angle between the glass and the horizontal plane should be between 60° and 120°. If the angle exceeds this range, WINBOT may circle on the spot, get stuck, or give an alarm.
