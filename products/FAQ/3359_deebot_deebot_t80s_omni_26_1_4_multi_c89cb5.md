>

## 问题 / Question

14

## 答案 / Answer

1. Remove the clean water tank, open its lid, and check that the float inside is in place. Ensure it returns to its normal position after being moved, indicating proper function.
2. Refill the water tank promptly with clean water (fill to capacity without overflowing).
3. Replace the water tank lid and ensure the latches are securely fastened.
4. Reinstall the water tank into the station.
Friendly reminder: Cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
