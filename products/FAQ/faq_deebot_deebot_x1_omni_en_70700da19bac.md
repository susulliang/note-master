# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 OMNI / EN - Why does auto-emptying make a strange noise?

## Question

Why does auto-emptying make a strange noise?

## Answer

When DEEBOT X1 returns to the charging station, there is a small possibility that it will not align with the emptying port. Call the robot out of the docking station and then recall it to start emptying using voice or the App.
If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
