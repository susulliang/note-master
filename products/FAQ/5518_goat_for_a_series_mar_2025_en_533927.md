# 我觉得边缘留草有点多? / Why is there a lot of grass left on the edges?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我觉得边缘留草有点多? / Why is there a lot of grass left on the edges?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

（1）GOAT会通过AI对草坪的边缘进行精细识别，根据不同的边缘类型采取不同的沿边策略，尽可能的少留草。
（2）GOAT的定位存在一定的误差，出于安全考虑GOAT对于悬崖、泳池沿边割草时会有一定的留边保护。
（2）由于GOAT的刀盘与机身外侧有一定距离，在墙边等区域会造成一定的留草，您可用打草机每两周进行一次修整。

(1) GOAT AI function will work when cutting along edge to fine identify the edge of the lawn and adopt distance to edge according to different edge types to leave as little grass as possible.  
(2) There is a margin of error in the positioning of GOAT. GOAT will will keep secure distance for cutting grass along the edge of cliffs and swimming pools.  
(2) Since there is a certain distance between the blade disc of GOAT and the machine cover, there will be some grass left in areas such as along the wall, which you can use a trimmer every two weeks.
