# Why is there no signal when a Navigation Beacon is shown in the Navigation Beacon list in the App?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is there no signal when a Navigation Beacon is shown in the Navigation Beacon list in the App?

## 答案 / Answer

G00037

Navigation Beacon

The GOAT at its current position is unable to receive a signal from the Navigation Beacon. 
 (1) The Navigation Beacon battery has been used up and needs to be replaced immediately.                            (2) If the navigation beacon is not powered on properly, unscrew it and reinstall the battery. Ensure that the beacon is tightened after the battery is installed
 (2) Obstacles between the Navigation Beacon and GOAT are blocking the signal.
