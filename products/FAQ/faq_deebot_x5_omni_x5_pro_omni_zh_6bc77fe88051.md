# DEEBOT / X5 OMNI/X5 PRO OMNI / ZH - YIKO voice assistant doesn't work or there is a response delay after waking up "OK YIKO".

## Question

YIKO voice assistant doesn't work or there is a response delay after waking up "OK YIKO".

## Answer

1. Make sure that your DEEBOT is connected with the ECOVACS HOME App and the YIKO voice assistant is enabled in the App.
2. Please use more standard voice while saying "OK YIKO" to DEEBOT so that YIKO can understand you. You can follow the voice assistant guidance in the App.
3.  If there is too much noise or DEEBOT is far away from you, YIKO may not recognize your voice commands.
4. Make sure your home WiFi signal is good, and keep both the ECOVACS HOME App and DEEBOT firmware versions are the latest.

If the issue persists, please try to reset your DEEBOT and try again.
