# DEEBOT / ECOVACS T80 Series / EN - How to Use Mopping Function (Auto Refill and Drain Version)

## Question

How to Use Mopping Function (Auto Refill and Drain Version)

## Answer

Remove the Dust Collection Cabin Cover and take out the Cleaning Solution Unit.
Open the unit's lid, pour an appropriate amount of ECOVACS-branded cleaning solution into it, close the lid securely, and return it to the Station.
Reattach the Dust Collection Cabin Cover and ensure it is fully sealed.
Power on the robot and place it directly in front of the Station. Press and hold the "Auto" button to initiate automatic return to charging. Charging will begin once docking is successful.
Press the "Auto" button briefly to start Vacuum and Mop Simultaneously.
