# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Is the GOAT more likely to get stuck when mowing along the edges?

## Question

Is the GOAT more likely to get stuck when mowing along the edges?

## Answer

When there are a lot of new obstacles along the boundary, it may cause some interference to the GOAT's positioning. It is recommended to clear away these obstacles first before mowing along the boundary.
