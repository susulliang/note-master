# 在遥控环节完成后为何还要进行地图学习？

## Question

在遥控环节完成后为何还要进行地图学习？

## Answer

The GOAT doesn’t know the obstacles within the map during controlling. It also needs to go through the mapping area to confirm the locations of the obstacles and generate a complete map.
