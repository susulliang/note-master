# DEEBOT / ECOVACS T80 Series / EN - How to Schedule a Cleaning?

## Question

How to Schedule a Cleaning?

## Answer

1.Use the app to schedule a full-house, custom, or zone cleaning.
2.Click "Schedule" to add a new cleaning task.
3.Set the "Repeat" frequency to choose weekly recurring times.
