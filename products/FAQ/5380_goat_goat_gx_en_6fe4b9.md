# How can I select the mowing efficiency for the GOAT?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How can I select the mowing efficiency for the GOAT?

## 答案 / Answer

21

Mowing

You can select the mowing efficiency in the App or on the robot's display: Precision and Efficiency 
 (1) Precision mode, which is recommended when you require precise mowing. GOAT will actively trigger secondary mowing.
 (2) Efficiency mode, which is recommended when you require fast mowing. There may be missed areas.
