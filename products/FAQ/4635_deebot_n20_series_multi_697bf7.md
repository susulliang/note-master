## 答案 / Answer

Robot Charging Guide.

Connect the power cord, keep the area around the docking station clear of objects ( within 0.5m left and right, 1.5m in front), especially objects with reflective surfaces. Long press the power button.                                                                                                                                   1.Place the Robot in front of the docking station,press the return button to make the Robot automatically return to the charging station, there will be a voice prompt "start charging".                                                                                                                                    2.Please push the Robot into the charging station manually. Then shake it left and right, and when it is successfully docked, it will have a voice prompt 'start charging'.
