# 31

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

31

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the cover of the ROBOT. Slide the Power Switch to "O" to turn off the machine.
2. Press the blue release button at the rear of the ROBOT to remove the Dirty water box.
3. Open the dirty water box lid and check if the float is in place.
① If not lost, reinstall the dirty water box onto the rear of the ROBOT. A "click" sound indicates proper installation.
② If lost, contact after-sales service for assistance.
