# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 如何变更冬令时和夏令时？How to change winter saving time and daylight saving time?

## Question

如何变更冬令时和夏令时？How to change winter saving time and daylight saving time?

## Answer

1.您可以在【App设置-机器人信息-时区设置】内，选择您所在的时区
2.您也可以将手机系统的时区设置为符合您居住地区的时区，然后在 App内【App设置-机器人信息-时区设置】选择同步手机时区
3.时区修改后，将同步更改预约和日志内的时间                                                                                                                                                                                        1. You can select your time zone in [App Settings - Bot Information - Time Zone Settings].
2. You can also set the time zone of your phone system to match the time zone of your residential area, and then select Sync phone time zone in the App Settings - Bot Information - Time Zone Settings
3. After the time zone is changed, the appointment and the time in the log will be changed synchronously.
