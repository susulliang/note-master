# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 搬动GOAT后触发割草，报重定位失败?

## Question

搬动GOAT后触发割草，报重定位失败?

## Answer

Triggering a task after moving the GOAT requires repositioning first.
(1) Relocation of the GOAT will fail in shaded areas.
(2) It is recommended to move the GOAT to a spacious and unobstructed area far from the boundaries (>1.5m) on the map and then try again.
