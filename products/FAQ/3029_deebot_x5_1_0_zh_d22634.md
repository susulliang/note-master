# How to clean the waste water tank and the clean water tank? / 1. Open the top cover of the station and take out the waste water tank. Open the cover of the waste water tank and clean up the waste water. (If the bottom of the tank remains dirty, you can use the cleaning tool to clean it up.)  
2. Open the top cover of the station and take out the clean water tank. Open the cover of the clean water tank, take out the filter assembly and clean the filter. Please put it back after cleaning.
