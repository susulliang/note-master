# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 基站指示灯一直不亮

## Question

基站指示灯一直不亮

## Answer

(1) Check whether the power supply is connected correctly
(2) Check whether the power supply voltage is between 100~240V
If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
