# 按动机身滤篮按钮，滤篮盖为何无法弹出？

> **产品线**：ULTRAMARINE　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

按动机身滤篮按钮，滤篮盖为何无法弹出？

## 答案 / Answer

您好，该问题多因清洁过程中，沙子等异物进入按钮区域，导致部件卡滞所致。请按以下步骤操作排查：
1.先将机器关机；
2.将机器搬运至便于清洁的区域；
3.一手持续按住机身滤篮按钮，另一手反复按压上盖，直至卡滞的沙子松动后，上盖弹开；
4.用清水持续冲洗按钮缝隙及仓盖周边贴合处，同时间断按压后盖按钮数次，直至按压时顺畅、无卡顿阻力；
5.盖好上盖后，重新使用。
温馨提示：
1.若泳池脏污严重或长期未使用，建议先进行人工预处理后，再使用本机进行后续清理。

This issue is mostly caused by sand or other debris getting into the button area during cleaning, resulting in jamming. Please follow these steps to troubleshoot:
1.Turn off the unit first.
2.Move the unit to an easy-to-clean area.
3.Press and hold the filter basket switch button with one hand, and gently press the filter basket lid repeatedly with the other hand until the jammed sand is loose and the lid pops open.
4.Rinse the button gaps and the contact area of the lid with clean water continuously, while pressing the button intermittently several times until it presses smoothly without jamming.
5.Refit the filter basket cover properly before using again.
 Note:
If the pool is heavily dirty or has not been used for a long time, it is recommended to carry out manual pre-cleaning before using the unit for further cleaning.
