# DEEBOT / T90 PRO OMNI With Auto Refill And Drain / ZH - Cleaning Guide for ROBOT Water Tray

## Question

Cleaning Guide for ROBOT Water Tray

## Answer

1. Remove the cover of the ROBOT. Slide the Power Switch to "O" to turn off the ROBOT. Then slightly lift the side where the roller mop is installed.
 2. Release the blue clip on the Water Tray and remove it. Press and rotate the drain button to thoroughly clean the entire Water Tray (a cleaning tool may be used for assistance).
 3. Turn the ROBOT upside down with its bottom facing upward. Clean the Water Tray mounting area on the ROBOT  (use a cleaning tool for thorough cleaning if needed).
 4. Reinstall the Water Tray onto the bottom of the ROBOT. A "click" sound indicates proper installation.
 TIPS: The cleaning tool is not included as standard equipment.
