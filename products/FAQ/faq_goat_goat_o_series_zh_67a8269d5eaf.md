# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 为什么GOAT 在充满电后的工作时间会缩短？

## Question

为什么GOAT 在充满电后的工作时间会缩短？

## Answer

(1) The lawn is dense and the growing conditions of the grass are complex.
(2) Efficient mode is in use.
(3) Battery aging.
