# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 地图学习大概需要多长时间？

## Question

地图学习大概需要多长时间？

## Answer

The time to learn a map depends on the size of the map and the complexity of the garden. In a relatively empty space, the GOAT can learn about 400 m2 in an hour.
