# Why do the driving threads produce a friction sound when moving on the surface?

## Question

Why do the driving threads produce a friction sound when moving on the surface?

## Answer

In order to ensure the mobility and safety of WINBOT W2 on the surface, it uses tires with high coefficient of friction and therefore friction sounds may be produced. Please do not worry, as this is a reliable characteristic of WINBOT W2 movement.
