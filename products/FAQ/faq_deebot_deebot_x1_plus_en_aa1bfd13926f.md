# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 PLUS / EN - How many dust-emptying modes does the docking station have?

## Question

How many dust-emptying modes does the docking station have?

## Answer

Emptying can be triggered in two ways: Auto and manual
1) Auto: The docking station will start auto-emptying after each cleaning task.
2) Manual: The emptying function can be controlled via the App or voice control.
