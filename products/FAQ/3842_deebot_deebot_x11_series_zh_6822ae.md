# 机器人出现漏扫情况，如何处理？ / Handling Missed Cleaning Areas

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人出现漏扫情况，如何处理？ / Handling Missed Cleaning Areas

## 答案 / Answer

1.打开App-点击"进入设备"-设置-地宝高级设定-AIVI3D3.0-标准；                                                                  2.房间门口放置了地毯或者地垫；确认APP地图管理-地图编辑-地毯属性，地毯属性更改为仅扫地。
3.地宝越障高度为2.4cm(扫地状态,基于实验室数据)；若超过2.4cm的高度或者厚度；机器不会尝试通过，会放弃清扫该区域；
4.机器人拥有智能避障能力,有时为避免撞坏房间门口的家具或门槛,可能会导致部分区域漏扫。
注：1.若使用环境中物品较多，为避免出现漏扫，建议您稍作整理后观察；                                                       2. 漏扫的定义：需等待机器语音提示清洁完成后，若有区域未清洁到，我们可判断为漏扫。

1.Open APP＞Enter＞Settings＞DEEBOT Advanced Settings＞enable AIVI 3.0, select Standard Mode;  2.Check for rugs/mats blocking doorways. and check in the app-Map Management-Edit Map-Carpet Settings-Bypass.
 3.The robot can climb up to 2.4cm. If thresholds/furniture exceed 2.4 cm in height/thickness, the area will be skipped.
 4.The robot has intelligent obstacle avoidance, but to prevent damaging furniture or thresholds, it may miss some areas.
 Friendly Reminder: 1. If there are many items in the environment, to avoid missing areas during cleaning, we recommend tidying up slightly before observing.                                                                            2. Definition of "Missed Cleaning Areas": After the robot's voice prompt confirms cleaning completion, any areas not covered during the cycle can be identified as Missed Cleaning Areas.
