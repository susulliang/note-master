# 机器人连接网络指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人连接网络指导

## 答案 / Answer

下载并注册登入ECOVACS HOME App
 1.将主机放在靠近充电座的地方，用手机扫描主机上的二维码(提示:您也可以从App Store下载ECOVACS HOME应用程序); 
 2.安装App，注册，然后登录到App主界面。
 配网
 1.扫描主机二维码，输入Wi-Fi密码，点击即可登录。 
 2.按照App界面提示，掀开主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“I”开机，等待声音。选择“下一步”短按配网按键”，确认听到“已释放蓝牙热点”语音提示后，选择“下一步”启动热点连接，等待网络连接。 
 3.连接完成后，您可以继续为DEEBOT命名。 备注：
 请将您的机器人，配网使用的手机在WiFi信号覆盖较强的位置进行配网操作。

Download, register, and log in to the ECOVACS HOME App
 1. Place the ROBOT near the charging dock. Scan the QR code on the ROBOT with your phone (Note: You can also download the ECOVACS HOME app from the App Store). 
 2. Install the app, register, then log in to the app's main interface.
 Network Setup
 1. Scan the ROBOT's QR code, enter your Wi-Fi password, and tap to connect. 
 2. Follow the app prompts: Lift the front cover, slide the Power Switch to "I" to turn on, and wait for the startup sound. Select "Next," briefly Press the network button, confirm you hear the "Bluetooth hotspot released" voice prompt, then select "Next" to initiate hotspot connection. Wait for network connection. 
 3. After connection completes, you may proceed to name your DEEBOT.                                                                                 Note:
 Ensure both your robot and the phone used for setup are in an area with strong Wi-Fi signal coverage during setup.
