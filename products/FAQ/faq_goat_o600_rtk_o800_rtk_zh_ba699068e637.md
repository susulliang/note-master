# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 哪些区域需要设置禁区？

## Question

哪些区域需要设置禁区？

## Answer

1. Fall hazards like pools and sand pits
 2. Flower beds or vegetable gardens with vegetation under 20cm and no fencing (may cause accidental mowing)
 3. Large trees with exposed roots or root slopes
 4. Slopes or depressions exceeding 24° (45%) gradient
 5. Recreational equipment areas like trampolines, swings, or slides
 6. Black outdoor furniture like tables and chairs
 7. Fragile transparent objects
 8. Exposed wires on the ground
 9. Steps or piles of stones with height differences of ±3cm
