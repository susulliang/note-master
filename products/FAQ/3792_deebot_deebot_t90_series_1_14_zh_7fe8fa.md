# 报警：清水箱异常

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

报警：清水箱异常

## 答案 / Answer

Friendly Reminder:
If you encounter a prompt indicating the clean water tank is not installed or low on water, please follow these steps to troubleshoot:
1. Open the clean water tank lid and check that the float inside is in position. Ensure it returns to its normal position after being moved, indicating proper State.
 2. Refill the clean water tank promptly (Fill until full but not overflowing)
 3. Replace the clean water tank lid and ensure the latch is securely fastened.
 4. Reinstall the clean water tank into the station.
