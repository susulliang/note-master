# 主机开机后屏幕按键无反应怎么办？

## Question

主机开机后屏幕按键无反应怎么办？

## Answer

(1) Please restart the machine and try again.
(2) Try to reset the machine.                                                                                                 （3）Simultaneously long press "START" and "CHARGE" at the same time on the GOAT for 5 seconds until the display show the PIN code input interface.
(4) Enter the right PIN code, then press "OK", and please Please wait for 30 seconds for the data cleanup process. The GOAT will automatically restart, indicating a successful reset.
If the problem is not solved after the above operation, please contact ECOVACS after-sales
