# DEEBOT / T30 S Combo / EN - The foot touch function cannot be used; how to deal with it?

## Question

The foot touch function cannot be used; how to deal with it?

## Answer

The foot touch function cannot be used; how to deal with it?
Note: When using foot interaction, please avoid the area of the Deebot structured light module and lightly touch the board.
1. Please confirm in the App that the foot posture interaction switch has been turned on;
You can open the app to enter the robot control > click "Settings" in the upper right corner > More function settings > Open "Foot touch control".
2. Foot trigger only works when the machine is standby or paused.
3. Foot position triggering To avoid accidental touch, respond within 0.3s-1s of kicking and hitting the board;
4. After the child lock function is turned on; the foot touch function cannot be used;
