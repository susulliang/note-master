# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - There is currently no rain outside. Why did the GOAT trigger the rain protection mode?

## Question

There is currently no rain outside. Why did the GOAT trigger the rain protection mode?

## Answer

The rain sensor is more sensitive. The rain protection mode will be triggered if the sensor is subject to long-term exposure to touch or water.
