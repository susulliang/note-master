## 问题 / Question

15

## 答案 / Answer

When the robot prompts "Low cleaning fluid level," you can try the following steps:
Disinfectant Cleaning Solution
 1. Remove the cleaning solution tanks' cover, take out the disinfectant cleaning solution tank, fill it with ECOVACS cleaning solution, then close the lid and place it back into the station.
 2. In the App, go to maintenance ＞Disinfectant Cleaning Solution＞ I Have Added.
 
 Heavy-Duty Cleaning Solution
 1. Remove the cleaning solution tanks' cover, take out the heavy-duty cleaning solution tank, fill it with ECOVACS heavy-duty cleaning solution, then close the lid and place it back into the station.
 2. In the App, go to maintenance ＞ Heavy-Duty Cleaning Solution ＞ I Have Added.
