# WINBOT / W2 OMNI / EN - How to charge the station of W2 OMNI?

## Question

How to charge the station of W2 OMNI?

## Answer

1. Place the station near the power outlet, open the back cover of the station, take out the charging cable and connect it to the power source.
2. During charging, the battery indicator of the station is flashing blue. After fully charged, the battery indicator is steady. The charging time at room temperature is about 3 hours.
3. Using WINBOT W2 OMNI is not supported while charging the station. For convenience, please fully charge it before use. When W2 OMNI is working, it will not charge even if the power is on.

Note:
1. Low temperature may cause slow charging.
2. WINBOT is suitable for the use between 0 ℃ -40 ° C, please do not charge it in locations where the temperature exceeds this range.
3. When charging, the fan can not work, please use it after charging.
4. If the WINBOT is not in use for a long time, it is recommended to charge the station regularly to protect the health of the battery.
