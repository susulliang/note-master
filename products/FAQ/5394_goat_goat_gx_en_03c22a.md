# Why is the mowing process in the schedule automatically cleared?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the mowing process in the schedule automatically cleared?

## 答案 / Answer

35

Mowing

(1) After the GOAT has been turned off and restarted, the mowing process in the schedule will be cleared. 
 (2) The GOAT has performed a firmware upgrade (check whether Silent Upgrade is turned on in the settings). It will automatically restart during the upgrade, which causes the mowing process in the schedule to be cleared. (3)If the GOAT is moved manually, the machine will determine that it has reached a new mowing area and start mowing again.
