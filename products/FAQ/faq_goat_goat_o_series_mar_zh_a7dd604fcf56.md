# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 我抬起了GOAT ，为何GOAT 没有被立即锁定并告警？

## Question

我抬起了GOAT ，为何GOAT 没有被立即锁定并告警？

## Answer

(1) Please confirm whether the related alarm is turned on in Settings > More Settings > Security Settings. If it is not enabled, the lift alarm will not trigger continuous beeping when the GOAT is lifted; it will only give the regular alert.
(2) If it is lifted during operation, the GOAT will automatically self-escape for 10 seconds to avoid any false triggering. Then it will automatically lock and sound the regular alert after 10 seconds.
