# What is the relationship between the auto-connection module and the SIM card?

## Question

What is the relationship between the auto-connection module and the SIM card?

## Answer

The auto-connetcion service is a service built into the additional connection module provided by ECOVACS. If you do not have Wi-Fi or a good Wi-Fi signal at home, you can use the auto-connetcion module to access the remote control function. The auto-connetcion module does not need to be activated, and can be used after installing the auto-connection module and turning on the robot. If you do not wish to use the auto-connetcion module, you can purchase a SIM card and upon activation, install the auto-connection module to use the SIM card. The GOAT uses the auto-connetcion module by default. You can switch it to use the SIM card in Settings > More Settings > Network Settings > Auto-connection Module. You can use all the functions of the GOAT under SIM card connection, although the firmware upgrade and video functions are not available under the connection of the auto-connetcion module.
