# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - Does edge optimization have to be set up first when using the automatic edge optimization function?

## Question

Does edge optimization have to be set up first when using the automatic edge optimization function?

## Answer

There are certain deviation in AI edge recognition. AI cannot distinguish between green objects and cannot make accurate judgments regarding height. Therefore, in order to avoid jamming and miscutting, it is recommended to select only:
(1) Edges with a drop of less than 3 cm.
(2) Edges of wall enclosures more than 20 cm in height.
(3) AI recognition optimization will be carried out in areas within the length of one mower from the edge where there are no enclosed plants below 20 cm in height.
 If your garden environment meets the above conditions, you can choose to optimize the entire outline.
