# How to Select Cleaning Modes？

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How to Select Cleaning Modes？

## 答案 / Answer

1. Long press the Start Button to power on the device and place it on the glass to adhere.
2. Select a cleaning mode via the App:
① Thorough Cleaning Mode: For windows ＞50 cm wide, the WINBOT follows a Z-path to wipe 1 time, followed by a cleaning pass along all four edges; for windows ＜50 cm wide, it performs N-path cleaning 2 times.
② Deep Cleaning Mode: the WINBOT follows a Z/N-shaped path to wipe 2 times. For windows ＜50 cm wide, it performs N-path cleaning 2 times; for windows ＞50 cm wide, it performs Z-path cleaning 2 times.
③ Fast Cleaning Mode: the WINBOT follows a Z/N-shaped path to wipe 1 time.
④ Edge Cleaning Mode: the WINBOT cleans along all four edges once in a clockwise direction.
⑤ Spot Cleaning Mode: activate via Remote Control or the App. The WINBOT will wipe up and down 4 times, centered on its current position.
⑥ Once the WINBOT is working, you can also use the remote buttons for 360° steering and manual water spraying.
