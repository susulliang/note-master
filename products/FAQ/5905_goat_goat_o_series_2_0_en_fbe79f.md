# 我将GOAT 拿出了很远，为何没有触发超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我将GOAT 拿出了很远，为何没有触发超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

（1）请确认是否在“设置”-“更多设置”-“安全设置”中打开了“超出地图范围报警”
（2）超出地图范围报警的默认范围值为200m，可能在实际使用中存在一些误差。
（3）若机器丢失网络连接，则无法发送报警。

(1) Please confirm whether Out of Map Alarm is turned on in Settings ＞ More Settings ＞ Security Settings. 
(2) The default range value of the alarm beyond the map range is 200m, which may have some margin of error in actual use. 
(3) If the device loses network connection, it will not be able to send alerts.
