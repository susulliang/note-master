# Why can sounds be heard from the docking station?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why can sounds be heard from the docking station?

## 答案 / Answer

G00008

Station

The docking station is installed with a motor to drive the G1、G1-2000's lens cleaning brush. It is normal for the docking station to make some noise when the lens is being cleaned.
