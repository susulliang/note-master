# Why does the 3D map displayed not match the actual walls in my home?

## Question

Why does the 3D map displayed not match the actual walls in my home?

## Answer

1.For walls with special shapes such as circular or curved designs, the current App may not be able to render them exactly. In such cases, these walls might be represented on the map as parallel, perpendicular, or angled lines.
2.If you have completed a full floor plan but the interior walls appear incorrect, it may be due to positioning anomalies during the mapping process, or external interference affecting mapping performance, leading to inaccuracies in the wall layout.
3.If an exterior wall or boundary has been mapped too narrowly, you can manually edit the wall in the Map Management page to adjust its position to what you consider correct.
4.You may also choose to delete the existing map and initiate a new mapping run.
