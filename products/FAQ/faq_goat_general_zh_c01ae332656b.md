# 600平米的草坪，割草大概需要多久？

## Question

600平米的草坪，割草大概需要多久？

## Answer

Fully Charged, it cuts until 15% battery remaining, then returns to recharge. If low-battery recharge is needed to continue mowing, it recharges to 80% before resuming. After cutting until 15% battery again, it returns to recharge. Mowing a 600 m² lawn requires a total of approximately 750 min, or 12.4 hours.

 Lawn area: 600 m²
 GOAT begins mowing from full charge. When battery drops to 15%, it returns to recharge to 80% before resuming mowing. This cycle repeats until the entire lawn is completed.

 Single mow and charging details:
 First mow session: 95 m² | 55 min
 Recharge (15% → 80%): 55 min
 Subsequent 6 mow cycles:
 75 m² each | 45 min each
 Charge time per cycle: 55 min
 Final mow cycle: 55 m² | 33 min

 Total operating time:
 Pure mowing time: 358 min
 Charging time: 385 min
 Total duration: approx. 12.4 hours


 The above test data is obtained under standard laboratory test conditions, using a standard lawn with approximately 1 cm grass height per mow. Actual mow efficiency and total time may vary depending on grass type, lawn density, grass height, terrain slope, number of obstacles, and environmental factors.
