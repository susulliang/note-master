# There are problems with the maps in the App (skewed or overlapping)?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

There are problems with the maps in the App (skewed or overlapping)?

## 答案 / Answer

D20265

Intelligence

The Restore Map function in Map Management on the App can be used to restore the original saved maps. Any scheduled cleaning, map edit, or virtual walls will be lost after restoring maps. Please reset them.
