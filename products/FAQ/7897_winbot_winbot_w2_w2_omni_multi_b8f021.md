# What is continuous cleaning?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

What is continuous cleaning?

## 答案 / Answer

W00109

Continuous cleaning means the cleaning can be continued after a pause but not after a power outage.
