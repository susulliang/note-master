DEEBOT does not finish cleaning after returning to the dock to charge in Continuous Clean mode. Why?

## 答案 / Answer

D20040

Enable

Schedule

Please ensure you have not reset the robot during the process. 
Please ensure you have turned on Continuous Clean in the App. 
Please ensure the robot is charging normally. Once charging completed, it will do continue cleaning.
