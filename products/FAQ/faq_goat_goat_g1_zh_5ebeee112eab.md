# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Do you have to nail down the docking station?

## Question

Do you have to nail down the docking station?

## Answer

The docking station might move if it is not nailed to the ground. The movement of the station will cause the positioning signal to shift and you will need to recreate the map if the moved distance exceeds 20 cm. Moreover, failure to secure the docking station for an extended period of time may cause the bottom plate to deform and affect its ability to recharge.
