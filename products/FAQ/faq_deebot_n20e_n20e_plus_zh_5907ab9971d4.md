# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 机器人找不到充电座或者返回充电座困难？ Can't the robot find the station or have difficulty returning to the station?

## Question

机器人找不到充电座或者返回充电座困难？                           Can't the robot find the station or have difficulty returning to the station?

## Answer

（1）保证充电座所在房间门未被关闭;
（2）确认充电座位置左右0.5m，前方1.5m内没有障碍物，且充电座周围没有反光物体（如镜子、金属、玻璃等）;
（3）建议机器人清洁时从充电座出发。（如果机器人在外面，请将机器人搬到充电座前方，短按回充键，机器人即可返回充电座）
（4）确保充电座中途未断电或被人为移动过；
（5）确保充电座未放置在地毯上，地毯上行进阻力较大，影响机器人的回充成功率；
（6）确保充电座正确放置，将科沃斯logo朝上。                                                                                                                                       （1）Ensure that the door of the room where the charging dock is located is not closed.
（2）Confirm that the charging dock is located 0.5m to the left and right, there are no obstacles within 1.5m in front, and there are no reflective objects (such as mirrors, metal, glass, etc.) around the charging dock.
  (3) It is recommended that the robot start cleaning from the charging dock. (If the robot is outside, please move the robot to the front of the charging dock, press the recharge button briefly, and the robot will return to the charging dock.)
（4）Ensure that the charging dock has not been powered off or moved by humans midway.
  (5) Ensure that the charging dock is not placed on the carpet, as there is greater resistance to travel on the carpet, which affects the success rate of the robot's recharge.
（6）Make sure the charging dock is placed correctly with the Ecovacs logo facing up.
