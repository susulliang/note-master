## 答案 / Answer

App Can’t Control WINBOT?

1.Make sure WINBOT is powered on and attached to the glass. (Remote control only works in suction/working mode.)
2.Stay within Bluetooth range (up to 5 meters).
3.WINBOT supports multiple phones, but only one can control it at a time. If phone A is in use, phone B cannot operate it.

Note:
① This WINBOT can only be remotely controlled via the app.
② Bluetooth must be enabled on your phone to use app remote control.
