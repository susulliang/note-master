# Can the Navigation Beacon be installed and used directly after it is powered on and its assembly is complete?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can the Navigation Beacon be installed and used directly after it is powered on and its assembly is complete?

## 答案 / Answer

G00021

Navigation Beacon

The Navigation Beacon needs to be paired with the GOAT to function. We recommend that you: 
 (1) Download the Ecovacs Home App first and connect the GOAT network to the app. 
 (2) Pair the Navigation Beacon to the GOAT as prompted in the app.
 (3) Read the installation guide in the App before installing the Navigation Beacon in the garden.
