# WINBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - What If the Flocked Spherical Sensors Are Worn? Will It Affect Performance?

## Question

What If the Flocked Spherical Sensors Are Worn? Will It Affect Performance?

## Answer

The spherical sensors use a flocked design with a dense velvet layer to reduce noise during operation.
If the surface appears flattened or shiny, try gently smoothing the fibers by hand.
It’s normal for the flocked surface to become shinier over time as it adapts to use.
This does not affect WINBOT’s cleaning performance — you can continue using it with confidence.
