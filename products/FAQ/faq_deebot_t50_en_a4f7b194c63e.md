# DEEBOT / T50降配版 / EN - Disassembly, clean and installation guide of mopping pad plate and mopping pad

## Question

Disassembly, clean and installation guide of mopping pad plate and mopping pad

## Answer

1. Remove the mopping pad plate by lifting it upward with a little force and tear down the washable mopping pad.
2. Cleaned and dried, then align the mopping pads with the mopping pad plates, stick it flat, then install the mopping pad assembly properly.
