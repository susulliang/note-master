# 使用手动模式割草时APP总是提示距离不满足要求，无法开启刀盘？

## Question

使用手动模式割草时APP总是提示距离不满足要求，无法开启刀盘？

## Answer

According to the safety regulation requirements, the distance between the person and the robot cannot exceed 6 m when remotely controlling the robot to mow the lawn. The GOAT limits the usage distance by detecting the strength of the Bluetooth signal. If it detects that the Bluetooth signal is insufficient, it will automatically stop working. It needs to re-detect the Bluetooth signal to restart, so there will be a certain period of delay. In addition, different models of phones will have different Bluetooth strengths. Try to get closer to the GOAT when using the manual mowing function. If the blades still don't move,  please try to use a different model of phone.
