# Why does the working time of the GOAT shorten after it has fully charged?

## Question

Why does the working time of the GOAT shorten after it has fully charged?

## Answer

(1) The lawn is dense and the growing conditions of the grass are complex.
 (2) Efficient mode is in use.
 (3) Battery aging.
