# RTK基站的信号覆盖范围有多大？ / What is the coverage of the RTK signal?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

RTK基站的信号覆盖范围有多大？ / What is the coverage of the RTK signal?

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

在无遮挡的情况下RTK基站的信号覆盖范围为以基站为中心300-400m的圆形区域

In the case of no shaows, the signal coverage of the RTK is a circular area about 300-400m centered on the station.
