# The robot did not automatically perform cleaning work at the scheduled time?

## Question

The robot did not automatically perform cleaning work at the scheduled time?

## Answer

1.Make sure DEEBOT is powered on;
2.Make sure the DEEBOT is fully charged;
3.Make sure that Do Not Disturb mode is not turned on in the app, as the Do Not Disturb time period does not perform the work of the appointment;
4.Check that the appointment tasks in the app are set up or saved correctly.
