# Can I install the navigation beacon amongst some plants?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can I install the navigation beacon amongst some plants?

## 答案 / Answer

G00026

Disable

Navigation Beacon

The navigation beacon may be installed amongst plants if they are less than 60 cm in height. However, make sure to periodically look out for plants that may have grown taller or begin to wrap around the beacon, as this may cause signal blockage and subsequent mispositioning.
