# GOAT / GX / ZH - Do you have to nail down the docking station?

## Question

Do you have to nail down the docking station?

## Answer

The docking station might move if it is not nailed to the ground, resulting in recharging failure. Moreover, failure to secure the docking station for an extended period of time may cause the bottom plate to deform and affect its ability to recharge.
