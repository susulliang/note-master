# 基站不出热水如何处理？ / What should I do if the station does not dispense hot water?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

基站不出热水如何处理？ / What should I do if the station does not dispense hot water?

## 答案 / Answer

1.请将清水箱清水加满
1.请打开APP中“热水净洗”功能；
温馨提示：清水加热是一个动态的过程，热水洗前段过程温度会略低。

1. Please fill the clean water tank with water.
2. Please open the app and set the wash method to standard/deep wash.
Note: Water heating is a dynamic process, and the temperature may be slightly lower during the initial stages of washing.
