# After the WINBOT sprays water, there are occasionally droplets on the robot, which will converge and fall.

## Question

After the WINBOT sprays water, there are occasionally droplets on the robot, which will converge and fall.

## Answer

To achieve a better spray effect, W1/W1 PRO uses the small-hole cross-spray technology that can cover the width of the robot in the walking direction. It is normal to have a few water droplets.
