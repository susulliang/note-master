# WINBOT / WINBOT NEO / EN - What is the maximum effective distance for the App remote control?

## Question

What is the maximum effective distance for the App remote control?

## Answer

It works well within a distance of 5m as per Ecovacs lab data. Please make sure that your phone is not too far from the robot during actual use.
