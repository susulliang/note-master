# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - GOAT 的电池包在待机的状态下能支持多久？

## Question

GOAT 的电池包在待机的状态下能支持多久？

## Answer

The GOAT O800 RTK / GOAT O1200 RTK battery pack lasts about 9 / 13 hours when it is standby.
