# GOAT为何不再避障了？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT为何不再避障了？

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

GOAT主要依靠前置TOF传感器和AI视觉识别障碍物：
1.TOF避障支持APP内3档高度调节（≥10cm/≥15cm/≥20cm），无法识别低于10cm的障碍物；
2.TOF无法识别机身两侧及后方障碍物；                                                                         3.TOF对黑色、白色及透明物体识别能力较弱；                                                                 4.地面颠簸可能影响TOF避障精度；                                                                                  5. AI避障目前仅支持刺猬等少数障碍物，需在APP中手动开启；                                            6.光线不足或雨天时，AI视觉无法正常工作。

GOAT primarily relies on its front-facing TOF sensor and AI vision to recognize obstacles:
 1. TOF obstacle avoidance supports three height settings within the app (≥10cm/≥15cm/≥20cm) and cannot detect obstacles below 10cm;
 2. TOF cannot detect obstacles on the sides or rear of the unit; 3. TOF has limited recognition capability for black, white, and transparent objects; 4. Ground vibrations may affect TOF obstacle avoidance accuracy; 5. AI obstacle avoidance currently supports only a few obstacles like hedgehogs and requires manual activation in the app; 6. AI vision fails to function properly in low light or rainy conditions.
