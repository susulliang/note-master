# Why does DEEBOT fail to completely detect carpets? Why does it recognize carpets as floors?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why does DEEBOT fail to completely detect carpets? Why does it recognize carpets as floors?

## 答案 / Answer

D20032

Cleaning

Check whether the carpets in your home meet any of these descriptions: 
Too thick (similar thickness to floor boards), pile height less than 2 mm or made with certain materials such as PVC
