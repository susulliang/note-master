# Why isn't the robot avoiding obstacles?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Why isn't the robot avoiding obstacles?

## 答案 / Answer

1.Check the laser sensor (centered on the front bumper) for dust. Wipe gently with a dry cloth if needed.
2.Ensure "AIVI 3D" mode is enabled in the app settings.
Note: The robot may slow down and lightly touch black or thin objects.
