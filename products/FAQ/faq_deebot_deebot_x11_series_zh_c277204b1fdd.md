# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 如何清理主机接水槽？

## Question

如何清理主机接水槽？

## Answer

1.Double-press the AUTO button. The roller mop will automatically extend. Lift one side of the mop assembly slightly.
2.Slide up the blue latch to release the water tray. Take out the filter and clean both the tray and filter thoroughly (use tools if needed).
3.Place the filter back into the water tray.
4.Reattach the tray back into the robot. A "click" sound confirms proper installation.
5.Double-press AUTO again to retract the roller mop component.
Note: If flipping the robot to clean, remove the dirty water box first to prevent leaks.
