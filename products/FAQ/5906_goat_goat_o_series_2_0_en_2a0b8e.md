# 我将GOAT 拿出了很远，为何没有触发超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我将GOAT 拿出了很远，为何没有触发超出地图范围的防盗报警？ / I removed the GOAT from the map. Why didn't it trigger the alarm beyond the range of the map?

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

由于该款GOAT无GPS功能，所以暂不能提供该功能
可以在APP的设置中，打开抬起报警，一旦抬起一段时间后，机器蜂鸣器就会长鸣，达到防盗的效果。

As this GOAT does not have GPS capability, it is not available at this time.
You can enable the lift alarm in the app settings. Once the device is lifted for a certain period of time, the device's buzzer will sound continuously, providing an anti-theft effect.
