# How do I upgrade the robot’s firmware?

## Question

How do I upgrade the robot’s firmware?

## Answer

1. Robot must be placed on the station and it must be in charging state.
2. Robot needs to be charged up to 50% (it's recommended to upgrade after charging for 3 hours).
3. Make sure DEEBOT is connected with the ECOVACS HOME App, and the WiFi signal is good.
4. Enter APP >Settings > About DEEBOT>Update DEEBOT

Note: If automatic update is enabled, DEEBOT will automatically update to the latest version.
