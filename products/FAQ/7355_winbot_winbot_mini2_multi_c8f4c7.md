# How to clean the WINBOT driving threads?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

How to clean the WINBOT driving threads?

## 答案 / Answer

1. Connect the power cord to the adapter and plug it in before cleaning.
 2. Long press the Start Button to power on, then long press it again to turn off the fan. Confirm that the indicator stays solid blue.
 3. Open the APP to enter the device, click "Settings", click "Accessory Maintenance", click "Clean Driving Threads", click "Rotate Forward/Backward". If dirt is found, pause the operation and wipe them clean with a cloth.
