>

## 问题 / Question

27

## 答案 / Answer

1. Turn the machine over, remove the wiping pad, and place it into the cleaning compartment of the station for cleaning. After washing, take it out and allow it to air dry to prevent bacterial growth and odor.
2. If there is dirt residue inside the cleaning compartment of the station, you can activate the "Self-Cleaning Mode" for cleaning. To prevent odors, open the cleaning compartment door after cleaning to allow ventilation, and close it again only after it has fully dried.
3. If you do not plan to use Winbot for an extended period, pour out any remaining liquid from the tanks to avoid bacterial growth and odor.
4. Use the ECOVACS HOME App to remotely control Winbot and inspect the driving wheel tracks. If dirt is found, wipe it off with a clean cloth.
5. Wipe the fan with a dry cloth to prevent a decrease in suction power.
6. Gently clean the spherical sensors and scrubber with a dry cloth to maintain their sensitivity.
7. Wipe the side rollers with a dry cloth to ensure normal operation of the machine.
8. Pour out any remaining liquid from the clean water tank and dirty water tank of the station, and rinse them thoroughly.
