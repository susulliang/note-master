# I lifted up my GOAT. Why did it not immediately lock and sound the alarm?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

I lifted up my GOAT. Why did it not immediately lock and sound the alarm?

## 答案 / Answer

49

Intelligence & Privacy

(1) Please confirm whether the related alarm is turned on in Settings ＞ More Settings ＞ Security Settings. If it is not turned on, the alarm will not be triggered when the robot is on standby or the task is suspended. 
 (2) If it is lifted during operation, the GOAT will automatically self-escape for 10 seconds to avoid any false triggering. Then it will automatically lock and sound the alarm after 10 seconds.
