# Do I need to empty the cleaning solution from the water tank after using the WINBOT W2?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Do I need to empty the cleaning solution from the water tank after using the WINBOT W2?

## 答案 / Answer

W00152

1. If you use the WINBOT W2 within a short period of time (such as every 1-3 months), there is no need to empty the water tank.
2. If it will be a longer period of time before you use the WINBOT W2 again (such as every quarter or longer), please empty the water tank to avoid any unpleasant odors..
