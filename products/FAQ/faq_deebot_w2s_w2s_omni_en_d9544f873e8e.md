# DEEBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - Fixing the station power port rubber cover

## Question

Fixing the station power port rubber cover

## Answer

You can use a toothpick or similar tool to gently press one end of the rubber plug into the socket to secure it in place.
