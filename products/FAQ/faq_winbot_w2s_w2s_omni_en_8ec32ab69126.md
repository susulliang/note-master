# WINBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - Selecting a Cleaning Mode

## Question

Selecting a Cleaning Mode

## Answer

1.Press and hold the START button to start the fan, place the WINBOT's bottom close to the window, then the WINBOT will attach itself.
2. You can also choose different cleaning modes,
①deep cleaning
②thorough cleaning
③fast cleaning
④edge cleaning
⑤spot cleaning
When the WINBOT starts to work, you can also click the remote control button to freely control WINBOT's direction and manually spray water with a 360° range of motion.
