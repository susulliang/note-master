# ULTRAMARINE / ULTRAMARINE P1 / ZH - How to replace the bottom scraper

## Question

How to replace the bottom scraper

## Answer

1.Use a tool to remove the the bottom cover plate.
2.Detach the old scraper’s mushroom head clips.
3.Attach the new scraper strips to the scraper cover plate (make sure to distinguish the left and right strips), and trim off any excess thread to prevent interference with the filter basket during installation.
4.Reinstall the scraper cover plate.
