# 任务未完成，GOAT为何自动结束了割草任务？

## Question

任务未完成，GOAT为何自动结束了割草任务？

## Answer

1. To avoid schedule conflicts, if GOAT's mow task is paused for over 48 hours, the program will automatically terminate the task.
