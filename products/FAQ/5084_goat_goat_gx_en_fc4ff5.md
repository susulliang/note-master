# I have adjusted the rain sensor delay time to start mowing immediately after the rain stops. Why is GOAT not working when there is no rain outside?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

I have adjusted the rain sensor delay time to start mowing immediately after the rain stops. Why is GOAT not working when there is no rain outside?

## 答案 / Answer

G00109

Intelligence & Privacy

The modified rain delay time will automatically take effect the next time the rain delay is triggered. If you want it to take effect immediately, you can turn the rain delay off and on again, and then it will work according to the latest settings.
