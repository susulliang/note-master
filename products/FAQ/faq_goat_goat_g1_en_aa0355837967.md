# GOAT / GOAT G1 / EN - How can I select the mowing efficiency for the GOAT G1?

## Question

How can I select the mowing efficiency for the GOAT G1?

## Answer

You can select the mowing efficiency in the App or on the robot's display: Precision and Efficiency
(1) The traveling speed is lower in Precision mode, which is recommended when you require precise mowing. In Precision mode, the robot is able to mow an area of approximately 80 square meters per hour in an open environment.
(2) The traveling speed is higher in Efficiency mode, which is recommended when you require fast mowing. In Efficiency mode, the robot is able to mow an area of approximately 120 square meters per hour in an open environment. However, there may be missed areas.
