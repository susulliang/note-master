# What should I do if the App prompts that DEEBOT is blocked, and the cleaning ends during area cleaning? / If the door to the area was closed when DEEBOT cleans it last time, it will prompt that DEEBOT is blocked, and the cleaning ends when you select this area to clean in your next cleaning .
Solution: Please open the door to this area, and then let DEEBOT complete an auto-cleaning cycle. After that, please try to do the area cleaning again.
