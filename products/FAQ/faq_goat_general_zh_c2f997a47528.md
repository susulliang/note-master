# 800平米的草坪，割草大概需要多久？

## Question

800平米的草坪，割草大概需要多久？

## Answer

Fully Charged mowing cycle: From 100% charge to 15% remaining, then returns to recharge. If mowing continues after low-charge recharge, it recharges to 80% before resuming mowing. After mowing until 15% remaining, it returns to recharge again.
 Mowing an 800 m² area requires approximately 870 minutes (14.5 hours).

 Lawn area: 800 m²
 Mow and charging process description:
 GOAT begins mowing from full charge. When battery level drops to 15%, it returns to recharge to 80% before resuming mowing. This cycle repeats until the entire lawn is completed.

 Single mow and charging breakdown:
 First mow session: 195 m² | 90 min
 Charging (15% → 80%): 100 min
 Subsequent 4 mow cycles:
 Each cycle: 140 m² | 65 min
 Charging per cycle: 100 min
 Final mow cycle: 45 m² | 20 min

 Total operating time:
 Pure mowing time: 370 min
 Charging time: 500 min
 Total duration: Approximately 14.5 hours

 The above test data is obtained under standard laboratory test conditions, using a standard lawn with approximately 1 cm grass height per mow. Actual mow efficiency and total time may vary depending on grass type, lawn density, grass height, terrain slope, number of obstacles, and environmental factors.
