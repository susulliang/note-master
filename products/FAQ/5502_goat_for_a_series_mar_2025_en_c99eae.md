# 我可以在晚上或是雨天进行建图吗？-Lidar / Can I create a map in the evening or on a rainy day?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我可以在晚上或是雨天进行建图吗？-Lidar / Can I create a map in the evening or on a rainy day?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

GOAT使用了LiDAR定位技术，LiADR不受光线影响。但如果您选择自动建图，机器需要通过视觉传感器识别草坪环境，如果光线差或者摄像头上有雨水会导致识别失效或识别准确率低，建议您在光线充足且晴朗的白天进行建图。

GOAT uses LiDAR positioning technology, and LiDAR is not affected by light. But if you choose automatic mapping, the machine needs to recognize the lawn environment through camera. Poor lighting or rain on the camera can cause recognition failure or low recognition accuracy.  It is recommended that mapping is done on a bright, sunny day.
