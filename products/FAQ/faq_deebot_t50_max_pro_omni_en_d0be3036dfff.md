# Disassembly, clean and installation Guide of side brush

## Question

Disassembly, clean and installation Guide of side brush

## Answer

Warm Reminder：
When your robot has been used for a period of time, something is wrapped on the side brush or a voice prompts that the side brush is tangled, etc., you can follow the following steps to try:
1.Remove the top cover, flip the power switch to "O" to turn it off, then turn the Robot over.
2.Remove the side brush, clean it, remove any entangled materials with cleaning tools.
3.Align the side brush with the mounting slot and press it down. When you hear a "click" sound, it means it has been installed in place.
