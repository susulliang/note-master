## 答案 / Answer

Disassembly, clean and installation of mopping pad plate and mopping pad(standard version)

Disassembly and clean of mopping pad plate assembly                                                                                                                                                         1.Remove the reservoir.                                                                                                                            2.Lift the mopping pad assembly slightly upward to remove the moping pad plate, and tear off the washable mopping pad.                                                                                                                                                                                        Install the mopping pad and mopping pad plate                                                                                                                                                     1.Remove the reservoir.                                                                                                                    2.Stick the mopping pad evenly onto the mopping pad plate (with Velcro side).                                    3.Align the mopping pad plate with the installation port,press slightly hard to confirm that you hear a click.                                                                                                                            4.Install the reservoir back to the Robot, confirm that you hear a click;
