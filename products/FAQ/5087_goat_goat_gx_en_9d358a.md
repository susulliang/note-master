# What is the range of the automatic edge optimization, and will the edges of restricted areas be optimized?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What is the range of the automatic edge optimization, and will the edges of restricted areas be optimized?

## 答案 / Answer

G00112

Intelligence & Privacy

The GOAT will only perform AI recognition optimization on the edges that are selected to be optimized. The edges of restricted areas will not be optimized by default. A single optimization allows for distance expansion equivalent to the size of the mower.
