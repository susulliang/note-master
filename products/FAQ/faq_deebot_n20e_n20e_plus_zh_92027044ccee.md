# DEEBOT / N20e&N20e Plus / ZH - 水箱不渗水如何处理？What to do if the reservoir doesn't seep？

## Question

水箱不渗水如何处理？What to do if the reservoir doesn't seep？

## Answer

1.请确认抹布支架是否正确安装；                                                                                                                                                                  2.请确认抹布支架上的小磁铁是否在；                                                                                                                                                            3.请打开水箱橡胶塞检查水箱水量；
4.请使用抹布擦拭机器底部出水孔，防止杂质堵塞。
5.请使用抹布擦拭机器尾部注水口，防止杂质堵塞。                                                                                                                                          1.Please check that if the mopping pad plate is installed properly.                                                                                        2.Please check if the magnet is still in the mopping pad plate.                                                                                                                      3.Open the water inlet to check if there is any water inside.                                                                                                                        4.Wipe the water outlet.                                                                                                                                                                       5.Wipe the water filling nozzle.
