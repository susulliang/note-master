# DEEBOT / T50降配版 / EN - How to clean the cleaning sink?

## Question

How to clean the cleaning sink?

## Answer

1.Remove the mop washing tray.
2.Clean it by rinsing with clean water.
3.After cleaning, reinstall it back into the station.
Note: Pay attention to the complete installation of the overflow float.
