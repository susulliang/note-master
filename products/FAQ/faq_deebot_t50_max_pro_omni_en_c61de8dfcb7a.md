# DEEBOT / T50 MAX PRO OMNI / EN - Network Connection Guide

## Question

Network Connection Guide

## Answer

Download and sign in to the ECOVACS HOME APP
1.Place the Robot near the station and scan the QR code (Tip: You can also download the ECOVACS HOME app from the App Store);
2.Install the App, register, and then log in to the App home page.
Network Connection
1.Scan the QR code on the Robot, enter the Wi-Fi password, and click to log in.
2.Follow the prompts on the App interface, remove the top cover, slide the power switch to “I” to power on, waiting for sound. Select "Next" and short press the reset button. After the voice prompt "Enter network setup mode" is heard, select "Next" and wait for the network to connect;
