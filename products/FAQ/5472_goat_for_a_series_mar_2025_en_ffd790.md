# 我的花园中有路应该如何建图？ / How should I map when there is a road in my garden?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的花园中有路应该如何建图？ / How should I map when there is a road in my garden?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

如果您的路面平坦，且与周边区域无高低落差，您可以将路面区域直接包含在地图内。如果您的路面与周围区域的高低落差高于4cm且将草坪分成了两个区域，您需要人为搭建一个宽度大于1.2m的通道来连通两个草坪区域。建图时需仅沿创建的通道边缘进行建图，将其他路面区域划出

If your road surface is flat and flush with the surrounding area, you can include the road surface directly in the map. If your road is higher or lower than the surrounding area by over 4 cm and divides the lawn into two areas, you will need to create a man-made access wider than 1.2 m to connect the two areas. When mapping, you need to map only along the edge of the created access, and delimit other road areas
