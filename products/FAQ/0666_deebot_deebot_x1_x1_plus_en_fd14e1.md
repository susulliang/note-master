# What should I do if the driving wheels are stuck?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What should I do if the driving wheels are stuck?

## 答案 / Answer

D20242

Cleaning

Turn and press down the driving wheel to check if there is any foreign material entanglement or stuck stuff, and remove them in a timely manner. If the problem persists, please contact Customer Service.
