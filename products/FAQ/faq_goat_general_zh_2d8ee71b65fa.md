# 我把GOAT拿得很远，为什么没有触发地图范围外的防盗报警？

## Question

我把GOAT拿得很远，为什么没有触发地图范围外的防盗报警？

## Answer

1. Please confirm whether "Out-of-Map Range Alarm" is enabled in "Settings" > "More Settings" > "Security Settings".
 2. The default alarm range is 200 meters, though actual usage may involve slight variations.
 3. If the device loses network connection, it will be unable to send alarms.
