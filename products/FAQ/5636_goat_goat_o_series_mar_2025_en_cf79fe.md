# 如果把RTK基站头取下后未按原位置放回，还能正常使用GOAT吗？ / Can I still use GOAT if the RTK station is removed and not put back in original place?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如果把RTK基站头取下后未按原位置放回，还能正常使用GOAT吗？ / Can I still use GOAT if the RTK station is removed and not put back in original place?

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

若RTK基站头发生了移动，GOAT无法正常使用。
移动会造成GOAT定位错误和出界、坠落等风险，甚至可能导致GOAT停止工作。

可将RTK基站归回原位，或移动基站并固定后，重新对RTK基站上电，重新建图。

The GOAT cannot be used if the RTK reference station is removed and not put back in original place.
The movement will cause the GOAT positioning errors and risks such as out of boundary or falling, even the GOAT may stop working.

To restore proper positioning, it is recommended to return the RTK station to its original location, or remap after moving the RTK station.
