# Why doesn't the 3D map show carpets?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why doesn't the 3D map show carpets?

## 答案 / Answer

D20151-11

3D Map

(1) When in Vacuum and Mopping mode, DEEBOT will avoid carpets and cannot display the full carpet. Please use Vacuum mode to complete a full cleaning and then the carpet will be displayed.
(2) In Quick Mapping mode, to save time, DEEBOT will not travel through the whole home. Please do a full cleaning in Vacuum mode and then the carpet will be displayed.
