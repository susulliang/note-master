# WINBOT / WINBOT mini2 .0 19langs / EN - Guidance for Firmware Version Upgrade

## Question

Guidance for Firmware Version Upgrade

## Answer

1. Connect the power cord to the adapter and plug it in, then wait for the startup voice prompt to finish.
 2. Long press the Start Button to power on, then long press it again to turn off the fan. Confirm that the indicator stays solid blue.
 3. Open the APP to enter the device, click "Settings", click "WINBOT Upgrade", follow the prompts to complete the upgrade.
 Note：After the upgrade is successful, the fan will automatically start and disconnect from the app. Once the fan stops, the device will automatically reconnect for normal use.
