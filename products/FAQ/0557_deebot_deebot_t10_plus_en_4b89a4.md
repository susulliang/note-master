# Why does emptying make a strange noise?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why does emptying make a strange noise?

## 答案 / Answer

D20302+

Station

(1) When DEEBOT returns to the charging station, there is a small possibility that it will not align with the emptying port. Take the robot out of the docking station and return it to charge, then use the App to empty. 
(2) If you have purchased a dust-collecting docking station separately, make sure that the dust bin in the DEEBOT has been replaced with the one with dust collection port. 
If the problem persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Service.
