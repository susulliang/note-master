# Learning is displayed as completed despite the GOAT only learning part of the map.

## Question

Learning is displayed as completed despite the GOAT only learning part of the map.

## Answer

(1) When learning the map, the GOAT only needs to travel briefly on the map to understand the whole layout.
 (2) Check whether there are obstacles or narrow passageways less than 2 m in width that are hindering the GOAT's normal route.
