# Upgrade your DEEBOT - firmware upgrade / For better product experience, sometimes you need to upgrade the firmware in the ECOVACS HOME App. Here are some tips and guidance for you about the upgrade.

Tips:
1. The robot needs to be placed in the base station and charged.
2. The battery level need to be more than 50%. (It is recommended to upgrade after 3 hours of charging.)
3. Make sure DEEBOT is connected with the ECOVACS HOME App, and the WiFi signal is good.
Steps:
1. Login in the ECOVACS HOME App
2. Tap the Enter Smart Cleaning
3. Tap the setting icon on the right top corner of the screen 
4. Select "About DEEBOT“ ＞ ”Update DEEBOT“

Notes: If automatic upgrade is enabled, DEEBOT will upgrade to the latest version automatically.
