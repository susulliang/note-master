# GOAT为什么配网时提示机器已被占用？ / Why does the GOAT indicate that the machine is occupied during network setup?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT为什么配网时提示机器已被占用？ / Why does the GOAT indicate that the machine is occupied during network setup?

## 答案 / Answer

6- APP其他

如果您的机器已被他人配对，将无法支持第二个人配对。
（1）如果是您的家人已配对过，可以请已配对的用户将机器分享给你，被分享者同样会拥有使用权限。
（2）如果您的机器被他人配对，可联系配对者解除配对（设备列表删除设备）
（3）可重置机器后重试。
若以上方式无效，请联系售后。

If your machine has already been paired by others, the new pairing is not allowed.  
(1) If the paired account is your family's, it can be shared through APP, you can also have the permission to use it.  
(2) If the paired account is others', you can contact the orevious user to unpair (device list delete device through APP)  
(3) Reset the machine and try again.  
If the above method is invalid, please contact the after-sales service.
