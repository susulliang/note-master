# 基站相关功能使用？ / What are the station-related functions?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

基站相关功能使用？ / What are the station-related functions?

## 答案 / Answer

1.打开App点击进入”进入设备”
2.点击“基站功能”图标进入基站控制面板，可依次根据机器的工作进程，选择集尘、烘干、清洗拖布
注：可实时在App上查看到基站工作状态

1. Open the app and click Enter;
2. Click the Station icon to enter the control panel, where you can select dust collection, drying, or mop cleaning according to the Robot's work progress.
Note: You can view the station's working status in real time on the app.
