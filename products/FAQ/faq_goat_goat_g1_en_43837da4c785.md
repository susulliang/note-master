# The App shows that the lens is being cleaned, but it is not actually being cleaned.

## Question

The App shows that the lens is being cleaned, but it is not actually being cleaned.

## Answer

(1) There is a delay of about 10 seconds when the docking station triggers lens cleaning.
(2) The communication between the docking station and the robot is sometimes unstable, and occasionally the brush does not move. If the lens is not automatically cleaned after every use, please contact customer service.
(3) Check whether the red light on the docking station is on. If it is on, this means that the cleaning motor may be faulty. Please contact customer service.
