# 下视传感器积灰，请擦拭 / Alert: Anti-drop Sensors Dust Accumulation

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

下视传感器积灰，请擦拭 / Alert: Anti-drop Sensors Dust Accumulation

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
当您在使用过程中若您有遇到提示下视积灰或下视异常时，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
1.取下主机污水盒，将机器翻转，其底部朝上放置。擦拭底部下视传感应器，防止灰尘遮挡。
2.重新装回主机污水盒，关机重启后观察。

Friendly Reminder:
 If you encounter an alert about "Anti-drop sensor is covered with dust" or "Anti-drop sensors error", follow these steps:
 1.Remove the dirty water box, flip the robot upside down, and gently wipe the Anti-drop sensor with a soft, dry cloth to remove dust or debris.
 2.Reattach the dirty water box, power cycle the robot (turn off, then on), and check if the alert resolves.
