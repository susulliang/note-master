# Troubleshooting: Bumper Stuck Alert

## Question

Troubleshooting: Bumper Stuck Alert

## Answer

1.Remove the robot's bumper protector strip.
2.Gently tap the left, center, and right sections of the bumper to ensure it springs back properly.
