# 遥控建图-我的前后院是不连通的，应该如何建图？ / My front yard and backyard are not connected. How should I map them?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

遥控建图-我的前后院是不连通的，应该如何建图？ / My front yard and backyard are not connected. How should I map them?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

（1）建议您仅针对前院或者后院进行建图。
（2）或为前后院自行搭建一个宽度大于1m且悬空高度高于45cm的通道将前后院连接成一个区域，建立可通行的通道

(1) It is recommended that you only create maps for the front or back yard.
(2)Map two areas, and build a path with a width greater than 1m and a suspended height higher than 45cm to connect the front and back yards.
