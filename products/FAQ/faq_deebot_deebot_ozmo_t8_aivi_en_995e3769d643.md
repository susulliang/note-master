# Can I replace DEEBOT's cleaning pad with paper towels?

## Question

Can I replace DEEBOT's cleaning pad with paper towels?

## Answer

This is not recommended. The DEEBOT cleaning pad was designed specifically for DEEBOT to clean and maintain floors.
Please do not use unofficial accessories.
