# 我搬动机器到新的草坪区域，为什么无法新增新的地图区域？ / I moved the machine to a new lawn area, why can't I add a new map area?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我搬动机器到新的草坪区域，为什么无法新增新的地图区域？ / I moved the machine to a new lawn area, why can't I add a new map area?

## 答案 / Answer

MAP

新增草坪区域为了保证与原有地图区域关系，尽量从基站或已创建地图内出发进行区域新增。

In order to ensure the positional accuracy between the new lawn area and the original map area, it is recommended to start adding the new area by remoting the GOAT from the Station or the original area.
