# WINBOT / W2/W2 OMNI / EN - What is the manual control in the ECOVACS HOME App used for?

## Question

What is the manual control in the ECOVACS HOME App used for?

## Answer

1. For scenarios with many obstacles, use the manual control in the ECOVACS HOME App to clean can avoid obstacles and have a better cleaning experience.
2. When the WINBOT W2 encounters obstacles and gets stuck, you can use the manual control in the ECOVACS HOME App to clean or control the robot to an easy-to-reach location to get the robot.
