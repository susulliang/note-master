# Is there a limit on how long the GOAT can work?

## Question

Is there a limit on how long the GOAT can work?

## Answer

There is no limit to the working hours set in the program of the GOAT. When power is less than 20%, the GOAT will automatically return to the docking station to charge. When power reaches 80%, the GOAT will automatically head to the breakpoint location to continue mowing. However, in order to ensure better positioning and mowing results, as well as animal safety, it's not recommended to mow in the rain or after dark.
