Robot Network Connection Guide

1.Make sure the Robot is near the charging station. Scan the QR code to download the ECOVACS HOME App. You can also search it on the app market.Register and log in.                                              
1. Scan the QR code，input wifi code, click to enter.
2.Turn on the Robot; Short press the reset button; Confirm that the voice prompt "Enter network setting mode" is heard; Follow the App prompt;                                                                             3.After it is done, you can name the Robot.
