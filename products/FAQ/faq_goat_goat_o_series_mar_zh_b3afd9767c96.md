# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - RTK基站的信号覆盖范围有多大？

## Question

RTK基站的信号覆盖范围有多大？

## Answer

In optimal open environments, the signal coverage range of an RTK base station can extend up to 300-400 meters. However, in complex courtyard environments, the effective coverage range can be reduced to approximately 80 meters due to environmental factors such as building structures and vegetation cover. This allows for effective coverage in most domestic scenarios. RTK base station location can be adjusted according to the specific scenario to optimize performance.
