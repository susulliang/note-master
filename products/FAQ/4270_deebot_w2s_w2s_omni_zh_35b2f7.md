## 答案 / Answer

Why Didn’t WINBOT Return to the Starting Point?

WINBOT is designed to return to the starting point after cleaning,
but there may be slight deviation due to calculation accuracy.

If external factors (e.g. large window handles)
or frequent mode changes during cleaning affect its return path,
please use the app’s omni-directional control to bring WINBOT back to a reachable position.
