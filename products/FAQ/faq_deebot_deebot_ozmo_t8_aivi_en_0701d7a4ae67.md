# DEEBOT / DEEBOT OZMO T8 AIVI / EN - Why does DEEBOT spill dust when I move the robot manually?

## Question

Why does DEEBOT spill dust when I move the robot manually?

## Answer

If dust is spilling when you move DEEBOT  by hand, the Dust Bin is probably too full. The Dust Bin should be emptied regularly for best cleaning performance. When moving DEEBOT  manually, please lift the robot with both hands and keep the end marked with “DEEBOT” tilted slightly up to avoid spillage.
