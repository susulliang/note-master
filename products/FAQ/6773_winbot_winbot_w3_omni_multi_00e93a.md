# 28

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

28

## 答案 / Answer

1.Press and hold the winbot’s start button for more than 2 seconds. Wait until the fan stops, then follow the voice prompt to remove the robot from the glass.
2.Open the filling opening on the top of the winbot. Check the cleaning solution level, If it is low, add the ECOVACS winbot cleaning solution into the tank, then close the sealing cap tightly.
3.Restart the winbot and reattach it to the glass. Let it run for a while and observe whether it operates normally.
