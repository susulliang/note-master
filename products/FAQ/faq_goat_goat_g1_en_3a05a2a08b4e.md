# Why is there no signal when a Navigation Beacon is shown in the Navigation Beacon list in the App?

## Question

Why is there no signal when a Navigation Beacon is shown in the Navigation Beacon list in the App?

## Answer

The GOAT at its current position is unable to receive a signal from the Navigation Beacon.
(1) The Navigation Beacon battery has been used up and needs to be replaced immediately.
(2) Obstacles between the Navigation Beacon and GOAT are blocking the signal.
