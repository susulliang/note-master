# DEEBOT / W2S&W2S OMNI海外 / EN - Why Does WINBOT Make a Rubbing Sound on the Glass?

## Question

Why Does WINBOT Make a Rubbing Sound on the Glass?

## Answer

To ensure strong grip and safe movement, WINBOT uses high-friction MPU material tracks.
The increased friction may cause a rubbing sound when it moves on glass.
No worries — this is normal and a sign that WINBOT is moving securely and reliably.
