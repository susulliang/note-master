# DEEBOT / DEEBOT OZMO T8 AIVI / EN - Will DEEBOT's audio reminders become a nuisance in daily life?

## Question

Will DEEBOT's audio reminders become a nuisance in daily life?

## Answer

(1) The volume of DEEBOT's audio has been set close to the volume of daily speech based on professional testing, and will not disturb you in your daily life. If you do not need the audio reminders or find them disturbing, you can turn on Do Not Disturb mode or turn off audio in the App.
(2) Please be aware that if you turn off the audio, you may not be able to find DEEBOT when it gets stuck.
(3) You can turn down DEEBOT's audio volume.
