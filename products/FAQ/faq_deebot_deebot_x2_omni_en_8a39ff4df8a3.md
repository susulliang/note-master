# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X2 OMNI / EN - How do I change winter and daylight saving time?

## Question

How do I change winter and daylight saving time?

## Answer

1. You can select your time zone in App Settings > DEEBOT Info > Time Zone.
2. You can also set the time zone of your phone system to match the time zone where you live, then select Sync With Phone Time Zone in the ECOVACS HOME App (App Settings > DEEBOT Info > Time Zone).
3. When the time zone is modified, changes to times in the schedule and log are synchronized.
