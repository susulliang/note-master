# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 OMNI / EN - DEEBOT X1 misses some rooms while cleaning.

## Question

DEEBOT X1 misses some rooms while cleaning.

## Answer

(1) If a carpet or floor mat is placed at the door of the room, DEEBOT will not go over it in Vacuum and Mopping mode so as not to wet the carpet or floor mat.
(2) DEEBOT can go over obstacles at a height of up to 2 cm. In order to pass smoothly at home, it will not attempt to cross any threshold or step that exceeds the maximum height the robot can cross.
(3) It will occasionally make a mistake when avoiding obstacles.
