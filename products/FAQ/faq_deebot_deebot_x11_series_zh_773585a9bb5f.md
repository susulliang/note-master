# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 如何设置虚拟墙？

## Question

如何设置虚拟墙？

## Answer

1. Click ‘Map’ and select ‘Virtual Wall’.
2. Select ‘Line’ or “Rectangle” to determine the location of the virtual wall.
3. You can also select the function of the virtual wall, which is divided into two types: no-entry’ or ‘no mopping’.
4. Once selected, DEEBOT will automatically avoid the area enclosed by the virtual wall when it is working.
