# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 补光灯该怎么设置？如果主机补光灯不亮该怎么办呢？

## Question

补光灯该怎么设置？如果主机补光灯不亮该怎么办呢？

## Answer

The Supplementary Light requires no manual setup or control; it is solely for recharging assistance:
 Setup instructions: The Supplementary Light automatically turns on and off throughout the recharging process and cannot be manually operated.
 Troubleshooting: The GOAT Supplementary Light only activates during recharging and automatically turns on when within 1.5 meters of the station. If it remains off, contact after-sales support.
