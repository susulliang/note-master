# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T20e OMNI / EN - How do I disassemble and clean the side brushes?

## Question

How do I disassemble and clean the side brushes?

## Answer

1. Power off DEEBOT then flip it over and place it with the bottom facing up.
2. Lightly pull the side brushes up and remove them.
3. Clean the bearing and remove any entangling debris from the side brushes.
4. Push the side brushes back into their mounting slots. You’ll hear a click when they are properly installed.
Note: The color of each side brush should be consistent with that of its mounting slot.
