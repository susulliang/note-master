## 问题 / Question

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the cover of the ROBOT and slide the Power Switch to "I" to turn it on.
2.Position the ROBOT directly in front of the station. Press and hold the "Start Button" to initiate the robot's automatic return to the station. Listen for the voice prompt "Start charging."
II. Off-Peak Charging Charging Instructions:
1. Open the App → Tap "Enter" → Settings → Advanced Station Settings → Enable Off-Peak Charging.
Off-Peak Charging Instructions:
1. The default setting is off. When enabled, the battery will fully charge only during scheduled time slots. Outside these periods, charging occurs only to maintain a safe power level.
2. To ensure normal operation, charging may occur outside off-peak hours. If tasks remain unfinished, the robot will charge to required levels during non-off-peak periods. If no tasks exist, it will charge to safety levels. (Default charging duration: 7 hours; cannot be set below 7 hours)
3. During non-charging periods, the battery must maintain at least 30% capacity. When the battery level is at or above 30%, it will hold the current charge level.
