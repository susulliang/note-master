# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 基站指示灯一直显示蓝灯闪烁

## Question

基站指示灯一直显示蓝灯闪烁

## Answer

When the GOAT is successfully connected to the docking station and charging, the  indicator light will continue to flash blue.
