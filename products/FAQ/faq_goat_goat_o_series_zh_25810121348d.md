# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - GOAT只学习了一部分地图就显示学习完成了

## Question

GOAT只学习了一部分地图就显示学习完成了

## Answer

(1) When learning the map, the GOAT only needs to travel briefly on the map to understand the whole layout.
(2) Check whether there are obstacles or narrow passageways less than 0.7 m in width that are hindering the GOAT's normal route.
