# GOAT的切割效率应该如何选择? / How can I select the mowing efficiency for the GOAT?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT的切割效率应该如何选择? / How can I select the mowing efficiency for the GOAT?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

可通过APP或者主机显示屏设置切割效率：精修、高效
（1）精修模式下，行走速度降低，建议在需要精细割草的时使用；精修模式下在空旷环境中每小时可切割约140㎡
（2）高效模式下，行走速度提高，建议在需要快速割草时使用；高效模式下在空旷环境中每小时可切割约180㎡，但可能带一些漏割

You can select the mowing efficiency in the App or on the robot's display: Precision and Efficiency 
(1) The traveling speed is lower in Precision mode, which is recommended when you require precise mowing. In Precision mode, the robot is able to mow an area of approximately 140 square meters per hour in an open environment. 
(2) The traveling speed is higher in Efficiency mode, which is recommended when you require fast mowing. In Efficiency mode, the robot is able to mow an area of approximately 180 square meters per hour in an open environment. However, there may be missed areas.
