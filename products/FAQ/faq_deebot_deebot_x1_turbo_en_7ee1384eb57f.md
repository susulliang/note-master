# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 TURBO / EN - How do I enable the smart voice assistant YIKO?

## Question

How do I enable the smart voice assistant YIKO?

## Answer

After connecting the robot with the ECOVACS HOME App successfully, confirm that voice wake-up function is enabled in the App settings. You can wake up DEEBOT by saying “OK YIKO,” so that it can respond to the corresponding voice commands.
