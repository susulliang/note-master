## 答案 / Answer

This may be caused by the following reasons:
① Abnormal dirty water box issues trigger frequent cleaning cycles. Check the app for any dirty water box error alerts. If present, clean the dirty water box following the corresponding steps;
② Check if AI stain recognition is enabled in the app. Complex floor textures or lighting conditions may cause misidentification. You can disable this feature in the app and observe the machine's performance afterward.
