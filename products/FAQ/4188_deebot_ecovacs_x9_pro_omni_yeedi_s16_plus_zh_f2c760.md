# Alert:Bumper Stuck

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

Alert:Bumper Stuck

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the robot's bumper protector strip.
2.Gently tap the left, center, and right sections of the bumper to ensure it springs back properly.                                                              3.Restart and try again.
