# App Connection Guidance

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

App Connection Guidance

## 答案 / Answer

1. Connect the power cord to the adapter and plug it in, then wait for the startup voice prompt to finish.
 2. Scan the QR code on the WINBOT or search for "ECOVACS HOME" in the App Store to download and install the App. Complete the registration and enter the main interface.
 3. On the App homepage, click "Add Robot", scan the QR code on the WINBOT, and follow the App's instructions to complete the connection.
