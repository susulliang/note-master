# Why is the “GOAT alarms when it goes beyond the station's signal coverage" triggered when the mower is inside the courtyard?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why is the “GOAT alarms when it goes beyond the station's signal coverage" triggered when the mower is inside the courtyard?

## 答案 / Answer

48

Intelligence & Privacy

GX is equiped with GPS Module. It occasionally has signal drift which may lead to a certain probability of false alarms. If there are frequent false alarms, please contact ECOVACS customer service.
