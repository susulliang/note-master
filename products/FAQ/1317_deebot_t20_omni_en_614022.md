# How to use the YIKO voice assistant?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

How to use the YIKO voice assistant?

## 答案 / Answer

D20342

YIKO

1. Make sure the YIKO voice assistant is enabled in the ECOVACS HOME App.
2. You can check the YIKO guidance and the voice commands in the App. Wake up the robot by saying “OK YIKO”, and then say your voice commands, such as "OK YIKO, start cleaning".

Notes：
* YIKO voice commands may be updated. Please refer to the voice commands in the ECOVACS HOME App.
* YIKO voice commands can be used after the robot is connected with the network.
