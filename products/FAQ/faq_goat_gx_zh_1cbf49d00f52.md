# GOAT / GX / ZH - Can the GOAT be used in the rain?

## Question

Can the GOAT be used in the rain?

## Answer

GOAT is IPX6-rated and able to withstand rain. However, it is easy for the blades to become blocked by wet grass, and rain on the camera will also interfere with the GOAT’s visual localization function to a certain extent. It is therefore recommended to turn on the rain sensor, so the GOAT will begin mowing after the lawn has dried.
