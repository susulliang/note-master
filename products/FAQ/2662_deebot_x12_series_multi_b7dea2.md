# 18

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

18

## 答案 / Answer

1.Remove the dirty water tank, open its lid, and empty the dirty water. (If there is dirt at the bottom of the tank, use a cleaning brush to clean it.) Then, close the tank lid securely.
2.Remove the clean water tank, open its lid, and clean it if you find any dirt. Then, close the tank lid securely.
Note: After cleaning the dirty water tank, make sure the lid is tightly closed.
