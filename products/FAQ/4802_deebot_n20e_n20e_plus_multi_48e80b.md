## 答案 / Answer

Can't the robot find the station or have difficulty returning to the station?

Ensure that the door of the room where the  station is located is not closed.
Confirm that there are no obstacles within 0.05 meters of the station and within 0.8 meters ahead, and there are no reflective objects (such as mirrors, metal, glass, etc.) around the station.
(3) It is recommended that the robot start cleaning from the station. (If the robot is outside, please move the robot to the station, press the recharge button briefly, and the robot will return to the station.)
Ensure that the charging dock has not been powered off or moved by humans midway.
(5) Ensure that the station is not placed on the carpet, as the traveling resistance on the carpet is high, which affects the success rate of the robot's recharge.
