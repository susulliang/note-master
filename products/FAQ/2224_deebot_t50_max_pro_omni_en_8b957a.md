# 如何设置预约清洁？ / How to schedule a cleaning task?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

如何设置预约清洁？ / How to schedule a cleaning task?

## 答案 / Answer

1.通过App，可对全屋清洁/定制清洁/区域清洁
2.点击预约可新增预约清洁任务
3.可通过设置“重复”频率选择每周重复执行的时间

1.Through the App, we can schedule  a Auto/Customize/Area cleaning task
2.Click schedule to add
3.You can also set the time and frequency of cleaning appointment
