# RTK基站指示灯为什么会闪烁？ / Why does the RTK station light flash?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

RTK基站指示灯为什么会闪烁？ / Why does the RTK station light flash?

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

（1）RTK基站指示灯闪烁表示当前位置GPS信号不佳，请确认是否有环境变化对RTK基站有遮挡；（房屋、树木、其他遮挡）
（2）请确定RTK没有发生倾斜，或者位置产生移动；
（3）确认是否阴天，天气情况不佳导致RTK基站信号不良，晴天后再观测。
（4）可尝试重启RTK基站，将RTK断电后，重新通电，完成重启
*若RTK基站持续蓝灯闪烁，无法解决需联系科沃斯售后服务部门

(1) The flash indicator indicates that the GPS signal of the current position is not good. Please confirm whether the RTK station is blocked by houses, trees or other kind of shadows.
(2) Make sure that the RTK is not tilted or the position is shifted.
(3) Confirm whether it is cloudy, poor weather conditions lead to poor signal of RTK station, Please check the light in sunny days.
(4) Please reset the RTK through power off the RTK, power on again. 
* If it cannot be resolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales Service
