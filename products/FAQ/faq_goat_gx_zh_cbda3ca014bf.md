# GOAT / GX / ZH - Can I rinse the mower with a pressure washer?

## Question

Can I rinse the mower with a pressure washer?

## Answer

Do not use a pressure washer, as the water will enter the seals and damage mechanical components.
 Tips: Before cleaning the GOAT, turn off the mower's power.During cleaning, always wear protective gloves to avoid being injured by the blades.
