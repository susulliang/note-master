# Why have DEEBOT's lights and voice response both turned off?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why have DEEBOT's lights and voice response both turned off?

## 答案 / Answer

D20021

Cleaning

DEEBOT is equipped with a Do Not Disturb mode. Check whether Do Not Disturb has been enabled for the current period of time (Cleaning Settings - Do Not Disturb). If it is not enabled, please contact our customer service.
