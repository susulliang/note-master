# What should I do if the voice assistant cannot be woken up?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

What should I do if the voice assistant cannot be woken up?

## 答案 / Answer

Check the APP settings:
1.Open the APP ＞ Enter ＞ Tap the settings in the upper right corner ＞ Tap YIKO settings ＞ Turn on  the OK YIKO voice assistant.
2.Try turning off the robot and restarting it, then try to wake up the voice assistant to see if it responds normally.
Note: Please ensure that the current device network is good. When waking up the YIKO, please stay close to the robot, speak at a constant speed and accurately, and ensure that the robot can recognize it correctly.
