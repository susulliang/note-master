# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 基站需要和GOAT主机进行配对吗？

## Question

基站需要和GOAT主机进行配对吗？

## Answer

Pairing is not required. The charging connector is used by the docking station and the GOAT for simple communication. There is no corresponding relationship between them.
