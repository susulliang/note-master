# Can DEEBOT always be left on the charging dock when not in use?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can DEEBOT always be left on the charging dock when not in use?

## 答案 / Answer

D20016

Enable

Charging

Yes, we recommend leaving DEEBOT on the charging dock powered on. Once the robot has fully charged, it goes to sleep and will not become overcharged. 
Please do not allow children to play with it. 
When not using the robot for an extended period, we recommend powering it off and removing it from the charging dock.
