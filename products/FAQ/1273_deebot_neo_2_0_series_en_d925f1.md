# About the function of the robot's spot cleaning button

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

About the function of the robot's spot cleaning button

## 答案 / Answer

1. Short press: trigger spot cleaning.Take the main unit as the center and clean the square area of 1.5m×1.5m around it;
2. Long press: 3 seconds and above will trigger the child lock;
3. Long press: 25 seconds and above when the robot is on the AES/charging dock will force the robot to shut down.
