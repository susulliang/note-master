# GOAT / GX / ZH - How do I turn on the AI recognition feature?

## Question

How do I turn on the AI recognition feature?

## Answer

In the App, go to Settings > Laboratory Features and turn on AI recognition. The GOAT will then avoid currently recognizable obstacles while working. The AI model supports hot updates, and we will continuously add and optimize models.
