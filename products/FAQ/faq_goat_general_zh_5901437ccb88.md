# 我已经根据APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回了原位，为何APP上的故障未解除？

## Question

我已经根据APP提示将异常的RTK基站放回了原位，为何APP上的故障未解除？

## Answer

Please reset the RTK on the APP, Setting - RTK - Detail（icon）- Reset.
* If it cannot be resolved, please contact Ecovacs after-sales Service
