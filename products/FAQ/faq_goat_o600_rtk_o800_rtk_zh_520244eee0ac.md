# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - APP上的RTK信号强度地图为何大面积显示无信号覆盖？

## Question

APP上的RTK信号强度地图为何大面积显示无信号覆盖？

## Answer

GOAT's current location cannot receive satellite signals, possibly due to tree canopy, shade, or building obstructions. If in an area with no signal coverage (including map boundaries or No-Entry zone borders), we recommend installing a physical barrier over 20 cm tall to prevent the device from leaving the boundary or entering No-Entry zones.
