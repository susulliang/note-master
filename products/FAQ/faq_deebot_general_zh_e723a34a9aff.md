# 机器人为什么充不上电？ Robot can't be charged, what can I do?

## Question

机器人为什么充不上电？                                                        Robot can't be charged, what can I do?

## Answer

1.请检查电源插座是否通电，可以更换电源插座测试；
2.请检查擦试充电座充电极片是否有凹陷，是否能正常回弹，并用干净的干抹布擦试；
3.同时擦试下地宝尾部的电极片；
4.长按地宝启动按键，确认主机电源开关已打开；                                                                                                                                      5.将主机放置在充电座正前方，短按返回键，机器自主返回对接充电;若主机无法开机，可手动将机器轻推至基站，左右轻轻摇晃，等待开机音乐响起；
6.可以在APP中查看是否有闪电图标，如果有，代表着正在充电；                                                                                                         1.Check whether the power cord is correctly plugged into the power outlet. You can also try a different outlet.                                                                                                                      2.Please check whether the charging contacts of the docking station is dented and whether they can rebound normally, and wipe them with a clean dry cloth;                                                                                                                                                        3.And wipe the charging contacts of the Robot too;                                                                                                                        4.Long press power button to turn it on;                                                                                                                                          5.Place the Robot in front of the docking station, short press the return button to make Robot return automatically; If Robot can't be turned on, manually push it in, shake it gently left and right, and wait for the startup music to sound;                                                                                                                                  6.Please check charging status in the app if there is a lightning icon.
