# 为什么GOAT的屏幕上有些奇怪的显示？我不明白是什么意思 / Why are there strange displays on GOAT‘s screen？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么GOAT的屏幕上有些奇怪的显示？我不明白是什么意思 / Why are there strange displays on GOAT‘s screen？

## 答案 / Answer

GOAT在主机的数码管显示屏上显示了主机的几个特殊状态
1.示意图1表示主机在OTA升级中
2.示意图2表示主机STOP状态中，需要手动在主机端触发工作或解除STOP状态
3.示意图3表述主机触发某种异常状态E代表Error，后面三位数字表示错误码
4.示意图4表示主机工作中
5.示意图5表示主机待机中
6.示意图6为主机显示当前电量

GOAT shows several special states  on the screen:
1.Diagram 1 means GOAT is under upgrade
2.Diagram 2 means GOAT is stoped, need to manually relieve on the scren(START--＞OK)
3.Diagram 3 means GOAT triggers some exception, E means Error, the nex three digits represent the error code.
4.Diagram 4 means GOAT is working.
5.Diagram 5 means GOATis in standby mode
6.Diagram6 means the current battery capacity of  GOAT.
