# 使用手动模式割草时，APP总是提示距离不足，无法开启刀盘。

## Question

使用手动模式割草时，APP总是提示距离不足，无法开启刀盘。

## Answer

Per safety protocols, maintain proximity to GOAT during manual remote operation. Distance is limited by Bluetooth signal strength detection; insufficient signal triggers automatic pause. Restarting requires re-detection, which may cause delays. Additionally, Bluetooth signal strength varies by phone model. Stay close to GOAT during manual mowing. If the cutter head still fails to activate, try using a different phone model.
