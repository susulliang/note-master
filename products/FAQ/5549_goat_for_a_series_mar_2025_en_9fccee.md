# 地图上GOAT 的位置和实际和位置不符？ / The location of GOAT on the map is not the actual location.

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

地图上GOAT 的位置和实际和位置不符？ / The location of GOAT on the map is not the actual location.

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

（1）请确认是否搬动了GOAT，搬动后若未启动任务GOAT将不会进行重定位，APP地图上将仍显示GOAT被搬动前的位置
（2）请确认是否在GOAT运动过程中拖移了GOAT，拖移GOAT将不会触发重定位，APP地图上将仍显示GOAT被拖移前的位置
（3）请确认是否触发任务时GOAT在基站外，GOAT需要一定的时间寻回定位，此期间APP上的GOAT位置显示可能不准

如已排除以上情况，请检查
（1）GOAT 的激光传感器是否有脏污
（2）当前天气是否晴好无雨
如果GOAT始终在地图上显示的位置不正确，可删除地图后重新建图

(1) Please confirm whether GOAT has been moved or dragged. If the task is not started after that, GOAT will not be relocated, the position of GOAT before it is moved will still be displayed on the APP map 
(2)Please confirm if GOAT was dragged during the GOAT movement. Dragging GOAT will not immediately trigger relocation, and the APP map will still display the position of GOAT before it was dragged
(3) Please confirm whether GOAT is outside the charging station when the task is triggered. GOAT needs a certain amount of time to locate, during which time the GOAT location may not be displayed on the APP

If the above situation has been eliminated, please check
(1) Whether the LiDAR of GOAT is dirty
(2) Whether the current weather is fine.
If GOAT consistently appears incorrectly on the map, you can delete the map and rebuild it.
