# 当前室外有雨，为何GOAT不触发雨淋保护？ / There is currently  rain outside. Why did the GOAT not trigger the rain protection mode?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

当前室外有雨，为何GOAT不触发雨淋保护？ / There is currently  rain outside. Why did the GOAT not trigger the rain protection mode?

## 答案 / Answer

Intelligence&Security

（1）请检查雨淋传感器是否脏污，导致无法导通，可用抹布，毛刷或细砂纸清理雨淋传感器

(1) Please check if the rain sensor is dirty and unable to conduct. You can use a cloth, brush, or fine sandpaper to clean the rain sensor.
