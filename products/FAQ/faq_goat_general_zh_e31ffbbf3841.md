# 为什么GOAT在基站待机时自动关机了？

## Question

为什么GOAT在基站待机时自动关机了？

## Answer

This may have triggered "Silent Mode." Pressing the GOAT's power button five times consecutively will activate Silent Mode, causing the device to automatically shut down within 10 seconds. Silent Mode will automatically deactivate upon restarting the device.
 If none of the above applies, please contact Ecovacs After-Sales Service.
