## 答案 / Answer

Disassembly, clean and installation Guide of side brush

1.Long press the power button to turn off the Robot,turn it over.
2.Remove the side brush, clean the side brush, remove any entangled materials with cleaning tools.
3.Align the side brush with the installation port and press down. When you hear a "click" sound, it means it has been installed in place.
