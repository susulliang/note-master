# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - How to Use the Reset Button

## Question

How to Use the Reset Button

## Answer

Reminder: If your WINBOT becomes unresponsive, you can use the reset button to restart the program and restore normal operation. Please follow these steps:
1.With the WINBOT in standby mode, flip it over to the backside and locate the small hole marked with【reset】; this is the reset button.
2.Use a pointed tool, such as a toothpick, to gently press and hold the reset button. Release the button once the indicator light on the bottom of the WINBOT turns off, indicating the reset is complete.
3.Power the WINBOT off and then on again to restart it, confirming the reset was successful.
