# 梳齿滚刷为什么还是有头发缠绕? Why is there still hair entanglement on the comb roller brush?

## Question

梳齿滚刷为什么还是有头发缠绕? Why is there still hair entanglement on the comb roller brush?

## Answer

(1)建议使用强劲档,防缠绕的效果更好;
(2)梳齿可以解决绝大部分毛发场景,但是遇到长头发打结等极端场景,还是会有少量缠绕,建议定期清理滚刷;                                                          (1) It is recommended to use a strong gear for better anti-tangling effect.
(2) The comb teeth can solve the vast majority of hair scenes, but in extreme scenes such as long hair knots, there will still be a small amount of entanglement. It is recommended to clean the roller brush regularly.
