## 答案 / Answer

What should I do if the robot sometimes cannot avoid obstacles?

1.Please check if obstacle avoidance module is dusty. If it is, please gently wipe it with a dry tissue;                                                                                                                                                                2.Check if the Automatic Obstacle Avoidance is turned on. Enter APP-Click Settings-Click Robot Advanced Settings-Automatic Obstacle Avoidance.                                                                   Note: For black and linear objects, the Robot may slow down and lightly touch them.
