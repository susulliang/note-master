# How do I disassemble and clean the side brush? / 1. Power off DEEBOT then flip it over and place it with the bottom facing up. 
2. Lightly pull the side brush up and remove them. 
3. Clean the bearing and remove any entangling debris from the side brush. 
4. Push the side brush back into its mounting slot. You’ll hear a click when it's properly installed.
