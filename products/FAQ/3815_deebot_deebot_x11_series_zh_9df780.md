# 机器人装箱指导视频 / Robot Packing Instructions

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人装箱指导视频 / Robot Packing Instructions

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
在装箱之前，请确保完成以下步骤：
1.清空清/污水箱中的水、清洁液盒中的清洁液；打开app设置-设备搬家-点击“清空地宝清水箱”可清空内置小水箱的水，并将潮湿的滚筒拖布晾干（上下水版本还需“清空基站水箱”中的水）
2.取下主机正面上盖，将电源开关至“O”关机。
3.取下主机污水盒并清空其内的水，重新安装回原位。
4.拔下基站电源线，取下基站斜坡底座。
5.先把基站稳稳放进箱底，再盖上泡沫保护层。主机则放进侧边的包装盒里
6.依次将电源线、基站斜坡底座等配件放回包装箱内。

Kind Reminder:
Before packing, please make sure to complete the following steps:
1.Empty the clean/dirty water tank and the cleaning solution box. In the app, go to Settings ＞ Device Relocation ＞ tap “Empty DEEBOT Clean Water Tank” to drain the internal tank and dry the wet roller mop. (For auto refill and drain module version, need to tap “Empty Station Water Tank" to drain the station clean water tank)
2.Remove the top cover of the robot and switch it off by turning the power to “O.”
3.Take out the dirty water box from the robot, empty it, and reinstall it.
4.Unplug the station and remove the base.
5.Place the station securely at the bottom of the box and cover it with the protective foam. Put the robot in the side compartment.
6.Pack the power cord, base, and other accessories into the box.
