# DEEBOT / DEEBOT T10 PLUS（US） / EN - Can I wash the Dust Bin in DEEBOT with water and how do I wash it?

## Question

Can I wash the Dust Bin in DEEBOT with water and how do I wash it?

## Answer

(1) Remove the Dust Bin and rinse with clean water. Make sure the filter dry naturally before use. Using a damp filter may cause the robot to emit an abnormal sound during cleaning and the robot may become damp and break down.
(2) While the original filter is air drying, the user can use the identical spare filter provided.
