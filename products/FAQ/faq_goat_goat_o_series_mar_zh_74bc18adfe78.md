# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 我可以远程遥控GOAT进行建图吗？

## Question

我可以远程遥控GOAT进行建图吗？

## Answer

(1) The GOAT and the App are connected by Bluetooth only when mapping manually. When your phone is more than 6 m away from the GOAT, the Bluetooth connection will be automatically interrupted. The GOAT can automatically mapping without controlling manaully.
(2) It is recommend that you follow the GOAT for remote mapping in case you need to switch into remote control mode of modify the route.
