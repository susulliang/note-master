# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - 日常使用中若RTK基站丢失或损坏，还能正常使用GOAT吗？

## Question

日常使用中若RTK基站丢失或损坏，还能正常使用GOAT吗？

## Answer

If the RTK station is lost or damaged, GOAT will not function normally. In this case, GOAT will automatically cease operation upon detecting signal loss due to the inability to receive positioning signals.
