# How do I open the Dust Collection Cabin and replace the Dust Bag?

## Question

How do I open the Dust Collection Cabin and replace the Dust Bag?

## Answer

Replace the Dust Bag
1. Press the release button of the Dust Collection Cabin.
2. Remove dust bag and discard.
3. Wipe the dust collection cabin with a dry cloth and install a new dust bag.
4. Close the dust collection cabin.
