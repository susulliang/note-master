# GOAT / GOAT O SERIES / ZH - 我可以完全贴着花园或草坪边界进行遥控吗？

## Question

我可以完全贴着花园或草坪边界进行遥控吗？

## Answer

Due to a certain error in the positioning of the GOAT, we recommend that you keep a safe distance of more than 10 cm from a boundary with a drop height of more than 30 cm while remotely controlling the GOAT, to prevent the GOAT from falling or getting stuck due to a positioning error.
