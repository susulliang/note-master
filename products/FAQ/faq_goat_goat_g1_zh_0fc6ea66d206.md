# The work log shows that the total mowing area is inconsistent with the sum of the single mowing areas？

## Question

The work log shows that the total mowing area is inconsistent with the sum of the single mowing areas？

## Answer

The calculation rule for the mowing area is to round down and omit the numbers after the decimal point. Therefore, the sum of the single mowing area and the total mowing area will be slightly different, about 1~2㎡.
