# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - GOAT 未割草完毕就停在原地？

## Question

GOAT 未割草完毕就停在原地？

## Answer

(1) When it is raining heavily or it is dark, GOAT will not be able to return to the charging location automatically once it detects a weak positioning signal. It will wait in place.
(2) Check the error message on the App. The GOAT may have encountered some errors and require assistance.
