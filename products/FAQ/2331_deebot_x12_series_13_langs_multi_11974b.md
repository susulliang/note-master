# 29

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

29

## 答案 / Answer

If the robot frequently returns to the base station, it is recommended to first turn off the robot and then try the following steps:
1.Slightly lift the side where the roller mop is installed, release the blue buckle on the water tank, and remove the water tray.
2.Remove the robot's top cover, slide the power switch to "O" to turn it off, and flip the robot over so its bottom is facing up.
3.Press the drain port on the bottom of the robot to check if it is stuck or blocked by debris. If there is any blockage, please clean it. Under normal conditions, water should come out when pressed.
4.Press and hold the charging contacts on both sides of the base station for 1-2 seconds, then release and observe the ejector pins on the base station. Under normal conditions, the pins should move up and down smoothly.
5.Reinstall the water tray onto the bottom of the robot. You should hear a "click" sound, which means it's securely in place.
