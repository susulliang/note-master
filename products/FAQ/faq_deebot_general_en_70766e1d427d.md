# Alarm: Abnormal dust bag installation

## Question

Alarm: Abnormal dust bag installation

## Answer

1. Disconnect the power supply and remove the dust collection cabin cover;
2. Remove the dust bag
3. Reinstall the dust bag and close the dust collection cabin cover.
4. Turn on the power, open APP,  click the auto-empty in the station function for testing.
