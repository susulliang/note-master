# Why does the location of GOAT on the map not match the actual location?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Why does the location of GOAT on the map not match the actual location?

## 答案 / Answer

G00118

Intelligence & Privacy

(1) Please confirm whether GOAT has been moved. If the task is not started after the move, GOAT will not be relocated, and the position of GOAT before it was moved will still be displayed on the APP map
(2) Please confirm whether the GOAT is dragged during mowing. Dragging the GOAT will not trigger relocation, and the location before being dragged will still be displayed on the APP map
(3) Please confirm whether GOAT is outside the charging station when the task is triggered. GOAT needs a certain amount of time to locate, during which time the GOAT location may not be displayed on the APP
If the above situation has been eliminated, please check
(1) Whether the camera is dirty
(2) Whether the navigation beacons are in normal condition.
(3) Whether the current weather is fine.
The above problems may cause anomalies in the location of GOAT. If GOAT consistently appears incorrectly on the map, you can delete the map and rebuild it.
