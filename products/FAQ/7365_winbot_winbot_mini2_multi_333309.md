# Does the wiping pad have a front and back side? Are both sides usable?

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Does the wiping pad have a front and back side? Are both sides usable?

## 答案 / Answer

Both sides feature microfiber and can be used for cleaning. Simply align the pad with the blue markers on the WINBOT. There is no need to distinguish between the two sides.
