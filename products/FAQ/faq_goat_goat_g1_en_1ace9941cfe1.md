# Can the GOAT G1 detect possible intruders while in the middle of mowing?

## Question

Can the GOAT G1 detect possible intruders while in the middle of mowing?

## Answer

The GOAT G1 is currently unable to perform long-distance human recognition for security purposes while in motion.
