# GOAT出现割草时割草路线乱的情况？ / Why is the GOAT moving in a chaotic route when mowing?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

GOAT出现割草时割草路线乱的情况？ / Why is the GOAT moving in a chaotic route when mowing?

## 答案 / Answer

Mowing

（1）花园中若障碍物较多，避障可能会让路线看起来较乱
（2）高于27°的斜坡区域以及狭窄通道区域无法完全进行弓字型切割

(1) If there are too many obstacles in the garden, the robot will avoid these obstacles, resulting in a chaotic route. 
(2) On slopes with an inclination of greater than 27° or in narrow areas, the robot is not able to mow in a zigzag pattern
