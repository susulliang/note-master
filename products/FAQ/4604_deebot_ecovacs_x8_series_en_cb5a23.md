# Warm Reminder：
During use, If you encounter situations where the robot is spinning in circles or has abnormal walking trajectory, etc. You can follow the steps to try:
1.Please check the laser module(in the center of the bumper)for dust, If yes, please gently wipe it with a dry tissue;
2.Check if there are many obstacles in the environment. It is recommended to tidy up a bit;
3.Check if the drive wheels are dirty, if so, please wipe them with a dry cloth.
