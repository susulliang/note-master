# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X1 OMNI / EN - After starting, DEEBOT reports that the anti-drop sensor is covered with dust and is unable to work.

## Question

After starting, DEEBOT reports that the anti-drop sensor is covered with dust and is unable to work.

## Answer

Move DEEBOT out of the docking station. Tap the recall button to recall it to the docking station for a charge, then start cleaning again.
