# What should I do if the robot automatically starts cleaning but scheduled cleaning has not be set?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

What should I do if the robot automatically starts cleaning but scheduled cleaning has not be set?

## 答案 / Answer

D20397

Intelligence

1. The robot starts cleaning at a fixed time but scheduled cleaning has not been set. What should I do? 
Open the ECOVACS Home App and tap My Scenarios (the small house icon) in the upper-right corner to check whether there is a scheduled cleaning, and if so, please delete it. 

2. The robot starts cleaning at random times but scheduled cleaning has not been set. What should I do? 
Check whether the continuous cleaning function is turned on. If the function is enabled, the robot will adjust the charging time according to the remaining tasks and continue any unfinished tasks.
