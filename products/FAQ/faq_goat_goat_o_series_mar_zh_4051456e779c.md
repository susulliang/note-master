# GOAT / GOAT O Series Mar / ZH - 通道可以横跨一个已经建好地图的草坪区域吗？

## Question

通道可以横跨一个已经建好地图的草坪区域吗？

## Answer

No, if the draw-path is created pass across the lawn on the map, the path creation will be automatically terminated.
