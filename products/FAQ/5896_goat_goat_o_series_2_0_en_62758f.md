# 下雨天，机器不回基站，雨淋传感器不起作用 / Why doesn‘t the GOAT return to the base station in rainy days?  Is the rain sensor broken?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

下雨天，机器不回基站，雨淋传感器不起作用 / Why doesn‘t the GOAT return to the base station in rainy days?  Is the rain sensor broken?

## 答案 / Answer

5- 智能&安全

（1）请先在手机APP中确认是否关闭了雨淋功能
（2）割草机使用了一段时间后，可能会有尘土粘附在雨淋传感器上，请清洁传感器表面并可以定期用布对雨淋传感器进行清洁
若以上可能原因均已被排除，但问题仍未解决，请联系科沃斯售后

(1) Please check whether the rain function is turned off in the mobile APP first
(2) After the GOAT is used for a period of time, dust may adhere to the rain sensor. Please clean the surface of the sensor and clean it regularly. 

If the possible reasons above are ruled out and the problem still persists, please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
