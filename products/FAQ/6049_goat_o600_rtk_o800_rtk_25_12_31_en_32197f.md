# 我的花园狭窄通道能否安装RTK基站？

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我的花园狭窄通道能否安装RTK基站？

## 答案 / Answer

10- RTK基站

不建议将RTK基站安装在狭窄通道。
RTK基站若受建筑物遮挡，可能无法正常接收卫星信号。建议根据以下原则并结合实际情况判断安装位置是否合适：
（1）RTK杆应远离墙体、大型树木等，建议距离＞2m。
（2）基站上方需无遮挡物，以保证卫星信号接收。
（3）信号头需竖直向上，不可倾斜；建议避免放置于松软地面，以防角度偏移。
（4）安装位置需确保基站信号良好，通电1分钟后，若基站蓝色指示灯常亮即可。

Installing the RTK station in a narrow path is not recommended. If obstructed by buildings, the RTK station may fail to receive satellite signals properly. Determine suitability based on the following principles and actual conditions:
 (1) The RTK pole should be positioned at least 2 meters away from walls, large trees, etc.
 (2) Ensure no obstructions above the station to guarantee satellite signal reception.
 (3) The signal head must point vertically upward without tilt; avoid placement on soft ground to prevent angle deviation.
 (4) The installation location must ensure good station signal. After powering on for 1 minute, if the station's blue Indicator is constantly lit, it means the station is working properly.
