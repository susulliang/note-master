# WINBOT / WINBOT W3 OMNI / EN - Alert: Let's install the clean water tank.

## Question

Alert: Let's install the clean water tank.

## Answer

1.Remove the Clean Water Tank and check whether the magnet at the bottom is in its correct position.
2.If the magnet is properly positioned, wipe its surface clean with a cloth to remove any debris, then test its magnetism with a coin. If the magnetism is normal, reinstall the Clean Water Tank and observe its operation.
3.If the magnet is missing or shows no magnetism, please contact after‑sales support for assistance.
