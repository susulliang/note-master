# 报警：接水槽未安装

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

报警：接水槽未安装

## 答案 / Answer

1.取下主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“O”关机，
 2.请将安装滚筒拖布的一侧稍微抬起。
 3.拨开接水槽蓝色卡扣，取下接水槽，检查接水槽磁铁是否丢失
 4.若未丢失，请将接水槽安装回机器底部。听到“咔哒”声音表示安装到位，重新开机使用观察
 5..若丢失，请联系售后处理

1. Remove the cover of the ROBOT and slide the Power Switch to "O" to turn it off.
 2. Lift the side with the roller mop slightly.
 3. Release the blue clip on the Water Tray, remove the Water Tray, and check if the magnet is missing.
 4. If the magnet is present, reinstall the Water Tray onto the machine's bottom. A "click" sound indicates proper installation. Power on and observe.
 5. If the magnet is missing, contact after-sales service for assistance.
