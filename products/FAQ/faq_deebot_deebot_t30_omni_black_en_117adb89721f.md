# What should I do if the robot does not perform continuous cleaning?

## Question

What should I do if the robot does not perform continuous cleaning?

## Answer

1. Confirm whether the continuous cleaning function is turned on;
2. Continuous cleaning will not be triggered during the Do Not Disturb mode;
3. Is there a fault alarm after the machine automatically returns to charge? If so, continuous cleaning cannot be triggered;
4. After triggering continuous cleaning, the machine cannot be restarted or restored to factory settings.
Note: The continuous cleaning function is only effective in automatic working mode.
