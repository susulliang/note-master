# 为什么我的GOAT出了外轮廓或者穿越了禁区？ / Why does my GOAT leave the map boundaries or enter the No-Entry zones?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

为什么我的GOAT出了外轮廓或者穿越了禁区？ / Why does my GOAT leave the map boundaries or enter the No-Entry zones?

## 答案 / Answer

4- 割草

（1）如果工作时有小雨或者光线暗可能导致定位偏差，机器出界。建议在天气晴好时启动工作。
（2）基站周围2米内为回充默认通行区域，可能出现出界和穿禁区情况
（3）草地里如果存在鹅卵石路面可能造成GOAT打滑出界

(1) If there is light rain or low light conditions while working, it may cause localization errors. It is recommended to start the work in clear weather.
(2) By default, the area within 2 m around the docking station is a free area, so that the GOAT can return and recharge. This may cause the robot to leave the boundaries or enter No-Entry zones.
(3)The presence of cobblestone in the lawn could cause the GOAT to slip out of boundary.
