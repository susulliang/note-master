# Which SIM card do I need to buy?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Which SIM card do I need to buy?

## 答案 / Answer

G00157

Additional Connection Module

The Cellullar Module is compatible with regular Nano cards (compatible with 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G cards), and you are free to choose the SIM card you need.  However, at the time of purchase, please confirm with the carrier whether the purchased SIM card requires APN and whether it has a PIN code.  Activate the SIM card and disable the PIN of the SIM card before inserting the SIM card into the module. Periodically check the data usage after inserting the SIM card
