# DEEBOT / T50 降配版 / EN - How to install and remove the station base?

## Question

How to install and remove the station base?

## Answer

1.Install:Please place the base at the desired installation location, and then press both sides of the slope base evenly with both hands until  you hear a "click" sound, it means it's installed in place.
2.Remove:Please lift the entire station slightly and lift the base up gently to take it off.
