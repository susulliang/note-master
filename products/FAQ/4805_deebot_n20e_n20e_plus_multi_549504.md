## 答案 / Answer

Why doesn't the robot continue the unfinished task after returning to charge when it resumes scanning at the breakpoint?

Please ensure that the host is not reset during the process.​
Please make sure to enable the intelligent continuous cleaning function on the APP.​
Please ensure that the robot is charging normally and will continue to perform unfinished tasks after reaching the required battery level.
