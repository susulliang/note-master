# Upgrade the firmware of the WINBOT W2

> **产品线**：WINBOT　**语言**：multi
>

## 问题 / Question

Upgrade the firmware of the WINBOT W2

## 答案 / Answer

W00169

Preparation for the firmware upgrade:
1. Please connect the WINBOT W2 to the power source and power it on, and the WINBOT W2 is in the non-absorption state.
2. The distance between the WINBOT W2 and your smart phone should not exceed 5 meters.

How to upgrade:
Please open the ECOVACS HOME App and go to Smart Cleaning ＞ Settings ＞ Firmware Upgrade. Follow the App instructions to complete the upgrade.

Note: Upon successful upgrade, the WINBOT W2 fan will automatically start and disconnect from the App. After the fan finishes running, it will automatically reconnect with the App and can be used again.
