# 如何添加清洁消毒液/重污清洁液？

## Question

如何添加清洁消毒液/重污清洁液？

## Answer

1.Take off the cleaning solution tank's cover, pull out the two cleaning solution boxes, and place them on a level, hard surface.
2. ①The right, bigger box is for disinfectant cleaning solution. Open the lid, add disinfectant cleaning solution, close the lid, and place it back into the station.
 ②The left one is for heavy-duty cleaning solution. Open the lid, add heavy-duty cleaning solution, close the lid, and reinstall it.
 3.Return the cleaning solution tank's cover and press the cover firmly to secure it in position. 4.Open App>Enter>Settings>Station Advanced Settings>enable Auto Disinfectant/ Heavy-duty cleaning solution dispense.                                                                                                                                           Note: This model features automatic detergent dispensing. You do not need to manually add cleaning solution to the water tank.
