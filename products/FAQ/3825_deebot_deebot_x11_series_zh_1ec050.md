# 机器人如何创建地图？ / Mapping Guide

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：zh
>

## 问题 / Question

机器人如何创建地图？ / Mapping Guide

## 答案 / Answer

一：创建地图前准备
1. 请打开房门并移除地面杂物并保证光线充足，以便地图能够覆盖整个家居环境；
2. 掀开主机正面上盖，拨动电源开关至“I”开机，放置在基站正前方;
3. 长按主机启动按键，让机器自主返回充电，对接成功后开始充电。
二：快速建图
提示：确认您的机器人已连接网络，并处于在线状态。
1. 使用APP
    打开ECOVACS HOME＞进入设备＞快速建图。
2. 建图完成后机器人会有语音播报“建图已完成，请在app中查看”此时地图已创建成功。
注：
①您也可以点击机器人“启动按键”启动，机器会边工作边建图，完成后会有语音提示“清洁完成，返回充电”此时地图也可以创建成功。
②若编辑地图后需手动进行备份地图。                                                                                           温馨提醒：
1.建图过程中请勿跟随或搬动地宝
2.建图后基站不要移动位置

I. Preparation Before Creating a Map
 1.Open all doors, remove obstacles from the floor, and ensure sufficient lighting to allow the map to cover the entire home environment.
 2.Lift the upper cover of the robot, toggle the power switch to "I" , and place it directly in front of the charging dock.
 Press and hold the robot's "Auto" button to initiate automatic return to charging. Once docked successfully, charging will begin.
 II. Quick Mapping
 Note: Ensure your robot is connected to the internet and online.
 1.Using the App
 Open the ECOVACS HOME app ＞ Enter＞ Mapping.
 After mapping is complete, the robot will announce, "Mapping completed. Please check the map in the app." This indicates the map has been created successfully.
 Additional Notes:
 1.You can also press the robot's AUTO button to begin cleaning. The robot will map while working. Upon completion, it will say, "Cleaning finished. Returning to charge," and the map will also be created.
 2.After editing the map, manually back up the map to avoid data loss.                                                     Friendly Reminder:
1.Do not follow or move the robot during mapping to avoid inaccuracies.
2.Do not relocate the charging dock after mapping to prevent positioning errors or failed auto-return.
