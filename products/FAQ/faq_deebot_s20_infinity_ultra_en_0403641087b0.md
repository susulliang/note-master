# DEEBOT / S20 Infinity Ultra / EN - How to Use the Corner Guards (Optional)？

## Question

How to Use the Corner Guards (Optional)？

## Answer

1.Take out a corner guard and remove the protective film from the double-sided tape.
2.Align the corner guard with one front outer corner of the station and press firmly to secure it.
3.Install the other corner guard on the opposite side in the same way.
