# Why is GOAT shut down automatically when in the station?

## Question

Why is GOAT shut down automatically when in the station?

## Answer

(1) The ECO mode may be triggered. Click the power button on the GOAT 5 times, and GOAT will enter silent mode and automatically shut down within 10 seconds. It will be automatically released after restarting. If the above situation does not apply,please contact ECOVACS Customer Care.
