# 我觉得APP上显示的轮廓位置与实际的不一致？ / Why is the outline position displayed on the App inconsistent with the actual one?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

我觉得APP上显示的轮廓位置与实际的不一致？ / Why is the outline position displayed on the App inconsistent with the actual one?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

APP上显示的轮廓线位置为遥控时GOAT两轮中心经过的位置，且定位存在一定的误差。如果APP上显示的位置与实际遥控的位置相差过大，建议检查RTK的状态，不要将RTK信号基站或充电基站放在有阴影遮挡的位置.
在天气好的时候删图重建。

The position of the outline displayed on the App is the position where the centers of the two wheels of the GOAT pass by when remote controlled. There has been a certain error in positioning. 
If the position displayed on the App is significantly different from the actual position being remotely controlled, please confirm that the RTK reference station or charging station in a location with shadow obstruction.
Proceed to remap it under good weather conditions.
