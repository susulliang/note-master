# Can DEEBOT get over the door sills in my home?

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

Can DEEBOT get over the door sills in my home?

## 答案 / Answer

D20013

Enable

Usage advice

DEEBOT can go over door sills up to 0.78 inch high, and should be able to cross most household door sills. DEEBOT has more difficulty getting over obstacles while mopping.
