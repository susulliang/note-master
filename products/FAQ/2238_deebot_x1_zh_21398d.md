## 答案 / Answer

Missing to clean the same area.

B. Different regions:
①Do not follow the robot when it is working to prevent it from missing cleaning due to obstacle avoidance.
②If there are many items in the environment, in order to avoid missing cleaning, it is recommended that you put things in order, or you can also turn off AIVI™3D in APP-settings-smart cleaning settings.
