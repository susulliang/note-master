# GOAT / GOAT G1 / ZH - Will the blades of the GOAT continue turning when passing through non-grass areas?

## Question

Will the blades of the GOAT continue turning when passing through non-grass areas?

## Answer

The GOAT is currently unable to accurately distinguish between grass and non-grass areas when mowing. The blades of the GOAT will continue to keep rotating when working in areas within the map.
