# 拆卸&安装滚筒拖布指导

> **产品线**：DEEBOT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

拆卸&安装滚筒拖布指导

## 答案 / Answer

温馨提示：
 当您在使用过程中若您有遇到机器人滚筒拖布未安装/滚筒拖布被缠绕等情况，您可以按如下步骤进行相关操作尝试：
 一：滚筒拖布的拆卸
 1. 双击主机AUTO按键，滚筒固定座会自动向外扩出。
 2. 手指按压滚筒固定座内侧的卡扣释放键，向外轻拉滚筒端盖；取出滚筒拖布。
 3. 稍微用力拔出滚筒端盖；可将滚筒端盖与滚筒拖布分离。
  注：滚筒上的拖布与滚筒为一个整体，拖布无法单独拆卸更换
 二：滚筒拖布安装
 1. 请将滚筒端盖重新安装到滚筒拖布上；
 2. 请将滚筒拖布对准滚筒电机往里轻推，听到“嗒”一声表示安装到位。滚筒端盖与滚筒固定座顶端保持在一个平面上为安装到位。
 3.双击主机AUTO按键，滚筒固定座会自动内收。
 注：安装与拆卸都需要外扩状态下操作，请勿在基站内双击AUTO按键。

Friendly reminder:
If you encounter situations such as the robot's roller mop not being installed or the roller mop becoming tangled during use, you can attempt the following steps:
I. Removing the roller mop
Double-click the AUTO button on the ROBOT. The roller holder will automatically extend outward.
2. Press the release button on the inner side of the roller holder with your finger, then gently pull the Roller End Cover outward; remove the roller mop.
3. Apply slight force to pull out the Roller End Cover; this separates the Roller End Cover from the roller mop.
 Note: The roller and mop are integrated as a single unit; the mop cannot be replaced separately.
II: Installing the Roller Mop
Reattach the Roller End Cover to the roller mop.
2. Align the roller mop with the roller motor and gently push inward until you hear a "click" indicating proper installation. The Roller End Cover should be flush with the top of the roller mounting bracket.
3. Double-click the AUTO button on the ROBOT to automatically retract the roller mop.
Note: Both installation and removal must be performed in the extended position. Do not double-click the AUTO button while inside the station.
