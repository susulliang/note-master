# GOAT / GX / ZH - The live video footage is not clear enough.

## Question

The live video footage is not clear enough.

## Answer

The current App for the GOAT only supports the display of 480p images, to ensure the smooth delivery of video playback. A definition switching function will be introduced in the future. Please check the App regularly for information on the GOAT's software updates.
