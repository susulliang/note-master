# DEEBOT / DEEBOT OZMO T8 AIVI / EN - How do I disable the AIVI function?

## Question

How do I disable the AIVI function?

## Answer

a) Once the network setup for your robot is completed, open the App and go to "More" - "AIVI Smart Detection" to disable the AIVI function.
b) Disabling the AIVI function does not affect any of the robot's functions other than AI.
