# 建图为何创建失败了？ / Why did the map setting fail?

> **产品线**：GOAT　**语言**：en
>

## 问题 / Question

建图为何创建失败了？ / Why did the map setting fail?

## 答案 / Answer

3- 建图

（1）确认RTK信号杆状态是否正常，充电基站和RTK信号站已正确放置了位置。
（2）确认建图过程中是否搬动或拖移了机器，搬动机器将会导致建图失败。建议您修整花园中的坑洼区域，减少遥控过程中GOAT被卡困导致的需要搬动或拖移的概率
（3）确认建图时光线充足且无雨
（4）确认已按照APP指示在遥控完成后正常闭合地图，遥控结束位置距离起点过远，会导致地图无法闭合。

(1) Make sure that the RTK signal is good without displacement during mapping. 
(2) Confirm if the GOAT has been moved or dragged during mapping, as moving the GOAT can cause mapping to fail. It is recommended that you level the garden ground to reduce the chance of moving or dragging the GOAT due to it becoming trapped when being remotely controlled 
(3) Make sure that there is sufficient light and no rain when mapping 
(4) Confirm that the GOAT is automatically connected to the docking station after the mapping is completed according to the instructions in the app. If you manually control the GOAT to the docking station, or push the GOAT to the docking station, the map cannot be closed properly.
