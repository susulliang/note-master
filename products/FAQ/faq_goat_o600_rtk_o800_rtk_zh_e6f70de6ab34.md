# GOAT / O600 RTK & O800 RTK / ZH - Goat割草时经常遇到卡困，怎么办

## Question

Goat割草时经常遇到卡困，怎么办

## Answer

1.Before mowing, trim the lawn to below 10cm using a standard mower.                                 。2. Clear debris prone to causing jams—such as large amounts of fallen leaves, stones, small toys, fruits, or strings—from the lawn beforehand.
 3. Inspect the stuck area for small pits or holes and fill them if found.
 4. Mow when the lawn is dry, as damp grass increases the risk of getting stuck.
 5. Designate frequently stuck areas as No-Entry zones. Later, use manual mode to remotely guide GOAT to trim these spots.
