## 答案 / Answer

Disassembly, clean and installation of mopping pad plate and mopping pad(high-end version)

Disassembly and clean of mopping pad plate assembly                                                                                                                                                         1、Remove OZMO Pro 2.0 Vibrating Mopping System.                                                                                                                            2、Lift the mopping pad assembly slightly upward to remove the moping pad plate, and tear off the washable mopping pad.                                                                                                                                                                                        Install the mopping pad and mopping pad plate                                                                                                                                                     1.Remove OZMO Pro 2.0 Vibrating Mopping System.                                                                                                                    2.Stick the mopping pad evenly onto the mopping pad plate (with Velcro side).                                    3.Align the mopping pad plate with the installation port.                                                              4.Install OZMO Pro 2.0 Vibrating Mopping System back to the Robot, confirm that you hear a click;
