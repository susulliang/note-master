# DEEBOT / DEEBOT X11 SERIES 中英文 / ZH - 清水箱不出清水，如何处理？

## Question

清水箱不出清水，如何处理？

## Answer

Phenomenon: No clean water coming out of the station during mopping and self-cleaning.
 1. Remove the water tank, check that there are no foreign objects between it and the Station, and make sure that the water tank is placed flat.
 2. Check that the sealing plug of the water tank on the station has been installed correctly and is tightly fitted.
 3. Open the water tank, remove the filter, disconnect the water hose and rinse the filter carefully with clean water. After rinsing, reinstall the filter, and secure the tank.
