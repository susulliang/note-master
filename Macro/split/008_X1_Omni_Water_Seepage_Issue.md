# X1 Omni: Too much water seepage/Water drops when mopping 鍑烘按杩囧

We would like to kindly inform you that the X1 Omni is suitable for most types of hard surface flooring, including wooden floors\. If you're concerned about the amount of water being dispensed, you can adjust the water level in the app settings to better accommodate your floor type\.

Please refer to the suggested settings below for optimal performance on wooden flooring:

- **Cleaning Times**: Once

- **Water Level**: Low

- **Mopping Mode**: Standard

- **Cleaning Interval**: Efficient

- **Hot Air Drying Time**: 4 hours

If you are experiencing issues with water droplets, please try the following steps:

- Ensure there is sufficient water in the clean water tank\.

- Check if the clean water tank and mopping plate are properly installed\.

- Inspect the water outlets at the bottom of the unit and clean them if necessary\.

- Make sure the magnets where the mopping plates attach are free of dust or debris \(please refer to the photo below for guidance\)\.
