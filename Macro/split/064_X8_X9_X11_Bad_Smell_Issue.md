1. Clean the Roller Mop:

- Double\-click the power button to release the roller mop component\.
*Note: Please avoid excessive force when handling the roller; it should be removed and reinstalled with care\.*



- Press the lateral unlock button to remove the roller



- Clean the roller mop and the inside of the roller\.
*Note: Do not use water to clean the main body of the unit\.*



- Once cleaned and dried, insert the roller mop into the end cover and double\-click the 鈥淵鈥?button to retract it\.

2. Clean the **Drip Tray**:

- Remove the drip tray\.
*Note: A small amount of residual water may leak out when flipping the robot if it's not in drying mode\.*



- Disassemble and clean all drip tray components\.



3. Clean the Dirty Water Box:

- Remove the dirty water box and the filter\.



- Clean the box, filter, and sewage outlet thoroughly\.



- Reinstall the filter securely and attach the dirty water box cover\. **Ensure everything is fully dry before reassembling\.**



4. Clean the Mop Washing Tray and Station Base:

- Remove and clean these components and rinse the mop washing tray accordingly with room temperature water\.



- Ensure they are completely dry before reinstalling\. 



For X11 Only: Kind reminder: Ensure the **Floating Sump** is installed properly under the sockets\.

- Use the Device Relocation function to trigger the station's self\-cleaning cycle\.



- Clean the internal pipes of the station using the Pipeline Check function\.



- Use Auxiliary Draining to transfer remaining water from the tray back to the dirty water tank\.



6. Adjust The Mop Wash Frequency and Hot Air Drying Time

- Please go to Settings \- Station Advanced Settings and adjust the Mop Auto\-Wash Frequency to 10 Mins
