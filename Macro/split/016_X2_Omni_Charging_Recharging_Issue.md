1. Improper Installation of the Detachable Water Tray: Ensure that the detachable water tray is correctly installed\. Incorrect installation can create a height difference between the charging contacts on Deebot and the charging pins on the OMNI station, leading to poor contact\. Make sure the detachable water tray is properly secured in place\.
