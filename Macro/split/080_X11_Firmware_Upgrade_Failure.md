# X11 Series: Firmware Upgrade Failure 鍥轰欢鍗囩骇澶辫触

We are sorry to hear that your X11 Series Deebot is experiencing a firmware upgrade failure\. Please be assured that we are here to guide you through the resolution process\.

## **Initial Setup Stage** \- Firmware Upgrade Failed:

1. **Ensure Stable Network \& Positioning:**

    - Ensure the robot is within close range of your Wi\-Fi router and that your home network is stable\.

    - Place the robot correctly on the charging base, ensuring the charging contacts are aligned\. The robot must be in a charging state for the update\.

2. **Confirm Charging Status or Ensure Battery is Sufficient:**

    - A slowly flashing white light on the three indicators \(the Y symbol\) confirms the robot is actively charging\. You will also hear the "Start charging" voice prompt\. \(The robot must be at the base under charging status in order to proceed with the firmware upgrade\)
    
    **Preparation:** Please ensure the battery is above **50%**\. This is crucial to ensure the firmware upgrade can proceed smoothly\. If the machine is low on battery, please charge it for at least** 2 hours**\.

3. **Mobile App Permissions:**

    - Please check your mobile phone's permission settings for the **Ecovacs Home app** and ensure all required permissions \(such as Location, Storage, and Notifications\) are granted\.

4. **Network Connection Switch:**

    - Kindly disconnect your mobile phone from your Wi\-Fi and switch to cellular data\. Then, re\-access the app and attempt the firmware update again\.

5. **Reinstall the Application:**

    - If the issue persists, please **delete the Ecovacs Home app** and perform a fresh installation from your app store\. 



**PS: **Once the firmware upgrade starts, allow the process to complete without interruption\. A sudden disconnection or turning off the DEEBOT can cause issues with the upgrade\.

2. **Connect Using a Mobile Hotspot \(Network Isolation Test\):**

    **A\. Using a Mobile Hotspot for Connection\.**

    You can connect the robot to a mobile phone hotspot\. Once connected successfully, you can set functions in the APP, such as scheduled cleanings\. Turning off the hotspot will only affect **remote control**, but the functions that have already been set will still work\. If you need to modify functions in the APP later, you will need to reconnect to the hotspot again\.

    **B\. How to set up the hotspot:**

    - Please try enabling a **2\.4GHz mobile hotspot** from another family member鈥檚 phone \(you can turn this on in your phone settings\)\.

    - Use your phone to connect to the Wi\-Fi signal shared by that hotspot\.

    - Then, use the same phone to connect the robot and check if the connection is successful\.

    - To make it easier, we recommend setting the hotspot name and password as a simple combination of numbers\.

    - After successfully connecting to the hotspot, you may also try changing your home Wi\-Fi name and password to match the hotspot, and then test the connection again\.

    **Reason for testing with a hotspot:**

    - This helps determine whether the issue is with the **robot** or with the **home network**\.

    - If the robot connects successfully to the hotspot, it means the robot itself is working fine, and the connection failure is most likely related to the home Wi\-Fi network\.

3. **Factory reset and retry:**

    **Gentle Reminder****:** Kindly be informed that performing a reset on Deebot will result in the deletion of all data, including the saved maps\. Following the reset, Deebot will go offline and will require the network setup process to be redone in order to restore its online functionality\.

    

    **How to reset your Deebot:**

    To initiate a hard reset of the Deebot, press the Reset Button \(a tiny wifi button near the power switch\) for approximately 5 seconds\. You will hear a voice prompt stating "Reset to default settings," followed by the commencement of the boot music\. This indicates that the default settings have been successfully restored\.
