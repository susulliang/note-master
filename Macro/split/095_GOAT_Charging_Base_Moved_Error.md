# GOAT: Charging base has been moved error 鍩虹珯琚Щ鍔ㄩ敊璇?
1. Has the GOAT been moved or dragged manually? If so, it may not be able to resume tasks properly until it is relocated\.

2. Is the GOAT outside of the charging station when the task is triggered? Please note that it may take some time for the GOAT to locate itself, during which its position may not appear on the app\.

3. If the GOAT is operating in an area with weak or no RTK signal \(e\.g\., shaded areas\), it will rely on the visual sensor for positioning\. This may result in slight positional deviations\.



If the above scenarios do not apply, please check the following:

- Ensure the camera on the GOAT is clean and unobstructed\.

- Verify that the RTK signal is stable and functioning properly\.

- Confirm that the weather conditions are clear, as poor weather may affect positioning\.


If the GOAT continues to trigger alarms, you may try deleting and rebuilding the map to recalibrate its location

If the problem persists, please try resetting the machine\. Please note that resetting will delete all data, including saved maps\. After the reset, the GOAT will go offline and will require network setup to restore online functionality\.



How to reset your GOAT:

1. Simultaneously press and hold the START and CHARGE buttons on the GOAT for 5 seconds until the display shows the PIN code input interface\.

2. When the countdown ends, enter the PIN code and click OK to confirm\. Please wait while the machine clears its memory\. The GOAT will automatically restart and display the PIN reset interface, indicating the reset was successful\.
