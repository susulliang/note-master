5. **Check the cleaning sink for water:**

- **If there is no water:**

- **If there is water residue in the cleaning sink, follow these steps:**

    - Open the **dirty water tank** and inspect the **sealing ring** for any visible foreign objects\. If there are any, clean and wipe the ring\. If the sealing ring is clear, ensure the cover is securely closed\.
    
    Please also consider uninstalling the sealing ring and reinstalling it to ensure it is attached properly\.
