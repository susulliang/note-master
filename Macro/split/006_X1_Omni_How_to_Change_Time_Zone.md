# X1 Omni: How to change the time zone 鏀规椂鍖?
After confirming in the app, please try to synchronize the time zone with your mobile device\. The app is designed to automatically sync with your phone's time zone\. You may follow the steps below for guidance:

1. Open the ECOVACS app\.

2. Swipe up to access more options on the Deebot control page\.

3. Tap on *Settings* and navigate to the *About Deebot* section\.

4. Select *Time Zone* for more details\.

5. You may either press the blue button at the top of the page to sync with your phone's time zone or manually select your preferred time zone using the "Use" button\.
