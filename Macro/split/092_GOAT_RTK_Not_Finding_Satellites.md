Thank you for contacting Ecovacs Robotics\.



If you're experiencing issues with satellite connectivity on your **GOAT O1000RTK / GOAT A2500RTK**, please follow the steps below to resolve the issue:
 

**Step 1: Ensure Latest Firmware is Installed**
To begin troubleshooting, make sure both your GOAT mower and RTK base station have the latest firmware\. Firmware updates can often improve signal reception and RTK performance\.
 

**How to Check:**

- Navigate to **Settings \> Robot/RTK \> Upgrade **to check if a firmware update is available\.

- Follow the prompts to install any updates\.



**Step 2: Power Cycle the RTK Base Station**

- Turn off the RTK base station for a few minutes, then power it back on\.

- This simple reset can help resolve temporary satellite connectivity issues\.
 

**Step 3: Ensure Proper Placement of RTK Base Station and Charging Station**

- **Open Area:** Ensure both the RTK base station and the GOAT's charging station are placed in a clear, open area, free from obstructions such as buildings, trees, or overhead structures that could block satellite signals\.

- **Minimum Distance: **The RTK base station should be placed at least 2 meters away from walls, large trees, or any other structures that may interfere with the signal\.

- **Clear Line of Sight: **The RTK base station requires an unobstructed line of sight to the sky to receive satellite signals effectively\. If there are nearby obstructions \(e\.g\., buildings or trees\), these can block the signal\. Relocate the RTK base station to a more open area for optimal signal reception\.
 

**If the Issue Persists:**
Please provide the following details to help us further assist you:

1. GOAT's current **firmware **version \(found in Settings \> Robot \> Upgrade\)

2. **GOAT's serial number **\(located at the rear of the GOAT next to the QR code or at the bottom of the mower\)

3. A **screenshot **of any **problem **or **alarm **notification sent by the GOAT through the Ecovacs Home App

4. A digital copy or screenshot of your **proof of purchase**, including order number, purchase date, purchase channel, and model details
 

Should you require any further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us\.
