**Automatic Use of Heavy\-Duty Cleaning Solution**

If **no "Heavily Soiled Areas"** have been set on the map, the Deebot will automatically recognize areas like the kitchen or any user\-defined "Kitchen" zone\. In these areas, the Heavy\-duty Cleaning Solution will be used during the mop washing phase鈥攊deal for tackling grease commonly found in kitchens\.



**Setting Heavily Soiled Areas on the Map**:

To customize these areas, follow these steps:

Go to **Settings** \> **Station Advanced Settings** \> **Auto Heavy\-duty Cleaning Solution Dispense**\.

Tap **Settings** under **Heavily Soiled Area Settings**, then tap on the map to mark the necessary zones\.

Once set, the Deebot will only use the Heavy\-duty Cleaning Solution in these specified areas during the mop washing process\.
