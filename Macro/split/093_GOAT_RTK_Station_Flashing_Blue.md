Why is the RTK Reference Station light flashing?

The flashing indicator on your RTK Reference Station typically indicates that the GPS signal is weak or unavailable\.

1. Check for Obstructions

    - The RTK Reference Station relies on GNSS/GPS signals to transmit to the host for positioning\.

    - The flashing light usually indicates poor GPS signal reception\. Please check if the RTK Reference Station is blocked by obstacles like houses, trees, or other objects that could create signal interference\.

2. Power Cycle the RTK Reference Station

    - If there are no apparent obstructions and the flashing persists, we recommend powering off the RTK Reference Station, waiting for a moment, and then turning it back on to reset the system\.

3. Optimal Installation Location

    - Open and Unobstructed Area: The RTK Reference Station should be placed in an open space with as few obstructions as possible\.

    - Install on High Ground: Consider installing the RTK Reference Station on a roof or other elevated location to improve signal reception\. Ensure that there are no obstructions around the station\.

    - You can adjust the location of the RTK Reference Station before you create the map\.

3. Installation Guidelines

    - Avoid Walls and Large Trees: Place the RTK Reference Station at least 2 meters away from walls, large trees, and other objects that may block the signal\.

    - No Overhead Obstructions: Ensure there are no structures or shadows above the RTK base station\.

    - Good Signal Indicator: The RTK Reference Station should show a steady light after 1 minute of powering on, indicating a stable signal\.

4. Installing in Plants

    - If you choose to place the RTK Reference Station near plants, ensure the grass or plants are no taller than 1\.2 meters\. Regularly trim any tall or winding plants that could interfere with the signal\.

    - Confirm the signal by checking if the indicator light stays steady after 1 minute\.

5. Avoid Invisible or Hidden Locations

    - The RTK Reference Station should not be installed in hidden or enclosed spaces\. Avoid installing it in locations where buildings or other obstructions will block the signal\.
