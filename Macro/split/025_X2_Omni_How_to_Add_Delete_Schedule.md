3. Select "Schedule" from the settings menu\.

1. To delete a cleaning schedule, swipe left on the schedule you wish to remove\. This action will reveal the deletion option\.

![Image](https://internal-api-drive-stream.feishu.cn/space/api/box/stream/download/authcode/?code=ZDU3Y2VlMzEwMDI3MTg0MGExNTdjNzE2ZWE0ZDVjNWFfMTdjZjBlZjM1MmI1YmQxMjA1YjUwNDI3ZjExOWEwNzRfSUQ6NzUwNzM2Mzk4MjI1Mzg4MzM5NV8xNzg3Nzg2NjY2OjE3ODc4NzMwNjZfVjM)

2. Click on "Delete", and the selected schedule will be permanently removed\.
