## Low suction power issue: 鍚稿姏灏?涓嶈冻

Please kindly note that the robot will use **very low suction power** in certain situations:

- During the **first cleaning** or when creating a **new map**

- When **relocating / repositioning / recharging**

This is normal behavior and not a malfunction\.

However, if you find the suction seems weak at other times, the intake may be blocked or the Main Brush / Side Brushes may be tangled with dirt and debris\. For best performance, please clean the dust bin, sensors, and filter regularly\.

**Cleaning Steps:**

1. **Dust Bin**

    - Power **OFF** the robot\.

    - Remove the dust bin, empty it, and reinstall it securely\.

2. **Main Brush**

    - Turn the DEEBOT upside down\.

    - Remove the Main Brush Cover, then take out the Main Brush\.

    - Remove any tangled hair or debris, and reinstall the brush\.

3. **Side Brushes**

    - Remove the Side Brushes\.

    - Wipe both the brushes and their slots with a clean, dry cloth\.

    - Reinstall them properly\.

4. **Charging**

    - Ensure that DEEBOT is fully charged before starting a cleaning cycle\.

    - You may check the battery status in the APP\.

    - If the robot is not fully charged, please allow it to charge completely before use\.

5. **Dust Bin \& Filters**

    - Confirm whether the dust bin or filters have been washed\.

    - Please make sure they are **completely dry** before the next use\.

6. **Auto Resume \(for models with advanced mapping\)**

    - It is recommended to turn **Auto Resume** ON\.

    - With Auto Resume enabled, the robot will:

        - Resume cleaning once the battery reaches about **50%**\.

        - Return automatically to the last unfinished area and continue cleaning until the next low\-battery cycle\.

    - 鈿狅笍 Please note: Auto Resume will not activate if the cleaning task is **manually interrupted**\.

**Additional Note:**
 Work time may decrease if the vacuum power is set to **Max** or if the robot is used in **Mopping Mode**\.
