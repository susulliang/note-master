**Possible reasons the DEEBOT may appear offline:**

1. If the DEEBOT is not operated or placed back onto the Charging Dock for a long time after being powered on or off, it may go offline\.

2. If the DEEBOT shows offline while on the Charging Dock, gently pull it back and forth to see if you hear the startup tone\. If you do, the DEEBOT may have powered off automatically\. In this case, please check whether the Charging Dock/Auto\-Empty Station is properly connected to power and that the socket connection is secure\.

**Check the Wi\-Fi indicator light:**

- **Wi\-Fi light ON:** Exit the APP, log in again, and refresh\.

- **Wi\-Fi light flashing:**

    1. Open the top cover, switch OFF the DEEBOT, then turn it back ON \(make sure the network coverage is good\)\. Refresh the APP to check if it comes online\.

    2. Restart your router, then refresh the APP to see if the DEEBOT reconnects\.

*If the issue persists, please reconnect the DEEBOT to Wi\-Fi by following the network setup steps in the APP\.*

**In a multi\-router network environment:**

- If your home has multiple routers \(up to 10 are supported\), the DEEBOT can automatically switch between them depending on signal quality\.

- If you are using a mesh or extended router system, please first connect the DEEBOT to the main router\. Once successful, turn on the extension routers one by one\.

**After changing Wi\-Fi/router:**

- Please reconnect your DEEBOT to the network using the setup steps in the APP\.
