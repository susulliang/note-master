If you're experiencing issues with the firmware upgrade on your robot, this general troubleshooting guide will help you resolve the problem and ensure a smooth upgrade process\.



**Steps for a Successful Firmware Upgrade**

1. **Ensure Your App is Up\-to\-Date**:

    Make sure the **Ecovacs Home App** is up to date\. An outdated app can sometimes interfere with the upgrade process\. Check for any available updates in your app store\. 

2. **Ensure the DEEBOT/GOAT is On and On the Charging Dock**:

    Make sure your DEEBOT/GOAT is turned on and properly placed on the charging dock\. The app will display a **green lightning icon** next to the power display when everything is set up correctly\.

3. **Charge the DEEBOT/GOAT or Ensure Battery is Sufficient**:

    Charge the DEEBOT/GOAT for at least **2 hours** or ensure the battery is above **50%**\. This is crucial for the firmware upgrade to proceed smoothly\.

4. **Check Internet Connectivity**:

    Ensure both your **mobile device and DEEBOT/GOAT** are connected to the internet\. A stable Wi\-Fi connection is necessary for the firmware to download and install properly\.

5. **Proceed to Click the Firmware Upgrade Option in the App**:

    After ensuring the above conditions are met, go to the app and select the **Firmware Upgrade** option to start the process\.

6. **Wait for the Upgrade to Complete**:

    Once the firmware upgrade begins, allow the process to finish without interruption\. Turning off or disconnecting the DEEBOT/GOAT during the upgrade could cause issues\.



***If the Update Fails:***

If the firmware upgrade fails, **do not attempt to update immediately**\. Instead, try the following alternative resolutions:

- **Re\-add the DEEBOT/GOAT to the App**:

    - **Delete the robot** from the app and re\-add it\. This helps reset the connection and can resolve issues preventing the upgrade from completing\.

- **Use Mobile Hotspot**:

    - Try connecting the DEEBOT/GOAT to your phone鈥檚 **mobile hotspot**\. This can help bypass potential Wi\-Fi network issues, allowing the upgrade to complete successfully\.



***If the Issue Persists:***

**Check the DEEBOT鈥檚 Firmware Version**:

Verify the firmware version in the app\. Take a screenshot of this screen for reference\.

**Contact Support**: 
If you're still having issues, please contact customer support\. Whien reaching out, provide us with the following details:

- **Ecovacs Home App version** 
Find this after you open the App, hit **Mine \> About \> Version**

- **DEEBOT/GOAT Serial Number** 
For **DEEBOT**, find it in **Settings \> Robot Advanced Settings \> About Your Robot \> Serial Number**, or underneath the DEEBOT's dustbin; 
For **GOAT**, the serial number is at the rear end of the GOAT next to the QR Code, or at the bottom of the GOAT

- **Screenshot of the robot's firmware version** 
For DEEBOT, go to **Settings \> Robot Advanced Settings \> About Your Robot \> Update**
For **GOAT**, go to** Settings \> Robot \> Upgrade**



By following this troubleshooting guide, most firmware upgrade issues should be resolved\. If you need additional assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to us\.
