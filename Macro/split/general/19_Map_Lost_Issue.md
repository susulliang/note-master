If your model supports the **Restore Map** function in the app, please go to **Map Management** and select **Recover Map**\.
 If your model does not support this function, kindly follow the steps below:

1. Remove the DEEBOT from the Charging Dock and turn it **OFF**, then turn it back **ON**\. After that, place it back on the Charging Dock\.

2. Stay a short distance away from the robot to avoid interference\. The DEEBOT will announce, **鈥淢ap is recovering\.鈥?* After a short while, it should confirm **鈥淢ap recovery succeeded\.鈥?* If recovery fails, please repeat the process several times\.

**Gentle Reminder:**
 Maps cannot be restored after performing a **map reset** or a **factory reset** on the robot\.

**Possible Causes of Map Loss:**

1. The robot is moved or lifted during cleaning\. If using multiple maps, always power off the robot first and restart it from the Charging Dock\.

2. The robot gets stuck during operation and is manually carried back to the Charging Dock\.

3. The robot is powered off before the map has been successfully saved\.

4. The Charging Dock is moved, or the surrounding environment near the dock is changed\.

5. Pets follow or interfere with the robot while it is cleaning\.
