## Data acquisition time out issue: 

If you encounter issues such as data submission exceptions, information acquisition timeouts, or the interface not loading, it may be related to the network\. Please try the following steps:

1. **Check Permissions**

    - Go to your phone鈥檚 settings 鈫?application permissions 鈫?ensure the APP has network access\.

    - Alternatively, uninstall and reinstall the APP, and allow all permission prompts during installation\.

2. **Clear Cache \& Restart**

    - Clear the APP鈥檚 cache and restart the APP\.

3. **Switch Network**

    - Turn off Wi\-Fi and switch to mobile data, then exit and reopen the APP\.

    - Or connect your phone to a mobile hotspot and try again\.

4. **Reinstall APP**

    - Delete the APP and re\-download it from the app store\.

5. **Reconnect Device**

    - Remove the device from the APP and reconnect it to the network\.
