1. **Use of Recommended Cleaning Solution**
Please ensure that you are using the [**Ecovacs Deebot Cleaning Solution**](https://www.ecovacs.com/us/deebot-winbot-accessories/deebot-cleaning-solution-1l)\. Using *other *cleaning solutions may affect the softness and fluffiness of the **mop pads** or the **OZMO ROLLER** mop, potentially leading to decreased cleaning performance\.

**If the foam persists**, we kindly suggest the following steps:

1. Temporarily disable the "**Intelligent addition of Cleaning Solution**" feature\.

2. Perform **3 \(three\) full cleaning cycles** \(**Auto Mode**\) without the solution feature enabled\.

3. After completing these cycles, enable the solution dispensing feature again and observe whether the foam issue continues\.
