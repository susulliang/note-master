- Keep the station away from electronics that may cause signal interference, such as routers or TVs\.

- Try to minimize reflections from mirrors or windows that could affect mapping accuracy\.

**To create the map:**

- Make sure the DEEBOT is fully charged before beginning the mapping process\.

- Place the Charging Dock in a central and open location\.

- Avoid walking around the DEEBOT while it is mapping to prevent interference\.

- Please ensure the **"Resumed Clean Mode"** is enabled in the app\.

- Keep doors open and tidy up the space to help the DEEBOT navigate smoothly\.

- For best results, it鈥檚 recommended to complete the initial mapping **without** the mopping system installed\.

**After Mapping:**

- Please run the machine in **Auto Cleaning Mode** again to help refine and update the map so it better fits your home layout\.
