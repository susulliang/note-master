# T30S: Mopping plate extension issue 鎶瑰竷鐩樻棤娉曚几灞?
1. Vacuum\-only mode:

    - The mopping pad plates remain in a raised state, which is normal\.

2. Mop\-only mode:

    - The mopping pad plates stay in a downward state and automatically rise only when encountering carpets, which is normal\.

3. Vacuum \& Mop mode:

    - The mopping pad plates remain in a downward state and rise automatically upon encountering carpets, which is normal\.

4. Mop after vacuum mode:

    - The mopping pad plates are raised during the first cleaning and lowered during the second cleaning\. They rise automatically when encountering carpets, which is normal\.



Additionally, please confirm if you have completed the following troubleshooting steps:

- **Remove and Clean**: Detach the mopping plates and use the multi\-function cleaning tool to clean the sockets and mopping pads\.

- **Reinstall Mopping Plates**: Reattach the mopping plates securely until you hear a 'click,' indicating proper installation\.

- **Power Cycle**: Power off and restart the DEEBOT before testing the cleaning process again\.

- **Switch Sides**: Try swapping the sides of the mopping plates to see if this resolves the issue\.

- **Disable and Re\-enable TruEdge Adaptive Edge Mopping**: Turn off this feature and then reactivate it for testing\. [You can refer to this video for guidance\.](https://youtu.be/xefKGsAYd8E)

- **Check Manual Movement**: Remove the mopping plate and manually move the slot to its original position to ensure smooth movement\.

**Additional Notes:**

- The mopping pad plate extends normally during full cleaning coverage in mop\-only or mop\-after\-vacuum modes\.

- In the raised state, the mopping pad plate retracts during specific scenarios, such as:

    - Quick mapping

    - Navigation or crossing rooms

    - Returning for washing or charging

    - Avoiding obstacles

    - Moving onto carpets

    - Task suspension, completion, or manual movement of the machine
