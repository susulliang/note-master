The slight sound you're hearing is a normal noise produced by the base station motor during the power\-on process\. It indicates that the motor is in a ready\-to\-work state\. During this phase, the motor remains in standby mode, rather than fully sleeping, so it can immediately respond to any commands you send\.

*Please note: This sound is typically only audible in very quiet environments and may vary in volume\.*
