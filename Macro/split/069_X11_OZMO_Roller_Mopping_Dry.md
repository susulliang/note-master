# X11 Series: OZMO Roller Mopping Dry 鍦板疂骞叉嫋 

Thank you for contacting us regarding the issue with your **Deebot X11 OmniCyclone**\. Please follow the steps below to troubleshoot and resolve the issue of the **OZMO Roller Mop** appearing to mop dry\.



Please confirm the **current **cleaning mode in use\. There are 2 scenarios depending on the cleaning mode we set\.



**Scenario 1: Mop After Vac**

Issue: 
When switching from vacuuming to mopping mid\-cycle, the robot may **not **return to the base in time for a water refill to the Deebot's **internal **water tank, leading to inadequate water seepage\.


**Recommended ****Solutions:**

- **Manually start the Mop Wash Process\. **Manually return** **the Deebot to the base station via the app for mop rinsing by starting the **Mop Wash** feature \- ensuring the mopping pads are moisturized accordingly during the **Wash Mop** Process\.

- **Resume the cleaning task** to ensure proper moisture levels for effective mopping performance\.



**Scenario 2: Vac And Mop**



Issue: 

The machine used up all the water in the internal water tank of the robot during cleaning, and the Mop Auto Wash frequency time is not up yet, leading to the machine not refilling the internal water tank yet\. 



**Recommended Solutions:**

- **Manually start the Mop Wash Process\. **Manually return** **the Deebot to the base station via the app for mop rinsing by starting the **Mop Wash** feature \- ensuring the mopping pads are moisturized accordingly during the **Wash Mop** Process\.
