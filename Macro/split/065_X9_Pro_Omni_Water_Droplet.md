# X9 Pro Omni: Water droplet issue 婊氱瓛鎶瑰竷鎺夋按鐝?
### 1\. Water Droplets from the Rear End:

If the water droplets are coming from the rear of the Deebot, near the water inlet/water filling port, this may be due to residual water that can occasionally drip out due to inertia\. This is a normal occurrence\.

![Image](https://internal-api-drive-stream.feishu.cn/space/api/box/stream/download/authcode/?code=NzQ3ZDc5ZmRkOTU1NzM0ZDI4MTgzZmI0OWU3MzA5MGNfYTkzYzE4NWQwMDg5NzdlYTY4YjdhZDExZWU3Njk5ZWFfSUQ6NzUxNDcyMzgxNjAwNjc5NTI2N18xNzg3Nzg2NjY1OjE3ODc4NzMwNjVfVjM)

Solution: We recommend adjusting the Mop Wash Frequency to a longer time gap or selecting a different option\. This will ensure the robot returns to the docking station for mop pad cleaning and water tank refills\.

### 2\. Water Droplets from the Bottom of the Deebot:

If the droplets are coming from the bottom of the Deebot, please check the following:

- Inspect the detachable dirty water box at the rear of the robot\. If it is full, please empty it\.

- Press and hold the right charging pin at the bottom of the docking station for about 3\-5 seconds, then release it to see if the rod lifts properly\.

### 3\. Potential Cleaning Solution Leakage:

- Check the cleaning solution container to ensure it is securely installed into the inlet of the base station\.

- If you're unsure about the installation, please refer to the attached picture to ensure the container is properly sealed\. Push the cleaning solution container all the way into the base to ensure a proper seal\.
