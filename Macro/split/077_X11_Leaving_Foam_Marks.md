- Verify the solution containers are not overfilled\. The liquid level should remain below the round pump piece to prevent leakage or over\-dispensing\.

![Image](https://internal-api-drive-stream.feishu.cn/space/api/box/stream/download/authcode/?code=NjcyMTlhZGRjNjkxZTI1NzVkY2E4MTcwOTIyMmU2NjhfMDcwNjMwYWZlZmYwYzAyNWE4MTAwNGU3MzBkZTlkZmVfSUQ6NzU1MzM2MjIzOTIwMjYwNzEwOF8xNzg3Nzg2NjY1OjE3ODc4NzMwNjVfVjM)

- Please ensure that the Ecovacs Deebot Cleaning Solution is used for the **blue **container, and the Ecovacs **Heavy\-duty** Cleaning Solution is used for the **dark **container\.

- If the solution is overflowing:

    - Pour excess solution back into the bottle until the level is below the round pump piece\.

    - Wipe the tank compartment inside the station to remove residual liquid\.
