# Winbot series: Leaving water streaks 绐楀疂鍦ㄧ獥鎴蜂笂鐣欑棔

Streaks on window glass can be caused by various factors, including mineral deposits, cleaning agent residue, and excess water from the cloth\. To minimize streaking, we recommend the following:

1. **Pre\-remove dust and impurities** from the glass surface before cleaning\.

2. **Wring out the cloth** after wetting it to avoid excess moisture\.

3. Use the **recommended Ecovacs WINBOT Cleaning Solution** to reduce residue\.

4. For windows cleaned infrequently \(e\.g\., quarterly or longer\), be sure to **wash the cloth regularly** and wipe the window multiple times to achieve optimal cleaning and avoid watermarks\.

We are continuously researching advanced cleaning technologies to address this issue more effectively\. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your understanding and support\.
