# X11 Series: OmniCyclone Dust Container Bad Smell 鍩虹珯灏樻《寮傚懗

To address the issue of bad smells in the dust container, the following prevention and solutions are recommended:

1. **Clean Promptly**: Ensure the dust container is cleaned regularly to avoid odors\.
*For detailed instructions on cleaning the dust container, please watch this *[***Dust Container Maintenance Guide Video***](https://youtu.be/JVoStYVcWFc?t=28)*\.*


2. **Address Water Puddles**: If you notice small water puddles on the floor, it鈥檚 recommended to address them before mopping\.


3. **Vacuum Before Mopping**: If there is no visible water accumulation, it's recommended to use the **Mop After Vac** cleaning mode\. This helps prevent moisture from entering the dust container through the **Auto Empty dustbin process** when the Deebot docks, which could cause bad smells\.
