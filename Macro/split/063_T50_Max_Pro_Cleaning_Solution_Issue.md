The T50 PRO OMNI supports the automatic addition of cleaning solution to the clean water tank at a ratio of 1 part solution to 200 parts water\.



If, after refilling the Cleaning Solution Unit with Deebot Cleaning Solution, no solution is being added to the clean water tank, please manually start the Wash Mop process by opening the app, pressing "Enter," selecting "Station," and tapping "Wash Mop\." Once the process is complete, open the cover of the OMNI Station鈥檚 Dirty Water Tank and check if there is any obvious foam in the wastewater from the procedure\.

Important Notes:

- We recommend using ECOVACS Deebot Cleaning Solution for optimal performance\.

- Do not use cleaning fluids from other brands, as they may cause blockages that could prevent the automated cleaning solution from being added successfully\. Additionally, we advise against adding cleaning solution directly into the base station鈥檚 clean water tank while the cleaning solution box is still filled\. You may choose either method, but mixing both is not recommended\.
