# Extended Warranty Cancellation Notice Incident (Aug 24)

> Source: General · Hoi · 2026-08-25

**Incident 事件说明：** 2026-8-24 美东时间遇到的"延保服务被取消"事件。
**Regarding the "extended warranty cancelled" notices seen on August 24 (ET).**

经 DTC 确认，目前是延保服务商的服务器数据进行迁移导致系统误触发了通知，客户的延保订单依然有效。客户咨询的时候我们先人为进行安抚。同时客户也可以联系延保公司确认延保订单的有效性。

Confirmed by DTC: the warranty provider's server data migration caused the system to mistakenly trigger cancellation notices. Customers' extended warranty orders remain valid. Reassure customers when they inquire, and let them know they may also contact the warranty company directly to confirm their order is still valid.
