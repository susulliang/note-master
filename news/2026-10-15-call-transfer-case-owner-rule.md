# Call Transfer Rule — Reassign Case Owner to the Answering Agent

> Source: 流程相关 · Alexia · 2026-10-15

**转电话须知：** 转接电话给 Premium Agent 时，请务必把该电话工单的 **Case Owner 改为实际接听电话的 Agent**，确保工单归属清晰、责任明确。请各位知悉并严格执行。/ When transferring a call to a Premium Agent, always change the ticket's **Case Owner to the agent who actually took the call**, so ownership and responsibility stay clear. Please acknowledge and follow strictly.

**补充（Hoi，同日）：** 任何类型的转 call 都是这个原则——如果接到法语 call 需要转给对应同事，也请把 case owner 转为对应的接线 agent。/ Follow-up (Hoi, same day): this principle applies to every kind of call transfer — e.g. French calls transferred to the designated colleague also require reassigning the case owner to the answering agent.
