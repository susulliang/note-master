# X12S 49mm Obstacle-Crossing Spec Clarification

> Source: 知识点分享群 · Hoi · 2026-08-26

**Issue 问题：** X12S 能越障 49mm？
**Question:** Can the X12S cross 49mm obstacles?

"至高越障 49mm" 指科沃斯地宝 X12S 家族在第一层高低 ≤25mm、第二层高度 ≤24mm 的情况下，最高实现双层合计 49mm 的越障。数据来源为科沃斯实验室，具体以家庭实际使用为准。

"Up to 49mm obstacle crossing" means the DEEBOT X12S family can cross a two-step obstacle totalling up to 49mm — first step ≤25mm and second step ≤24mm. Figure sourced from the ECOVACS lab; real-world results depend on the home environment.
