# WINBOT Cleaning Fluid — No Nano Particles (Clarification)

> Source: 知识点分享群 · Hoi · 2026-09-15

科沃斯窗宝专用清洁液不含纳米颗粒、纳米粉体或纳米功能性材料。

The ECOVACS WINBOT dedicated cleaning fluid contains NO nano particles, nano powders, or nano functional materials.

配方成分为：去离子水、异丙醇、非离子表面活性剂、防腐剂、亮蓝二钠盐、芳樟醇，没有添加纳米粒子/纳米材料 — The formula consists of deionized water, isopropyl alcohol, non-ionic surfactants, preservatives, brilliant blue disodium salt, and linalool. No nano particles or nano materials are added.

部分窗宝机型宣传的 "nano-level spray / 纳米级喷雾"，不是清洁液里面含有纳米颗粒，是主机超声波雾化技术，把液体打散成极细小雾滴，属于液滴物理形态，不是添加纳米固体粉末 — The "nano-level spray" advertised for some WINBOT models does not mean the fluid contains nano particles. It refers to the robot's ultrasonic atomization technology, which breaks the liquid into extremely fine droplets — a physical droplet form, not added solid nano powder.
