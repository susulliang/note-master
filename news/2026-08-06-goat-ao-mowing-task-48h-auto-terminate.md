# GOAT A&O Series — Mowing Tasks Auto-Terminate After 48h Pause

> Source: 知识点分享群 · Hoi · 2026-08-06

为避免 2026 年 A&O 系列与计划表中的割草任务产生冲突，当割草任务暂停超过 48 小时时，系统/App 将自动终止该任务。

To avoid conflicts between the 2026 A&O series and scheduled mowing tasks, when a mowing task is paused for more than 48 hours, the system/app will automatically terminate that task.
